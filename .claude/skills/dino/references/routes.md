# Migrationsrouten

Aus den Profilkarten nachgezeichnet (weiße bzw. rosa Routenlinien). Wandern ist nur in den aktiven Stunden erlaubt (Regel 1.4). Die Reihenfolge der POIs steht unter „Verbindungen“: dort geht die Route von einem POI direkt in den nächsten über.

## Gemischte Herden-Route (Mixed Species Migration Route)

The big shared loop of the hadrosaur herds: from Tallbrush Coasts and Kelp Vale along the volcano coast, down through Stillwater Bog and Coastland Swamp to East Passage and Wollemi, back west over Hollow Hills and the Redwoods.

- **Wer:** Camptosaurus (ganze Route), Iguanodon (ganze Route), Lambeosaurus (ganze Route), Parasaurolophus (ganze Route)
- **POIs:** Tallbrush Coasts, Volcano Islands, Kelp Vale, Coastal Bluffs, Palm Islands, Cedrus Forest, Twisted Forest, Black Fern Hills, Big Tree Overlook, Stillwater Bog, Redwoods, Coastland Swamp, East Passage, Crag Bluffs, Wollemi Forest, Hollow Hills; vorbei an Rotwood Bog, Frog Pond
- **Verbindungen:** Kelp Vale ↔ Tallbrush Coasts; Kelp Vale ↔ Volcano Islands; Coastal Bluffs ↔ Volcano Islands; Big Tree Overlook ↔ Cedrus Forest; Black Fern Hills ↔ Cedrus Forest; Stillwater Bog ↔ Twisted Forest; Cedrus Forest ↔ Stillwater Bog; Coastland Swamp ↔ East Passage; Crag Bluffs ↔ Stillwater Bog; Coastland Swamp ↔ Crag Bluffs; East Passage ↔ Wollemi Forest; Tallbrush Coasts ↔ Volcano Islands; Coastal Bluffs ↔ Palm Islands; Black Fern Hills ↔ Redwoods; Hollow Hills ↔ Redwoods

## Parasaurolophus-Route (Parasaurolophus Migration Route)

The Parasaurolophus' own northern loop (pink on its profile map); each herd keeps its own generational route.

- **Wer:** Camptosaurus (ganze Route), Parasaurolophus (ganze Route)
- **POIs:** Tallbrush Coasts, Dry Fang Canyon, Volcano Islands, Steep Run, Twisted Forest, Cedrus Forest, Mudflats, Coastland Swamp, Cliffedge Falls, Redwoods
- **Verbindungen:** Cedrus Forest ↔ Steep Run; Mudflats ↔ Twisted Forest; Coastland Swamp ↔ Mudflats; Cedrus Forest ↔ Cliffedge Falls; Cliffedge Falls ↔ Redwoods; Dry Fang Canyon ↔ Tallbrush Coasts; Dry Fang Canyon ↔ Steep Run

## Ceratopsier-Route (Ceratopsian Migration Route)

From the northern nesting canyon via Steep Run, Cedrus Forest and Cliff Edge Falls through the Redwoods and Hollow Hills to the Wollemi nesting grounds. Pachyrhinosaurus, Styracosaurus and Albertaceratops share it as a mixed ceratopsian herd.

- **Wer:** Albertaceratops (ganze Route), Pachyrhinosaurus (ganze Route), Psittacosaurus (ganze Route), Styracosaurus (ganze Route)
- **POIs:** Steep Run, Cedrus Forest, Cliffedge Falls, Redwoods, Hollow Hills; vorbei an Redwood Wind, Frog Pond
- **Verbindungen:** Cedrus Forest ↔ Steep Run; Cedrus Forest ↔ Cliffedge Falls; Cliffedge Falls ↔ Redwoods; Hollow Hills ↔ Redwoods

## Eotriceratops-Route (Eotriceratops Migration Route)

The matriarchies' route between their seasonal nesting grounds in the north and the south, along the western forests (Dry Fang Canyon, Black Fern Hills, Redwoods) with a branch over Cedrus Forest to Stillwater Bog. Psittacosaurus follow it too.

- **Wer:** Eotriceratops (ganze Route), Psittacosaurus (ganze Route)
- **POIs:** Dry Fang Canyon, Black Fern Hills, Cedrus Forest, Big Tree Overlook, Stillwater Bog, Redwoods, Crag Bluffs; vorbei an Mud Perch, Littleclaw Pond, Redwood Wind
- **Verbindungen:** Black Fern Hills ↔ Dry Fang Canyon; Big Tree Overlook ↔ Cedrus Forest; Cedrus Forest ↔ Stillwater Bog; Black Fern Hills ↔ Redwoods

## Sauropoden-Route (Sauropod Migration Route)

The giants' corridors: the western route from Dry Fang Canyon over Steep Run and Cedrus Forest to Stillwater Bog and Cliff Edge Falls (Argentinosaurus and Amargasaurus use this part), and the Apatosaurus branch from Tallbrush Coasts over the Volcano Islands nursery to the Mudflats.

- **Wer:** Amargasaurus (nur ein Teilstück), Apatosaurus (ganze Route), Argentinosaurus (nur ein Teilstück)
- **POIs:** Tallbrush Coasts, Dry Fang Canyon, Volcano Islands, Steep Run, Black Fern Hills, Twisted Forest, Cedrus Forest, Cliffedge Falls, Stillwater Bog, Redwoods; vorbei an Dark Pond
- **Verbindungen:** Dry Fang Canyon ↔ Tallbrush Coasts; Tallbrush Coasts ↔ Volcano Islands; Dry Fang Canyon ↔ Steep Run; Black Fern Hills ↔ Steep Run; Twisted Forest ↔ Volcano Islands; Cedrus Forest ↔ Steep Run; Cedrus Forest ↔ Stillwater Bog; Cliffedge Falls ↔ Redwoods; Cedrus Forest ↔ Cliffedge Falls
- **Amargasaurus nur durch:** Steep Run, Black Fern Hills, Cedrus Forest, Stillwater Bog, Cliffedge Falls, Redwoods
- **Argentinosaurus nur durch:** Dry Fang Canyon, Steep Run, Black Fern Hills, Cedrus Forest, Stillwater Bog, Cliffedge Falls, Redwoods

## Leedsichthys-Route (Leedsichthys Migration Route)

The great sea loop through the north-eastern waters around the Coastal Bluffs, Palm Islands and Stonebed Shoal.

- **Wer:** Leedsichthys (ganze Route), Spinosaurus (Saltwater) (laut Profiltext: Tours the Leedsichthys loop peacefully outside its own sea territory (profile variant).)
- **POIs:** Coastal Bluffs, Kelp Vale, Abyssal Depths, Volcano Islands, Big Tree Overlook, Twisted Forest, Palm Islands, Cedrus Forest, Stonebed Shoal, Mudflats, Stillwater Bog, Coastland Swamp; vorbei an Rocky Lake, Cedar Valley
- **Verbindungen:** Coastal Bluffs ↔ Kelp Vale; Abyssal Depths ↔ Coastal Bluffs; Kelp Vale ↔ Volcano Islands; Coastal Bluffs ↔ Volcano Islands; Twisted Forest ↔ Volcano Islands; Abyssal Depths ↔ Palm Islands; Palm Islands ↔ Twisted Forest; Big Tree Overlook ↔ Cedrus Forest; Stonebed Shoal ↔ Twisted Forest; Big Tree Overlook ↔ Stillwater Bog; Mudflats ↔ Stonebed Shoal; Mudflats ↔ Twisted Forest; Cedrus Forest ↔ Stillwater Bog; Coastland Swamp ↔ Mudflats; Stillwater Bog ↔ Twisted Forest; Mudflats ↔ Stillwater Bog; Coastland Swamp ↔ Stillwater Bog
