# Yutyrannus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season 1-5 🥚 ☀️ Dry Season: 1 🥚 Yutyrannus must roll for the number of eggs they receive in rainy season. You must link your roll in your nesting post. Yutyrannus  nest in dens with multiple chambers. Once all offspring have died or reach sub-adult, Yutyrannus may court and nest again. Yutyrannus may form same-sex pairs, but may not nest. Both same-sex and opposite sex pairs may adopt during the Rainy Season.
