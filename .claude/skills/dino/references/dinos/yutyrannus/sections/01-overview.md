# Yutyrannus – Overview

Large Cathemeral Terrestrial Carnivore During the Dry Season, Yutyrannus exclusively hunt at night. However, they may take part in all other activities during the day. Yutyrannus form temporary bonds that may develop into lifelong bonds after 5 successful nests or mutual choice. Yutyrannus are territorial in full groups, claiming large territories consisting of an entire POI. Otherwise, they are sedentary. Yutyrannus have a tolerant nature. Yutyrannus are not scavengers. Yutyrannus tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.
