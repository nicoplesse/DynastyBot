# Yutyrannus – Meeting Yutyrannus Outside of the Group

Unless nesting or in a full group, Yutyrannus are generally tolerant of other Yutyrannus. Yutyrannus will only allow unrelated individuals to join their groupings in the Rainy Season, if there is room for them. In the Dry Season, clowders do not accept unrelated individuals (with the exception of already formed mates to the related individuals). Even if the group is not full, they will simply be nipped at and driven away from resting or nesting dens claimed by the group. Colonies will only accept familiar unrelated individuals. Unknown individuals will be driven away in the same manner. In the Rainy Season, males may attempt to put on performances in order to impress potential group members. Mollies, on the other hand, approach and coo submissively, waiting for an affectionate call in return to be accepted. If the clowder or colony opts to not allow these individuals into their group, they will turn their backs and ignore the other Yutyrannus. This individual typically picks up the sign quickly and will leave the area, in order to avoid being aggressed. Should the individual not leave, the grouped Yutyrannus will become irritated and warn the individual with aggressive calls before nipping in its direction. If this is still ignored, Yutyrannus will bite the individual until they flee from the group’s line of sight and will not be chased or killed.
