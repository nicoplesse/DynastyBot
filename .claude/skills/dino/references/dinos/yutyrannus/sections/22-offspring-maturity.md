# Yutyrannus – Offspring Maturity

At Sub-Adult, kits have now developed all of the skills they need to be successful as adults. If there is room with their current grouping, they may stay. Otherwise, they disperse with their siblings or alone to find courtship opportunities themselves. If it is close to dry season, parents with kits often disperse from a group with them in order to maintain closeness with them as the harsher dry season nears, where Yutyrannus are much less sociable.
