# Yutyrannus – Engagement Limits

Up to 4 Yutyrannus may enter an engagement. Yutyrannus are territorial in full groupings. Up to 4 adults may work together to keep their territory free of other carnivorous species within their POI other than Yutyrannus. If the intruders are Yutyrannus, the entire grouping (up to 6 adults) may engage. Up to 4 Yutyrannus may group together to challenge for a body or to challenge other predators away from a den used for nesting when not a part of a full group. Yutyrannus will challenge small tier and above carnivores away from the nesting site, if they approach with 5 adult Yutyrannus bodylengths. Yutyrannus may hunt up to Medium tier when solo, Large tier when in a pair, and Apex tier when in a hunting party of 3 or 4. Two adolescent individuals may assist with hunts. If a kit dies, the fight must be forfeit. Two adolescent individuals may also work together to hunt independently, but may only hunt species smaller than them without the assistance of an adult. Yutyrannus can only use a maximum of 4 of the following buffs during an engagement: Primal Mend, Healing Call and Coagulating Call. This is a limit of 4 total for the entire party, not per individual. They can use an unlimited number of combat calls in an engagement. One individual in the hunting party should be assigned to using heal calls. The rest may use combat calls. It is the responsibility of the individual using the heal calls to track the number of heal calls used, in group chat or otherwise. Preferred Prey Yutyrannus, with 3 or 4 adult individuals in their hunting party, enjoy hunting Apex carnivores such as Tyrannosaurus, Giganotosaurus, and Tyrannotitan. However, they may hunt other prey if available. Yutyrannus may always attempt to hunt protected and unprotected offspring. Offspring that is considered Apex (protected by an Apex parent) may not be hunted without 3 adults in the hunting party. In the dry season, Yutyrannus prefer to perform easier hunts, and will target those who are sleeping, injured, albino, or individuals of Adolescent or below first. Yutyrannus are not cannibalistic and do not feed from the bodies of individuals they are bonded with. They do not have to claim Yutyrannus bodies, but may do so to defend their corpses.
