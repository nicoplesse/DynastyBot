# Yutyrannus – Physical Dimorphism

Yutyrannus do not exhibit physical dimorphism. High t and low t individuals are not observed in this species. ♀️ A female, known as a molly, introduces herself to new individuals via crouching and heart calling. ♂️ A male, known as a tom, introduces himself to new individuals by making a chortling sound :claw_emote:, then displaying a dance :question_emote: . Individuals may not use the incorrect greeting for their sex to trick others. OPTIONAL: Yutyrannus shift their pelts from rainy season into dry season. During the rainy season, Yutyrannus have more contrasting colors and bright patterns on their pelt; and in the dry season, their pelts are duller in order to better blend in with their environment. OPTIONAL DISEASE: Mange is a skin-and-feather disease commonly found in orphaned Yutyrannus, particularly those who experienced difficult or neglectful upbringings. This is indicated by using the Lack of Feathers subspecies upon developing the disease, and by using exclusively dull skins, regardless of the season. An individual may heal from mange with hot spring water baths and assisted grooming from other Yutyrannus, therefore allowing them to shift pelts with the season and display color on their pelt. Over time, their feathers may grow back in full, and they then may shed back out of the Lack of Feathers subspecies.
