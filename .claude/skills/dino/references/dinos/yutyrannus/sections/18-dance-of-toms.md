# Yutyrannus – Dance of Toms

The Dance of Toms begins with both toms interested in a molly posturing at one another and issuing a series of chortling (the claw emote) calls three times. This draws attention to the battling toms as well as gives the molly time to find a suitable place to watch. Toms then begin to dance, stretching out their arms, lifting their tails, moving their heads from side to side and bowing (? emote). Some males may twirl, turn, or step side to side or back and forth in order to put on a more dazzling display. Toms will also add to their performances by using their large range of vocal calls; some high pitched and soft; others loud and screeching. Each tom has their own performance that they put on for mollies; typically one that stays largely the same throughout their entire life, unless it sets them on a streak of failure. At times, toms may get close to one another in an attempt to intimidate the other and throw off their dance– if one backs off, misses one of his moves, or nips the other tom, he immediately becomes less attractive to onlooking mollies. When a molly is ready to make her decision, she will issue the chortling grunts herself. The contesting toms both issue one final display before stopping and turning to her to await her decision. The molly approaches the tom of her choice and affectionately calls the male, nuzzling up to the tom’s cheek. He will return the gesture eagerly, and then give her another dance - which she will happily join. Toms who have lost the dance may do so again with no disadvantage, especially for any mollies nearby who seemed to enjoy his antics.
