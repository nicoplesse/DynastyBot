# Yutyrannus – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Albino and melanistic individuals are not treated any differently from other individuals in the group. Albino individuals are more likely to develop mange. When confronted by another Yutyrannus when in a group and nesting, a Yutyrannus’s first instinct is to find a way to make themselves bigger, jumping up onto a rock or nearby object to make them taller and posturing. With aggressive hisses, loud calls, and spitting, all Yutyrannus within a group will fiercely defend their nesting or resting den, and will refuse to let other Yutyrannus into the nest or near their offspring. If the initial posturing and aggression do not deter the other Yutyrannus, a territory challenge begins. In full clowders or colonies, Yutyrannus will not tolerate any other Yutyrannus in the POI. The entire group may work together in order to drive off intruders, with some opting to fall behind if there are kits in a den nearby, and the intruding group is fleeing. This is a territory challenge in which all Sub/Adult Yutyrannus on both sides may take part in. The losing side may be escorted out loudly by all or several of the winning individuals to ensure they leave the POI, though this is not mandatory. Those escorting do not attack further. Conflicts between groups in the dry season are more frequent and significantly more aggressive, often times launching immediately into a challenge. Groups will still posture at lone individuals before moving to challenge them out. When in a full group, Yutyrannus will not tolerate any other carnivores in the POI, other than Tiny tiers. Yutyrannus enjoy and tolerate the presence of Compsognathus and Rhamphornycus, as they assist in preening and the maintaining of one’s feathers. Yutyrannus do not tolerate any other carnivores other than Rhamphornhycus and Compsognathus near their nesting dens.
