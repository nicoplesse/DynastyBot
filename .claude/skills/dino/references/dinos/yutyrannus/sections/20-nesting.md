# Yutyrannus – Nesting

Dry Season: In the Dry Season, Yutyrannus have established dens or caves where they spend a majority of their time to avoid the heat of the day. They will nest exclusively in these dim-lit dens, members of the group opting to spread through the chambers in an attempt to share as little bodyheat as possible. Rainy Season: Mollies lead wet season groupings to a den in their favored location, often one where she has nested before or where she grew up. Mollies communally make the decision of where to nest, and prefer to pick dens with multiple chambers so each individual can raise her offspring separately in their own space. Toms will begin hunting frequently to provide the mollies and kits with plenty of food. In Clowders, all other individuals also take part in this behavior to support their dominant pair.
