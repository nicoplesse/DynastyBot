# Iguanodon – Defending from Predators

Up to 3 Sub/Adult Individuals can step forward and defend their herd from being hunted. This is almost always a group of 3 bulls, but cows are happy to act as defenders if not enough bulls are available. The 3 defenders can chase down and attack predators hunting them so long as the defenders do not leave line of sight of the herd. They can also ignore forfeits by predators that engaged with them and can chase them down to kill them, so long as the attackers do not leave line of sight of the herd. The rest of the herd bunches together tightly, forming a thick group to slowly back away from the engagement. They remain together, and do not chase down the predators nor follow the defenders, but can land a hit if the predator approaches them. A mother defending her young is excluded from the engagement limits. See more under Predator Reaction.
