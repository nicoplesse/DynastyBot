# Iguanodon – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Iguanodon usually tolerate albino and melanistic individuals, but some personalities may not tolerate them at all. Whilst they won't expel them from the herd, they may show shunning behaviors towards them, such as moving away when they approach, and bullying them off of food bushes. Iguanodon are relatively passive and tolerant with others of their species, though individual personalities do vary and some - particularly cows - can be somewhat cantankerous. However, this never escalates beyond a kick or a smack here or there.
