# Iguanodon – Overview

Large Diurnal Terrestrial Herbivore Iguanodon form harem bonds. Iguandon are nomadic but become temporarily territorial over food and water resources. Iguanodon have a tolerant nature, but are aggressive when defending food and water resources. Iguanodon may group with: Bonapartenykus, Camptosaurus, Citipati, Lambeosaurus, Parasaurolophus, and Struthiomimus. They may not defend these species, even when grouped with them. Iguanodon usually tolerate albino and melanistic individuals, but certain individuals may not.
