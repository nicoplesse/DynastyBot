# Spinosaurus (Freshwater) – Suchomimus tolerance & adoption

Suchomimus are sometimes adopted by Spinosaurus, due to their similar resemblance to Spinosaurus young. Lacking a large sail, Suchomimus are regarded as stunted young. Some individuals readily accept Suchomimus nearby, while others push them out. Adoption chances for Suchomimus vary, and both pairbonded and solitary Spinosaurus have been noted to adopt. The likelihood of acceptance depends on the Spinosaurus' tolerance of young in general. Once adopted, Spinosaurus dote on their new charges, treating the Suchomimus as true offspring and bringing them food. They defend them fiercely from any threats - even when the Suchomimus reaches maturity. Even Sub-Adult or Adult Suchomimus are mistaken for stunted young, with Spinosaurus continuing to nurture them without realizing their true maturity. This parental bond either continues indefinitely, or until the parent(s) grow tired of their "offspring's" continuous demands, and drives them out of their territory.
