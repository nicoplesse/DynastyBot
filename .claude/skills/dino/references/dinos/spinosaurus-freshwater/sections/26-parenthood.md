# Spinosaurus (Freshwater) – Parenthood

As eggs hatch, the mother lures them into the water, while the father keeps watch over his mate and brood. Parental care is primarily the responsibility of the mother - who must wrangle very attached and clingy young. The supportive father protects his pairbond and young, casting a watchful eye over his family and fiercely defending them from threats. He steps in to care for his young when the mother needs to break away, and frequently brings them pieces of food in her absence. Both parents are exceptionally affectionate and loving towards their offspring, who are doted on and nuzzled at regular intervals. At adolescence, offspring become more independent from their mother, and fathers sometimes find their young trailing behind them, keen to mimic his behaviors.
