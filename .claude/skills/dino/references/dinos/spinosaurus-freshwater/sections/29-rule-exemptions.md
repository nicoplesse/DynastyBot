# Spinosaurus (Freshwater) – Rule Exemptions

Spinosaurus does not receive a species timer after a territory or resource challenge if Spinosaurus wins. However, if they lose a resource challenge with a terrestrial, they must move out of line of sight of the resource. 6.2 Spinosaurus is not exempt from rule 6.9. When challenged by a territorial terrestrial species on land, Spinosaurus may retreat into the water - but may not enter swimming animation. If  swimming during a challenge while in a territory challenge with a terrestrial species for the land, Spinosaurus forfeits the challenge and must therefore tolerate the challenger drinking from its water source and existing within 10 body-lengths of the water. Spinosaurus has exceptions to 4.2 and may not hunt in unpreferred POI unless the target is a Tylosaurus. Spinosaurus are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects. If the Spinosaurus is successful in the contest, then they claim the body down as well as the territory, and will pursue the crocodilian until it flees from the claimed territory. This challenge is a territory challenge, not a body contest. 6.7.4 Spinosaurus does not respect the forfeit calls of rival Spinosaurus during a territory challenge. [6.5]
