# Spinosaurus (Freshwater) – Invasive species: Deinosuchus and Sarcosuchus

Spinosaurus within claimed territory readily kill crocodilian species to remove direct competition with prized resource locations. If in a pairbond, both Spinosaurus will work together to kill Deinosuchus and Sarcosuchus that enter prime territory locations. Even if these invasive species are actively engaged with something else, Spinosaurus will intervene and take advantage of their rivals' distraction. When opting to drive out these rivals, Spinosaurus finishes ongoing engagements and recovers strength before confrontation. While they treat these species with utmost disdain, a Spinosaurus will not sacrifice themselves if a successful outcome seems unlikely. If they opt not to engage with these species, Spinosaurus must leave the water source, returning only when willing to drive them out. Outside of claimable territory, Spinosaurus watches these species with disdain. With no reason to challenge rivals from undesirable water, Spinosaurus either remain to watch the crocodilians pass, or leave the area themselves. Choosing to ignore rather than risk wounding themselves unnecessarily.
