# Spinosaurus (Freshwater) – Orphaned Behavior (Wild Spawn)

Orphaned Spinosaurus are shy and fearful, instinctively wandering near water in search of food. If lucky enough to find an adult Spinosaurus, they meekly call their new parent and swim or sit by their tail. If an unwilling adult is approached, the youngling will be promptly shoved away and chased from the water - a clear sign the orphan is unwanted. Even when rejected, the orphan will stay in the area, too nervous to explore further without a caregiver. Spinosaurus spend most of their lives either in water or just beside it, and are well adapted to different water types. Within a lifetime, Spinosaurus will exist both in fresh water and salt water. Over time, some Spinosaurus develop a particular preference - with some individuals ultimately preferring a lazy lifestyle touring saltwater areas, while most possessively claim territory in freshwater lakes and rivers. With their narrow jaws and conical teeth, they are expert fishers, capable of grabbing slippery fish and taking down semi-aquatic prey. All Spinosaurus bask frequently, aiming to direct their large sails directly in line of the sun's rays. The blood vessels on the sail help control their body temperature, either absorbing or releasing heat as needed. However, Spinosaurus must be careful not to stay out of water for too long. Their skin is prone to drying out and cracking, and require frequent dips to keep themselves hydrated. Spinosaurus claim a medium sized stretch of water within key hotspot areas, which they patrol and viciously defend from others - including other Spinosaurus. They tolerate other species in the area, but become highly aggressive when their territory is approached. They tolerate all species on land, but often follow along from their water source, tracking along the waterline to make sure the individual doesn't dare to touch it. More information can be found in Cross-Species Interaction. Spinosaurus can be fickle over their water source, and move on to new territories when resources are low.
