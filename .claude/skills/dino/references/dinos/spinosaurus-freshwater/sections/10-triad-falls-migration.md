# Spinosaurus (Freshwater) – Triad Falls Migration

Instinctively, Spinosaurus seeking a new partner will travel to Triad Falls. This area serves as a neutral gathering place, where Spinosaurus gather in large numbers to bask, court and lazily relax. Territorial behavior is suppressed within Triad Falls and the area is not considered suitable territory to claim. All Spinosaurus loosely tolerate one another here - only protecting their personal space if it is invaded. Spinosaurus residing in this area ignore terrestrial species drinking - but do not tolerate Tylosaurus or semi-aquatics sharing the same pond. Individuals will take turns pushing out Tylosaurus or semi-aquatics attempting to enter the water, or bask on the beaches. This location is a safe haven for Sub-Adults who - after being pushed from their parents' territories - migrate here to mature into adults and hopefully secure their first pairbond.
