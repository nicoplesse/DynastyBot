# Spinosaurus (Freshwater) – Protecting their water territory

Spinosaurus are selfishly aggressive to almost everything that approaches their water when within claimable territory areas. When not hunting, and another creature approaches (within 10 body lengths of the waters edge), Spinosaurus becomes alert and regards the intruder with deep suspicion. Shadowing them along the waterline, Spinosaurus vigilantly watches and makes sure to display their sail fully. If the rival is undeterred by this and continues approaching, Spinosaurus will start roaring aggressively :threaten_call:, issuing a firm warning of their presence. If the intruder leaves the territory borders and moves on, it is ignored. However, if the intruder continues to approach (after issuing territory challenge calls and 10 seconds after the last aggressive call is made), Spinosaurus responds immediately by lunging from the water to drive it away. Should the Spinosaurus lose the engagement, it has no choice but to tolerate the victor's presence for 15 minutes before attempting to drive the creature out again. During a challenge with a territorial terrestrial, if Spinosaurus retreats into the water and enters the swimming animation, this is an automatic forfeit of the land surrounding it's water territory and must now abide by resource challenge rules. Should the intruder flee or retreat from the Spinosaurus' territory (10 body lengths from the waters edge), Spinosaurus calms and does not pursue, satisfied that the threat has been repelled.
