# Spinosaurus (Freshwater) – Established Strong Bonded Pairs

Strong bonded pairbonds: These animals often choose to nest with one established partner frequently. They are closely bonded, but may choose to form pair bonds with others if their mate is not around or online. When Spinosaurus already have an established pair, nesting is a mutual decision and the pair do not migrate to Triad Falls or Wollemi Forest, instead, they court within their claimed territory. Courtship between a strongly bonded pair is acted within the water, with both individuals circling each other and calling softly in a personalized dance. This underwater display is unique to each pair and some are more elaborate the longer they have been paired together. In the unfortunate situation where a strongly bonded partner is caught with another, the previous mate can choose to ignore the pairing and wait for their partner to return to them, or promptly sever the pairbond. When crossing paths in the future, the partner that was caught "cheating" is promptly chased away and treated the same as an unknown Spinosaurus.
