# Spinosaurus (Freshwater) – Nesting

Nesting is only done from within claimable territory areas. Pairs will spend great time and effort to secure the best locations for their future young. Females construct the nest, while the male watches over protectively. Once built, they both settle around the eggs - or practice doing so, if no eggs are laid - with one leaving to patrol the area while the other keeps the eggs - absent or otherwise - warm.
