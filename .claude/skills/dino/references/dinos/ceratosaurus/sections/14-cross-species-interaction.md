# Ceratosaurus – Cross-Species Interaction

Ceratosaurus are not territorial, but will defend the area around their den-site from all other dinosaurs when they have whelps. Small to medium species are hunted when possible, while larger dinosaurs are mostly ignored unless the pack is very hungry. Carnivores of a similar size are particularly disliked, but are tolerated if the pack is not hungry. They will keep an eye on them and hunt at the next opportunity. Ceratosaurus that are challenged by territorial dinosaurs take great pleasure in displaying themselves aggressively towards the challenging species. A challenge is met with equal force if the pack matches the strength of the opponents. Ceratosaurus are a confident, ferocious species that show bloodthirsty tendencies, and enjoy combat if given the opportunity. Even if winning the fight seems unlikely, the Ceratosaurus pack will still call loudly and may mock-charge or use mock-bites from a distance before fleeing the challenge.
