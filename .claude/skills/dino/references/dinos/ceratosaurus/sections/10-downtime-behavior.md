# Ceratosaurus – Downtime Behavior

At dawn, the pack gathers at a chosen sheltered location known as a den-site. They sleep in close proximity to one another through the daylight hours, and if offspring are born they are raised at this site. One individual in the pack may opt to stay awake briefly to watch over the rest of the pack while they rest. During the daytime, Ceratosaurus may challenge for bodies and move around, as long as they make an effort to rest at some point throughout the day.
