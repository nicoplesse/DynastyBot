# Utahraptor – Pack Rank Changing:

These ranks change only when the Alpha pair shifts from their position as family lead. When both Alphas either die or retire as elders, the Beta they choose takes over as the new Alpha, along with their mate if they are bonded. If there's no clear choice when this time comes, or both Alphas have died, Betas may challenge each other for leadership. These challenges are often peaceful - with the pack voting on the new leadership based on who has the most allies in the pack to support them. If there is no clear winner, the two Betas with the most allies compete to prove who is most capable to lead the family. Their mates, ranking as unrelated, watch nervously from the sidelines. Their relation to the bloodline does not permit them to fight with their mate. This challenge is fought until the loser reaches deathscars - an embarrassing proof that one is not fit to lead the large family.
