# Utahraptor – Exile of Unrelateds:

Should an unrelated's mate die, and they have no offspring within the pack to support them, the Alphas will exile them from the pack. If the Alphas wish to exile an unrelated, they risk the mate and/or offspring leaving with the exiled member. When an unrelated's mate dies, they are not permitted to bring another unrelated into the pack. They must disperse, or court a relative in the pack.
