# Utahraptor – Nesting Limits

The parent related to the bloodline will create a nest and nesting post. In the case of an alpha pair, either parent may act as the primary caregiver to the nest. The first clutch to any pair produces only 1 egg (🥚). No nesting multiplier items may be used on the first clutch between a pair, or on any pairing containing an albino parent. Albinos will always face fertility issues and their clutches will never exceed 1 egg (🥚).
