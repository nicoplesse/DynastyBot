# Utahraptor – When a new Alpha pair takes over:

The Beta who won will assume their position as new Alpha. If they are bonded, their unrelated mate is promoted into the Alpha rank with them. If they have no mate when they become Alpha, whomever they court will automatically be promoted to Alpha rank and the pair begins their new reign together. Any children related to both male and female in the new Alpha pair are promoted to Beta rank. Previous offspring born to a deceased partner become Omega. Offspring must be related to both the Alphas to become Betas. Siblings and grandchildren to the new Alpha become Deltas. Any of their children become Omegas. Generations further than that are exiled. Parents to the new Alphas remain as former Alphas, ranking just below their new Alpha child proudly.
