# Utahraptor – Exile from the Pack

The Alpha rank has exclusive rights to exile packmates. If caught in any of the following situations, an individual, or both individuals in the Alpha rank will readily force out the offenders. Former Alphas are never exiled from the pack and take no part in pushing out packmates. However, Former Alphas - understanding that the family is becoming too distantly related to them - will willingly disperse from the pack when a new Alpha takes over that is not their child. New alphas push out excess relations to focus on their core bloodline, exiling members who are not their former alpha parents, siblings and offspring. Family members in the Omega rank are never permitted to breed. When caught trying to nest, they are exiled along with their mate and offspring immediately, as they pose a threat to the new bloodline. Offspring to any rank under the Alpha pair must be presented to at least one of the alphas and be accepted into the pack. Packmates that are caught with offspring not yet presented to an Alpha will be exiled immediately along with their offspring, regardless of age. While these nests may occur, being caught with offspring when an Alpha was not present at the time of the nest results in exile. If only one Alpha is present, the offspring must be reintroduced again to the other Alpha when they are next online. Siblings, offspring, mates or new grandchildren with unfavorable feather patterns or colors are exiled once they reach sub adulthood. Melanistic individuals integrate well into dark-feathered family packs and Albinos are only permitted if the pack’s new colors are light in coloration.
