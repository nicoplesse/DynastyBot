# Utahraptor – Physical Dimorphism

Utahraptor has physical subspecies dimorphism. This profile does have high and low T individuals. Individuals conforming to this can have any subspecies, but must roleplay as the gender they are. This means they cannot nest with another of the same sex. ♀️ Females lack brow feathers. ♂️ Males have brow feathers. Physical dimorphism in Utahraptor is not mandatory, but those not conforming may struggle in courtship and be mistaken for the opposite sex.
