See 📜・server-guideOPTIONAL - Illnesses & Injuries for more information. 4.4 Infinite Nests The adults must still remain within the 20 limit and the exception for "breaking family grouping" limits is only allowed for those involved in the infinite nest. This applies through adol and changes once they hit sub-adult. Example: The pack has 12 adults. One pair is infinite nesting and another pair is normal nesting. The normal nest children would be with the group of adults and must be accounted for in the count of 20. The infinite nest children will form their own group and not account towards the 20 (please read further for more clarification on what occurs once they hit sub) Once the children of the infinite nest hit sub-adult they must do the following: Undergo skin check immediately, regardless of time of day in game. Once accepted they will count towards the 20 sub/adult count of the pack and now fall within the limits of "one group per pack" In order to remain within the 20 limits; the pack will have to figure out who will be logging or the entire pack logs.
