# Utahraptor – Newly Formed Pairs

If starting their own pack, the process is relatively simple. The pair will move away to find a territory to claim, and usually start with nesting - doing so to become the Alphas in a brand new family pack and beginning their own bloodline. Individuals who both belong to a family pack can opt to start their own with their new partner at any time. If they choose to leave their old pack and form a new one, they have no obligation to say goodbye, but may individually approach their old family members with their new mate to bid farewell. However, many individuals choose to remain in their family pack, bringing their new partner back as an unrelated. The choice of which family to return to is down to the new couple - and may end up with them opting to go with the second choice if the first family does not accept their new mate. If they choose to return home to a family pack, both individuals will look for a suitable gift to gain favor. Food is always the most guaranteed to please, and unsuitable gifts may ruin an unrelated's chances of being accepted into the family. When returning to the dominant pair with their new mate, all pack members will come to inspect the new addition, circling and sniffing them. The family member will stay very close to their mate, who must remain crouched and submissive. The new pair drop their gifts for the Alpha by their feet, and resume crouching and cooing beside each other as they are inspected. The Alpha has the only say in this choice, and will always approach the partner before deciding whether or not they are a good fit for the pack. Each Alpha pair has their own set of rules and standards to decide whether an individual will be accepted, and the decision is at their discretion. If the Alpha's mate is not available to oversee the introduction, their mate will introduce the new pair to them and help soothe the stress of seeing an unfamiliar Utahraptor in the pack. If both alphas are unavailable, new mates are promptly chased out the territory by the whole family. Should the alpha accept the new member, he or she will be greeted with affectionate calls and shaking of feathers, a clear sign and indication of their acceptance. The new partner joins the family pack as an Unrelated.
