# Yunnanosaurus – Group Limits

Dry Season Groupings 12 Sub/Adult Females including one lead female Unlimited Offspring Unlimited Adoptees This group is known as a rove. 12 Sub/Adult Males including one lead male No offspring or adoptees This group is known as a ramble. Rainy Season Groupings 8 Sub/Adults, comprising up to 2 males and up to 6 females, one of which is known as the lead female Unlimited Offspring Yunnanosaurus do not adopt during the Rainy Season. This group is known as a clade.
