# Yunnanosaurus – Overview

Large Cathemeral Terrestrial Herbivore During the Rainy Season, Yunnanosaurus are territorial and form medium territories, have an aggressive nature, and form harem bonds. During the Dry Season, Yunnanosaurus are nomadic, have a tolerant nature, and form temporary pairbonds Yunnanosaurus do not group with any other species. Yunnanosaurus tolerate melanistic individuals, but shun albino and overly pale individuals.
