# Pycnonemosaurus – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Albino individuals are never tolerated by other Pycnonemosaurus; they will always be driven away and never accepted into a group. The overly pale individuals are seen as fragile, and would not cope with the constant rough play. Pycnonemosaurus are not intentionally cruel to one another, and prefer to exclude sickly appearing Albinos because they cannot socialise in the same way as healthy ones can. Albinos are rejected by shoving, pushing them out of the nearby area. If the Albino is persistent, the drove will leave the POI or may resort to actual attacks to prove their point - not aiming to kill, just to push them out. Melanistic individuals are treated no differently from non-mutated individuals. Pycnonemosaurus rarely escalate their rough play into an actual fight. Aggressive interactions are seen as playful, and responded to in kind. Rough play includes biting, shoving, charging, and chasing one another about. Those that become injured will pull away to rest and recharge before their health gets too low.
