# Pycnonemosaurus – Courtship

Only the dominant boar within a drove is permitted to court the sows in the group. He will seek out an interesting item to present to the rest of the group. This item is often a small curiosity; a mushroom, a pinecone, a shell from the beach, for example. Anything small and interesting that prompts the females to come and investigate. The dominant boar places his item in a clear space, and calls loudly to attract attention to himself. When the sows notice, they come and look over his chosen artifact and watch him as he displays to them by dancing - with swaying head bobs. Subordinate boars that see the dominant boar displaying for his sows will sometimes try and copy his behavior - choosing a trinket of his own, and laying it carefully on open ground. Dominant boars that witness this are extremely confused, and take the trinket to add to their own, giving the subordinate a shove out of the way to prove his point. Subordinate boars are not permitted to court, and the dominant boar will shove any other boars away from sows if they attempt it. Sows that are interested in courting may be impressed by this display, and will mimic his head swaying behavior, stepping closer and calling affectionately towards him. Multiple sows may all accept courting advances from the dominant boar at the same time.
