# Tylosaurus – Engagement Limits

Up to 2 Tylosaurus may enter an engagement. Up to 1 adolescent may assist with a hunt. However, if the adolescent reaches half health, they will flee. They do not assist in defence. Tylosaurus may hunt up to Apex tier. Tylosaurus will not hunt adult Spinosaurus or Deinosuchus unless desperate hunger (35%), and will only hunt adult Apex terrestrial carnivores when they are swimming in water. Kaiwheka, Eurhinosaurus, and Leedsichthys must be the first targets in the POI when hunting. Parasaurolophus and Lambeosaurus are the next preferred targets. Solo Adolescent Tylosaurus do not hunt other species. Preferred Prey Kaiwheka, Eurhinosaurus, and Leedsichthys must be hunted before all other prey in the POI. Parasaurolophus and Lambeosaurus are the next preferred targets when they are on the beach or when swimming. If neither of these prey options are available, Tylosaurus can hunt other species. Tylosaurus do not eat the corpses of members within their matriline if they die, but may feed from corpses outside the matriline. They do not have to claim the body down of a member of their matriline. Tylosaurus will only hunt adult Apex terrestrial carnivores if they are swimming in water. Tylosaurus will not hunt Spinosaurus or Deinosuchus unless 35% hunger, but may respond to territory challenges from them. They may hunt their offspring freely. Tylosaurus may hunt creatures within 1 bodylength from the water, but will always target those they can easily clamp first, in order to drag them into the water. Tylosaurus may third party engagements that enter the water, or come within 1 bodylength of the water.
