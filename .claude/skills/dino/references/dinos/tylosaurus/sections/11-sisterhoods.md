# Tylosaurus – Sisterhoods

Sisterhoods are temporary groupings of sisters who have dispersed from their natal matriline and not yet formed one of their own. Hunting and living together, these groups are highly capable and are usually strongly bonded - but do not adopt or raise offspring. In order to raise offspring, sisterhoods must disperse to form their own matriline. If uninterested in courtship or forming their own matriline, they may opt to remain together instead.
