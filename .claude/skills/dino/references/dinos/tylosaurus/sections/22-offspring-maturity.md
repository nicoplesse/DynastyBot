# Tylosaurus – Offspring Maturity

Once offspring reach Sub-Adult, if there is room for them, they may stay within the matriline. Otherwise, they disperse to form their own groups and matrilines. However, at times, some mothers (who are not the mother of the matriline) may opt to disperse entirely with their own offspring, and travel alongside their natal matriline as a newly formed pod. Mothers continue to maintain doting and loving relationships with their offspring throughout their adult lives, licking them, ensuring they are fed, attempting to tuck them beneath her, and initiating play.
