# Tylosaurus – Pods

Pods consist of up to 3 matrilines or sisterhoods that are traveling together, and up to 1 rover. These pods may be of any relation to one another, and may split apart at any time. Travelling within a pod provides the benefit of being able to court, but matrilines, sisterhoods and rovers do not defend each other when being hunted, nor do they assist others with hunts. Pods often socialize amongst each other, especially if they have encountered one another before. Matrilines may return to areas where they have been before to find other matrilines that they have bonded with. Pods often circle together in a continuous motion, spinning and calling to one another. This is an observed from of play between individuals, especially those that are strongly bonded.
