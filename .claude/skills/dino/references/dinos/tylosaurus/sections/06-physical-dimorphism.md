# Tylosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

This profile does not have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Females, known as cows, are thicker than males, with large, smooth tails. Suitable subspecies include: nepaeolicus ♂️ Males, known as bulls, are thinner and smaller than females, with a pointed fin jutting from their tail. Suitable subspecies include: proriger, kansasenis, rex Physical dimorphism for Tylosaurus is mandatory.
