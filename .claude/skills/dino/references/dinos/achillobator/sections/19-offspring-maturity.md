# Achillobator – Offspring Maturity

Pups are permitted to remain with their parents until they reach Sub-Adult. Already frequently drifting from their parents, the pups, which had been doted on, are ignored and snapped at if they come too close. Should a Sub-Adult attempt to eat from their parents meals, its parents will deliver swift nips - often to the face. Having received the message, the pup moves on from their parents' care.
