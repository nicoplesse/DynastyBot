# Achillobator – Hunting with Latenivenatrix

Achillobator hunt alongside up to 3 Latenivenatrix companions. When hunting alongside 3 Latenivenatrix companions, Achillobator may hunt up to Apex tier - excluding sauropods such as Amargasaurus, Apatosaurus, and Argentinosaurus. As a result, they target larger prey first, such as: Iguanodon, Parasaurolophus, Pachyrhinosaurus and Eotriceratops if multiple prey options are available in the POI, within hunting limit requirements. Achillobator naturally take the lead in hunts while working with Latenivenatrix. They may flush small prey into dens for Latenivenatrix to rush into. Latenivenatrix are better at getting into these small dens, and are able to successfully flush prey back out of the dens and back to the waiting Achillobator, if not kill the prey in the den outright. Achillobator attempt to provide their Latenivenatrix companions with windows of opportunity for them to pounce onto the sides of large prey in order to further deepen wounds they have cut with their claws.
