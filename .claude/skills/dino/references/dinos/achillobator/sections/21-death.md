# Achillobator – Death

Achillobator often die in cave systems, or stuck in mud or water in coastal areas. This makes them, incidentally, great prey for aquatic creatures. If an individual from a mated pair dies, the other mourns them for a short time, calling softly at the place where they last saw them. The grief might not even occur to them until they have lost sight of them, and their calls do not bring them running back. Achillobator’s fondness for Latenivenatrix extends to entire rookeries and may be seen socializing with several Latenivenatrix. Achillobator only bonds to 1 rookery, but is allowed a maximum of 3 in hunts. While able to hunt alongside Latenivenatrix, Achillobator is not able to form an in game group with Latenivenatrix, due to server limits. Achillobator may still hunt offspring and Albinos during the day time. Achillobator share timers with their Latenivenatrix companions when hunting with them. Achillobator may defend their Latenivenatrix companions at any time, but only hunt alongside them at night. Achillobator may only hunt Apex tier species with 2+ Latenivenatrix and 1+ Achillobator. They will not hunt sauropods such as Amargasaurus, Apatosaurus, or Argentinosaurus, and will not hunt pairs of Apex carnivores.
