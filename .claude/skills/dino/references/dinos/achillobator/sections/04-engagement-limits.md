# Achillobator – Engagement Limits

WITHOUT LATENIVENATRIX: Up to 2 Achillobator, without Latenivenatrix, may hunt up to Large tier. Achillobator may attempt to hunt offspring of Adolescent and below, even if attended by Apex parents. WITH LATENIVENATRIX: Up to 2 Sub/Adult Achillobator may hunt with up to 3 Latenivenatrix. With at least 1 Achillobator and 3 Latenivenatrix, Achillobator may hunt up to Apex tier, excluding Apatosaurus. They may only hunt solo Apex carnivores. Territory defence: All Latenivenatrix and Achillobator may participate in defending their shared small nest territory. Driving out Deinonychus: Achillobator do not assist Latenivenatrix in driving Deinonychus out of the POI. Achillobator do not tolerate scavengers, except for their bonded Latenivenatrix. Achillobator may defend their bonded Latenivenatrix and adoptees at any time. Achillobator avoid Apex carnivore pairs, and other Achillobator pairs. Adolescent Achillobator do not assist with their parents’ hunts, nor do they hunt alongside Latenivenatrix. They may, however, attempt to hunt critters or species smaller than them alone. If the hunt becomes dangerous, they forfeit and rush to be protected by their parents. Preferred Prey With 3 Latenivenatrix, up to 2 Achillobators may hunt up to Apex tier. They may only hunt solo Apex carnivores. Parties with less than 2 Latenivenatrix may only hunt up to Large tier. When hunting alongside Latenivenatrix companions, Achillobator target larger prey first, such as; Iguanodon, Parasaurolophus, Pachyrhinosaurus and Eotriceratops if multiple prey options are available in the POI, within hunting limit requirements. Achillobator do not hunt Deinonychus when with Latenivenatrix companions. Achillobator will not eat the bodies of fallen Latenivenatrix companions or other Achillobator. Achillobator do not hunt Amargasaurus, Apatosaurus, or Argentinosaurus. Achillobator are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects.
