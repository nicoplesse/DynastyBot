# Achillobator – Group Limits

2 Sub/Adults, consisting of either a mated pair, a parent and child, or 2 siblings Unlimited Offspring (if the pair are mates) Achillobator may adopt up to 3 Latenivenatrix or Achillobator offspring total. Latenivenatrix may be adopted at any time by any individual or pair type; but Achillobator will only be adopted by mated pairs of Achillobator. Achillobator may bond and socialize with 1 Latenivenatrix rookery. When the pair have offspring or Latenivenatrix, the group is known as a pack. Otherwise, it is simply known as a pair.
