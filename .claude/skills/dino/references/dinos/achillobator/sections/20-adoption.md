# Achillobator – Adoption

Achillobator does not adopt orphaned Achillobator pups unless in a mated pair. Achillobator may always adopt Latenivenatrix. Mated pairs who find an orphaned pup regard them with curiosity, crouching down and inspecting the newly found pup. If willing to accept it, new parents fondly chatter at the pup and quickly accept it as their own. Mated pairs who do not want the pup or pairs who cannot adopt will snap in the direction of the pup or call aggressively to cause it to flee, sending a clear message. As Achillobator age, they go through cognitive declines, indicating that the end of their life is near. Elderly Achillobator often struggle with higher levels of anxiety, confusion, and disorientation. They may run in circles through the same cave systems or through the same forests, unable to find their way out alone. Elderly Achillobator may also suffer from reduced mobility, or pain after intense hunts, slowing them down and even resulting in accidental death following hunts. Mated Achillobator may be much clingier to their pairbonds. This can often mean a poor outcome for both individuals if they are both elderly; trusting one another to guide each other further into caves, where they may never come out from.
