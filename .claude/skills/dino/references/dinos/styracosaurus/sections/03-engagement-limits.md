# Styracosaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Headstrong Unlimited Styracosaurus may form a defensive circle. They stand their ground in the circle, but can move as far as 1 body length forward from the circle to land a hit, before resuming the formation. Up to 4 Styracosaurus may actively engage threats outside of the formation, which include chasing down to land hits - but not further than line of sight of the herd. These 4 Styracosaurus may chase the predator even after the predator forfeits until they leave line of sight of the herd. If there are 4 or less Styracosaurus in a herd, and all members of the group are active defenders, they may not chase predators who have forfeited for more than 5 bodylengths. Other species they group with may be defended inside the protective circle, or accepted to join the formation. Psittacosaurus of any age may be actively defended. Styracosaurus avoid conflict with: Eotriceratops, Ampelosaurus, Apatosaurus, Argentinosaurus. They will not start engagements against these species, and back off/forfeit when challenged by them. Styracosaurus become nervous around Daspletosaurus. They prefer to avoid conflict and are less aggressive until attacked. They will defend themselves if attacked, but do not attack first. Styracosaurus flee from pairs of Apex carnivores. Preferred Diet Flowers, nuts, roots, fruit.
