# Styracosaurus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 2 🥚 per 2 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 3 🥚 per 2 hours. Styracosaurus nest in open areas with good visibility when in a large herd (5+ members), but nest in dense foliage when in a smaller herd. Their nests are constructed out of reeds, branches and roots. Styracosaurus opt to nest near other ceratopsians if herding with them.
