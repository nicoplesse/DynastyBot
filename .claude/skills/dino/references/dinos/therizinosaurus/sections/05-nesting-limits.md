# Therizinosaurus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 3 🥚 per 3 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 1 🥚 per 3 hours. Therizinosaurus prefer to nest in areas of dense foliage and trees, where their chicks can remain hidden in the bushes. If forced to nest in the open, Therizinosaurus will nest in areas of shade and piled rocks. Therizinosaurus construct their nests out of reeds, branches, and roots. Therizinosaurus males may form same sex temporary or lifelong pairs. Pairs who adopt at least 5 chicks, over time, and raise them to adulthood together may become a lifelong bond.
