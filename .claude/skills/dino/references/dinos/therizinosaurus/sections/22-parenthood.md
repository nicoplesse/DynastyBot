# Therizinosaurus – Parenthood

Males are responsible for most of the rearing of chicks. They bring chicks food and water, and will keep them beneath his tail and under his feathers to keep them warm. He will not leave the nest to follow the rest of the Sect on patrols during this time, but mothers always will. Males will always allow chicks to enter their personal space, and will always be affectionately calling to them, especially when his chicks are hatchling, in order to provide them with comfort and to ensure they imprint on him. While hens will protect their chicks, they are not tolerant of any intrusions to their personal space, and will sweep them into the coverage of bushes and shrubbery rather than beneath their tails. As chicks get older, Therizinosaurus parents become gradually less tolerant of them getting so close to them. Males will never shout at or hurt their chicks, but will flex his claws at them to shoo them off. Females on the other hand, may peck a chick that is being too persistent. Lifelong pair bonded individuals will gently “defend” each other from their chicks, and will co-exist in each other’s personal space to guide stubborn chicks away. Parents lead their chicks in formations known as “parades” - with the female in the lead, a gap for her to exist comfortably, the male behind her, and the chicks in a single file line behind him. If the male of an opposite-sex pairing dies, the female Therizinosaurus will immediately push the chicks onto a different male, whether paired with him or not. If there is not a suitable male in the group, she will actively reject her chicks, abandoning them to be orphans rather than raising them herself. Chicks will still attempt to follow their mother - and risk being aggressed away from her and the sect entirely. If the female of an opposite-sex pairing dies, the male will carry on raising the chicks as usual. Children who respect their parents’ personal space are more treasured than some others. Male offspring may deliver gifts to their mothers at times in order to receive affection. While their fathers are welcoming, their mothers typically have high standards and rarely approve.
