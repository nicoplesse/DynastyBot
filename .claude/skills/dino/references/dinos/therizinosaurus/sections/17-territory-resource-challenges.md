# Therizinosaurus – Territory/Resource Challenges

Female Therizinosaurus are defensive of territory towards all other herbivores, and ferociously protect their nests and resources. Upon discovering an herbivore, nesting or eating/drinking from a resource within claimed territory, this greatly infuriates the females and they will issue a territory challenge towards the offending herbivores. Female Therizinosaurus do not defend their resources from non-Apex tier terrestrial carnivores. They will defend their resources from Tyrannosaurus, Giganotosaurus, and Tyrannotitan. Two of the herbivores that they hesitate in aggressing are Argentinosaurus & Apatosaurus – these gigantic sauropods are a formidable foe, so Therizinosaurus opt to screech and vocalize their protest, hoping this will be enough to move the sauropods on. In the event that Argentinosaurus retaliate physically, Therizinosaurus will immediately move away. However, Therizinosaurus do not leave their territory in this circumstance, and will move away in order to scream and screech at the looming sauropod, preferring not to engage directly. The Dame may elect one of her daughters to be a sentry to the Argentinosaurus – this daughter will watch the Argent. This Therizinosaurus will never get in its personal space or attack it, but will call loudly at it in disapproval, and will be responsible for escorting it out of the territory once it starts moving again. This is done by following at a distance where the Therizinosaurus may avoid the Argentinosaurus’s tail. A female who has been sent as a sentry in this manner will stand at the border of the territory until the Argentinosaurus is no longer in her line of sight. In the event that Apatosaurus retaliates physically - or refuse to move on - Therizinosaurus will engage them, aiming for their back legs with their sharp claws. Therizinosaurus females may chase another herbivore during territory challenges, and will only stop if the herbivore forfeits or if the said herbivore or Therizinosaurus do not land an attack on each other for 15 seconds. If the herbivore turns around at that point and attacks again, the Therizinosaurus is again allowed to chase. This is until the other herbivore leaves line of sight of the resource, moves at least 3 body lengths away, or leaves the POI in a claimed territory.
