# Therizinosaurus – Apex Carnivores

Therizinosaurus hens have an innate hatred for all apex terrestrial  carnivores. A Therizinosaurus hen cannot exist within the same POI as an apex carnivore, and will go as far as aggressing it during the daytime, if woken by one. Roosters will instead attempt to leave the area, rather than challenge an apex predator. Therizinosaurus do not have rivalries with Spinosaurus or Deinosuchus, and only respond with aggression if challenged by them. Dames, partnered with one other female or her lifelong bond, will challenge apex carnivores upon sight. They will never flee from a apex carnivore unless the fight is lost (deathscars), or a Therizinosaurus has died. The rest of the Sect aggressively call and flex their claws in a show of unity. Confrontations between Therizinosaurus and Apex carnivores may result in long standoffs, as Therizinosaurus does not attack on sight. If the apex carnivores do not aggress back and instead attempt to avoid the Therizinosaurus, they follow it to escort it out of the POI, calling aggressively. If the Therizinosaurus is ignored and the carnivore does not move to leave the POI, Therizinosaurus may attack first after issuing a :broadcast:  call followed by three aggressive calls :threaten_call: and waiting at least 10 seconds for a response. She may also attack first if the predator visibly prepares an attack move, moves towards her, or enters her personal space. She will not enter the personal space of an apex carnivore. Looms work together to kill apex carnivores once an engagement starts. Therizinosaurus will kill all Apex terrestrial carnivore offspring if discovered, and will not stop until all offspring are dead. If a Therizinosaurus kills a mother apex carnivore, and its offspring are within line of sight of the body down, the Therizinosaurus may immediately attempt to chase down the offspring, forcing them out of the territory or killing them.
