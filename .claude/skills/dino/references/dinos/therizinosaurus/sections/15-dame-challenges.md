# Therizinosaurus – Dame Challenges

Dames are always the most aggressive Therizinosaurus within a sect, over her position, territory, and personal space. She is especially aggressive towards other females, but if males are too pushy, she will yell and push them out of her personal space as well. Dames may be challenged for their position within a sect any time during the night. These challenges are initiated by a subordinate female; she will call loudly, flexing her claws, shaking her tail, and swiping the air in order to collect the attention of the entire sect. The Dame will respond by calling back and doing the same, flexing her claws, shaking her tail, and posturing to show off her scars and feathers to the sect. Depending on the Dame, this show is enough to make the subordinate female forfeit. If the subordinate female does not forfeit, she will approach the Dame, entering her personal space bubble to begin the engagement. These engagements are bloody, resulting in a lot of torn feathers and screeching. A female will always attempt to target the other female’s tail, in order to destroy her honor and ruin her chance at tempting males. A fight between a Dame and a subordinate female is typically short and brutal. It will end in either party forfeiting, or the death of either the Dame or subordinate, if no forfeit is issued. The losing female, her lifelong pairbond (if she has one) and all other daughters of hers (if the Dame) will be driven out of the group and territory. Males will rarely, if ever, get into fights with one another, which at the most will be verbal and a few swipes. Typically, the Dame will interfere, shouting at them both and pushing them away from one another. Sisters will often bicker with each other over resources, nests, favored males, and personal space. If these boundaries are continuously disrespected, they may spar with one another, but these matches will never go below half-health.
