# Therizinosaurus – Courtship

Solitary Therizinosaurus meet at Hunter’s Thicket - while courtship does not occur here, those looking for prospective mates and groups meet here. Courting for a Therizinosaurus is a very gradual and intentional process. Therizinosaurus are more likely to accept another Therizinosaurus based on how long they have been grouped, the types of gifts they have received from them, and if they have regularly respected their personal space. Females display to prospective males by shaking their feathers towards them, enticing them with her coloring and patterning and the sound of her feathers moving. They affectionately call to him, and circle him to admire him. Males who respect her personal space and do not move, simply look at her and affectionately call back, are preferred. Younger males may be more excited, rushing to approach her - which will result in her rejection. Males, no matter whether they have been subject to a female’s display, will often select a female that he would like to devote his attention and time to. This is most often the Dame, but if too many males are opting to court the Dame, he may choose one of her daughters instead. Males court by bringing gifts, thoughtful and intentional, to a female. A female Therizinosaurus will collect many gifts throughout her lifetime, putting them in small hoards in a set location within her territory. A female may have favored gifts that she will respond more positively to, and gifts that she does not enjoy, which will result in her rejecting a male for the courting season. If a male has successfully impressed a female with his gifts and attention, she will begin to follow him around more closely, or allow him to approach her. They will then affectionately call at each other and shake their feathers. If two females have received affections and attention from the same male, and are both interested in nesting with him, they may fight each other. These fights will never go below half-health before forfeiting, and do not result in an exchange of dominance between parties. Males who are won in this way will immediately accept the winning female and nest with her.
