# Therizinosaurus – Group Limits

6 Sub/Adults, comprising at least one lead female (known as the Dame), unrelated males, and sisters or daughters of the Dame. 9 Total Offspring OR Adoptees This group is known as a sect. or, if only male: 3 Sub/Adults, comprising males of any relation/unrelated No Offspring Up to 3 Adoptees This group is known as a tiding. or, if only female: 2 Sub/Adults, comprising females of any relation/unrelated No Offspring or Adoptees This group is known as a loom.
