# Therizinosaurus – Death

If a Therizinosaurus dies, those who are bonded to it may stay close to its body, observing it and watching over it. They will only disperse if the sun begins to rise or if a predator approaches to claim the body. Even then, they will watch from a distance, in silent mourning of the fallen. If a lifelong pair bonded Therizinosaurus dies of age, it is typical for the pairbond to stay with its body until it peacefully passes alongside it. Male same sex pairs may be formed in Sects and Tidings. No female same sex pairs are observed in these species. Egg multipliers used when nesting may allow Therizinosaurus to temporarily exceed the offspring count, but not permit any new adoptees. Therizinosaurus do not imprint on any Apex carnivores. Therizinosaurus do not have rivalries with Spinosaurus or Deinosuchus, and only respond with aggression if challenged by them. Therizinosaurus do not attack first, with a few exceptions. In challenges against apex predators, Therizinosaurus will attack first if the apex carnivore ignores a broadcast and three aggressive calls and 10 seconds following, if the carnivore visibly prepares an attack move, or moves towards the Therizinosaurus. When being stalked, Therizinosaurus will only attack first if the carnivore visibly makes an attack move or moves into the group’s personal space. Only female Therizinosaurus, or groupings with female Therizinosaurus, are territorial. Only female Therizinosaurus display competitive behaviors with apex carnivores. Female Therizinosaurus may chase Apex carnivores past their forfeit. Therizinosaurus respect forfeit calls from all predators except Apex carnivores.
