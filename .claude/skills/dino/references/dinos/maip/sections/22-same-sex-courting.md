# Maip – Same-Sex Courting

Males When males take interest in other males, they watch from afar, analyzing this potential mate before approaching. The interested male creeps close, body close to the ground, cooing and issuing affectionate calls to show his intent: courtship, and the potential of a lifelong partnership. Males approached by another male for courtship may be confused at first, backing away to make space between them. This allows both males to become familiar with one another, and assess each other's intentions. The approaching male, to further signify his intention of courtship, will roll to show his vulnerable, soft belly to his potential partner. With this show of trust and affection, the opposite male approaches to giggle at the newcomer. Male x Male courtship is particularly unique. Rather than stalking one another, males will engage in a game of copy-cat. If both males are interested in courting, they begin with an affectionate call. One male will initiate, using calls and body language in various patterns before stopping, watching intently. The opposite male will copy the first's calls, with the process repeating at least once more before the two approach and nuzzle together affectionately. Male x Male pairs are often the quickest pairing to strengthen, with both males constantly reassuring one another and comforting their partner through life's scariest moments. Male pairs often see a stark increase in bravado - perhaps due to a lack of female protector. Having only one another to rely on, their aggression increases, with anxieties fading away as their bond strengthens with their mate. Females Opposite to their more timid male counterparts, female Maip take courtship several steps further. Due to their aggressive nature, encountering another female rarely ends with a peaceful encounter. If a female is only interested in courtship with another female, and no other females approach, she will continue shrieking, ignoring any males that gather entirely. Females seeking to court another female still travel to Burned Forest and claim a rock for themselves, or search for a female who is already atop the rocks, shrieking to draw attention. Rather than gathering below the rocks like males, females in search of another female companion attempt to join her on the same rocks, calling out with shrieks of her own. If the female Maip who had first taken residence on the rocks is uninterested in a female mate, she will aggressively ward off the intruder with a scream, claws outstretched in warning. If the Maip atop the rocks will tolerate a female mate, she will only respond with a low hiss. Not a rejection, but an invitation for a challenge. Both females will disembark from the rocks, circling one another to gauge their strength, hissing and shrieking at one another. Younger, less experienced females may flee at this confrontation. However, if neither back down, they will begin to nip at the air, testing eachother’s resolve. After this display of strength, the females engage in the same copy-cat game as a male pair, testing their ability to copy one another correctly by following one another’s patterns of calls. Once complete, and both females accept one another, they approach and groom one another - before leaving to solidify their newfound bond. Female x Female pairings are especially rare due to how aggressive they are. However, if they do succeed, they form a particularly formidable bond.
