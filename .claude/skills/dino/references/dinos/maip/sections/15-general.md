# Maip – General

Maip spend much of their time, when not actively hunting, looking to terrorize and torment smaller dinosaurs. Chasing, nipping at, and cackling are all behaviors these megaraptorans indulge in. Maip prefer species that react to their torment with fear. Species that are their tier or higher that react with aggression to their taunts will cause Maip to back off unless they are hunting, seeking out an easier form of entertainment. However - should the other species be smaller than Maip, or simply break out into a run, Maip will engage in the chase, carefully employing piercing shrieks and air snaps to further terrorize the creature. This chasing is solely for fun and entertainment, and never results in death unless a Maip somehow ends up trampling the target of their violent game. This is separate from their hunting behavior.
