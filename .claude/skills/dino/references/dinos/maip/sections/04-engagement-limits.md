# Maip – Engagement Limits

3 Maip may enter an engagement or hunt. Maip flee from Adult Tyrannotitan. 1 Maip may hunt up to Large tier. 2-3 Maip may hunt up to solo Apex tier, excluding Adult Tyrannotitan. Adolescents may participate in a hunt, but only a maximum of 2 may assist, and must individually forfeit at half health. Adolescents may only assist with hunts against other species up to solo Large tier.
