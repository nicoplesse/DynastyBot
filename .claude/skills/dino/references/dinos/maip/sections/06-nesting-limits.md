# Maip – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 2 🥚 per 2 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 4 🥚 per 1 hour. Maip nest in caves and caverns large enough to fit themselves, their mate and their offspring. However, if unable to nest in an enclosed space, they choose heavily secluded areas shrouded in foliage and leaves, hidden from the sunlight. Maip may form same-sex pairs, but do not nest or adopt.
