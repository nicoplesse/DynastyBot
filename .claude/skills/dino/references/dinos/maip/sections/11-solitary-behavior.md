# Maip – Solitary Behavior

Despite being a social species, Maip can easily thrive alone in the wilderness of Gondwa. However, these solitary carnivores must be cautious, as they do not have the benefit of a partner or siblings alongside them for safety. This lack of protection causes both male and female Maip alike to choose their hunts and battles wisely. Solitary Maip find comfort in the confines and cover of the trees, keeping to the undergrowth as opposed to wandering in the open. Solitary males are often unnoticed, being the more cautious, wary gender. Solitary females, however, may gain a reputation in an area as a nuisance. If not seeking a courting partner, confident females will often turn their dominance displays onto unsuspecting males, or more submissive, younger females. Solitary Maip are much more wary of others of their species, regardless of gender.
