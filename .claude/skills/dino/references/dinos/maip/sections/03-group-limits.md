# Maip – Group Limits

3 Sub/Adults, consisting of a mated pair and a favored adult offspring. Unlimited Offspring Maip does not adopt, and will cannibalize unattended pups. However, an orphaned Maip pup may attempt to sneak into nests. If discovered, Maip parents may cannibalize stowaway pups. This group is known as a skulk. Up to 3 Sub/Adults, consisting of bonded siblings. No Offspring or Adoptees. This group is known as a cackle.
