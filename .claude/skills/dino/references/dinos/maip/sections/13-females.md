# Maip – Females

Female Maip, if they find another female trailed by an interested male, may interject herself between them in an attempt to flash her display to the male. The previous female, if having been receptive to the male’s advances, may outright attack this newcomer to drive her away. This can result in a bloody fight, where the loser backs off at deathscars - with some even going as far as to fight until the death!
