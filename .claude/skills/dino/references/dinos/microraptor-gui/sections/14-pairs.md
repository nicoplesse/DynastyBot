# Microraptor (Gui) – Pairs

Microraptor in pairs are able to enjoy the company of each other as well as that of Apex Carnivores. Pairs may consist of any relation between two Microraptor, guiding the direction of the interactions. Siblings are more prone to bickering over the smallest of issues - scraps of food, which side of a companion they're latched onto, or where to cross between POIs. Two uncourted individuals may only travel together briefly, benefiting from the company momentarily, but eventually going separate ways - or deciding to visit the courtship grounds together to make the bond official. Courted pairs are typically much more agreeable with each other and bicker far less, happily investigating objects together and exchanging mimicked calls to strengthen their bond. Pairs of Microraptor are more likely to remain with the same carnivore companion for longer than a solitary individual would.
