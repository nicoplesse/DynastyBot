# Microraptor (Gui) – Preferred Prey & Diet

Microraptor are primarily scavengers and insectivores, but may occasionally attempt to hunt. Microraptor can hunt attended or unattended Deinonychus, Latenivenatrix, Psittacosaurus, and Struthiomimus offspring of Juvenile and below. Microraptor may hunt Rhamphorhynchus. Microraptor are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering adverse side effects. See 📜・server-guideOPTIONAL - Illnesses & Injuries for more information.
