# Microraptor (Gui) – Solitary Behavior

Though Microraptor have the general tolerance to associate with others, many individuals choose solitary lifestyles where they are less likely to be bothered by persistent noise and bickering. This action is common in orphaned individuals, but equally, those who grew up with an unusual number of siblings may prefer the tranquility of it in contrast to their upbringing. Solitary individuals find increased happiness in following other species about, straying from their claimed carnivore to investigate other options and more frequently switching between choices on a whim. Despite this, even the most solitary Microraptor seeks occasional stimuli from others of their own kind. As such, despite often having no interest in courtship, solitary individuals may choose to gather at the courting tree within Big Tree Overlook, simply to marvel and gawk at others. Both female and male Microraptors actively choose to linger within the audience, rather than partake in courtship attempts down below. However, persistent visits may sometimes spark a reluctant interest in some otherwise solitary Microraptors, transitioning them into forming a pair when particularly impressed by a display.
