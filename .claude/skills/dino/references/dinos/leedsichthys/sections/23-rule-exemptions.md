# Leedsichthys – Rule Exemptions

Leedsichthys do not claim bodies that have fallen in an engagement, bodies that they come across, or bodies that fall in their own defense, as they are unable to feed from them. (5.3) Leedsichthys always respect the forfeits of other carnivores, as they hold no interest in hunting or actively killing other species. (4.8.2.3) Leedsichthys offspring (Hatchling, Juvenile, and Adolescent) do not gain the tier of their parent when within two bodylengths of them. (1.10.1) Adolescent Leedsichthys are still considered Large tier. Hatchling and juvenile Leedsichthys have no tier and can be hunted by any carnivorous species. Leedsichthys do not require a mutation roll to shed into the Albino skin when actively dying. Once shed into the Albino skin, Leedsichthys must die within 10 minutes. (1.12)
