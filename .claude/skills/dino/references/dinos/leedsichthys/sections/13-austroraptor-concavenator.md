# Leedsichthys – Austroraptor & Concavenator

While usually treated with the same tolerance as other species, experienced fathers - or at times, Leedsichthys who have encountered these species in their youth - become much more cautious of them. These nimble semi-aquatics are not one to turn down the opportunity of hunting a Leedsichthys pup, despite the size of accompanying adults.
