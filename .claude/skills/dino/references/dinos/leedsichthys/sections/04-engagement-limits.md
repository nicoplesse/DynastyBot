# Leedsichthys – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Defensive Leedsichthys do not hunt other players. Leedsichthys can have up to 1 in an engagement. A father Leedsichthys may defend his offspring at any time. Adolescents and below do not participate in defence and always choose to flee, unless cornered. Preferred Diet Leedsicthys exclusively feed from krill while travelling their migration paths.
