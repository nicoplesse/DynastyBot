# Leedsichthys – Adoption

Leedsichthys pups have a powerful instinct to school with other young of a similar size and age, regardless of whether they are related. An orphan encountering an established nursery group may begin following its movements, and gradually insert itself amongst the other pups. Fathers make no distinction between biological offspring and an orphan that successfully joins their school. There is no adoption display or period of introduction - once an orphan is swimming amongst his pups, the father simply begins treating it as another member of the school. Adopted pups receive the same tolerance and protection as a father's biological offspring. Elderly Leedsicthys grow sluggish, moving extremely slowly during their migration and often opting not to swim into shallower waters. These Leedsicthys are often found living the rest of their lives in the deep ocean, floating along and allowing the currents to carry them forward. The typical migration path is often abandoned, preferring not to expose themselves to the great dangers of shallow water. As a result, these typically solitary creatures often grow reclusive, avoiding other Leedsicthys. Once vibrant scales may even begin to fade into a more uniform coloration.
