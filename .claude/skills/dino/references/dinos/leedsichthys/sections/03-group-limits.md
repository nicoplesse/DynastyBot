# Leedsichthys – Group Limits

1 Sub/Adult Unlimited Offspring (if parent is male) Unlimited Adoptees (only when biological offspring are present with father, and same size as adoptees) Groups are known as a nursery group when offspring are present. Unlimited offspring may group together under Sub-Adult. Large amounts of Sub/Adult Leedsichthys may migrate and feed alongside each other, but do not group in game.
