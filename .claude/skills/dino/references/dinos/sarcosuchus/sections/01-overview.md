# Sarcosuchus – Overview

Large Cathemeral Semi-Aquatic Carnivore Sarcosuchus form polyandrous bonds. Sarcosuchus are nomadic. Sarcosuchus have a tolerant nature in the Rainy Season, and are aggressive in the Dry Season. Females are cannibalistic towards unrelated young of adolescent and below. Sarcosuchus may not scavenge from claimed corpses and must contest for a body. Critters and abandoned corpses may be freely eaten. Sarcosuchus tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.
