# Sarcosuchus – Physical Dimorphism

♀️ Females have scales that are all of one color, with minor differences. They have a more uniform appearance, sometimes with speckled patterns or small variations in color. ♂️ Males have variable patterns, often with bold stripes. Their undersides and necks always feature bright flashes of color. Color dimorphism for Sarcosuchus is mandatory. Mutated males are not required to abide by dimorphism.
