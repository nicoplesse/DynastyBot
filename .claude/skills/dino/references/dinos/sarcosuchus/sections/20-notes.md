# Sarcosuchus – Notes

Females may nest with up to 2 males. If nesting with 2 males, the female may decide how many eggs are fathered by each male, up to the nesting limit. If they can lay 2 eggs, then each father will sire one egg. Nesting limits cannot be exceeded. In the day, Sarcosuchus must start a hunt within 2 body lengths of water. If they land a hit and their prey runs in land, they may pursue indefinitely after them. If not, they must forfeit the hunt. At night, Sarcosuchus may start a hunt from water or fully in land. Sarcosuchus must be in water to third party an ongoing engagement. They can 3rd party if hunter or hunted reaches up to 2 body lengths from the water. Sarcosuchus hunts in the water during the day and night. Night hunting grounds are considered unpreferred POIs during the day, and Sarcosuchus must be 35% hunger or below (and log their hunger if in a group) in order to hunt there. Sarcosuchus may hunt from the water at any time of day following POI hunger requirements. Sarcosuchus may only initiate hunts on land outside of highlighted areas on their POI map if 35% hunger at night. Sarcosuchus may hunt from small bodies of water, but must leave after 15 minutes. They must move on and are only permitted to return after an entire day/night cycle.
