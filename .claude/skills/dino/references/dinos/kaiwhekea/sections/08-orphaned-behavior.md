# Kaiwhekea – Orphaned Behavior

Calves are able to survive on their own from the moment they hatch. Orphans without visible adults in the area become skittish, hiding in shallow water, the water surface, or around rocks away - from deep water predators. They take longer to emerge into deeper water than their counterparts with parents. Orphans may grow up to be more antisocial than most in their species.
