# Kentrosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

♀️ Females lack quills on their back ends. Suitable subspecies include: aethiopicus, multiscornus ♂️ Males have quills on their back ends. Suitable subspecies include: parvusplectrum Males that do not take the parvusplectrum subspecies are considered to be "sneaky males" and are treated as females by all. Females that choose the parvusplectrum subspecies are infertile and cannot produce eggs.
