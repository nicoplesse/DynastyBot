# Concavenator – Engagement Limits

Up to 4 Concavenator may participate in an engagement. Only Sub/Adults are permitted to hunt or defend, unless in a group containing no Sub/Adults. Concavenator flee from Spinosaurus if they are aggressed. Solitary Concavenator hunt up to Medium tier. 2-3 Concavenator hunt up to Large tier. A full hunting group of 4 Concavenator hunt up to solo Apex tier, excluding sauropods. Concavenator may third party targets it can hunt if the target comes within two body lengths of its current water source. Adolescent Concavenator do not assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Concavenator may attempt to hunt up to Small tier. In a group with no adults present: Up to 2 Adolescent Concavenator may hunt up to solo Medium tier. Preferred Prey Solitary or paired Sarcosuchus, solitary Deinosuchus, and Megalania Fish and meat Concavenator are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects.
