# Concavenator – Courtship

Though naturally possessive, Concavenator displays a rare moment of selective and meaningful gifting during courtship. Individuals - regardless of sex - will trade  cherished items in a rare display of affection. These gifts are not exchanged lightly; the act of giving is deliberate and symbolic, especially among adults. A gifted item reflects the suitor’s worth and attentiveness to the other’s preferences, or perhaps their shared interests. When accepting a gift, the courting individuals will place their gifts together and both lie on the pile. Grooming their suitor and exchanging affectionate calls with their new mate. Courtship can end in two ways: If a suitor drops their gift and approaches empty handed, the one approached grows weary. They might walk away or drop their item in confusion, quickly snatching it back or biting to reclaim it if taken. The individual carrying the item drops it immediately and steps back with a gentle coo. In this situation, the approached will snatch their gift back and walk away, rejecting this potential mate in favor of another. Sometimes, upon closer inspection, a gift might not be as favourable as originally perceived. After a gift is piled on another, a suitor may take a moment to inspect the pile. If determined unsuitable, the individual reclaims their gift and walks away. Effectively rejecting this potential mate and their offering. If two suitors seek the same mate, and no clear decision is shown initially. The suitors may grow agitated and challenge the one vying for their mates attention. Courtship challenges between rival suitors are intense but brief. It begins with posturing and emitting low, guttural growls. If agitated displays alone do not settle the matter, the chosen mate must pick before both suitors abandon their attempt. If a suitor is not immediately chosen, grudges begin to form. Sometimes not being picked or losing to a rival during courtship is enough form a personal grievance. True compatibility often lies in subtle social responses - such as how a suitor handles possessiveness or jealousy. Some pairs may court each other multiple times in different seasons and know the perfect trinket to win them over.
