# Concavenator – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 4 🥚 per 2 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 2 🥚 per 1 hour. Concavenator nest communally in Sharptooth Marsh among the bushes and reeds, close to water. Their nests are spaced at least 5 body lengths apart from other nesting pairs. Concavenator may form same-sex pairs, and still travel to the communal nesting grounds, where they construct a nest together. Though no eggs are laid, sometimes a pair may be surprised by an orphaned kit sneaking into their nest, and will adopt it.
