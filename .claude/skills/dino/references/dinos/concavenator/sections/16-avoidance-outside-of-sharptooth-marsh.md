# Concavenator – Avoidance outside of Sharptooth Marsh

Outside of Sharptooth Marsh, Concavenator avoid interactions with other gangs within the same POI. If one group is actively hunting in the area, others steer clear and relocate to a different POI to avoid direct competition. If outsiders are spotted they are ignored. Concavenator are generally intolerant of other species near their gang, though they are defensive over their personal space. If approached by 2+ Apex or 3+ Large carnivores, Concavenator chooses to flee rather than engage, prioritizing their own survival. If challenged or outright attacked, 4 members defend, but the group will never risk fighting Apex pairs. When threatened, Concavenator can flee toward water sources, using them as natural escape routes. On occasion, they even bait other species to the water, where hidden gang members lie in wait to ambush. While defending kills or nesting grounds, they exhibit a range of threat displays before escalating to violence. If these warnings are ignored, they attack - but always with caution, seeking to avoid unnecessary injury. Smaller carnivores and herbivores are usually tolerated in their vicinity, often observed for potential hunting opportunities. However if an item is carried by these smaller creatures, inquisitive Concavenator will follow relentlessly in hopes of stealing the item from them.
