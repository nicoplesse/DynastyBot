# Concavenator – Spinosaurus

Concavenator are one of the only two species tolerated by Spinosaurus within their territories. This tolerance is likely due to their smaller size and a visual similarity to juvenile Spinosaurids. The tolerance goes both ways, as long as they are allowed to fish and linger around the Apex's protected territory. However, Spinosaurus often help themselves to Concavenator kills, and in order to maintain peace, Concavenator are forced to surrender their catch with minimal resistance - though not without visible signs of irritation. Concavenator form agile and opportunistic hunting groups of up to 4 individuals, Sub-Adult or older. These groups rely on coordinated ambushes, endurance, and tactical strikes rather than raw strength. When stalking prey, Concavenator use the terrain to their advantage - dense vegetation, rocky terrain, and especially swampy or marshy regions where their strong swimming skills come into play. Hunting behaviour is quiet and calculated, often with the group approaching from multiple directions before launching a sudden, rapid strike. A favored tactic involves one individual darting forward to deliver a targeted slash to their prey’s vulnerable areas, such as feet or neck, while others distract the prey and await their own turn to strike. These attacks are swift and followed by immediate retreat, minimizing risk and allowing the inflicted wounds to weaken the prey over time. If an ambush fails or the target escapes, the hunting group may persistently follow it, using their stamina to wear it down. However, Concavenator avoid prolonged fights - if majority of the hunting group is badly wounded and the prey shows no visible signs of severe injury, Concavenator will abandon the hunt in favor of seeking easier opportunities. Concavenator are adaptable hunters and utilize water if able to. Hunting as a group, prey is chased into difficult terrain or trapped against embankments. These hunters rely on water to conceal their next move - readily darting in and out of its depths and using it as a tool to confuse or corral fleeing animals. After a successful kill, Concavenator devours their prey. There is no structured feeding order - it is a first-come-first-served frenzy. Individuals tear off large chunks and gorge quickly, often leaving nothing behind for other members. This competitive feeding habit means that slower or more submissive individuals are forced to hunt again. However, only those who are hungry participate in repeated hunts. If a Concavenator has already eaten its fill, they do not risk themselves for others and will not join consecutive hunts, prioritizing rest or guarding their trinkets instead. Concavenator are efficient, strategic and relentless - true opportunists that thrive on precision, patience and teamwork. Up to 4 Concavenator may participate in an engagement or hunt. If a target, whether being hunted or hunting, is a valid prey for the Concavenators and comes within two body lengths of a water source they occupy, the Concavenators are allowed to attempt thirdpartying the encounter.
