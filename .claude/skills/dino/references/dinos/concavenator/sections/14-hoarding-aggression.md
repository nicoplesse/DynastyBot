# Concavenator – Hoarding Aggression

Concavenator are not territorial, but are possessive over resources and proximity. Groups and solitary individuals are extremely particular about their immediate space, especially if it contains a kill, nesting site, or item stash. If another Concavenator lingers too long or tries to approach the personal space of another, threats will follow - starting with visual displays and growls, and ending in physical attacks if the warning is not heeded. Concavenator are tolerant of eachother, from a distance. However if personal boundaries are crossed, aggression quickly surfaces. This behavior is most frequently triggered by item theft, unwanted close proximity with another, or challenges over mates. Even in social groups, tempers can flare with little warning when a Concavenator feels their possessions or space are under threat. Concavenator are extremely possessive, especially when it comes to their collected items. If another Concavenator attempts to take their trinkets, they react with a warning growl or defensive display. Should the offender persist, quick lunges, tail slaps, or slashing bites may follow. Usually, the initial attack deters another, but unrelated individuals may test each other's limits by trying to snatch unattended valuables. If this behavior becomes habitual, the tension can escalate to the point where both individuals can no longer coexist in the same gang peacefully. Concavenator hold grudges and will always drive away a personal rival who has wronged them in the past.
