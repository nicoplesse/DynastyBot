# Concavenator – Forming a gang

When a Concavenator comes across a situation where they may form or join an existing gang, they will cheerfully chirp at the other individual(s) in hopes of gaining their attention and interest. If a Concavenator is met with friendly body language and their call is mimicked back to them, they are welcomed in without issue. However, if they’re met with aggression, they will quickly retreat without resistance to avoid conflict or injury.
