# Daspletosaurus – Solitary Behavior

Daspletosaurus do well on their own more often than not. Solitary Daspletosaurus are less haughty than their pack counterparts, and become more strategic about the sound they make and their movements. While they do not hide, they avoid engagements they do not feel they can win. Solitary Daspletosaurus target up to Large tier or Adolescents of any tier, even if protected by an adult. Solitary Daspletosaurus, unless attempting to join or take over a pack, avoid other Daspletosaurus entirely.
