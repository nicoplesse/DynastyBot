# Daspletosaurus – Packs Without Males

Packs without males may consist of up to 4 Sub/Adult females of any relation, with two slots reserved for potential males. They will adamantly refuse any submissive males that approach, uninterested by his oftentimes lack of color or confidence. Females will work together to hunt with incredible coordination, forgoing the showy parts of their hunts in order to hunt precisely and dangerously. For this time, females will provide for one another; but may be observed hoarding food for themselves and refusing to allow others close.
