# Daspletosaurus – Death

When it comes time before a Daspletosaurus to pass, they often return to the caves where they were born, curling up in the place where their nest was placed. No attempt to rouse them is successful, with a pack often moving on without the individual unless strongly bonded or related. Dominant male pairs will stay together through the end of their lives. If distant calls from preferred prey are heard by Daspletosaurus but the prey is not in the POI, Daspletosaurus is not required to move to the next POI in order to hunt that prey. Packs can have any number of submissive males, though typically only one or two are tolerated at a time. Daspletosaurus will aggress any carnivore in 5 body lengths of themselves or their pack. This is not a challenge for territory, but may be considered a challenge for personal space. Dominant males exiling or correcting a submissive male will never kill the male intentionally. They may only bring him to deathscars.
