# Deinocheirus – Physical Dimorphism

♀️ Females have less feathers on their head, and have smaller back humps. Suitable subspecies include: grandis. ♂️ Males display brighter colors, more feathers on their head, and have bigger back humps. Suitable subspecies include: balanced, hirsutae. Physical dimorphism for Deinocheirus is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
