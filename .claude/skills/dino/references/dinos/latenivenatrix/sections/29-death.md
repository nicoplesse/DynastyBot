# Latenivenatrix – Death

Latenivenatrix mourn heavily when members in their rookery die. They may remember the locations and bring gifts to the place where their lost one passed away, particularly items that were used to decorate their nest. While able to hunt alongside Achillobator, Latenivenatrix is not able to form an in game group with Achillobator, due to server limits. Latenivenatrix may still hunt offspring and Albinos during the day time. Latenivenatrix share timers with their Achillobator companions when hunting with them. Latenivenatrix may defend their Achillobator companions at any time, but only hunt alongside them at night. Latenivenatrix may only hunt Apex tier species with 2 Latenivenatrix and 1+ Achillobator. They will not hunt sauropods such as Amargasaurus, Apatosaurus, or Argentinosaurus, and will not hunt pairs of Apex carnivores. Latenivenatrix do not have to claim Latenivenatrix body downs.
