# Latenivenatrix – Downtime Behavior

During the day, Latenivenatrix settle for a rest period. The group stops migrating through POIs and together they construct a huge cluster of nests in the middle of a clearing. These nests always consist of large branches, creating an almost cave-like system. Great care is taken to constructing these nests, and every individual gathers resources to ensure their contribution is perfected. Once all nests are constructed, the group converge together in the middle of the nests and cuddle together in a large pile. Individuals take it in turns to keep a watch out for danger, while the majority take naps. They guard a territory border of 5 body lengths around this nesting site, and work together as a group to ward off threats. Rookeries with less than 6 Latenivenatrix present may only ward off threats of Medium tier and below. With at least 1 Achillobator, and 2+ Latenivenatrix present, Latenivenatrix may ward off threats of up to Solo Apex. Latenivenatrix form strong bonds with other members of their rookery overtime, strengthening relationships with gentle preening, games of chase, gift-giving, and singing together. Latenivenatrix often stretch their wings open at each other excitedly in greeting, crowing and calling loudly when they reunite with an individual they have not seen for a while. A particularly popular individual in the rookery may be incidentally swarmed by other Latenivenatrix wanting to preen them, sleep near to them, or rest alongside them.
