# Latenivenatrix – Preferred Prey & Diet

Latenivenatrix target Lambeosaurus, Styracosaurus, Pachycephalosaurus, Citipati, Camptosaurus and Dryosaurus first and foremost if multiple prey options are available in the POI, within hunting limit requirements. When hunting alongside Achillobator companions, Latenivenatrix target larger prey first, such as; Iguanodon, Parasaurolophus, Pachyrhinosaurus and Eotriceratops if multiple prey options are available in the POI, within hunting limit requirements. Latenivenatrix will not eat the bodies of fallen Achillobator companions or other Latenivenatrix. Latenivenatrix are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects. See 📜・server-guideOPTIONAL - Illnesses & Injuries for more information. Latenivenatrix do not hunt Deinonychus.
