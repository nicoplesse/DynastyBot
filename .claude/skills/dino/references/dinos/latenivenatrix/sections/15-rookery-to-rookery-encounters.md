# Latenivenatrix – Rookery to Rookery Encounters

When 2 rookeries encounter one another, interactions are cautious, but rarely violent. Initial contact begins with careful assessments of the other group, with individuals looking to see if the other rookery comprises individuals they recognize. If familiar individuals are present - especially dispersed offspring or relatives - brief displays of affection may occur as both Latenivenatrix reconnect with one another. This sometimes brings a shift in members within a rookery. Members may opt to disperse to join the other rookery in order to remain with these relatives or closely bonded individuals. Otherwise, these exchanges are generally short-lived, with one of the rookeries moving on afterwards. Only 1 rookery may occupy a POI at a time. The decision on which rookery stays is ultimately based on mutual agreement - but otherwise: If one of the rookeries are nesting, the one nesting has the automatic right to remain in the POI. If neither are nesting, but only one rookery has Achillobator companions, the one with the companions has the automatic right to remain in the POI. If neither of these conditions apply - and neither group are willing to depart - a POI challenge occurs, with up to 6 Latenivenatrix from each rookery battling for the right to remain in the POI. These battles are never fatal, and individuals forfeit before reaching deathscars. Achillobator do not intervene in these battles.
