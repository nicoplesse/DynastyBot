# Latenivenatrix – Apex Carnivores

Up to 3 Latenivenatrix that do not have Achillobator companions or a rookery may cautiously trail behind Apex carnivores that tolerate their presence, especially during the night. When tolerated, they observe kills from a distance, watching carefully for scavenging opportunities. If permitted, these Latenivenatrix may risk getting closer to their trailed carnivores, and especially bold individuals attempt to groom and pick insects and pests off of their bodies. Any sign of aggression, however, sends these individuals fleeing, preferring not to push their luck. Rookeries of more than 3 individuals - or any number of Latenivenatrix with Achillobator companions - never trail behind Apex carnivores or attempt to scavenge. Direct confrontation with these species are avoided - unless hunting alongside Achillobator companions, escape is impossible, or their offspring are at risk. Should an Apex carnivore approach a rookery's nesting territory, Latenivenatrix respond with coordinated warning calls and aggressive posturing, escalating to harassment and mobbing attacks until the threat leaves the territory. With bonded Achillobator present, and 2+ Latenivenatrix, displays may escalate to harassment and mobbing attacks so long as the Apex is solo.
