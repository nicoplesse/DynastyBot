# Latenivenatrix – Deinonychus

When Deinonychus are noticed, Latenivenatrix shift into a heightened alert behavior. They freeze, lower into crouched positions, and communicate with each other with quiet calls. If outnumbered - or simply caught off guard - Latenivenatrix attempt to flee, prioritizing their own survival over confrontation with this vicious smaller species. This extends even to Latenivenatrix that are nesting or have their nest territory placed. Deinonychus are relentless and pose a considerable problem to Latenivenatrix that they outnumber, and Latenivenatrix prefer to relocate their nests and offspring to a safer location. However, if numbers are equal - or in Latenivenatrix's favor - the rookery responds with aggressive mobbing behavior, and work together in unlimited numbers to drive the rival Deinonychus out. Achillobator do not assist in these matters. Achillobator grouped with Latenivenatrix, and Latenivenatrix groups, do not hunt Deinonychus. Latenivenatrix must challenge the Deinonychus out, or flee the POI.
