# Latenivenatrix – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 4 🥚 per 1 hour. ☀️ Dry Season: 2 🥚 per 1 hour. Latenivenatrix nest in creches, with all other Latenivenatrix in the group placing a nest at the same time. They claim small territories around these nesting sites. These nest sites are placed every day to form a small territory, regardless of whether the group is nesting or not. Latenivenatrix may form same-sex pairs and bonded trios.
