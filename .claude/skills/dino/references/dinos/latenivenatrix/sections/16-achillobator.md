# Latenivenatrix – Achillobator

Latenivenatrix share a rare mutualistic relationship with Achillobator. This companionship is based around hunting together and protecting one another from threats, and often develops into close bonds between Latenivenatrix and their Achillobator companions. Typically, Achillobator seek out Latenivenatrix companions - but solitary or lonely Latenivenatrix may seek out Achillobator themselves, especially if they are unable to find their way back to their rookery. These bonds form quickly, with Latenivenatrix doting on their Achillobator friend every chance they get, through preening, alerting to prey and threats, and bringing them to food and other resources. Once this bond has been formed, if a Latenivenatrix does find or makes their own rookery, Achillobator will follow - and quickly become doted on by the entire group! With repeated successful hunts and considerable time spent together, Latenivenatrix rookeries eventually develop a “lifelong bond” with the same pair of Achillobator, opting not to pair with other Achillobator. This extends to Latenivenatrix deeply mourning Achillobator who pass, to the point they may never find another Achillobator bond again. Orphaned Latenivenatrix may also be adopted by Achillobator. While these chicks are typically much quieter than other Latenivenatrix, they form strong bonds with their Achillobator parent(s), and defend them fiercely. Similarly, if Latenivenatrix have no bonded Achillobator, they may choose to adopt any orphaned Achillobator they come across, treating them as one of their own offspring, even when the Achillobator grows to be much larger than them! Latenivenatrix will only hunt with Achillobator at night. However, they may defend a nearby and bonded Achillobator at any time. Achillobator often nest at the same time as Latenivenatrix, and all offspring will be mixed together in the same creche. All offspring are doted on equally. When offspring is raised in this manner, newly mature Achillobator and Latenivenatrix often disperse together.
