# Stegosaurus – Group Limits

4 Sub/Adults comprising of up to 1 male and 3 females The male must be unrelated to the females. Unlimited offspring Unlimited adoptees This group is known as a zeal. Additionally, Stegosaurus can group with up to 2 adult Miragaia and up to 8 adult Kentrosaurus. Unlimited offspring and adoptees below Sub Adult of these species are permitted. When herding with these other species, the group is known as a stegosaurid herd.
