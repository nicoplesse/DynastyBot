# Stegosaurus – Stegosaurid Herd vs Stegosaurid Herd

Stegosaurus vs Stegosaurus If both groups contain a stegosaurus male, Miragaia and Kentrosaurus bunch tightly together with any female Stegosaurus in the group and step aside to let the males battle. They do not take part in these challenges and are forced to watch the challenge play out. Miragaia and Kentrosaurus can choose whether to stay with their current group, or join the other. If no stegosaurus males are present, and both groups contain a female stegosaurus,  Miragaia and Kentrosaurus allow a single female Stegosaurus to make decisions for the greater herd. This is either to merge, or challenge for a nearby resource. Stegosaurus on one side only With a Stegosaurus only in one group, the group without one is forced to abandon the resource.
