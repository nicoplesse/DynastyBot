# Stegosaurus – Courtship

Courtship only occurs during the night activity schedule. It initially begins with a male circling a female slowly, letting out soft, rumbling growls and giving a shake of his head and body, rustling his plates for her. This begins shortly after a female joins the zeal. Females - even if interested - tend to require a bit of persistence from the male and prove his willingness to put effort in for her, and use this as a form of 'test'. The more persistent a male, the more likely a female will accept his courtship advances. So, females turn away from the male as he circles her, feigning disinterest and seeking to walk away slowly, pushing him to up his persistence to continue circling her. Inexperienced males may initially be confused by this and may even give up their pursuit, prompting frustrated and aggressive calls from the female - further adding to the male's confusion. Experienced males recognize this game and continue their persistence, circling and shaking at the female. Eventually, if she accepts his advances, she will turn to face him, affectionately call and shake back, and the pair will look to find a suitable place together to construct a nest. However,  if the female has no interest in courtship whatsoever, she will lay down during his advances, immediately stopping the "game" and giving a clear signal to the male that she does not wish to nest. Males - even inexperienced ones - always respect this, and simply move on to another female in the group, or save their efforts to try again another time. Males can court with all the females in his group at the same time and usually will - aiming to ensure the females rear young at the same time.
