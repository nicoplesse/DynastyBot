# Stegosaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Headstrong Group member defence - hunting 2 Sub/Adults may take part in an engagement. If a male is present in the zeal, he always defends the targeted individual. Adolescents and below do not count to this engagement limit. Defence of resources (food, water and nesting sites) All grouped Stegosaurus of Sub/Adult age may take part in territory defence against all species. Cross-species protection Stegosaurus may actively defend and join engagements with Miragaia and Kentrosaurus that are grouped with them. Group members outside of these engagement limits must move 10 body lengths away. When hunted: 2 Sub/Adult Stegosaurus may defend the herd, alongside 1 Miragaia and up to 4 Kentrosaurus. Defending resources: If Stegosaurus is present, Miragaia and Kentrosaurus do not take part. Preferred Diet Berries and soft fruit When berries and fruits are scarce, Stegosaurus may opt to eat flowers, nuts and roots.
