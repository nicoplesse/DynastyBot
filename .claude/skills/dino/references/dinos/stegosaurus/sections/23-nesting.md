# Stegosaurus – Nesting

Nests are placed reasonably close to water sources to enable young easy access to drinking water. Females seek to place their nests nearby each other, fostering more of a group environment in which to raise their offspring. Dense coverage close to another female's nest and close to water is an ideal location to place a nest. Once nests are built and eggs laid, females stay by their nests until their young start to hatch. As babies emerge, mothers nuzzle and show affection to their offspring, and encourage them onto their feet for their first stumbling moments.
