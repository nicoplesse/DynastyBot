# Stegosaurus – Stegosaurid herd: Kentrosaurus

Kentrosaurus are accepted into Stegosaurus groups. They serve as a highly useful sentry species for the poorer sighted Stegosaurus. Their speed and alert nature helps Stegosaurus defend their resources, and bring their attention to any potential threats. When finding Kentrosaurus orphans, Stegosaurus are known to adopt this species and raise them. Stegosaurus willingly defends Kentrosaurus from threats, and joins them in engagements.
