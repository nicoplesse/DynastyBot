# Stegosaurus – Zeals

When approached by a predator, Stegosaurus react with aggressive posturing to ward off the threat and appear more formidable. By broadcasting (:broadcast_call:) and pacing, they aim to showcase themselves as dangerous prey. Should a predator choose to hunt them, the male of the zeal always rushes to the defense of the attacked female, and a female will readily defend her bull if in a harem. If no bull is within the Zeal, a female will step in to protect a targeted female. A maximum of 2 Sub/Adult Stegosaurus may engage the threat (active defenders); charging with their tails swinging and pacing around the herd. The remaining Stegosaurus stand their ground, swinging their tails when the threat enters personal space and making room for the engaged Stegosaurus to retreat into safety, should they become wounded. The Stegosaurus not actively engaged only moves away if the threat proves too great (a fellow Stegosaurus reaches heavy death scars) - and hopes their defenders follow suit. (If the entire herd is only the active defenders, and a predator forfeits, the herd can pursue until the threat is killed. Once 1 body falls, combat must stop.) Offspring are always defended by their mother, and usually get pushed out of the way of their swinging tails.
