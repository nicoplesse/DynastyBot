# Stegosaurus – Zeal vs Zeal

Stegosaurus have no tolerance to other Stegosaurus unless they have room in their zeal (group) for other members. If the newcomer cannot be integrated into their zeal, and is not adolescent or younger, they are met with fierce aggression. If two Zeals encounter each other: Individuals instinctively react by posturing upon noticing a rival. This behaviour includes broadcasting (:broadcast_call: ) and standing in line of sight of the ungrouped Stegosaurus. Males and females react in different ways during this engagement: ↪︎ Females stand their ground. They raise on their hind legs to intimidate (:looking:), and broadcast (:broadcast_call: ) to appear more formidable. Even if a Zeal has a male already, females do not aggress another male approaching them. Patience is given to allow both males to compete, or open opportunities to be courted. If a harem has no room for additional females, approaching males simply aggresses the rival group away. Knowing they are outnumbered, the rival females are forced to leave without conflict. However if both groups do not have a male, a single female from each group may step in to challenge the rivals for ownership rights over nearby drinkable water and food resources. ↪︎ Males, when confronted by a rival Zeal, approach boldly. They posture with broadcast calls (:broadcast_call:) and walk towards the approaching group. If a rival male steps forward, the two exchange aggressive bellows (:threaten_call:) and compete for courtship rights over both female groupings. This fight is incredibly risky, and ends on a forfeit call (:danger_call:). The winning male is given an opportunity: court the rival group's females, or chase them from their claimed resource. If he aggresses the rival zeal, he is joined by his females, and they work together to chase out the rival group.
