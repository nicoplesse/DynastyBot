# Dilophosaurus – Engagement Limits

Up to 5 Sub/Adult Dilophosaurus may take part in an engagement. In a leash, the lead breeding group forms the defenders against other predators. Other adults in the group fill the gaps. If adolescents are present, up to 3 may also assist in engagements against up to Large tier, but must attempt an individual forfeit at half health. Their numbers do not contribute to the total engagement limit, and may join in addition to the maximum number of adults. 1 Sub/Adult Dilophosaurus may hunt up to Medium tier, but prefer to scavenge. 2-4 Sub/Adult Dilophosaurus may hunt up to Large tier. A full hunting party of 5 Sub/Adult Dilophosaurus may hunt up to solitary Apex tier, excluding sauropods and carnivores. Dilophosaurus may not use the ability Regurgitate before a hunt. Regurgitate may only be used during an ongoing engagement, when fleeing, or after forfeiting a hunt. All Dilophosaurus flee from Apex carnivores when hunted. However, if the predator catches up to a Dilophosaurus who is fleeing, any other grouped Dilophosaurus within LOS may rush to their aid. Solo Adolescent Dilophosaurus may hunt up to Small tier, but prefer to scavenge. When in dispersal group: 3 Adolescent Dilophosaurus may enter an engagement or hunt. They may hunt attempt up to solo Medium tier. Preferred Prey Solitary Dilophosaurus may hunt up to Medium tier, but prefer to scavenge. 2-4 Dilophosaurus may hunt up to Large tier. A full hunting party of 5 Dilophosaurus may hunt solitary Apex tier, excluding Apex sauropods and carnivores. Dilophosaurus may hunt Apex carnivore offspring, but must flee if the parents return to defend them.
