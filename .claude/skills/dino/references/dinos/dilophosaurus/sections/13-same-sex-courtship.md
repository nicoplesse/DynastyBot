# Dilophosaurus – Same-sex courtship

Individuals hoping to form a same sex pair will still travel to the pond at Stego Mountain. Males or females seeking a same sex partner will place a nest down at the courtship ground, and decorate it clearly with bones and ribcages, highlighting their resourcefulness and ability to provide for their partner. The nest owner then stands and broadcasts :broadcast_call:, mimicking the behavior of vixens seeking a reynard. Interested Dilophosaurus that locate one of these individuals will approach, cooing softly, and walk around the nest to inspect it. Dilophosaurus that have built these nests only accept same sex partners, and aggress away any opposite sex suitors. If happy to accept, they return the :friendly_call: affectionate call, and the new pair settle together on the bone pile, nuzzling against one another. After this, they form a mask grouping, or integrate into an existing one, happy to adopt any orphans they come across to raise with their partner.
