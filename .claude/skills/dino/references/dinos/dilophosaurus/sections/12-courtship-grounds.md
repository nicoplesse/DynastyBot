# Dilophosaurus – Courtship Grounds

To form, or expand a leash, individuals travel to the courtship grounds at Stego Mountain's northern pond. Vixens signal their readiness to pair by locating a good standing place, hoping to make themselves as visible as possible. They begin to scream, letting out loud, wailing calls and lifting their faces to the sky, in the hopes of attracting an interested reynard. Reynards flock to the calling female - sometimes multiple at a time - and observe her, cooing repeatedly to indicate they are interested. Vixens may sometimes have multiple reynards showing interest in her at a time. In this case, she quietens and settles down, watching back and forth as the reynards battle to impress her. Reynard Posturing Competing reynards engage in an intense competition of posturing. They click their jaws and raise further onto their hind legs, showing off their colors, scars, and the size of their crest (❔ emote). They shove themselves in front of other reynards. This action quickly prompts angry nips from the offended male, and sometimes escalates into a full brawl between a pair of males. Should a pair of males fight, this battle continues until one of the pair is reduced to half health, forcing him to forfeit and make the other the victor. The vixen always selects the victor - if another male has not wooed her successfully in the meantime! When physical prowess fails, a skilled reynard knows that the gift of food is the best approach to win over vixens. The more impressive the critter, the better chance he has of securing her as his partner. To indicate acceptance, vixens affectionately call at their chosen male before standing closer to his side and nuzzling him. The reynard shakes proudly for a moment before nuzzling her in return. He leads her to meet the rest of his breeding group, or taken to his preferred biome to start a brand new leash together.
