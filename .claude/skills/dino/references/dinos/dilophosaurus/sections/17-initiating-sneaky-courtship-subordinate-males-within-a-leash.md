# Dilophosaurus – Initiating Sneaky Courtship - Subordinate Males Within a Leash

Subordinate males may attempt to sneakily court females in their leash - but doing so is extremely risky. These courtships must be initiated when the dominant male is distracted, or away entirely. The subordinate male waits until the dominant male is not in the area, and affectionately calls to his chosen female to lure her away from other leash mates. Other females (or subordinate males) may broadcast :broadcast_call:  to call the dominant male over if they catch a pair sneakily courting. Once in a secluded area, he continues affectionate calling, tilting and bobbing his head to flash his crests and garner her attention. Uninterested females have two possible reactions, depending on personality. Those with a more vengeful nature may opt to broadcast :broadcast_call: and draw the attention of the dominant male - while more passive vixens simply turn around and leave to find the rest of their leashmates, ignoring the subordinate's advances. Interested females react with curiosity and interest, standing still and watching (❔ emote) for the male's next move. The subordinate male continues to call affectionately, and then turns to lure the female towards a resource pile. This resource pile can consist of any resource - from mushrooms, flowers, bones, etc. Satisfied females respond with an affectionate call, to seal their acceptance to the courtship. The female then, after successful courtship, runs back to locate the dominant male in order to initiate courtship with him. This is essential to ensure that the dominant male believes the new offspring are his. Failure to do so - or rejection from him - results in the female either being forced to disperse and raise her illicit young alone, or destroy her eggs after they are laid. The subordinate male also seeks to find the dominant male. The subordinate behaves especially submissive at this time, crouching down and cooing softly. In addition, he gathers various gifts to present to him, highlighting his obedience, and hopefully throwing the dominant male off the scent. Caught in the act! If caught, the dominant male will immediately attack group members caught sneakily courting outside his group. Frightened and caught off guard, the guilty sink to their belly and call pitifully using :danger_call: fear calls. Depending on the dominant male's mood at the time, the guilty are either exiled on the spot (removed from group and chased out the POI) or killed. If the dominant male chooses to remove competition by killing the guilty, the guilty can protect themselves.
