# Hatzegopteryx – Physical Dimorphism

This profile does have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Females have dull colors or little contrast on their crests. ♂️ Males have bright flashes of color or high contrast on their crests. Physical dimorphism for Hatzegopteryx is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship. Melanistic individuals are considered female. Melanistic males often experience difficulty finding a mate of the opposite sex, but females suffer no difficulty.
