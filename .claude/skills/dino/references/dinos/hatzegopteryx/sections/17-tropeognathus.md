# Hatzegopteryx – Tropeognathus

The coast-and-sea-dwelling Tropeognathus can pose a risk for young Hatzegopteryx, as they enjoy shoving Hatzegopteryx chicks from the nest with a single beat of their wings. Due to this, Hatzegopteryx avoid nesting on cliffsides in coastal regions, and will aggressively hunt or chase any Tropeognathus out of their territory on sight, during active hours.
