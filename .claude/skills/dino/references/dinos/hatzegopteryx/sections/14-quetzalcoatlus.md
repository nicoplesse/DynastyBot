# Hatzegopteryx – Quetzalcoatlus

Hatzegopteryx will not tolerate the other large flier, Quetzalcoatlus, at any stage in its growth. They view Quetzalcoatlus as prey and baby stealers, and the fiercely parental Hatzegopteryx shriek and call loudly, using their brute size and aerial maneuvers to bludgeon Quetzalcoatlus out of the sky. Quetzalcoatlus is never tolerated by Hatzegopteryx. Hatzegopteryx cannot exist in the same POI as Quetzalcoatlus. If spotted in the same POI, Hatzegopteryx will attempt to drive the Quetzalcoatlus out or hunt it if able - this can be done even during the night, and at any hunger level. Pairs that have territory and fail to drive out or hunt Quetzalcoatlus must move to a different POI and claim new territory. If the pair have young unable to fly, they may carry their young to a new location. As only two parents can carry at a time (or three if an adult offspring is present in the group), other chicks are left behind - and promptly savaged by the Quetzalcoatlus. If the chicks can survive long enough, parents always return to try and carry them to the new nesting location.
