# Hatzegopteryx – Rhamphorhynchus

Rhamphorhynchus and Thalassodromeus are viewed as easy hunts for younger and inexperienced Hatzegopteryx, but can also be seen as risky, as the smaller and nimbler Rhamphorhynchus can take a Hatzegopteryx out of the sky by jumping onto its back. Hatzegopteryx, therefore, attempt to drive out or kill Rhamphorhynchus if it flies into their territory during their active hours. However, if woken by a Rhamphorhynchus in the night, Hatzegopteryx may chase it out - or kill it if able.
