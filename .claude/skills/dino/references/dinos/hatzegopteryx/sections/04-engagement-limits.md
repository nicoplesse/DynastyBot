# Hatzegopteryx – Engagement Limits

Up to 3 Sub/Adults Parents always fiercely defend their grouped offspring, and each other. Sub-Adult/Adult grouped offspring likewise defend their parents. Sibling groups will defend each other. HUNTING LIMIT: Solo Hatzegopteryx may hunt up to Medium tier. Exemption: A solo Hatzegopteryx can choose to hunt a solo Quetzalcoatlus if it encounters one. Hatzegopteryx may hunt up to Large tier when in groups of 2 or more. Hatzegopteryx may hunt any tier of hatchling or juvenile - even if they are protected by an adult. Adolescent Hatzegopteryx do not assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Hatzegopteryx do not hunt other species. DEFENDING A TERRITORY: When in a mated pair, Hatzegopteryx claim large territories consisting of an entire POI, and aggressively chase out any other Hatzegopteryx individuals they are not grouped with. These engagements can turn into a hunt if the other Hatzegopteryx do not leave the territory. If the pair fail to chase out (or kill) the other Hatzegopteryx, they must leave their territory to claim a new one. These engagements can happen at any time of day, and can be done at any hunger level. Hatzegopteryx defend their territories from all other aerial species  and must chase them out upon seeing them. Pairs may not begin a hunt with other aerial species in their LOS if they are within their territory. Only a mated pair claim a territory. Solitary Hatzegopteryx prefer to avoid each other, and do not chase out other Hatzegopteryx or aerials, besides Quetzalcoatlus. Preferred Prey Hatzegopteryx hunt and chase out Quetzalcoatlus whenever possible. They cannot co-exist in the same POI as Quetzalcoatlus, and must chase out and kill the Quetzalcoatlus, or leave the POI. Hatzegopteryx can hunt Quetzalcoatlus at any time of day. This can be done at any hunger level. Solitary Hatzegopteryx may hunt Quetzalcoatlus. Hatzegopteryx prefer to hunt whatever they can carry. They have a strong preference for Deinonychus, Latenivenatrix and Utahraptor. Hatzegopteryx may grab prey mid-flight. Hatzegopteryx will cannibalize unrelated Hatzegopteryx hatchlings and juveniles. Hatzegopteryx may grab prey mid-flight. Hatzegopteryx may not grab prey that is swimming. Hatzegopteryx are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects.
