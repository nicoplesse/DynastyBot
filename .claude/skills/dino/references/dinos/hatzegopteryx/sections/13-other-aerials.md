# Hatzegopteryx – Other Aerials

Hatzegopteryx are aggressive towards all Aerial species, including Rhamphorhynchus, Thalassodromeus, Tropeognathus, Quetzalcoatlus, and even other Hatzegopteryx when in a mated pair. They view all other aerials as a threat to their resources and nests, and therefore fiercely push them out of their territory upon seeing them. When solitary, Hatzegopteryx do not actively chase out these species, except Quetzalcoatlus.
