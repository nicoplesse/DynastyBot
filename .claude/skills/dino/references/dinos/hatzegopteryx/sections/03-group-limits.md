# Hatzegopteryx – Group Limits

Up to 3 Sub/Adults Hatzegopteryx Unlimited Offspring Hatzegopteryx does not adopt. However, Hatzegopteryx will adopt a maximum of 1 orphaned offspring in a same sex pair. This group is known as an aerie. Additionally, up to 3 siblings may group together once they disperse from their birth aerie. They usually stay grouped until each one splits to find a partner.
