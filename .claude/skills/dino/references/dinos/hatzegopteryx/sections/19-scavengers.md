# Hatzegopteryx – Scavengers

Hatzegopteryx do not tolerate scavengers. Hatzegopteryx specialize in hunting smaller prey that they are able to grab. This is always their primary target and preferred method of hunting, only choosing larger prey they cannot pick up if there is nothing else available. Hatzegopteryx may swoop down from the skies and clamp their prey mid-flight, though some will land fully before grasping their victims. Hatzegopteryx - when clamping their prey - immediately fly as high as they can into the sky before releasing their victim to the ground, hoping the impact from the fall kills or at least severely wounds them - and swoop in to finish the job if not. Hatzegopteryx happily descend on heavily wounded prey, and hatchling or juvenile offspring of any tier - preferring easier kills where possible. When grouped, Hatzegopteryx gain confidence from the other members and may try their luck at hunting larger prey, though it is not a guaranteed meal, as their heavy bodies are not built for running long distances or carrying heavy prey for long periods at a time. The more experienced an aerie is, the more easily they communicate during hunts, chirping excitedly if they spot their prey is getting weary. Though - if the hunt changes in the prey's favor or one of them gets increasingly hurt, they will back off, not willing to risk each other's lives for a simple meal. Whether the hunt succeeds or not, the group come together afterwards and dote on each other, tending to their wounds. Up to 3 Sub/Adult Hatzegopteryx may engage.
