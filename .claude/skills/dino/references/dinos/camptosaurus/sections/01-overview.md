# Camptosaurus – Overview

Small Nocturnal Terrestrial Herbivore In a mixed-species herd, Camptosaurus may adapt to a more Diurnal lifestyle. Camptosaurus form Harem Bonds. Camptosaurus are territorial towards other Camptosaurus and claim small territories. Camptosaurus have a Tolerant nature. Camptosaurus may group with: Amargasaurus, Apatosaurus, Argentinosaurus, Bonapartenykus Citipati, Iguanodon, Lambeosaurus, Parasaurolophus, and Struthiomimus. They may not defend these species, even when grouped with them. Camptosaurus tolerate mutated individuals.
