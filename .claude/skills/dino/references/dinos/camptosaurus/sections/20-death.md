# Camptosaurus – Death

Elderly Camptosaurus end up becoming easy meals for carnivores. Worn out by age and no longer capable of fleeing dangers, these individuals end up freezing in place, hopeful in blending in with their surroundings. They’re incredibly committed to this new line of defence. Even as a predator approaches closer, they hold still, with this overconfidence proving fatal. Those that attempt to flee at the last moment are too slow to escape most predators, unable to sprint for long periods, and completely incapable of hopping; they're easily caught. In rare instances, some elderly Camptosaurus pass on peacefully, often in their sleep. While able to group, Camptosaurus are inherently solitary and unsocial, they form only loose friendships with each other. Male hierarchies are built up of dominant and subordinate figures, but rankings may be more intricate between colonies. Camptosaurus does not alert Camptosaurus and other species to dangers and threats; this is a mandatory behaviour. Challenges and fights do not go below half health.
