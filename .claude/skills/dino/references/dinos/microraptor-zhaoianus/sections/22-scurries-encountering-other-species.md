# Microraptor (Zhaoianus) – Scurries encountering other species

Microraptor are known for their hyperaggression in retaliation to either themselves or their groupmates being hunted. When a member of a scurry is attacked, Microraptor will climb up high, swoop, and dive at their aggressor. The rest of the scurry - often witnessing this behavior - join in until the entire scurry is swarming the attacker. Learned vocal ability calls are not used against the attacker, their noisy defence signaled by their own broadcasts and aggressive calls during this time. Once the aggressor flees or forfeits the hunt, or is able to kill a Microraptor, the scurry will stop attacking and return to perch in high places, opting to watch their attacker’s retreat momentarily before resuming as normal. The goal is to harass the hunter until they give up, never going as far as to continue pursuing once they have left the vicinity. If intruders appear to be ignoring them, Microraptor grow relentless, scaling their intimidation display by introducing the mimicked calls of other species in retaliation. Scurries still flee from feared species and do not retaliate against Apex Carnivores, instead calling out in distress before moving out of reach.
