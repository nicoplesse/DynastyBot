# Microraptor (Zhaoianus) – Showdowns

When two male Microraptor have lost patience in sharing the spotlight with each other, they perform an escalating performance of mimicry to both intimidate their rival, and further capture the audience’s attention. Facing one another, both males continue posturing by spreading their wings wide and lifting their heads to the sky. The challenge begins with one of the males vocalizing a mimicked call, always beginning with the meekest they can recall, akin to that of a small-tier species. The opposing Microraptor produces a similar call of their own in response, adhering to the tier set by the first male. If successfully met, the first male responds back, either to another call within the same tier, or escalating their mimicry to a larger tier if they have no further calls in the same tier, and the cycle continues. A male that rushes the process, either by starting with their loudest call, or not allowing for the calls to build-up appropriately, instantly lose the crowd’s favour, seen as inexperienced and immature by spectating females. The showdown continues for as long as necessary, until one male has no choice but to escalate to an apex-tier call. If one male is able to produce an apex-tier call, and the other isn’t, then the first male is deemed the winner of the showdown. If both males are able to produce a replication of an apex-tier call, it is entirely up to a female’s preference in whose performance she had been most captivated by. Female Microraptor judge by different aspects within a showdown, but commonly attractive traits are: The male able to replicate the most calls before producing an apex-tier call. The male able to produce the most unique calls, particularly from species harder to come by. After each vocalization, males posture as they await their rival’s response. A male will never replicate the same call twice in a single showdown. If at any point, a male wishes to forfeit the showdown, he will do so by fear calling and retreating a short distance, allowing for the opposing male to bask in his victory.
