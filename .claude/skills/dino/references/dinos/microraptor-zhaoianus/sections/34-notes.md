# Microraptor (Zhaoianus) – Notes

Microraptor do not assist in the hunts of their carnivore companions. However, Microraptor may remain latched to their companion until an engagement begins. If their companion is chasing, but an engagement has not started, Microraptor may remain latched. Microraptor do not taunt hunters who have failed a hunt against them. Microraptor may not use mimicked calls to purposefully scare or trick other species except during hunts on the species they are hunting. Vocal abilities obtained through “Mimic” may not be used near engagements to avoid any side effects that typically would not occur. Microraptor may not apply vocal buffs to their carnivore companion before they enter an engagement. Microraptor may not issue or accept challenges from other species. When within LOS of an active engagement, Microraptor may not mimic the heart calls of either engaged species. If Lucky Feather activates during a hunt, this is considered an individual forfeit, and the Microraptor must flee.
