# Microraptor (Zhaoianus) – Nesting Territory

When nesting, pairs of Microraptor claim individual trees or cliff ledges. Otherwise, members in a scurry claim only the general location around their nest. Microraptor only issue territory challenges to other Microraptor if their claimed space is landed on, but may begin to aggress sooner in an attempt to warn others that this is their territory. In a scurry, these challenges are still issued to signify claims. Nesting pairs, even within a scurry, do not tolerate other Microraptor close to their nest despite previous relations. Females in a harem will extend this same behaviour towards each other. Challenges are only accepted if another pair desires to use the same location for nesting. If a losing pair of Microraptor have live offspring within their nest, the parents will carry them to safety, forfeiting frequently between carrying to request the winning pair provide them with leniency. The winning pair will permit the losers to gather their chicks, but will not tolerate further aggression, and may continue to attack if provoked, potentially resulting in death.
