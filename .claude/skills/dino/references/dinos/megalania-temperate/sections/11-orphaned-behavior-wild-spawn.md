# Megalania (Temperate) – Orphaned Behavior (Wild Spawn)

Megalania that spawn into the world without a parent fare no differently to those with a mother present. They are immediately driven by instinct, and scurry out of the nest to locate their first requirement - food. Orphaned Arid variants are especially reclusive. They avoid open ground unless necessary, spending their time hugging cliffsides, fallen logs, and rocky areas for cover. They learn fast that visibility is dangerous, and often freeze in coverage when they hear a worrying sound. They are more aggressive when approached, and may bluff charge or snap if cornered - even at Juvenile age. Temperate variant orphans, while also wary, are slightly more explorative and curious in their environments. They trail behind other creatures briefly - at a safe distance - and show more boldness in investigating claimed carcasses, or basking spots used by others. Temperate orphans flee when threatened, until they feel able to defend themselves, and scurry back into the undergrowth for safety. Both variants are fully capable of surviving without their mother's presence. They begin scavenging early, using their speed and agility to dart away from other aggressive carnivores after snatching a bite. Social interaction is rare between orphans, but those that hatched together often stay together until Sub-Adult for safety. Unrelated orphans never group together in game. Megalania are a solitary carnivorous species that inhabit different biomes, quietly stalking to hunt prey. While they often move slowly and observe their surroundings, they are highly sensitive to movement. A rustling bush or flash of motion can provoke them into immediate pursuit, racing in the direction of the movement. This reflexive drive to chase is especially noticeable in juveniles, who often charge at anything that moves. Adults display more restraint, but the instinct remains, and sudden movement is always investigated. Megalania rely heavily on their senses. They constantly flick their tongue to taste the air, gathering scent particles to understand their surroundings. When particularly intrigued or uncertain, Megalania rear up onto their hind legs, turning their heads back and forth to get a better view. Megalania have variants, based in different POIs on the map. Arid variants are native to dry scrubland and deserted areas, while temperate variants inhabit wetter areas, such as woodlands, swamps, and river systems. Regardless of variant, all Megalania enjoy swimming, and are capable of diving below the depths to catch fish or hunt small prey. They regularly soak their bodies in puddles of water, and can be spotted sleeping or basking beside a riverbank or water source.
