# Halszkaraptor – Engagement Limits (Defence)

Predator Reaction: Flighty Unlimited Halskaraptor may participate in an engagement, but this species will flee from almost all predators. When hunted, Halszkaraptor flee from any carnivores that are Small tier or higher. Deinonychus prompt a high fear response in Halszkaraptor, being one of the few carnivores speedy and agile enough to hunt them. Halszkaraptor always stand their ground against Compsognathus and Rhamphorhynchus. One exception is when Small tier carnivores threaten a nesting site with offspring. In these cases, members of the raft will team up in unlimited numbers to mob the predator and defend their nests and young. Preferred Diet Halszkaraptor feed by skimming through the water, searching for lakeweed, small fish, and roots near to water as their main food sources. They have a special fondness for fruit. The high sugar content fuels their quick and agile movements. Their love for fruit sometimes sparks conflicts with Dryosaurus, which fiercely guards their berry bushes. While they enjoy a varied diet, female Halszkaraptor will sometimes switch to exclusively eating fish to prepare for their egg laying season. Halszkaraptor shun others of their species that are seen consuming meat or bones.
