# Halszkaraptor – Overview

Tiny Cathemeral Semi-Aquatic Herbivore Halszkaraptor form promiscuous pairbonds. Halszkaraptor are sedentary. Halszkaraptor have a passive nature. Halszkaraptor may group with: Deinocheirus and Lurdusaurus. Halszkaraptor may not defend these species, even when grouped with them. Halszkaraptor tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.
