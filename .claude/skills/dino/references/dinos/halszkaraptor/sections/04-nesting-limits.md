# Halszkaraptor – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 8 🥚 per 1 hour. ☀️ Dry Season: 3 🥚 per 1 hour. Halszkaraptor  nest in dense foliage by rivers and ponds in their preferred POIs. Halszkaraptor prefer to choose small islands amid rivers or ponds to nest on. Their nests are constructed out of soft and bushy materials that are easy to navigate through and hide within, should a predator appear.
