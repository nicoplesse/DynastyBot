# Deinosuchus – Hunting Strategy

Deinosuchus are ambush hunters, using the water to their advantage to surprise and kill their prey. They spend plenty of time under the water beside the shore, waiting for terrestrial dinosaurs to venture just a little bit too close. Deinosuchus may third party any engagement that comes within two body lengths of the water. Deinosuchus may hunt any tier of creature, but do not bother with Rhamphorhynchus or Compsognathus. A hunt must begin with Deinosuchus in the water. Prey can then be pursued in land while sprinting until their stamina runs out. If the Deinosuchus has not inflicted fracture, or reduced their prey to deathscars, they must forfeit and return to the water. They lay motionless beneath the surface, waiting for the right moment to strike and crush their prey with their powerful jaws. When managing to hunt others in water, they clamp onto their victims and begin "death rolling", turning violently in the water to tear their prey apart. Sarcosuchus are a priority target for Deinosuchus, and they are hunted above other prey at most instances if the situation is favorable for the Deinosuchus.
