# Apatosaurus – Overview

Apex Diurnal Terrestrial Herbivore Apatosaurus form temporary pairbonds that can form into lifelong pairbonds through mutual decision. Apatosaurus are nomadic. Apatosaurus have a tolerant nature. Apatosaurus may group with: Amargasaurus, Argentinosaurus, Citipati, Struthiomimus. Apatosaurus do not defend these other species, but allow themselves to be used as meatshields. Apatosaurus tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.
