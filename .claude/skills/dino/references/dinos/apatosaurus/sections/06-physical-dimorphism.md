# Apatosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

This profile does not have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Females, known as cows, have smooth, spineless backs, necks and tails. Suitable subspecies include: Standard Variation 5, Thin, Stocky Variation 7, Standard Variation 8 ♂️ Males, known as bulls, have a row of spines that run along their back, neck and tail. Suitable subspecies include: Standard, Slim, Stocky, Standard Variation 4 Physical dimorphism for Apatosaurus is mandatory.
