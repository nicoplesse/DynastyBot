# Apatosaurus – Musth

Mature bulls enter periods comparable to "musth" during courtship periods. These periods occur during rest stops, after females have signified they are ready to court. They become restless and even territorial over particular females, and show confrontational behavior toward rival males. Bulls in musth wander from their ordinary routine and display a heightened swagger, with exaggerated posturing and low, booming vocalizations. More can be found under Courtship & Parenthood.
