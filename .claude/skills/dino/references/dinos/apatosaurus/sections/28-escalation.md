# Apatosaurus – Escalation

Should a carnivore be deemed to be a threat - or show clear hunting intent - Apatosaurus begin moving close together with the rest of their pod, quickly shoving Adolescent calves, females, and older and more frail members behind bulls, who instinctively pace the outside. One bull from each pod - or the matriarch, if no bulls are available - take it upon themselves to issue warning bellows and stomps, signifying their readiness to defend if necessary.
