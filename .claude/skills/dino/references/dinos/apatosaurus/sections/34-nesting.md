# Apatosaurus – Nesting

Once all interested cows have courted successfully, the herd begins a migration to one of their dedicated nesting grounds: Volcano Island or Burned Forest. Apatosaurus instinctively nest in this location, where the warm sand helps to incubate their eggs, with the sauropods being simply too large to brood over a nest. Eggs are laid in clusters, with multiple cows preferring to place their nests close together, positioned just on the border of thick foliage - a spot ideal for fresh hatchlings to scatter to for safety. After placing their nests and laying their eggs, the herd moves on to rest and wait for their offspring to grow. In Riparia, they move to the hot springs area in Volcano Islands, and in Gondwa, they move deeper into Burned Forest. Bulls stay with their courted female for the entirety of this period, leaving their natal pods to remain with the female.
