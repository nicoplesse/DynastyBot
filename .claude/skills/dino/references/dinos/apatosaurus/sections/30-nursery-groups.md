# Apatosaurus – Nursery Groups

Adults do not become involved in the defence of calves below Adolescent while they remain within their nursery group. Too large to safely navigate the dense forest, their involvement often proves more dangerous than protective, with a high risk of injuring the calves they intend to defend. Calves, meanwhile, do not flee towards adults when attacked or if they spot predators. They rely on hiding to prevent a carnivore from hunting them - but if discovered, quickly scatter deeper into the heart of the forest. Unlike adults, up to a maximum of 4 calves below Adolescent may work together to defend each other from predators. This includes the attacked individual and three defenders, who whip their tails and smack their heads to try and overwhelm the predators into forfeiting.
