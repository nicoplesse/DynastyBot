# Apatosaurus – Citipati & Struthiomimus

Both Citipati & Struthiomimus are highly regarded as companions by Apatosaurus, and their presence is welcomed around both pods and young nursery groups. Their alertness and sharp alarm calls make them invaluable sentries, enabling Apatosaurus to have more rest periods and reduced vigilance at feeding grounds and sleep spots. When herds have young and calves retreat into the undergrowth, both species are expected to remain nearby and follow them, helping watch over these hidden nursery groups and sounding alarm at the first sign of danger.
