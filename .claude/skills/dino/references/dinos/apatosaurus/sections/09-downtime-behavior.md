# Apatosaurus – Downtime Behavior

Apatosaurus take short naps overnight, with many individuals preferring to doze standing up, their heads lowered. The older they become, the longer these naps seem to last. Older individuals sometimes take to sleeping an entire night period, while younger Apatosaurus sleep in shorter, quick bursts. Noise and "chatter" amongst individuals is lessened during night times, with awake individuals opting to stay quiet, keenly listening out for their sentry's alarm calls.
