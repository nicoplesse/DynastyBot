# Quetzalcoatlus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 1 🥚 per 3 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 3 🥚 per 3 hours. Quetzalcoatlus prefer to nest in high places, such on sheer cliffsides, tall rocks, and etc, allowing them to watch for threats and keep young out of reach of hungry carnivores. Their nests are made out of roots, reeds and mud. Same sex pairings do occur. Female same sex pairings may adopt orphaned Quetzalcoatlus if feeling broody, whereas male same sex pairings do not. Opposite sex pairings never adopt.
