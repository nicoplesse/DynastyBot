# Quetzalcoatlus – Offspring Maturity

Quetzalcoatlus chicks may temporarily flock together at sub-adult to ensure survival until they find another grouping opportunity. Male Quetzalcoatlus who have survived their bold youth begin to dull in color, becoming less saturated and therefore less likely to successfully court or impress other females. Female Quetzalcoatlus, however, remain the same in coloration. Elderly Quetzalcoatlus will typically be solitary, weakened by their age and with no one to help or care for them. Their beaks become more fragile and they struggle to move as quickly on the ground as they did when they were younger. Elderly Quetzalcoatlus will prefer to keep their hunts as easy as possible, focusing on critters, unattended younglings, and other sick or dying creatures, in order to feed themselves and stay alive.
