# Quetzalcoatlus – Preferred Prey & Diet

Quetzalcoatlus is primarily a lazy hunter, and prefers to challenge and bully other carnivores up to their hunting tier off of their kills over hunting, or to steal hunts from other carnivores. Before hunting anything else in LOS, Quetzalcoatlus will hunt offspring from adolescent and below and deathscarred creatures of any tier. The secondary preference are injured / sick and Albino species up to Large tier. This is up to Apex tier when in a group of 2 or more. Finally, healthy targets up to Medium tier when solo, and Large tier when in a group. Quetzalcoatlus will readily cannibalize others of its kind that are adolescent and below and unrelated to them. However, in times of desperate hunger, they may even cannibalize one of their own offspring. Quetzalcoatlus will also hunt Hatzegopteryx if they outnumber them. When Hatzegopteryx outnumbers Quetzalcoatlus, Quetzalcoatlus prefers to avoid them entirely. Quetzalcoatlus may feed from the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects.
