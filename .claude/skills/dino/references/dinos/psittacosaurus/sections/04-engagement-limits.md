# Psittacosaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Flighty All Psittacosaurus may engage in defensive retaliation against Tiny and Small species in any numbers, or up to 1 Medium. Psittacosaurus flee from 2 or more Medium, Large, and Apex tier when hunted. Hatchlings, juveniles, and adolescents do not participate in defensive retaliation and always flee, attempting to evade attention. Preferred Diet Fruit, roots, nuts, berries and insects. A Psittacosaurus may attempt to consume noxious meat in dire circumstances - however, this brings a short bout of sickness.
