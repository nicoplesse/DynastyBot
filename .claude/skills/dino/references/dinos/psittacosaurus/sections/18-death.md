# Psittacosaurus – Death

As the burden of age becomes too great, an elderly Psittacosaurus moves slowly and with visible discomfort. Years of exertion and leadership often result in malnutrition and increased vulnerability. Sensing the end of their life, the aging ceratopsian withdraws from the passel and travels to a familiar, warm region where the climate eases the ache of stiff joints. There, the Psittacosaurus selects a secluded location and burrows deep into the earth. Entering a final state of deep rest, they remain underground, allowing their body to settle into stillness as they pass away peacefully, unseen and undisturbed. Psittacosaurus cannot defend other species. However, they may use their calls to aid allies they are grouped with.
