# Psittacosaurus – Challenges for Quillguard

Occasionally, another elder in the passel may wish to challenge for the title of ‘Quillguard’. To initiate this, the individual approaches the Quillguard and calls aggressively. Both Psittacosaurus then engage in a ritualised display of dominance, shaking their bodies and waving their tails skyward in an elaborate contest of movement, stamina, and confidence. As the display continues, members of the passel gradually align themselves beside the contender they find most impressive. Support is expressed through proximity, posture, and vocalisation. The Psittacosaurus with the greatest visible support is recognized as the new Quillguard, and the rival steps down without further conflict. In the rare event of a tie, a secondary ritual is enacted. A randomly chosen member of the passel positions themselves between the two challengers before fleeing without warning. The first challenger to pursue and deliver a soft nip to the fleeing individual is declared victorious and assumes the role of Quillguard, ending the dispute immediately.
