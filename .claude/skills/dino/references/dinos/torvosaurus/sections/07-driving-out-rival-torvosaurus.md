# Torvosaurus – Driving Out Rival Torvosaurus

Engagement Limits: Up to 4 Sub/Adults may work together to drive out rival Torvosaurus from the POI. Torvosaurus must allow rivals to leave the POI rather than hunt them if they are on a timer. Exceptions: Packs with space for another female may tolerate rival females to encourage recruitment. If another pack arrives, the male may decide to challenge the other male for his female(s), depending on how many they have room for. This is signified by the male challenging the other male directly, and not the females. Females watch from the sides and do not join in, unlike when the pack normally drives out rivals. Coalitions and solitary males tolerate solitary females or sisterhoods within the POI. Sisterhoods tolerate males and other females if there is space for them. Sauropod Presence: If Argentinosaurus, Apatosaurus or Ampelosaurus are in the POI, all Torvosaurus groups permit other Torvosaurus in the area to facilitate mob hunting. Amargasaurus does not count.
