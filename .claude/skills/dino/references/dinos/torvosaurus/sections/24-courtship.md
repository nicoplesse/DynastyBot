# Torvosaurus – Courtship

Females typically come into season all at the same time - a biological strategy that works out well for them, as they prefer to raise young together, and often pool offspring into one general 'creche'. When a female is ready to court, they begin hounding the male in the pack relentlessly. They follow him around, growling and huffing, shaking at him, and laying down beside and in front of him. Some males can be particularly dim when it comes to recognizing these signs in his females - and may result in the female getting annoyed at his lack of attention and nipping at his tail. If a male fails to court females when they are in season, repeatedly ignores their advances, or nestings consistently result in infertile eggs, he runs the risk of being driven out by all females in the pack. If he accepts their advances, he will settle down beside them and shake, affectionately calling :friendly_call: to them and signalling his readiness to assist in parenthood.
