# Torvosaurus – Group Limits

Up to 4 Sub/Adults, comprising 1 unrelated male and up to 3 females of any relation. In addition, up to 3 Sub/Adult female offspring that remain with the pack while the parents are not nesting. These adult relations must disperse before the parents can nest again. This must be logged in the 🩸・bloodline-logs, and can only be the most recent offspring. Unlimited Offspring Torvosaurus in this grouping do not adopt. This group is known as a pack. Up to 2 Sub/Adult males of any relation. No offspring or adoptees. This group is known as a coalition. They must separate before females can join them. Up to 3 Sub/Adult females of any relation. Unlimited Offspring Unlimited Adoptees This group is known as a sisterhood. Additionally, Torvosaurus may form dispersal groups consisting of unlimited individuals of Adolescent and below.
