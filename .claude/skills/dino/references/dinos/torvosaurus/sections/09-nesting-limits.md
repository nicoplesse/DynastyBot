# Torvosaurus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ ☀️ Rainy & Dry Season: 1-3 🥚 per 3 hours. You must use the bot command /rolleggs Torvosaurus in 🎲・genetics-rolls to determine the eggs your female lays before making your nesting post. You must link this roll inside of the nesting post. Torvosaurus nest anywhere in their preferred POIs, though they prefer to nest near sources of roots or bones if possible. If a rival male kills or chases away all the offspring of a female, her nesting timer is reset and she may nest again. Females may form same-sex bonds but can still nest with males.
