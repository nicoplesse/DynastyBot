# Torvosaurus – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Melanistic: Melanistic females are treated no differently from others. However, melanistic males, lacking the typical bright coloration, may not always be accepted by females. Their success depends heavily on the preferences of the females they encounter. Albino: Albino individuals are consistently targeted and cannibalized on sight by other Torvosaurus, with no tolerance shown toward them. Established packs rarely have aggression among them beyond typical playfights - but there is a stark difference between consensual fighting and active aggressive behavior. Coalitions have the highest chance of group-related aggression, with the dominant of the pair opting to push his weight around by forcing the other male to wait until he has eaten before he can take his fill, and pushing him around as they patrol. Males almost never show aggression to the females in their pack. Doing so is deadly - females will never tolerate aggression towards them and will unify in response to any of their bonded females meeting hostility from their male. The group bands together and responds with violence - launching a series of attacks and vicious bites until he is forced to submit. In extreme cases, they may go even further, and drive him out of the POI and away from them entirely, rejecting him and dissolving their group.
