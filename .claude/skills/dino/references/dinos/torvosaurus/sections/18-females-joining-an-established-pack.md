# Torvosaurus – Females joining an established pack

After a female has been successfully wooed by a male, she must next be approached by the other female(s) in the pack - who likely have been observing the interactions silently from afar. The new female must show submission to the other females - approaching them in a crouched position, with head bowed in a show of gentleness and acceptance. The existing females will stand over the newcomer, sniffing the air around her and calling over her as they inspect her. This inspection is brief, and all parties relax the tension by shaking off afterwards, accepting the new female into the group and allowing her to participate in hunts and group grooming activities. Very rarely, females may reject the new addition. In these instances, the females will launch an aggressive attack towards her - one that won't stop until the male intervenes and breaks up the fight. The male may have to make a decision in these circumstances. If the females show no sign of being able to settle, he will have to drive the new female away - or opt to leave with her himself.
