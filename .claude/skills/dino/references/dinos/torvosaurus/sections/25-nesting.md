# Torvosaurus – Nesting

Bonded females happily share nesting sites - opting to place their nests close together to better pool their offspring. Females that are new to the pack may have to place their nest a little further away until they bond more, but all females nest within a similar vicinity to one another. Males assist females in nest building by ferrying them resources to construct them, and spends time patrolling around the borders of the nesting grounds to drive away any threats to the future young.
