# Torvosaurus – Death

The death of a closely bonded member is mourned by all members of the pack. If daughters opt to stay once they reach Sub-Adult, a post must be logged in the 🩸・bloodline-logs, linking to the nesting post and detailing relationships. Torvosaurus may only hunt during the day, but can hunt outside of their hunting schedule if the target is albino, on deathscars, or hatchling/juvenile. You must use the bot command /rolleggs Torvosaurus in 🎲・genetics-rolls to determine the eggs your female lays before making your nesting post. You must link this roll inside of the nesting post. Torvosaurus have mandatory greetings: females greet others with ⤵️ emote and males greet others with ⤴️ emote. In a full pack only females are permitted to hunt. Torvosaurus only tolerate other packs in their territory if they wish to mob a sauropod that is not Amargasaurus. Torvosaurus have a special relationship with Thalassodromeus (look under Cross-Species Interaction for more detail). Packs with space for another female may tolerate rival females to encourage recruitment. Males without a full pack of females can challenge other males without a full pack for their female(s). They challenge the male only. Females do not intervene in this, on either side. Male torvosaurus will always cannibalize orphan young, and may commit infanticide should he successfully takeover a pack with young offspring under adolescent. 4.8.1, 8.4.1 Full sisterhoods or packs may hunt rival Torvosaurus if they have no available room. 8.4 Rival torvosaurus may be hunted or challenged out at any time of day. 1.4 Torvosaurus are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects.
