## Besondere Risiken

- **Same-species territory risk:** Giganotosaurus do not hunt adult Tyrannosaurus, Tyrannotitan, or other Giganotosaurus, but may challenge them and eat from their bodies.
- **Same-species territory risk:** They must challenge the other Apex carnivores out of the POI before initiating a hunt on any other species, or leave themselves.
- **Mutation-specific risk:** Giganotosaurus may hunt Tyrannosaurus, Tyrannotitan or Giganotosaurus who are Albino, deathscarred, or Adolescent and below.

_Methode: Profile-specific hunt, prey, rivalry and avoidance rules are checked before tier eligibility. Relevant land, water or flight caps and sprint duration determine whether contact can be forced. By default you are solo and the other side brings its full legal group; both sides can be set to any size up to the profile caps. Unlimited groups are never turned into an invented number. Every hit is resolved as damage × (attacker CW ÷ victim CW) ÷ victim armour from the adult stat curves. Each side focus-fires one target at a time with its best spammable attack plus up to two stronger abilities. Times assume every swing lands, so agile, smaller players outperform them by dodging. Bleed stacks, ignores armour and blocks healing; it is shown as pressure and only converted into health when you switch on the estimate, because that rate is not documented. Mapped area overlap lowers or raises relevance, but an explicit cross-species rule can override sparse map overlap._
