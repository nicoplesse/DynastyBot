# Giganotosaurus – Overview

Apex Diurnal Terrestrial Carnivore Giganotosaurus form temporary pairbonds. These can develop into lifelong pairbonds after 5 clutches together. Giganatosaurus are nomadic. Giganotosaurus have an aggressive nature. Giganotosaurus are not scavengers. Giganotosaurus cannibalize albino and tolerate melanistic individuals.
