# Giganotosaurus – Low Testosterone / Melanistic Courtship

Males who cannot flush their crests must attempt a different style of courtship. Once he has identified that a female is within the same POI as him, he must stay out of sight in order to not be aggressed. The first step is to collect an appropriate gift for the female, typically a large critter, or killing another species to present to her. Once he has done so, he broadcasts (:broadcast_call:) repeatedly to call for her. Females broadcast in return and rush to the area. However, she stops when there is five body lengths of distance between herself and the male, pausing to eye him suspiciously. This is when the male must then attempt the same behavior as males who can flush their crests - moving slowly and carefully so as to not scare her away, or launch into a fight with her. As he is unable to soothe females with the bright color of a flushed crest, he must be extra careful to not stress her or aggravate her beyond the point of tolerance.
