# Giganotosaurus – Offspring Maturity

As the kits begin to reach sub-adult, they begin to develop further in their independence and will drift away from their parents and bonded siblings. Some offspring may stay with their mother once reaching sub-adult, after their father disperses to seek other courting opportunities. A father will disperse from the group after the they have successfully raised the offspring to sub-adult, pairing up again with one of his children or setting off alone to continue to spread his bloodline across Gondwa. Otherwise, if the pair is interested in courting again, they may remain grouped. Elderly Giganotosaurus stop having interest in nesting, and opt to remain solitary as they live out the last years of their lives. Their scales begin to grey out, desaturate or lighten, and they prefer to spend most of their days napping in the sunshine rather than taking on other Giganotosaurus or large prey.
