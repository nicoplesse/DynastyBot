# Giganotosaurus – Engagement Limits

Up to 2 Sub/Adult Giganotosaurus may enter an engagement. Grouped Sub/Adult Giganotosaurus may always defend each other. Giganotosaurus may hunt up to any tier. Giganotosaurus do not hunt adult Tyrannosaurus, Tyrannotitan, or other Giganotosaurus, but may challenge them and eat from their bodies. They must challenge the other Apex carnivores out of the POI before initiating a hunt on any other species, or leave themselves. Giganotosaurus may hunt Tyrannosaurus, Tyrannotitan or Giganotosaurus who are Albino, deathscarred, or Adolescent and below. Parents always defend their grouped offspring. Adolescent Giganotosaurus do not assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Giganotosaurus may attempt to hunt Solo Large tier. Preferred Prey Giganotosaurus target any Apex herbivores first, then Large if available. However, if Tyrannotitan, Tyrannosaurus, or Giganotosaurus is within the same POI and within LOS, Giganotosaurus must challenge the apex before hunting anything else in the POI. If they are unwilling, they must leave the POI. Solitary Giganotosaurus do not hunt Apatosaurus or Argentinosaurus unless a kill seems likely (the individual is deathscarred, Adolescent or below, or Albino). Giganotosaurus openly hunt and cannibalize Albino members of their species. Giganotosaurus cannibalize unattended ungrouped offspring of Adolescent and below. Giganotosaurus are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects. See 📜・server-guideOPTIONAL - Illnesses & Injuries for more information.
