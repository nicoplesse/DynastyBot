# Eurhinosaurus – Orphaned Behavior

Orphaned Eurhinosaurus are born into the waters alone and defenceless. With natural survival instincts these orphans tend to hide within overgrown kelp and coral rocks. They remain hidden feeding off of small fish and crustaceans. As they mature into sub-adult these orphans explore more bravely, venturing out into open waters in hopes they may find a shoal to join.
