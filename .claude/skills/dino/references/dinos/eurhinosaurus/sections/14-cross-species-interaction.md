# Eurhinosaurus – Cross-Species Interaction

Eurhinosaurus display a strong fascination and affection for Deinocherus, admiring these docile semi-aquatics for their ability to spurt water, creating playful environments that the Eurhinosaurus eagerly enjoy. They will often attempt to trade fish or decorative shells with Deinocherus in exchange for water spouts, turning the encounter into a joyful game. One of the species Eurhinosaurus finds the most entertaining to interact with is Kaiwhekea. They view these slow-moving aquatic creatures as easy and amusing prey, and will otp to hunt this species above all other prey choices. Often turning the hunt into a cruel sport, they dart in and out, nipping off chunks of flesh, and disorienting the Kaiwhekea with high-pitched cries. They use their speed to overwhelm it, and continue chasing and nipping the Kaiwhekea until it succumbs to its injuries or manages to escape. Eurhinosaurus consider most other species in a neural manner, except for Spinosaurus and Deinosuchus which they will flee from. They sometimes come across as arrogant, enjoying their speed and perceived dominance over the ocean territories. Terrestrial species that they encounter are sometimes teased, they especially enjoy engaging in screaming matches with Pycnonemosaurus, who rarely back down from the noisy challenge.
