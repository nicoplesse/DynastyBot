# Eurhinosaurus – Aggressive Interaction

Eurhinosaurus are generally not aggressive toward members of their own species. When encountering smaller shoals, they often choose to group together, favoring safety in numbers. However, when approached by a large shoal, while in an area with limited resources, the existing shoal may show defensive behavior and line up to show a challenge calling out and not moving from their defensive line. This display is to show the other shoal that there is no room for them to join the POI, and they must find another. If the other shoal do not leave, the members may attack one another. Typically, shoals strive to maintain harmony among their own kind. Aggressive encounters are rare and usually occur only when resources are scarce. In most cases, they prefer to relocate to another point of interest where resources are more abundant.
