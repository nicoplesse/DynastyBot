# Pachycephalosaurus – Overview

Small Diurnal Terrestrial Herbivore Activity schedule exception: Pachycephalosaurus participates in defence and challenges over territory at any time. Pachycephalosaurus form harem bonds. Pachycephalosaurus travel along migratory paths and are sedentary. Additionally, they defend small territories on a rock, cliff or within caves. Pachycephalosaurus have a defensive nature. Pachycephalosaurus do not group with other species. Pachycephalosaurus are tolerant of all mutations.
