# Pachycephalosaurus – Group Limits

1 Adult Male, Unlimited Females, Unlimited Non-Dimorphic Males Unlimited Offspring and *Yearlings Unlimited Adoptees This group is known as a band. Adult males that do not have a group of females live solitary lives and cannot group with other adult males. However, yearlings are mistaken as female and are accepted by an Adult male, which forms a band together.
