# Pachycephalosaurus – Offspring Maturity

It's only when yearlings mature (shed out of the yearling skin) is the understanding made by the adult male and the youngling is promptly exiled from the Band (removed from the group immediately and aggressed away by all adults). Even females will participate in shunning these young males, the behavior always initiated by a lead and supported by followers. Females do not permit her sons to remain within the band to avoid inbreeding. Elderly Behavior is mandatory for females, but optional for males. The likelihood of a Pachycephalosaurus surviving beyond 20 clutches is slim, but those who do grow demanding in their twilight years, hardened by experience and loss. Scarred, deliberate, and respected, these elders no longer yield - they embody the authority of a leader, whether or not the role was once theirs in their youth.
