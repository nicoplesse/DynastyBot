# Pachycephalosaurus – Rules Of The Yearling Growth Stage

All newly created Pachycephalosaurus characters must have the  DR Yearling skin  equipped after you spawn. After growing to full adult, may you switch to antoher skin. Growing out of the yearling growth stage is optional. you may remain a yearling for as long as you want. But must still abide by the limitations of this profile feature. The DR Yearling skin is not a mutation.
