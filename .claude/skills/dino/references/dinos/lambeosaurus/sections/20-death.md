# Lambeosaurus – Death

Lambeosaurus deeply grieve the losses of other members in their herd, particularly offspring, parents, siblings, and mates. Lambeosaurus may enter a temporary period of grieving where they move more slowly or stay in areas the deceased individual enjoyed for extended periods of time. Elderly Lambeosaurus typically pass away in their sleep, if not lost to opportunistic carnivores when they get left behind by their herd. When they settle down to sleep one night, they may never rise again, surrounded by the comfort of their herdmates. Lambeosaurus may use Family Chorus in a mixed species herd. Defenders are only allowed to use a maximum of 3 buff calls during an engagement/challenge that directly involves them. This limit applies to Primal Mend, Healing Call, Rejuvenating Roar and Guiding Beacon. Lambeosaurus can use water to its advantage in order to flee from predators. It may defend itself from predators in water (but may not stop crossing to do so). Lambeosaurus must cross water, not stand in the water, but may cross as many times as is necessary to deter predators, as long as it has appropriate stamina.
