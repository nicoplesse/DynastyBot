# Lambeosaurus – Overview

Medium Diurnal Terrestrial Herbivore Lambeosaurus form temporary pairbonds that may develop into lifelong bonds after 5 successful clutches. Lambeosaurus are nomadic. Lambeosaurus have a passive nature. Lambeosaurus may group with: Bonapartenykus, Camptosaurus, Iguanodon, Parasaurolophus, Citipati and Struthiomimus. Lambeosaurus may not defend these species, even when grouped with them. Lambeosaurus tolerate albino, melanistic, and mutated individuals.
