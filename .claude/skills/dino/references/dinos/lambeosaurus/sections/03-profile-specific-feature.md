# Lambeosaurus – Profile Specific Feature

Some female Lambeosaurus born to a majority male clutch, or with a male twin, may be born as a freemartin. This means that, while she is biologically female, she may exhibit some male behaviors, and cannot nest, even if courted successfully. This is further indicated by using the DR Freemartin skin. Male Lambeosaurus who are born to a majority female clutch, or with a female twin, may use the DR Freemartin skin. They typically father smaller, limited clutches, and express more dominance than their male counterparts.
