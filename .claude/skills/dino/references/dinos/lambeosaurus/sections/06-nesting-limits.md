# Lambeosaurus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 4 🥚 per 3 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 2 🥚 per 4 hours. Freemartin does have no successful eggs in their clutches; Freemartin bucks may only father up to 1 egg at a time, in either season. Multipliers may not be used to increase the number of eggs. Lambeosaurus nest in dense foliage nearby fresh water sources. Their nests are built out of rocks and mud. Lambeosaurus may form same-sex pairs, and may adopt. Lambeosaurus in same sex pairs will not nest with the opposite sex.
