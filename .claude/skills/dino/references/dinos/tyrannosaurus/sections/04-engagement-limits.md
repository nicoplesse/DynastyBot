# Tyrannosaurus – Engagement Limits

2 individuals may enter a hunt or engagement. In a mated pair, one individual usually acts as a diversion to allow their mate to take down the prey. Adolescent Tyrannosaurus do not assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Tyrannosaurus may attempt to hunt Solo Large tier. Tyrannosaurus may hunt any tier. Tyrannosaurus are ambush hunters. They do not pursue over long distances, and must make a kill quickly. If they do not land a bite on their prey before their stamina runs out, they must abandon the hunt. Tyrannosaurus cannot sit or stop running in order to re-gain stamina. Tyrannosaurus must push out ALL terrestrial Apex carnivores from the POI. This must be done before any other hunts can take place. If already engaged or with a body, the body or engagements must be finished first. Tyrannosaurus are highly territorial, and push out Large tier and above carnivores. Tyrannosaurus may tolerate scavengers, but always tolerate Compsognathus. Preferred Prey Eotriceratops, Barsboldia, Parasaurolophus, Anodontosaurus. These species are targeted first in the POI. Tyrannosaurus cannot hunt when other Apex terrestrial carnivores are in the POI. Tyrannosaurus never hunt Compsognathus. Tyrannosaurus only hunt Giganotosaurus or Tyrannotitan when at desperate hunger (35% or below), unless the target is Albino, deathscarred, or aged Adolescent and below. This must be initiated as a successful ambush. Tyrannosaurus adults may cannibalize unrelated offspring of Adolescent and below, and Albinos. Otherwise, they do not hunt other Tyrannosaurus, but may eat from their corpses. Tyrannosaurus are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects.
