# Tyrannosaurus – Giganotosaurus & Tyrannotitan

Highly territorial, Tyrannosaurus do not tolerate the presence of other Apex terrestrial carnivores. After other Tyrannosaurus rivals are driven out, Tyrannosaurus turn their attention to Giganotosaurus and Tyrannotitan. These species must be driven out of the POI even if not the Tyrannosaurus' territory. If Tyrannosaurus wishes to remain in the POI, they must drive out any rival Apex terrestrials, or move on by themselves. Tyrannosaurus approach these species, calling loudly :broadcast_call: , before issuing low, threatening roars :threaten_call: to highlight their willingness to fight. Tyrannosaurus do not hunt these species unless at desperate hunger (35%), unless the species appears Albino, are deathscarred, or aged Adolescent or below. If hunting them, a successful ambush must be employed.
