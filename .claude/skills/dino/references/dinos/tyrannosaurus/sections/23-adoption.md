# Tyrannosaurus – Adoption

Tyrannosaurus do not adopt, and will cannibalize unrelated offspring of Adolescent and under. As Tyrannosaurus ages, subtle but significant changes begin to emerge in their bodies and behavior. While not all individuals reach old age - a Tyrannosaurus' life is often short and brutal - those that do carry the marks of a hard life, bearing scarred hides, worn down teeth, and slower movement. Reaction times and stamina overall decline, forcing Tyrannosaurus to target weaker or slower prey. While an Anodontosaurus may have been an exciting hunt to a much younger adult, these extremely tough herbivores could cause immeasurable pain and cracked teeth to elders. Pairs remain together even in their elder stage, refusing to abandon their lifelong mate.
