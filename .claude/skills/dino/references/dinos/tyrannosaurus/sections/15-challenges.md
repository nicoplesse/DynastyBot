# Tyrannosaurus – Challenges

Tyrannosaurus must challenge any intruders out of their territory, and intruders wishing to take over a territory must make their intentions clear. Tyrannosaurus entering a claimed territory with the intention of taking it for themselves step into the middle of the POI, broadcasting loudly to draw the owner of the territory to them. When the territory holder arrives, the two begin circling the other, roaring and snarling aggressively and snapping at the air with their jaws. If neither backs down and flees the POI, the encounter erupts into a vicious battle. Battles are bloody and ferocious, and do not stop until one party either dies or manages to flee the POI. If the opponent flees, the victor chases after it, nipping at the air behind them to ensure they leave. Once they leave the POI, the victor stops their pursuit, roaring loudly to declare their dominance and ownership over the territory. The loser must repeatedly call out their defeat, either through heart :friendly_call: calling or fear :danger_call: calling as they leave. If they stop, eat, drink or quest on the way out of the POI, the winner may kill them.
