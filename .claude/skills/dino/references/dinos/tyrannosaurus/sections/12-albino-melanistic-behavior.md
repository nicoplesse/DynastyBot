# Tyrannosaurus – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Melanistics suffer no issues when it comes to finding a mate and acceptance from other Tyrannosaurus. Unfortunately, albinos face an especially lonely existence. They are never accepted as mates, and do not accept other albinos as a potential partner. Albinos are shunned by their parents as soon as their skin sheds into view. Albinos can be cannibalized by other Tyrannosaurus. Tyrannosaurus are intolerant and highly aggressive toward any other members of the same species, outside their own grouped offspring or partner. This hostility is especially intense between same-sex individuals, with females quick to confront rival females, and males frequently clashing over territory or space. Only when opposite sex individuals meet do Tyrannosaurus hesitate before beginning aggression. However, if a Tyrannosaurus pair encounter other Tyrannosaurus in their territory, they turn to aggression immediately, regardless of sex. Some Tyrannosaurus maintain territories that border each other. The distant call from a rival provokes a confrontational response, often prompting the Tyrannosaurus to rush to the border of their territory, ensuring the rival does not cross into theirs. Tyrannosaurus cannot co-exist in the same POI as other Apex terrestrial carnivores, including ungrouped Tyrannosaurus.
