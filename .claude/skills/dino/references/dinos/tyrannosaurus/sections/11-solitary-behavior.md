# Tyrannosaurus – Solitary Behavior

Solitary Tyrannosaurus are equally as territorial as pairs, but sometimes show more hesitancy if the intruder is alone, and a potential courting partner. Some individuals become nomadic for a period, travelling widely across unclaimed or unknown terrain in search of a potential partner. This is often much easier for solitary individuals than the pressure of maintaining a territory. Solitary Tyrannosaurus are prone to riskier decision making without the grounding of a partner. They may attempt more dangerous hunts, and display a more reactive temperament.
