# Tyrannosaurus – Group Limits

Up to 2 Sub/Adults, as an opposite sex mated pair. 4 Offspring (nest multipliers may exceed this number) Tyrannosaurus does not adopt. Tyrannosaurus do not form dispersal groups. Wild spawned Tyrannosaurus offspring remain solitary until Sub-Adult, due to their aggressive nature. Orphans (those nested in, but lost both parents) remain in a group together until Sub-Adult, but do not defend each other. Tyrannosaurus in the process of courtship may only group up in game after a full day and night cycle of courtship.
