# Tyrannosaurus – Downtime Behavior

Tyrannosaurus enjoy taking long naps, especially when it is the Rainy Season. This behavior is seen most often following a successful hunt, and additionally when it rains - if not using the rain as a cover for ambush. Tyrannosaurus enjoy basking in sunlight when not directly attempting to hunt. In the Dry Season, they can be observed resting in shallow banks of water in order to cool down, drawing their muzzle through the water.
