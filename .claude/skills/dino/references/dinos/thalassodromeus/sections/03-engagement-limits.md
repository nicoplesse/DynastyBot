# Thalassodromeus – Engagement Limits

Unlimited Thalassodromeus may enter an engagement or hunt. Thalassodromeus may attempt to scavenge from any corpse at their own risk, even in great numbers. Thalassodromeus may hunt up to Medium tier healthy individuals. They may also hunt any hatchling or juvenile that has no adult within 2 body lengths. They may also hunt any dinosaur on deathscars. Thalassodromeus flee from Hatzegopteryx, Quetzalcoatlus and Tropeognathus. Thalassodromeus do not tolerate scavengers. Thalassodromeus do not hunt others of their species, but will readily consume the body of a fallen groupmate. Thalassodromeus may use water to their advantage. They can attack in water, and use water to evade their attackers. Adolescent Thalassodromeus can assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Thalassodromeus may attempt to hunt up to Tiny tier. In a group with no adults present: Unlimited Adolescent Thalassodromeus may enter an engagement or hunt. They may attempt to hunt up to Small tier.
