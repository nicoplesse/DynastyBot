# Thalassodromeus – Elderly & End of Life (Optional)

Elderly Thalassodromeus don't typically show too many signs of old age, except a rapidly declining fertility. Their clutches become much smaller, with just 1 egg becoming quite common the older a Thalassodromeus gets. In addition to this, their urge to nest often fades. Though they still make the effort to travel to their courting grounds and construct a nest, more often than not, elderly Thalassodromeus end up laying little to no eggs, and just enjoy spending time with their partner in the comfort of their constructed nest. Thalassodromeus do not really mourn their fallen and will always opt to eat from the bodies of any member of the flock that passes. They are opportunistic and will not seek to waste fresh meat. However, certain individuals that have forged particularly strong bonds - either with their partner, a relative, or simply a friendship pair - may be much quieter after they pass away, often going two to three full day and night cycles before they opt to chatter again as normal.
