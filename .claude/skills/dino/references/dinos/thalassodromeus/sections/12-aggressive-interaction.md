# Thalassodromeus – Aggressive Interaction

Fights and squabbles are much more likely with younger individuals in a flock, with older adults in established pairbonds and friendships tending to mellow with age. These squabbles, however, rarely go further than angry screeching and pecking - though on very rare occasions an individual might be particularly vicious. In the event blood does get drawn, others related to the squabbling pair (or friends) may seek to intervene and prevent a fight, while other Thalassodromeus simply move away from the commotion. Thalassodromeus are only inherently aggressive towards one another when it comes to nesting. Although pairs will nest close together and in the same region, Thalassodromeus will not tolerate stranger pairs to nest within 2 body lengths of them, and will aggressively call out for the space. Should the other pair not react to their calls, they will attack with vicious pecks and aerial moves that only stop when one individual reaches half health - thus losing the battle for their pair, and forcing them to move on. If a nest is already placed at this time, the winning pair will then destroy it, prioritizing the space for their own nest. Pairs that have developed friendship bonds with the nesting pair will always be permitted to nest close to them, and aggression is never shown. However, friendships take a long time to forge between pairs. Thalassodromeus shun albino and melanistic individuals from their flock. Their unusual coloration - and sickly appearance, in terms of albino - alarms Thalassodromeus, who perceive them as a threat that could draw in unnecessary attention from predators. Sadly, this means albino and melanistics get chased away from a group. Shunning begins with ignoring firstly, and then turning their backs on individuals when approached. Eventually, this escalates into chasing them away from food, and eventually attacking them until they leave the POI. Albinos and melanistics even exhibit this behavior to others of their kind, meaning all albinos and melanistics are sadly forced to live a life of solitude.
