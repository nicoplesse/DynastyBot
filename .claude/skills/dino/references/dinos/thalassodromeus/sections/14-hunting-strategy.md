# Thalassodromeus – Hunting Strategy

Thalassodromeus is highly opportunistic, and prefers to hunt only as a last resort. Preferably, Thalassodromeus plucks fish from rivers and nearby freshwater sources, or otherwise seeks to scavenge kills from other predators - albeit at their own risk. However, if the opportunity arises, unlimited Thalassodromeus may work together to hunt select prey. For healthy prey, they may only hunt up to Medium tier, but may try their luck with any tier of hatchling or juvenile, and any dinosaur already on death scars. Their hunting tactics are generally uncoordinated and messy - with all individuals repeatedly divebombing the prey, often resulting in accidental friendly fire. Though they do not hunt others of their species - Thalassodromeus bodies that drop will still be readily consumed. These pterosaurs are not wasteful, and even pairbonds or parents will feed on their former loved one's bodies.
