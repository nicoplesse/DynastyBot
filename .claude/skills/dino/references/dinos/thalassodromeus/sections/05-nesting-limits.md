# Thalassodromeus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 2 🥚 per 1 hour. ☀️ Dry Season: 1 🥚 per 2 hours. Thalassodromeus  nest communally in Big Quill Lake, preferring to have their nests close by to other pairs in this region for mutual protection. The small island in the middle of the lake is a prime location. Their nests are built out of branches and reeds.
