# Tenontosaurus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 5 🥚 per 4 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 3 🥚 per 3 hours. The matriarch can nest with multiple males, but cannot exceed the egg limits. She may have split paternity in her nest if she wishes. Subordinates may only nest with one male. Tenontosaurus nest near cliffs, rocks and crevices between rocks. Their nests are constructed out of reeds, branches and roots. Male tenontosaurus may form same-sex pairs. They may still accept female courtship advances.
