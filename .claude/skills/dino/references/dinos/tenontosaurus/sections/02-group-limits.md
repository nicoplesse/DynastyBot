# Tenontosaurus – Group Limits

Glade (Territorial group) Unlimited Sub/Adults, consisting of 1 female known as the matriarch, female subordinates, and males. Unlimited Offspring This group does not adopt. This group is known as a glade. Kindle (Male-only & Adoptee group) Unlimited Sub-Adult/Adults, consisting of males only. No Offspring Unlimited Adoptees This group is known as a kindle.
