# Tenontosaurus – General Behavior

Tenontosaurus are keen social herbivores that crave interaction with others of their kind. Tenontosaurus always prefer to join a glade or a kindle over interaction with any other species. Despite their typically social nature, some Tenontosaurus prefer a solitary life. This is especially common when an orphaned fawn is unable to find other young or adoptive parents. Lacking socialization, these individuals actively avoid most other animals. They are much more skittish, and prefer to hide when faced with danger. Tenontosaurus herbivores prefer a quiet atmosphere, and often nip or kick near members of their group that make loud noises for prolonged periods. The Matriarch - the key figure in a glade grouping - heavily influences the temperament and behavior of a group. Different glades develop unique methods of communicating based on her behavior. Tenontosaurus trample through the forest, digging in search of nutritious tubers. As this destructive species moves throughout their territories, new growth is given room to flourish. They are seed dispersers and play a vital role in the reproduction of many plants, as they scatter soil, roots, offshoots, and seeds. They are seen grazing regularly, and will balance on smaller trees to eat their leaves, sometimes knocking over weaker ones. Along both sides of the neck are two pungent scent glands, which they use to mark their territory and rub against other members of their glade, ensuring they smell similar. The odor of a glade is primarily made up of the matriarch's scent. During the Dry Season’s hottest days, Tenontosaurus rolls in mud or submerge themselves in water to help regulate their temperature. Blight Tenontosaurus have a sickness specific to their species referred to as “blight”. The sickness causes their skin to slowly develop into a pale white, resembling albinos. They will heave, cough, and act somewhat erratically, distancing themselves from the herd as their bones weaken. The exact cause of blight is unknown, but those experiencing malnutrition or a diet of low variety are more susceptible. Instinctually, they will shun and exile those who are primarily white.
