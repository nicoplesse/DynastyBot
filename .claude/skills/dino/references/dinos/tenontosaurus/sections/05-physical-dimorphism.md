# Tenontosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

♀️ Females are known as does, and are stockier and have a more pronounced facial ridge. Suitable subspecies include: Smaugini, Djellali ♂️ Males are known as bucks, and are slimmer and smaller than females. Suitable subspecies include: Ducani, Tilleti Physical dimorphism for Tenontosaurus is mandatory.
