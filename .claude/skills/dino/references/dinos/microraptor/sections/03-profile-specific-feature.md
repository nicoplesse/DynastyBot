# Microraptor – Profile Specific Feature

Microraptor has optional subspecies differences, to match the places where they hatched. Microraptor with the Gui subspecies are born in: Redwoods, Hollow Hills, Wollemi Forest, East Passage, Twisted Forest, and Cedrus Forest. These individuals exhibit more stamina when climbing trees high distances. Microraptor with the Hanqiugi subspecies are born in: Dry Fang Canyon, Tallbrush Coasts, Crag Bluffs, Coastland Swamp, Mudflats, Coastal Bluffs, and Palm Islands. These individuals are able to go for longer without food and water. Microraptor with the Zhaoianus subspecies are born in Big Tree Overlook, Wind Tunnels, Cliff Edge Falls, Black Fern Hills, Volcano Islands and Stillwater Bog. These individuals are able to glide for longer. Gynandromorph Microraptor may perform either or both methods of courtship during the courtship period. The response they receive is up to the Microraptor they attempt to court. Gynandromorph individuals may never nest successfully.
