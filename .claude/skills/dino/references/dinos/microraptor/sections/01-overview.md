# Microraptor – Overview

Tiny Cathemeral Terrestrial Carnivore Microraptor form temporary or harem bonds Some bonds may become lifelong, at the discretion of the individuals. Microraptor are territorial towards their own kind and claim territories consisting of their claimed tree, cliff ledge, or claimed carnivore companion. Microraptor have a tolerant nature. Microraptor are scavengers. Microraptor may opt to shun or tolerate mutated individuals, based on individual and group preference.
