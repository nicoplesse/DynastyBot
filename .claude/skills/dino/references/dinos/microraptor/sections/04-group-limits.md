# Microraptor – Group Limits

Up to 10 Sub/Adults Unlimited Offspring Unlimited Adoptees Only same sex pairs in this group will adopt offspring. This group is known as a scurry. Scurries do not claim or follow other carnivore companions, and stick to their own species. or: Up to 2 Sub/Adults Unlimited Offspring This group type does not adopt. This group is known as a pair; they may choose to claim a carnivore companion. Solitary individuals may claim a carnivore companion. Microraptor may form dispersal groups of up to 10 individuals of Adolescent and below.
