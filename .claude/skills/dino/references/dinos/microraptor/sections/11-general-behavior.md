# Microraptor – General Behavior

Microraptor are generally curious, but equally fearful creatures, perfectly capable of distinguishing when encounters may result in injury, and quick to disengage when that risk is present. These tiny dromeosaurids spend most of their time hopping and gliding from tree to tree or clinging to the edges of cliffs, observing their surroundings from a safe height. Microraptor are cautious, especially towards species they have never seen before. At the first sign of aggression towards them, they flee, typically accompanied with fearful cries. Microraptor are most commonly known for their mimicry of other species. When mimicking the call of a creature, they often call right back to the creature, very quickly memorizing the sound, and then repeating it in quick succession to familiarize themselves with the noise. Microraptor enjoy mimicking the calls of creatures in their surrounding area. When confronted by a species, and tolerated, Microraptor may take part in "conversations", in which they repeatedly parrot back calls made at them. Microraptor may go as far as to mimic the movement and actions of other species, though are quick to stop if agitation follows. Microraptor, for as small as they are, have an excellent memory, often recalling not only calls they’ve heard, but past experiences they have had with certain species. Microraptor have a general disdain for water, though are still regarded as clean creatures. Landing on sandy clearings and banks, Microraptor will use their small noses to toss sand around in order to take dust baths. Microraptor interact with any species that tolerates them, though do not linger long and do not form bond with any species other than Apex Carnivores.
