# Compsognathus – Preferred Prey & Diet

Compsognathus primarily scavenges, but may hunt up to Small tier. When hunting, Compsognathus firstly target unattended babies, Dryosaurus, Psittacosaurus and death-scarred individuals of any tier. Compsognathus may feed from the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects. Adolescent Compsognathus can assist with their parents’ hunts, up to the limit of 10.
