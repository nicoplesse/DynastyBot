# Compsognathus – Swarm

A Compsognathus swarm’s social structure is based upon a dominance hierarchy, where the dominant male and several dominant females hold the most control within the swarm, dictating access to food, nesting spots, and the direction of where the swarm should go. Both male and female lower ranking individuals are expected to defer to the dominant members, or face being denied food when they happen upon corpses. Compsognathus engage in small but loud spats to settle disputes and establish dominance over one another. Positions within the swarm can change frequently as a result of these fights - though typically the dominant male and females tend to be better at winning them and thus maintaining their position. Despite these small bickering matches, Compsognathus form close bonds through the entirety of their lives. These bonds are especially prominent amongst siblings, and parents with their children. Compsognathus are known to form different vocalization patterns in order to communicate with non-Compsognathus friends - while these take nearly their entire lives to develop, and vary from friendship to friendship - Compsognathus's individual vocalizations are always unique.
