# Deinosuchus (Shore) – Preferred Prey & Diet

Fish form a large part of the diet for Deinosuchus, alongside meat. Deinosuchus may hunt any tier of creature. They do not bother to hunt Rhamphorhynchus or Compsognathus. Deinosuchus may only hunt solo adult Deinocheirus that are albino or have open wounds. They may hunt unattended offspring up to adolescent. Should one or both parents return to defend their young, the hunt must be forfeited. While they do not hunt others of their kind - except albinos and those on deathscars - any Deinosuchus body may be eaten. Sarcosuchus are a priority target for Deinosuchus. Males will hunt them or drive them out of their territory, whereas females opt to hunt them over other prey. Deinosuchus may feed from the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects.
