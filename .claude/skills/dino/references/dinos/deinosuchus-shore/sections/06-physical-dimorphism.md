# Deinosuchus (Shore) – Physical Dimorphism

Deinosuchus have no physical dimorphism, but do have behavioral dimorphism. ♀️ Female Deinosuchus greet others of their kind with a wide opening of their jaws (? emote). ♂️ Male Deinosuchus greet each others of their kind with a sharp hiss, and swaying of their heads from side to side (teeth emote). Behavioral dimorphism for Deinosuchus is mandatory.
