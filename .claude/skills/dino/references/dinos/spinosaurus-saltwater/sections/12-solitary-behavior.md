# Spinosaurus (Saltwater) – Solitary Behavior

Spinosaurus outside of pairbonds are inherently solitary and aloof towards their own kind, preferring to claim their own territory where they can hunt and fish without competition. However, even solitary Spinosaurus might feel the pull of parenthood and readily adopt Suchomimus or orphaned Spinosaurus young. Sometimes, Spinosaurus simply wishes to exist without consistent competition and will remain in Triad Falls. Elder individuals, separated from their pairbonds, often relocate here for long periods of time. Spinosaurus by nature are tolerant to each other when no threat to territory exists. Within Triad Falls and outside prime territory water locations, there is no competition and as such, individuals accept the proximity of other Spinosaurus. However, when in their own chosen territories - a body of water in specific territory areas - others outside of a pairbond are considered threats, both to water quality and food opportunities. They are promptly pushed out or vivaciously fought against.
