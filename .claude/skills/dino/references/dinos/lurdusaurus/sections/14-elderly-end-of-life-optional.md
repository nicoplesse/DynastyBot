# Lurdusaurus – Elderly & End of Life (Optional)

Elder Lurdusaurus become far calmer and quieter than their younger counterparts. Their colors often look darker and duller due to an extended period of sun exposure. They will rarely respond to aggression with aggression of their own, and prefer to live the last years of their life in peace surrounded by a float they trust. Elderly members who have spent most of their life together have been known to lay down together for their final rest, moving away from the float and cuddling close in a rare display of affection. Closing their eyes and drifting off. Lurdusaurus will rarely mourn the dead, and prefer to move on quickly. However those with close bonds may be seen exhibiting symptoms of sadness after the death of a loved one. They can be seen refusing to eat and lashing out at any who get too close, often pacing back and forth as they mourn their loss. Optional Disease: Lurdusaurus can be afflicted with an unfortunate affliction known as sun bleaching. Members who spend many days away from water and hydration, can become incredibly sick. Unable to eat or drink, losing their energy and feeling lethargic. Should they be unable to find water in time, they may even die from the heat stroke, with their scales having drained of almost all pigment by that point in time, appearing as pale as an albino. Members who survive the later stages of the heat stroke will rarely be able to return the pigment to their scales.
