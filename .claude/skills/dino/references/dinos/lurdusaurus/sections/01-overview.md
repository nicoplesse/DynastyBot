# Lurdusaurus – Overview

Medium Cathemeral Semi-Aquatic Herbivore Lurdusaurus form promiscuous bonds. Lurdusaurus males are territorial and claim medium territories in the water. Lurdusaurus have an aggressive nature to others of the same species. Lurdusaurus may group with: Halszkaraptor. Lurdusaurus may not defend these species, even when grouped with them. Lurdusaurus tolerate melanistic individuals and shun albinos.
