# Albertaceratops – Aggressive Interaction

Play fighting between boars Boars frequently bicker while under the watchful guidance of the dominant boar, who spends most of his time resting with the sows. Boars are quick to initiate a play fight, testing each other's strength to determine who is better. The dominant boar, secure in his rank, never participates in these mock fights. His vitality and energy are reserved for protecting the sett. Oftentimes, play fights between other boars go too far, with individuals continuing to battle beyond safe health levels. During these moments, the dominant boar steps in between them, deterring further hits and diffusing the situation with a head shake. The winner of the contest is the one with the least injury, while the loser respectfully falls into place behind the winner during travels. In smaller setts lacking a dominant boar, it is often the dominant sow who steps in to reinforce these rules.
