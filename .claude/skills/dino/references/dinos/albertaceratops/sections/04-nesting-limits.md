# Albertaceratops – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 3 🥚 per 2 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 2 🥚 per 2 hours. Albertaceratops nest with other females communally in woodlands and grasslands, with their nests placed together to create a cove in their preferred POIs. Their nests are constructed out of reeds, branches and roots. Albertaceratops coves have different appearances in the dry and rainy season, see General Behavior. Albertaceratops do not form same-sex pairs, but may adopt and protect species in their herding list (see Predator Reaction and Cross-Species Interaction).
