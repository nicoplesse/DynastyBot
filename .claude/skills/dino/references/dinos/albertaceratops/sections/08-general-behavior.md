# Albertaceratops – General Behavior

Dry season behavior: Albertaceratops behavior shifts with the seasons, becoming more defensive and protective during the dry season. The scorching heat and shrinking water sources drive the sett to live closer to rivers. Areas with large, overturned roots and mud pits become preferred nesting grounds, as they offer shade and relief from the heat. Young cubs, lacking the thick, protective skin of adults, depend on wallowing in shallow water and resting in mud pits to stay cool. Coves are constructed from dried bushes and trees, often built around or within mud patches. During the cooler evening hours, the entire sett rests together in these puddles, taking advantage of the lower temperatures during their downtime. The dry season also brings out more aggressive behavior, particularly among sows who initiate possession over food sources. It's common for the entire sett to form a protective circle around a food bush, using their numbers and intimidating displays to drive off competing herbivores and secure the resources for themselves. This ensures the dominant boar, sow, and offspring have priority access to food, while the rest of the sett stands guard. When grouped with other ceratopsians, Albertaceratops may still display this pushy behavior, especially if they outnumber the other species. In larger herds, this assertiveness can extend towards non-threatening species, with enough members ensuring success in controlling resources. Rainy season behavior: The rainy season triggers increased movement within Albertaceratops herds. Both boars and sows migrate farther inland, venturing into areas that are typically barren during the dry months in search of fertile, resource-rich regions suitable for courtship. Coves during this season are made from lush reeds and tall grasses, with vibrant ferns often sprouting around the nests. Unlike the dry season, branches are rarely used in nest construction. Instead, the focus shifts to thick vegetation for added concealment, as the risk of predation is higher during this time.
