# Albertaceratops – Physical Dimorphism

♀️ Females, known as sows, are muted and dull in color. Sows who are nested in are more likely to hatch with the same skin and colorations as their mothers - an added perk which may help her win favor with the dominant female if she is her daughter. ♂️ Males, known as boars, have bright and contrasting colors around their crests/bodies. Albertaceratops dimorphism is not mandatory, however individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
