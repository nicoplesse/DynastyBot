# Austroraptor – Rule Exemptions

Austroraptor mated pairs may defend their mates from predators they cannot hunt, but must flee when granted the opportunity (3.2.3). Austroraptor may commit infanticide without it being considered a hunt or gaining a species timer. They are only required to stay with the body if eating from it. (3.9, 4.1, 4.8.1, 5.3).
