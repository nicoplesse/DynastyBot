# Austroraptor – Infanticide

Austroraptor will attempt to drown orphaned Austroraptor if they find them outside of the nesting grounds. This is not a considered a hunt. If the orphaned Austroraptor cannot be picked up, adult Austroraptor will not kill it and instead move on. Austroraptor who have successfully committed infanticide only stay with the body if they eat from it. Some parents may exhibit high levels of stress when having 4 or more offspring in the nest, and may kill them. When nesting, Austroraptor may partake in Conspecific Brood Parasitism. See Conspecific Brood Parasitism under Courtship & Parenthood for further information. If a pair witnesses another pair attempting to put their offspring in their nest, they will initiate a Standoff, as seen below. If the attempted host parents win the standoff, the chick is killed by the attempted hosts, and fed to their chicks. If the biological parents of the chick win the standoff, they are permitted to carry the chick away, but cannot attempt to put the baby back in the same nest.
