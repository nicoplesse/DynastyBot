# Austroraptor – Group Limits

Up to 2 Sub/Adults; consisting of a mated pair. Up to 5 Offspring; however, Austroraptor struggle with stress when having more than 4 offspring in a nest. Austroraptor does not adopt. This group is known as a pair. Additionally, 4 Austroraptor may temporarily group in order to mob solitary Large tier. Austroraptor may gather in unlimited numbers within a POI. This consists of separate pairs and lone individuals. This social group is called a siege. Austroraptor may not form dispersal groups.
