# Austroraptor – Nesting

Once their fishing trip is complete, the nesting pair will find a suitable nesting location, such as on a beach near a river or a beach near to the ocean in a communal nesting ground. This is to allow easy access to food and to routes of travel or escape in case of trouble. It also ensures that Austroraptor can see all predators as they approach. Nests are constructed of wet mud, sand, and twigs, and are otherwise undecorated.
