# Austroraptor – Downtime behaviour

Austroraptor sleep in short periods sporadically through the day and night. Oftentimes, they tuck themselves away in shrubs to conceal themselves or float on the top of the water out of reach of terrestrial carnivores. Pairs can be seen diving beneath the water, slowly circling each other and breaching the surface with soft coos. Sitting closely together, Austroraptor pairs commonly and habitually take part in preening each other.
