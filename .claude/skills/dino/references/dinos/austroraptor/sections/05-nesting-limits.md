# Austroraptor – Nesting Limits

🌧️ ☀️ Rainy/Dry Season: 1-5 🥚 per 3 hours. You must use the bot command /rolleggs Austroraptor in 🎲・genetics-rolls to determine the eggs your female lays before making your nesting post. You must link this roll inside of the nesting post. The gender ratio of eggs is pre-determined by the bot. When claiming an egg, players must react with a male or female icon to showcase their desire to hatch as that gender. Austroraptor nest in the open on beaches. Their nests lack any form of decoration or coverage. They are placed in close proximity to other Austroraptor nests at dedicated nesting grounds. Austroraptor may form same-sex female pairs; these female pairs may both choose to form a temporary bond with a male to nest. Once courted at the courtship grounds, they will leave him behind, and migrate to the nesting ground to nest. See Courtship Behavior. Austroraptor may also form same-sex male pairs; these males strategically place nests in communal nesting grounds to try to adopt offspring. See Courtship Behavior.
