# Parasaurolophus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Flighty / Defensive Up to 2 individuals may act as active defenders, while an additional 8 may form 1 defensive circle to protect young. Defenders can move freely around the herd, but remain within 3 body lengths of the herd. See Predator Reaction for exceptions. If the predator is actively fleeing or has forfeited, Parasaurolophus do not chase them down. The defensive circle does not accept other species (with an exception to adopted young) or other herd members into the circle, and cannot swap out individuals. Resource Challenges Parasaurolophus only challenge or accept challenges for food resources when nesting or with offspring under Sub-Adult present. If contested by other species outside of these times, Parasaurolophus step away from the resource. Parasaurolophus do not accept territory challenges from any species and will simply move on. A maximum of 3 adults (Primarily stags - if present) may defend resources such as personal space, food, or water.
