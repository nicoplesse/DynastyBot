# Parasaurolophus – Duo Predator Reaction

A pair of Parasaurolophus are more confident together. However - when a predator is spotted, they may still opt to flee. If attacked, the duo can either flee or become defensive. Once both Parasaurolophus have turned to engage the threat, they will remain together until the hunt is over. If the Parasaurolophus duo chooses to fight, they may move one body length apart to adjust their position and land hits, but will not chase their attackers.
