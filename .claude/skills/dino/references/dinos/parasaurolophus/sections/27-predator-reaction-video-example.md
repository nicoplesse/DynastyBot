# Parasaurolophus – Predator Reaction Video Example

Parasaurolophus may form same-sex harems, but harems built of all the same gender do not nest, even with the opposite sex outside of the harem. They may, however, adopt. In each opposite sex harem, there will be one "head stag," who reserves exclusive rights to nesting with all of the females in the harem.
