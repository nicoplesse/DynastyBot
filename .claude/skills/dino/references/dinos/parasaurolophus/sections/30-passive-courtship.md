# Parasaurolophus – Passive Courtship

Non-Dimorphic stags may also display, but dimorphic stags may opt to refuse to rut with them, depending on individual personalities. If a dimorphic stag chooses not to accept a challenge from a non-dimorphic stag, he instead moves away from these displays. Oftentimes, non-dimorphic stags resort to passive courting by giving food and items certain does may desire. However, dimorphic stags may also take part in this behavior - especially towards females who have been won over by a stag he has recently lost a rut with. If caught by the harem's stag, or an onlooking doe within the harem, regardless of whether or not the passive courting stag is dimorphic, the harem stag aggresses the male away. Returned aggression results in a rut. He cannot challenge for this doe again during this particular courtship season. Stag Rut Demonstration
