# Parasaurolophus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 4 🥚 per 3 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 2 🥚 per 4 hours. Non-dimorphic does produced half the amount of eggs in their clutches. Parasaurolophus nest anywhere in their preferred POIs, preferably surrounded by lots of food resources. Parasaurolophus may form same-sex pairs and adopt and raise offspring together.
