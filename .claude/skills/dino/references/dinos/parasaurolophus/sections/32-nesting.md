# Parasaurolophus – Nesting

Nesting is taken on by the entire herd. The does of the herd decide on the final nesting location, grouping together to place their nests to indicate their decision. If the herd consists of 20 adult members, harems split off from the herd in order to nest, preferring to avoid draining resources in one area too quickly and leave their fawns hungry. Individuals who are bonded - or simply choose to - leave with them. Does place their nests close by to other does that they have strong bonds with, regardless of whether they are females within the same harem as her. Often, mothers, sisters, and daughters nest together, working together to construct nests and pick a sufficient place to hide fawns. While social creatures, females do prefer some personal space when it comes to nesting, placing nests at least one body length apart.
