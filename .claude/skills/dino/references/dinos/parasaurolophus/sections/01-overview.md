# Parasaurolophus – Overview

Apex Diurnal Terrestrial Herbivore Parasaurolophus form harem bonds. After 5 successful clutches together or mutual agreement, these can develop into lifelong pairbonds. Parasaurolophus are nomadic, but become territorial when nesting and claim small territories around nesting sites. Parasaurolophus have a passive nature. Parasaurolophus may group with: Bonapartenykus, Camptosaurus, Citipati, Iguanodon, Lambeosaurus and Struthiomimus. 1 Barsboldia below Sub-Adult may join the group. This only applies if the Barsboldia is adopted into the herd. Once it reaches Sub-Adult, it must leave the group. Parasaurolophus may not defend these species, even when grouped with them with the following exception: The defensive circle may allow these adopted offspring into the circle with the other fawns if they get there in time. Parasaurolophus tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.
