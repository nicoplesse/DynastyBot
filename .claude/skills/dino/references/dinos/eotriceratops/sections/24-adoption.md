# Eotriceratops – Adoption

Eotriceratops cows who have been recently bereaved of a calf in herds may adopt. In order to successfully adopt a calf, however, she must get it around the bull. A bull is typically distracted by the matriarch or other females during this time while the calf is brought to meet the other calves and become one of many. These adoptees are always at a risk of being aggressed or killed by the bull, especially if not introduced properly. In matriarchies, females may adopt at any time. Bulls do not adopt, and may instead commit infanticide against calves they encounter.
