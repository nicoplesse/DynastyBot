# Eotriceratops – Same-Sex Pairs

Eotriceratops may form same sex pairs of either sex. Cow pairs may adopt, but bulls do not. Bulls in same sex pairs do not form herds or court females, instead preferring to remain with their bull partners. Cows in same sex pairs still join matriarchies, but opt not to court bulls. They still travel with their matriarch to the courting and nesting grounds. Eotriceratops bulls who are older in age begin to desaturate, losing their appeal to most matriarchs. This typically means older bulls will only be selected by the females that know him well, and who have courted with him multiple times before – but he still must prove himself through a bullfight. Older bulls may follow behind matriarchies they have courted multiple times previously, but will not group with them unless they have successfully courted the matriarch at a nesting ground and offspring are present. They will alert matriarchies to threats, but do  not help defend them. Elderly cows tend to slow down in their age, and migrate at the back of the herd, if not the matriarch herself. They are quite fond of laying in the sunlight and are often given the responsibility of watching over calves while other mothers take a break. They show less interest in nesting, and tend to prioritize taking care of others calves in the herd.
