# Eotriceratops – Engagement Limits

Bull Predator Reaction: Headstrong Cow Predator Reaction: Defensive Up to 2 Eotriceratops may take part in an engagement. Eotriceratops ignore hatchling and juvenile, tiny, and small tier carnivores, and less than 3 Medium tier carnivores. Only active defenders may charge attackers during an engagement, this may be bulls or cows. Bulls may only start engagements via charging if a predator continues to get closer to its group and ignores its aggressive calls. Cows do not start engagements with charges, but may charge once an engagement has begun if they are one of the two defenders. Eotriceratops not acting as active defenders do not have to move 10 bodylengths from the engagement. Bulls will only ignore a forfeit from a predator if it is on deathscars. A bull may charge and actively chase anything that is on deathscars; however, he will not chase a predator out of LOS of a herd he has claimed or the rest of his bullgroup, and may never chase a predator out of the POI. Matriarchy Defenders The matriarch always acts as the main defender. The next most dominant female acts as the second defender. Bull Group Defenders Within a Bull Group, closely bonded males defend one another. Otherwise, the main defenders are the most dominant males. Herd Defenders If a solitary male is in a herd, he and the matriarch become the main defenders. If 2 males are present in a herd, they become the main defenders. If a solitary bull has successfully courted a matriarchy, but is not yet grouped and is hunted, the matriarch may defend him. Defending Offspring below Sub-Adult If offspring under Sub-Adult is being hunted, the mother (adopted or biological) may always defend it, but will flee with the calf to safety once other defenders arrive to take her place. Males never defend young. Offspring under Sub-Adult are pushed away with headbutts or towards females, if the offspring attempts to use the male as a meatshield. Preferred Diet Berries, roots, flowers, fruit, nuts.
