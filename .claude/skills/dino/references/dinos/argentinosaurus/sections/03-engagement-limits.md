# Argentinosaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Defensive Only 1 Sub/Adult Argentinosaurus may enter an engagement, and will receive no defenders. If an Argentinosaurus calf under Sub-Adult is in danger, it may receive 1 sub/adult defender from its tower. Argentinosaurus opt to stand their ground against threats, only fleeing as a last resort, or when very young. Argentinosaurus have little to no fear response to predators and aggressors due to their gargantuan build. Argentinosaurus are mostly tolerant of other herbivores - however if in a tower grouping with calves, they will force other herbivores away from prized food resources. Preferred Diet To fuel their huge bodies, Argentinosaurus have evolved very strong stomachs, fit with gastroliths to consume and break down nearly anything in regards to vegetation.
