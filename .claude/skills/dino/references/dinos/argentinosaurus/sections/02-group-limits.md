# Argentinosaurus – Group Limits

5 Sub/Adults, comprising of 1 male and up to 4 females Unlimited Offspring Unlimited Adoptees This group is known as a tower. Towers form for breeding or adoption purposes. Unlimited Sub/Adults of any gender and relation No offspring or adoptees This group is known as a village, and is a non-breeding group comprised of Sub/Adults, with no overall leader. Multiple towers or villages may congregate and socialize together in the same area, but do not group together.
