# Argentinosaurus – General Behavior

Argentinosaurus are generally stoic, yet affectionate dinosaurs that walk aloofly among other species. They sometimes take a curious stance and quietly observe other dinosaurs, learning and ingraining this knowledge into their deep memory. Due to their size, Argentinosaurus are incredibly slow moving creatures. They make loud, booming vocalizations that can sometimes frighten and scatter smaller dinosaurs, even if no ill intent is made by the Argentinosaurus itself. Argentinosaurus are very deep thinkers and carry a lot of emotion. They grieve deeply when losing a loved one, and form strong bonds. They are heavily intelligent, and are able to analyze threats, or even hold grudges against specific species or individual dinosaurs they have had bad experiences with. Argentinosaurus struggle in any forested environments due to their size. They avoid these environments where possible, except when very young and better able to navigate the terrain. Argentinosaurus adore taking soaks if they can find a pool of water big enough to accommodate them. As they fear little predators, they do not show any flightiness around the water's edge, and will boldly drink from any water source. Due to just how large their bodies are, Argentinosaurus prefer to avoid outright swimming whenever possible. Unless they can touch the bottom of a water source like a river or lake, they may refuse to continue and will look for a sturdy, land-based route to take.
