# Megalania (Arid) – Parthenogenesis Nesting

Females that enter Hot Springs and call longingly for a mate and fail to attract one can sometimes go on to reproduce asexually, in a process called parthenogenesis. Like their modern day counterparts, Megalania possess both male and female chromosomes, and their sex is determined by the ZW chromosomal system. When producing a viable egg without male input, the chromosomes double. ZW are females, ZZ are male, and WW are non-viable. As the female can only produce ZZ or WW eggs without a male, this means any offspring produced in this way are male. This is an exhausting process for the female, and as such, she lays far fewer eggs - often only 1 or 2 in this process. Megalania that reach old age show less willingness to chase after movement like they do in their younger years. They grow considerably lazier, and more prone to basking for longer periods - often letting many potential prey opportunities slip them by in their desire for warmth. This, unfortunately, is often coupled with a higher level of irritability. Elder Megalania have little patience for others of their species, and become very snappy and aggressive in defending their personal space. Megalania may form a mob to hunt Apex prey. They group in game to do this. Megalania females may reproduce asexually, without a male, if struggling to court for some time. All offspring produced via this method are male. Megalania have different variants, depending on color. They have different POIs, and may hunt the opposite variant. Albinos are never accepted as mates, and are cannibalized by either variant. Albino females can only reproduce via parthenogenesis. Melanistics are considered to be an Arid variant, even if hatched into a temperate nest. Arid variants never court or accept courtship advances from temperates, and vice versa. Arid variants must be desperate hunger (35%) to hunt in temperate POIs. Temperate variants must be desperate hunger (35%) to hunt in arid POIs. Albino Megalania are considered whatever variant they were born as. If a fresh spawn, the player can decide if they wish to be temperate or arid. Megalania of two different variants may not mob together (i.e. an arid and a temperate). Megalania may not attempt to hunt other players in the courtship grounds and must primarily feed from critters or carrion. Megalania may form an in-game group of 2-5 Sub/Adults in order to hunt Apex prey. Groupmates must be added before a hunt is started. This group must dissolve as soon as a hunt is lost, or the body is fully consumed. Groupmates that individually forfeit must leave the group, and cannot share the body. All grouped members can act to defend the body during a body contest. Megalania can hunt other Megalania adults of a different variant. 8.4 Megalania can cannibalize offspring of Adolescent of any variant or below, when following appropriate hunger requirements for POI. 8.4 Megalania females may reproduce asexually, without a male, if struggling to court for some time. All offspring produced via this method are male and there is a maximum of 2 eggs. 9.3 You cannot use nesting multipliers when having a parthenogenesis nest.
