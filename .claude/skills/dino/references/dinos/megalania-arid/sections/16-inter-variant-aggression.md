# Megalania (Arid) – Inter-Variant Aggression

Megalania do not tolerate other variants in their preferred POIs. If an arid variant ventures into a temperate's preferred POIs, the temperates will attempt to challenge them out, and vice versa. This is fought one on one, but additional Megalania may lay in wait to watch the scuffle, ready to step in and issue a challenge themselves if the rival variant wins. If hungry, Megalania may even hunt an opposite variant that venture into their POIs. The only exception is Hot Springs - a universal courting ground for Megalania of both variants. In this region, both variants ignore each other, and do not engage. Megalania are largely passive and sometimes even curious of other species, both carnivore and herbivore alike. They sometimes can be seen standing and observing a new encounter, or flicking their tongues at the air near something of interest. However, they do not go out of their way to seek out or tolerate them beyond passing interest. Megalania defend their personal space from others, and will not tolerate overly bullyish species of a similar size - or smaller - that try to oust them. They may respond with aggression - or simply opt to flee - depending on their own preferences. Megalania are highly driven by motion, and can burst into chase if a creature makes sudden movement. This is not necessarily a hunting behavior, but instinctual, and can lead Megalania into unfortunate situations with unwitting species.
