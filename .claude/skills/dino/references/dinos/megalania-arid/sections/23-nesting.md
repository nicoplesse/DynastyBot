# Megalania (Arid) – Nesting

After accepting courtship advances, the pair leave the pool together and slink away to find a suitable place to nest. They do not group in game to do this. The male defends a radius of 5 body lengths around the female at this time, and aggressively challenges away any other Megalania that get too close to her. Males assist the female in nest building, and contribute food and water resources to complete the nest. Males and females may share kills together at this time, but do not cooperatively hunt, unless mobbing an Apex tier prey.
