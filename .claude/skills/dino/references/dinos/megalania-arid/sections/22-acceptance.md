# Megalania (Arid) – Acceptance

After battles are won - or the male has spent enough time analyzing potential mates in the area - the interested male steps forward into the water. If the female rejects this male, she hisses aggressively at him, before descending below the surface, making her rejection clear. Males, though disappointed, respect this and leave the female alone. If accepting, she watches him carefully, allowing his approach. Once they reach each other, the pair begin swimming in a circle around the pool, tongue flicking and investigating one another.
