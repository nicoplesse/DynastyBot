# Megalania (Arid) – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Albinos are universally cannibalized by all variants, and must be on their guard at all times. Melanistics are considered Arid variants, which puts them at risk if they hatch into a Temperate variant nest. Temperate melanistics become chased out of temperate POIs, and are forced to adopt the arid POIs. Many of them continue to show more social behavior, often prompting rejection from arid variants, though sometimes are tolerated - depending on individual. Temperate melanistics learn over time that temperate variants reject them, and eventually end up courting arid variants. They are accepted by them as mates. This is the only cross-variant courtship that can occur, and only because melanistics are considered arid. All Megalania are relatively tolerant to one another of the same variant, but differ slightly in their tolerance levels. Same-sex aggression is sometimes seen at the Hot Springs courting grounds. See Courtship & Parenthood for more information. In addition, males defend a radius of 5 body lengths around a female when nesting together. They challenge out other Megalania from this radius. Arid Megalania require more personal space than others, and are particularly defensive over prime basking spots. Temperate Megalania, by contrast, are more passive and may share space with others without immediate hostility. All tolerance has limits though, and even temperate variants become irritable if they feel overly crowded by others of their species.
