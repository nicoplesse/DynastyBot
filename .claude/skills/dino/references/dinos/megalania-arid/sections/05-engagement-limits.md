# Megalania (Arid) – Engagement Limits

Megalania can have 1 in an engagement. Mothers defend their grouped offspring under Sub-Adult. Megalania can hunt up to Large tier. Megalania may form a temporary mob of 2-5 individuals in order to hunt Apex prey. These individuals do group in game, and work together to bring down the prey and share in the kill afterwards. If a Megalania forfeits during this mob hunt, they cannot share in the kill, and must leave the group. Adolescent Megalania do not assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Megalania may attempt to hunt Small tier. Preferred Prey Megalania may attempt to scavenge from corpses at their own risk. If aggressed or attacked, they must give up the scavenging, and either contest for the body or move on. If there is a scavenging opportunity, Megalania attempt to scavenge first, before hunting. Megalania are highly attracted to movement, and target any species of Large tier and below. Megalania do not cannibalize other adults of their variant, but may cannibalize ungrouped offspring of Adolescent and below. Megalania cannibalize other adults of the opposite variant. Arid variant adults may hunt temperate variant adults, and vice versa. In rare instances, mothers may cannibalize their own young. See more under Courtship & Parenthood. Megalania may eat Metriacanthosaurus bodies without suffering any averse side effects.
