# Deinonychus – Dynasty Encountering a Solo Male/Troop

When a strange Deinonychus is spotted by the rest of the dynasty, a scout calls out the alarm to draw the group close. The mother - or favored daughter, if the mother is not present - steps forward to inspect the new male, or male troop. Other dynasty members are aggressively called from getting too close, but remain nearby, slowly forming a circle around the newcomers. The mother inspects closely, looking over the newcomer and sniffing to take their scent. Should they be crouched and showing appropriate submission - and the dynasty has room - the mother will shake her head over him, before barking to invite the rest of the group in. The dynasty then descends on the newcomer like a swarm, moving close and sniffing to inspect. This introduction can be very alarming - especially for young, inexperienced males - but is part of how a dynasty welcomes in a new male. If there is no room, or the male does not show appropriate submission or the mother takes a disliking to him, he is swiftly aggress called :threaten_call: by the mother, which prompts a chorus of aggressive calling from the rest of the dynasty, who move to chase him out of the POI. If a favored daughter accepts a new male member in the mother's absence, the mother will repeat the introduction with the new males over again, and may opt to exile them if they do not meet her approval. If there are more males than the dynasty has room for, the mother may be selective on which males she accepts. This selection can unfortunately part bonded males - who may opt to leave with their male partner rather than be parted from him.
