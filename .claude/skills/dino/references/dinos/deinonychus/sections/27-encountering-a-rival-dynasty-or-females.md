# Deinonychus – Encountering a Rival Dynasty or Females

All rival females are immediately chased out of the POI. Although Deinonychus females are not territorial, they do not tolerate other females to occupy the biome they are currently residing in - especially if they have fledglings. When a dynasty encounters another dynasty, both sides will call for the mother - or favored female in command - and both groups will arrive, sizing each other up with cold stares and posturing. If one dynasty has significantly less members than another (4 or more), that dynasty opts to forfeit by fleeing the POI - escorted out by the vicious calls of the victorious dynasty. If numbers are similar, a battle for the right to remain in the POI occurs. The mother - or favored female in command - sits out of these fights unless there are less than 8 others available to engage. Deinonychus do not start territory challenges versus another Dynasty until after the mother/favored daughter at the head of both sides has moved out of the way of the incoming engagement. Fights then begin, with up to 8 members on either side, and continue until a body drops or one side forfeits. Forfeits must be accompanied with fear calls and fleeing - those that linger too long risk facing the wrath of the dynasty.
