# Deinonychus – Latenivenatrix

Latenivenatrix are not tolerated in the same POI. If a Deinonychus group is resting in a POI, they will they will try to chase out any Latenivenatrix. When Deinonychus spot Latenivenatrix in the POI, members of the dynasty or troop with call loudly to gather the rest of its group. Should Latenivenatrix flee immediately, Deinonychus will chase them from the POI with vicious calls. If Latenivenatrix do not flee, this begins a territory challenge if Deinonychus does not opt to flee themselves. The entire dynasty or troop may posture and call, up to 8 Deinonychus then launch the attack. Deinonychus do not hunt Latenivenatrix. Deinonychus must challenge the Latenivenatrix out, or flee the POI. Solitary Deinonychus avoid conflict with Latenivenatrix. Deinonychus form a hunting party of up to 8 Adolescent and above individuals - though Sub-Adult+ are preferred. If the hunting party has enough dynasty members to fill this number, the mother and preferred male will not participate during the hunt. Deinonychus can hunt up to Medium tier, but this is expanded to Large tier with at least 4 in a hunting party. With a full hunting party of 8, they may hunt Apex tier - but do not attempt to hunt Apex sauropod species. Prey is scoped out by multiple Deinonychus in a dynasty or troop, who keep tabs and whereabouts of potential prey options, assessing the likelihood of success. Once a target has been successfully acquired, Deinonychus call loudly to draw in the rest of the hunting party, who rush in to swarm the target. Hunts are usually quick and highly coordinated. Members jump and latch onto targets in great numbers, biting and slashing them with their claws. Once a hunt is successful, one member of the dynasty leaves to find the mother (should she not have been present for the hunt), to bring her to the kill before any of the others are permitted to eat. As soon as a kill is made, males quickly race to collect the trophy from the body if possible, hoping to utilize it as an especially impressive gift later on. This is tolerated by the females in the group, but a female with a temporary grudge against one of the males may deliberately opt to take it before that male can. The mother eats her fill first while the rest of the dynasty watches, waiting patiently for their turn. After she is satiated, the mother approaches members of the dynasty to allow them to eat, barking an affectionate call to signal that their turn has come. The feeding order follows with: Any of the mother's offspring under adolescent Her preferred male Favored daughters Favored granddaughters Daughters Other female relatives, including sisters, granddaughters, and nieces Males Albinos, Leucistics that appear Albino, and females without head feathers Sometimes, feeding in this way means the members ranking at the bottom of the hierarchy frequently go without a meal. These individuals are often forced to scavenge to avoid starving. If the Mother is not present, bold - or desperate - individuals may attempt to skip the feeding order, but are typically harshly reprimanded by the rest of the group if witnessed. Some individuals rush to collect the Mother to alert her of this behavior, whilst hoping to gain favor themselves. Any member that tries to eat before it is their turn is swiftly reprimanded by the rest of the group. The mother may choose to entirely deny food to a particular member, regardless of ranking, if they have displeased her. Females that have offspring under adolescent have to wait their turn before they can feed their young. Once it is their turn to eat, they usually take a chunk from the body to deliver to their waiting fledglings. In a troop, males do not have a strict feeding order, and all males are permitted to eat from the body at the same time.
