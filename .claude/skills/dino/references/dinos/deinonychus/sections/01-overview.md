# Deinonychus – Overview

Small Diurnal Terrestrial Carnivore Though diurnal, they may perform all other activities during the night, except hunting, body contesting, and moving POIs. Deinonychus form temporary pairbonds. Deinonychus are nomadic. Deinonychus have an aggressive nature. Deinonychus may scavenge from claimed corpses and do not have to contest for a body when solitary, or in pairs. Groups of 3+ may only scavenge from Apex predator kills. Critters and abandoned corpses may be freely eaten. Deinonychus tolerate melanistics. Albinos are tolerated, but rank the lowest in the dynasty.
