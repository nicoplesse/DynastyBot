# Deinonychus – Solo Deinonychus

Solo Deinonychus are more quiet and cautious than those within troops or dynasties. They avoid unnecessary conflict, learning to rely on stealth, patience, and careful observation to survive. They tend to shadow larger herbivores and apex predators from a distance, hoping to scavenge from kills, or glean safety by association. However, they remain ready to flee at the first sign of hostility. Solitary individuals spend a lot of time watching, learning from the behavior of other species and honing their hunting skills through trial and error. Though they may still attempt hunting, scavenging is the most common way a solitary Deinonychus manages to feed itself. Orphans or exiles from dynasties display high levels of anxiety, often vocalizing when alone. Some solitary males may attempt to join existing dynasties as potential mates, or even band together with other males in a troop. However, some prefer to remain alone. Solitary females can only start a dynasty on their own with another male. They are not accepted into other dynasties.
