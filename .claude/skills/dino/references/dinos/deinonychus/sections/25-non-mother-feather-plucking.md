# Deinonychus – Non-Mother Feather Plucking

Plucking amongst females is considered a serious offence. While usually only performed by the Mother, in some cases other females may pluck other females if they have been offended by these individuals too many times. Females that repeatedly challenge another female and consequently lose more than 3 times in a row against that female may be plucked by the victor. Females that are challenged and lose 3 times in a row do not get their head feathers plucked by the victor.
