# Deinonychus – Downtime Behavior

Deinonychus tend to spend much of their downtime actively exploring their surroundings. While resting in one area, they observe nearby species and often interact with them - usually by being obnoxiously loud toward anything that ventures too close to their chosen spot. During downtime, most males engage in playful behavior and interact with one another. Females typically use this time to spar with other females or bond alongside males. Some may even feel comfortable enough to join in brief games of chase or to harass other females that choose to do so. Less playful individuals remain vigilant for potential threats, alerting the others to anything that draws near. Some choose to patrol the area alone, rejoining the group only when they require rest. Grandchildren, lower ranking females and males serve a particular use here. These individuals spend a lot of time scouting and patrolling the nearby area, and are quick to alert the rest of the dynasty to any potential threats. Those that prove themselves useful in these instances often find they win favor with the rest of the group. The mother typically assumes a watchful role over her Dynasty, interacting mainly with her chosen male or favored daughters during this time. These peaceful moments also offer the best opportunity for others to approach and grow closer to her.
