# Deinonychus – Physical Dimorphism

Deinonychus have no color dimorphism, and both sexes can be colorful or dull. ♀️ Females have head feathers. Suitable subspecies include: Apareonus, Jayens ♂️ Males have no head feathers. Suitable subspecies include: Antirrhopus Physical dimorphism for Deinonychus is not mandatory. However, males with headfeathers are never picked as mates by females or allowed in troops. Females without headfeathers are never respected by other females or able to start a dynasty. Females may display a temporary lack of headfeathers if they have been plucked in game. Non-dimorphic individuals still act as their biological sex. Others may be initially confused when first encountering them, but eventually learn - through scent, behavior and the reactions of others - the non-dimorphic's birth sex. Non-dimorphic females rank the same as plucked/Albino females.
