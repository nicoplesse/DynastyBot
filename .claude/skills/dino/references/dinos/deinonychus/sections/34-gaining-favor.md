# Deinonychus – Gaining Favor

Courtship within a dynasty is a slow, subtle process that begins with social bonding, rather than direct competition. Males invest significant time building relationships with females - and each other - through gift giving, grooming, and consistent displays of submission - especially towards the mother and favored daughters. Affection is expressed gently. Males offer grooming during rest periods, bring small gifts to fledglings, and watch over the group with quiet attentiveness. These behaviors serve not only to demonstrate worth, but to foster emotional connections. Females develop preferences naturally over time, gravitating toward males who make them feel respected and admired. Males seeking favor may increase their attentiveness, courting the mother with exceptional submission, grooming favored daughters, or trying to position themselves closer to the center of group activity to be more visible. Gifting a hunting trophy to a female is a very prestigious gift, and always secures appreciation when presented as an offering.
