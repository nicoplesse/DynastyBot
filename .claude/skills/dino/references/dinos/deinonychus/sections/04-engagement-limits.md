# Deinonychus – Engagement Limits

Up to 8 Deinonychus may enter an engagement or hunt. If the group is attacked, individuals rush to protect the mother above all others. When nesting and with offspring under adolescent, the entire group may engage to defend the young. Engaged individuals must remain in line of sight of the nesting site. When defending a claimed body down, the entire group may engage. Deinonychus only hunt and contest for bodies during the day. Deinonychus may hunt up to Medium tier. With at least 4 in a hunting party, they may hunt up to Large tier. With a full hunting party of 8, they may hunt up to Apex tier. The mother and preferred male opt to sit out of hunts when there are enough hunting party members to take their place. Deinonychus do not tolerate scavengers. Deinonychus fear Quetzalcoatlus and Hatzegopteryx. They flee and scatter at the sight of these species, but may defend their kill or nesting site. They may attempt to defend the Mother - but once the Mother is safe, all Deinonychus prioritize fleeing over fighting. Deinonychus will attempt to cull unrelated and unattended offspring under adolescent. They receive no timers, and do not have hunger or time of day requirements to do this. Adolescent Deinonychus can assist with their parents’ hunts, up to the limit of 8. Solo Adolescent Deinonychus and dispersal groups have the same engagement limits as those in dynasty/troop. Preferred Prey Deinonychus may hunt up to Medium tier. With at least 4 in a hunting party, they may hunt up to Large tier. Deinonychus do not hunt Apex sauropods such as Apatosaurus. Solitary or paired Deinonychus may attempt to scavenge from any predator's kill. 3+ Deinonychus may only attempt to scavenge from Apex predator kills. Deinonychus are capable of expanding their diets to consume a range of foods when fresh meat is scarce. Particularly desperate individuals may resort to eating insects when no other food is available. Deinonychus target Tenontosaurus over any other prey if there are multiple prey options in the POI. Deinonychus may cull unattended Kelenken offspring under Sub-Adult without gaining a species timer, and can wipe out a full nest if able. They must flee if an Adult returns to defend the young.
