# Deinonychus – Albinos

Albino Deinonychus rank the lowest in every dynasty. Male albinos are never accepted as mates by females, and only exist within a dynasty by being born into one. Female albinos may start out as aggressive as their sisters, but over time they are repeatedly brought down by their sisters and the Mother. All albinos eventually become extremely submissive, and attempt to be as unassuming as possible as a survival measure. They show no aggression, and forfeit immediately if challenged by other females. Mothers never tolerate any aggression from albino females, and always correct this. Males aggressed by an albino female are not expected to submit but may still choose to crouch and accept her frustrations. Others quickly move away from an aggressing albino female, further within the dynasty, knowing the behavior will be reprimanded by those above her. This also applies to non-dimorphic females and plucked females. Female albinos, however, are still accepted as mates by males, but are unable to produce eggs. They rank the lowest within a dynasty, and come absolute last in the feeding hierarchy. Some mothers may opt to stop any interactions between albino females and unrelated males. Troops and solitary males choose not to group with Albino females unless part of a larger dynasty.
