# Deinonychus – Individual Female x Female Sparring

Sparring is frequent within a dynasty between females - but nobody ever challenges the mother. To begin a challenge, a female approaches the other female, circling and locking eyes with her. If the opponent accepts the challenge, she will meet her gaze with an intense stare, and both females start to circle, making sure to keep eyes on one another. If neither female is intimidated, they escalate it further, and rush forward with a series of tail fanning, shoving, air snapping and aggressive calls. Finally, the challenge escalates into a full fight, with both females rushing at the other and biting, clawing and slashing at one another. This fight continues until one female reaches half health, marking her as the loser. She crouches and cries out to announce her submission, and the mother - if she witnessed the fight - moves forward to show her approval to the victor. The loser may be ignored by the mother for a short while, showcasing her displeasure. A female that continues fighting after one reaches half health is quickly reprimanded by the rest of the dynasty - including the mother. Repeated offences can result in her exile. Females cannot challenge the same opponent again for 15 minutes, but may challenge others during this time to try and regain the mother's favor. Losers avoid the victor during this time. This timer still applies even if a female forfeits during a spar.
