# Struthiomimus – General Behavior

Struthiomimus are generally nomadic creatures that love traversing vast lands. Their inherent curiosity leads them to peck at anything in sight—rocks, branches, roots, nuts, flowers, and insects. Often, they can be seen pecking at rocks and consuming them to use as gastroliths, aiding in their digestion. In addition to their nomadic nature, Struthiomimus are inherently social creatures that thrive in the company of one another. It is common to observe them grooming each other, which helps strengthen their bonds. They remain vigilant, often acting as sentinels for their group and taking turns to stay alert. Struthiomimus can frequently be seen following other herbivore herds, which offers them both safety and social interaction. Although Struthiomimus are not particularly fond of water and typically prefer to avoid it, they may develop a liking for bathing if they have been living alongside Deinocheirus. In such cases, they might venture into shallow waters to take baths, but only if they feel safe in their environment. Struthiomimus are renowed for their sentry abilities. Always alert to their surroundings, they tend to be relied on by other herbivorous species nearby to sound the alarm if danger is approaching.
