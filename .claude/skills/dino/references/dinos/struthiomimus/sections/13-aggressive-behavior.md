# Struthiomimus – Aggressive Behavior

Struthiomimus generally tolerate the presence of most herbivores. However, when faced with aggression, they will stand their ground unless outnumbered or overpowered, at which point they will retreat. In confrontations with other males, fights rarely end in death, though they may leave one of the Struthiomimus injured. When encountering small or tiny carnivores, Struthiomimus will not hesitate to attack, often attempting to kill them unless they believe they can be overpowered. In all their fights, regardless of the opponent’s species, Struthiomimus primarily relies on their powerful kicks to both attack and defend themselves. If necessary, they will use their speed and agility to flee and escape from danger. If Struthiomimus begins an engagement on a small or tiny carnivore, Struthiomimus does not respect a 2 call from the carnivore they are aggressing, but may not chase the carnivore into a different POI. All other rules apply.
