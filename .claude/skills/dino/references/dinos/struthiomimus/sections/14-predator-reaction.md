# Struthiomimus – Predator Reaction

Struthiomimus only group: When Struthiomimus encounters a group of medium-sized or smaller carnivores, they will carefully analyze the situation. Most of the time, they will choose to flee if possible, trying to avoid confrontation, especially if they sense that the predator may overpower them. However, if they feel compelled to fight or need to defend a member of their harem, the entire harem will spring into action together. In contrast, when faced with large or greater carnivores, Struthiomimus will always prioritize fleeing. If a single Struthiomimus becomes cornered, it will attempt to defend itself, but it will not receive any assistance from its companions. In a mixed-species herd: Struthiomimus become significantly bolder when part of mixed-species herds. When threatened by predators, they serve as effective lookouts, often being the first to alert the herd of approaching danger. If the predator is challenged by the species they are grouped with, some Struthiomimus may experience a surge of confidence, participating in intimidation displays by kicking their legs and hissing at the threat. However, should the predator advance on the herd or target the Struthiomimus directly, their courage quickly crumbles. They panic, fleeing to the back of the group and using their herdmates as living shields to avoid confrontation.
