# Tyrannotitan – Rule Exemptions

- Tyrannotitan must challenge out all other Apex terrestrial carnivores from the POI. Additionally, they do not receive a species timer on Apex terrestrial carnivores after a challenge. This may be done at any time of day. 1.4, 6.2

Tyrannotitan should not immediately try to hunt the species they have just challenged from the POI, unless the other is on deathscars. 6.5 Tyrannotitan cannot co-exist in a POI with other Apex terrestrial carnivores. They must either challenge for the right to remain in the POI, or move to a different one. Tyrannotitan ignore body downs in order to challenge other Apex terrestrial carnivores from its territory. This includes other Tyrannotitans. If the aggressor is successful in the contest, then they claim the body down as well as the territory. This challenge is a territory challenge, not a body contest. 6.7.4 Tyrannotitan may not interrupt the hunts of other Tyrannotitan, Giganotosaurus, and Tyrannosaurus, or any other carnivore to issue a territory challenge. If unwilling to wait for the hunt to conclude, they may seek out new territory. Tyrannotitan that cull unattended offspring of Juvenile or below may continue to cull offspring after a body drops, and do not have to claim the bodies. They do not have to be hungry in order to do so. 4.8.1 5.1.3. If a parent shows up to defend the offspring, Tyrannotitan females must challenge them for the right to the POI. This grants them a temporary exemption to species timers. 3.9. Sows with offspring may court and nest with a new boar if he wins a territory challenge against the boar who previously owned the territory. The sow will ungroup from her offspring if interested in nesting again. Offspring must be culled by the male or chased from the POI before the female may nest again. 9.3.2 Tyrannotitan are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering adverse side effects.
