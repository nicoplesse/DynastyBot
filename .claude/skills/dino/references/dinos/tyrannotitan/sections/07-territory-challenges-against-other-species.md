# Tyrannotitan – Territory Challenges Against Other Species

Tyrannotitan males defend their territories from other species, and rush to confront other carnivorous species. This begins with a broadcast :broadcast_call: , and then three aggressive  :threaten_call: calls. Tyrannotitan challenge out Medium and above carnivores from their territories, excluding Austroraptor at their nesting sites. Tyrannotitan do respect the forfeits of Tyrannosaurus & Giganotosaurus - but these rivals must be seen to be fleeing the POI for Tyrannotitan to let them go. The losers can be attacked and killed if they are not fleeing -  especially if standing still, stopping to sit, sleep, eat, or drink water. If leaving the POI, but out of stamina, Tyrannotitan rivals must continuously heart call or fear call to indicate their forfeit. They must run when they have the ability to do so. Tyrannotitan are permitted to challenge semi-aquatics if found within their land territory. If a Tyrannotitan male or sett lose a territory challenge against other species and are forced to leave the POI, any females nesting with them either follow, or disperse the POI without them. This often means being forced to leave their nesting sites behind - beginning a deep resentment within the females against the male(s) that lost the territory. Females will only challenge non-rival Medium and above carnivores IF she or her pair is nesting. Females will challenge other female Tyrannotitan, Tyrannosaurus, Giganotosaurus or Therizinosaurus out of the POI at all times. If males are within LOS, they must attempt to challenge the intruding species first. If a female loses a territory challenge, she is forced to take her whelps (if applicable) and leave the territory immediately. Preferred Prey Tyrannotitan may hunt any tier. Tyrannotitan do not hunt adults of their own species, except if the individual is Albino. They may hunt unrelated, unattended individuals that are Adolescent or below. If a parent arrives to defend unattended offspring, the hunt must stop, and a challenge must be issued instead. If hunting offspring, hunger must be logged and only one offspring may be hunted. However, Tyrannotitan often have bloody and vicious battles over POIs with same sex rivals, that frequently result in death. They claim and eat the body down afterwards, and may eat from or challenge for available body downs. Tyrannotitan will not feed from the bodies of their bonded pair in the case that they die. Tyrannotitan cannot start a hunt on any other species if other same sex rival Tyrannotitan, Tyrannosaurus, Giganotosaurus, or Therizinosaurus are knowingly within the POI. The other same sex rival of Tyrannotitan, Tyrannosaurus or Giganotosaurus must be challenged out first. In the case of Therizinosaurus, Tyrannotitan must attempt to hunt them first if able - or challenge them out. Tyrannotitan do not hunt Tyrannosaurus or Giganotosaurus unless Albino, deathscarred, or Adolescent and below, but may eat from their corpses. Otherwise, Tyrannotitan always hunt Therizinosaurus, Parasaurolophus, Eotriceratops, Apatosaurus, and Barsboldia before all other prey. Tyrannotitan may mob in groups of up to 3 to hunt Argentinosaurus, but must have at least 2 present. This typically involves a pair of one sex, and a solitary individual or a half of a pair from the other sex. They do not group during this time. Only courted pairs will tolerate one another in a mob. If the Argentinosaurus is successfully hunted, both pairs (if applicable) may feed from the kill at the same time, including the individual who did not take part in the hunt.
