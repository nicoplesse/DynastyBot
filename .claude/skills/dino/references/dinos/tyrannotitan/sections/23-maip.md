# Tyrannotitan – Maip

Tyrannotitan, intolerable to most carnivores, find a specific interest in Maip. The cackling and screaming of this large carnivore holds the attention of Tyrannotitan for a brief period of time, calling back to the smaller predator. This back and forth appears as a competition and entertainment to Tyrannotitan, who attempt to drown out Maip with their own calls until either species gets bored or moves on from the game. This creates a slightly higher tolerance for Maip and some Tyrannotitan may even allow them to remain within their territories, only aggressing them when they come too close or become obnoxious. Tyrannotitan do not hunt Maip if other options are within LOS and rarely challenge them from the POI. They will always defend themselves and their offspring from Maip if attacked.
