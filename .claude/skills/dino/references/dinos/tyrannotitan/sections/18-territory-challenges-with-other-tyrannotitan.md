# Tyrannotitan – Territory Challenges with other Tyrannotitan

When encountering another ungrouped Tyrannotitan within the same POI, all Tyrannotitan must immediately issue a greeting in order to declare one’s sex. If they are the same sex, both Tyrannotitan will continue to posture and begin to approach one another. Males, especially young confident males, will begin to broadcast in efforts to make themselves appear as intimidating as possible. This begins a mandatory period of circling one another; mirroring one another’s behaviors, stretching, showing one’s scars and colors, and demonstrating the gendered greeting in order to display strength. There must at least be ten seconds of circling before any challenge calls are issued. If one side has a pair, the younger or less dominant individual will stay directly next to their bonded companion, but will remain on the outside of the dominant individual, mimicking their behaviors. During this stage, Tyrannotitan take the time to evaluate the risk of the battle. Tyrannotitan take several factors into account in order to determine whether or not they intend to territory challenge the other individual, such as those listed below: Size, scarring, and age of their rivals; Whether or not there are resources or potential mates in the POI that they are trying to claim/keep; If they are alone, if they believe they can fare against paired individuals; Whether or not they are acting in defense of nests or their offspring. Younger individuals may behave more confidently and more willingly charge into combat, but older individuals take their time to examine the risks of remaining in or challenging for a territory. Once both sides have taken the time to evaluate their options, they have multiple choices: They may leave the territory during the initial circling phase - this is considered a forfeit, and the Tyrannotitan may not return to the territory for thirty minutes. They may initiate a territory challenge (1 broadcast call and 3 aggressive calls) or accept the challenge after it has been issued (1 aggressive call). They may allow the challenge to be issued, but may forfeit with a heart call, and may be escorted out of the POI by the winners. They may not return to the territory for thirty minutes. Once a challenge is accepted, the fight is brutal and bloody, with pairs working together in order to attempt to rip bloody wounds in the sides of their opponents. These brawls do not stop until one side forfeits or a body drops. If a body drops, a solitary Tyrannotitan may opt to stay with the body or may escort the loser out - though always returns to the body afterwards. If paired, one individual will stay with the body while the other escorts the loser out (if applicable). If a forfeit is issued, Tyrannotitan must allow their opponents reasonable time to leave the POI. If a Tyrannotitan runs out of stamina and is unable to keep running out of the POI, they must continually heart call to indicate that they are still leaving, and run when possible. If a Tyrannotitan does not heart call at all, stops, drinks, or eats while in the same territory, they may be attacked.
