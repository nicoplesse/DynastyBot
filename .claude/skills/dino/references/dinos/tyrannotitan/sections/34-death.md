# Tyrannotitan – Death

Bonded pairs often die together of advanced age, curling up alongside one another on beaches. If one in a pair is older than the other, they may repeatedly attempt to rouse their resting companion, pacing around them anxiously and yawning. If nothing rouses them, they eventually settle alongside their dying companion, issuing soft calls in attempt to comfort and get them to respond. Once their companion passes, they loudly cry out their grief and confusion, standing over the body. They may bite and lash out at the air around them, but refuse to leave the body. They may treat the body as claimed in the case of body challenges, but will not eat from it. They will stay with the body until it is gone, staring down at it - if able. If their partner died as a result of a fight and they must leave the body, they are forced to withdraw. Individuals who have lost their bonded pair, whether in battle, sickness, or old age, may claim the body (if able) for the purpose of defending it in a body contest, but will not eat from it. If hunted or challenged by another carnivore, Tyrannotitan may not claim the body, and must move on. If challenged and unwilling to fight for the body, Tyrannotitan will forfeit. These individuals may never group again– and any bonds take a much longer time to build up to the strength of that with their former bond. These individuals may be observed calling loudly into the night for a familiar voice that never responds. Tyrannotitan must greet other Tyrannotitan with their gendered greeting and either push them out of the POI or court them before doing any other activity in the POI. Staff will assume that opposite sex Tyrannotitan groups who are in the same POI have courted. Courted pairs and individuals may not engage the same individuals within 15 minutes of the other, regardless of whether the engagement (hunt or challenge) was forfeited or won. During and after successful courtship, male Tyrannotitan may share any kills they make within their territory with females and their offspring. They will not share kills otherwise. Females do not share their kills with males, instead prioritizing their own offspring. Females will bring chunks to their offspring, and will not lead offspring to the corpse. Females do not assist males in defending a body down unless they are actively eating from it. Female Tyrannotitan do not have to stay with bodies claimed by courted males. Orphaned Tyrannotitan may not group with other orphans unless they were nested in to the same clutch, and abandoned by their mother, or their mother dies. Boars may not group with sows when courting and nesting. However, they are welcome to use local chat to communicate anything as needed for nesting. Solitary boars and solitary sows that nest together may form a temporary nesting alliance. This is not grouped in game, but they may defend one another while the female is raising the male's offspring under Sub-Adult, whilst in his territory. When working together to bring down Argentinosaurus, Tyrannotitan may not group up with additional members and must remain in their in-game group limits. You may group together as half siblings, full siblings, or parent and offspring. You must have been nested in and posted in the 🩸・bloodline-logs channel to be able to group. This post should link the nesting post and detail relations. Tyrannotitan may commit infanticide on any unprotected and unrelated Tyrannotitan offspring of Juvenile or below, regardless of hunger levels. Tyrannotitan offspring with their mother present cannot be culled - with the exception listed in Tyrannotitan Uninterested in Courtship. Tyrannotitan may hunt any unrelated Adolescent Tyrannotitan that are unprotected, at appropriate hunger levels. Tyrannotitan do not challenge Austroraptor from their nesting grounds. Tyrannotitan only exhibit blue eyes when they are permanently blinded. Tyrannotitan that are in a sett or stand are considered to be within a sett or stand for the purposes of defending against predators, meaning that if separated from the other individual in their sett or stand, they may not seek the protection of a solitary mate within the POI. Only their bonded pair may act in their defense at all times. Tyrannotitan cannot hunt healthy adult Giganotosaurus or Tyrannosaurus - they must attempt to challenge them. However, they may hunt albinos, deathscarred, or adolescent and below individuals. Tyrannotitan do not hunt Tyrannotitan, unless the individual is Albino, or they are unprotected offspring of Adolescent and below. If they kill them following a challenge, or find a Tyrannotitan body down, they can eat from their corpse. Tyrannotitan will not eat the bodies of their bonded pairs. ALL Tyrannotitan that group together must log their relations in 🩸・bloodline-logs before they can group. You MUST have been nested in and have proof via a nesting post to prove relations. Tyrannotitan have mandatory behavioral greetings, with males/boars using the :question_emote: emote and females/sows using the :shake_emote: emote. Using the incorrect emote to greet is not permitted, and may result in being cannibalized. Males may use the :shake_emote: emote when they are not actively greeting a stranger Tyrannotitan. Females may use the :question_emote: emote when they are not actively greeting a stranger Tyrannotitan. Tyrannotitan males may share kills with females and their offspring, but may not take from a female’s kill. Female Tyrannotitan do not have to stay with bodies claimed by courted males. When committing infanticide on other Tyrannotitan, body down rules do not apply, and Tyrannotitan do not have to claim bodies dropped. This only applies to unprotected Tyrannotitan offspring of Juvenile and below. Tyrannotitan must drive out all other Apex terrestrial carnivores from the POI, with exceptions. A maximum of 4 Tyrannotitan may co-exist in one POI, so long as it is one group of males, and one group of females. All other Tyrannotitan, Tyrannosaurus and Giganotosaurus must be driven from the POI, or the Tyrannotitan must move on themselves. They may do this at any time of day, but should not move around the POI looking for rivals outside of their activity schedule. They may drive them out if the rivals encounter them.
