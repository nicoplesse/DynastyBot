# Tyrannotitan – Orphaned Behavior (Wild Spawn)

Whelps who are born to abandoned nests grow up entirely alone, as adult Tyrannotitan do not adopt. Male whelps often grow into more aggressive and louder adults, demanding the attention of any Tyrannotitan who come near to them. Males who grow up alone display powerful dominance - even over their own sons. Female whelps, on the other hand, are quieter, but extremely deadly. They exhibit a deep cunning, following prey for extended distances. The bonds they form with daughters are lifelong, and only end if one or the other dies. Orphaned Adolescent whelps may hunt and defend themselves if attacked. Whelps who grow up alone are often more perceptive of their surroundings, and more intentional about every move and call they make. Whelps who grow up alone often tend to be very light sleepers, and wake at the slightest of sounds. Whelps that lose their mother - whether from her death or abandonment - usually stick together after their loss. These groupings are often chaotic and dangerous. With no mother to intervene in sibling-on-sibling fights, orphans frequently squabble and fight, with many falling prey to a more dominant sibling. As a result, most orphans usually disperse from one another by the time they are Adolescent, only sticking with a same sex sibling that is closely bonded and could go on to become their partner. Tyrannotitan are imposing nocturnal predators that rely primarily on sound and smell to guide them. Tyrannotitan males claim territories, which they patrol in search of meals or potential partners. Females, on the other hand, take on a more nomadic lifestyle, traveling across Gondwa and Riparia. Their loud calls echo at an impressive distance, attracting competition and interest alike. Tyrannotitan struggle with “day blindness,” their eyes unable to adapt to the sunlight during the day. Spending too much time awake during the day causes harmful side effects to any Tyrannotitan. Repeated exposure to the light of day can cause the Tyrannotitan to become blind, displayed through their eyes turning blue. Tyrannotitan enjoy and are drawn to the scent of blood, able to detect small trails at an impressive distance. These massive predators may even cover their hides in it as they battle or hunt, giving the predator a fearsome reputation. Able to turn quickly and charge at a target in short bursts, Tyrannotitan take advantage of their mobility and alarming speed. Tyrannotitan are intelligent creatures with the capacity to remember and learn from their past experiences. Even as adults, Tyrannotitan exhibit exploratory biting. This is against new objects and experiences. Players may use the yawn emote to simulate this on other dinosaurs without beginning an engagement. Tyrannotitan teeth are constantly regrowing and replacing themselves, as they fall out in combat with other Tyrannotitan and their prey. The eldest and most experienced of Tyrannotitan’s bodies often are riddled with the teeth of other Tyrannotitan. During warm nights, Tyrannotitan can be found basking in shallow pools of water or along the edge of a shore. This helps them regulate their temperatures. Tyrannotitan also enjoy swimming, and take to water with no issue. Within a bonded pair, some Tyrannotitan will rub their faces against their counterpart, acting as both a means to scratch an itch or simply to reaffirm the mutual bond between them.
