# Tropeognathus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 4 🥚 per 2 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 1 🥚 per 3 hours. Due to poor physical health, Albino individuals are infertile in the Dry Season. In the Rainy season, they can only produce 1 egg. Tropeognathus nest at Ocean Pillars communally, with multiple females constructing nests close together close to - or in - the water. Their nests are decorated with reeds and shells and are constructed out of kelp, rock and branches.
