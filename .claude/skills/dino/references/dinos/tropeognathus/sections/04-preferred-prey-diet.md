# Tropeognathus – Preferred Prey & Diet

Tropeognathus may hunt up to Medium tier. However, they only hunt healthy species at night. Tropeognathus may also hunt lone Hatzegopteryx and lone Quetzalcoatlus in a group of 3 or more members. Tropeognathus may hunt any tier of hatchling or juvenile at any time of day, even if protected by an Adult. Tropeognathus hunt Thalassodromeus at any opportunity, regardless of hunger requirements. They may only do so in their preferred POIs. Tropeognathus especially enjoy hunting Lurdusaurus, who frequent similar POIs to Tropeognathus. Tropeognathus do not hunt Rhamphorhynchus, but will aggress them away from their food. Tropeognathus prefer, ultimately, to exist on a diet of fish and shellfish. They spend the entirety of their lives in coastal and sea-based regions, and are adept fishers. However, they are not opposed to hunting at opportunistic moments. Tropeognathus will also eat bones, fruit and insects when fish or meat is not available. Tropeognathus do not willingly cannibalize others, but are not wasteful, and happily consume the bodies of any fallen groupmates. Tropeognathus may attempt to scavenge kills made and owned by other carnivores at their own risk. Tropeognathus do not tolerate scavengers, and will readily swoop and peck to chase them away.
