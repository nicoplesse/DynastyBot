# Tropeognathus – Aggressive Interaction

Tropeognathus often bicker over food. Dominant males are the first to push themselves to the front of the queue when hungry, and rapidly move others out of the way with well-aimed pecks to ensure they get the choice cuts of meat. Females and chicks usually back down when faced with a dominant male like this, though do let out various screeches to show they are upset. Males, however, are much more likely to become aggressive when faced with this rude behavior, resulting in it escalating to an aerial battle between the two males - only stopping when one reaches half health and forfeits. Tropeognathus generally welcome any and all other Tropeognathus into their cluster when they encounter them, and aggression is usually reserved for specific instances - such as around food, offspring, and courting.
