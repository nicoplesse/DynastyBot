# Metriacanthosaurus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 3 🥚 per 2 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 2 🥚 per 2 hour. Metriacanthosaurus nest in swamp environments, along the coastline, or in caves. They build their nest out of reeds, roots and branches. Metriacanthosaurus may form same-sex pairs, but may not nest and do not adopt.
