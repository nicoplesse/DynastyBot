# Metriacanthosaurus – Cannibalization

Metriacanthosaurus first turn to Adolescent or below Metriacanthosaurus or Albino Metriacanthosaurus before looking for other meal options. Unlike most carnivores, Metriacanthosaurus is immune to the effects of the poison scales and toxic meat of their own kind, and may happily consume the body of any fallen Metriacanthosaurus - even their own pair if they are able. Eating other Metricanthosaurus may even bolster their own toxins, resulting in a more brightly colored individual.
