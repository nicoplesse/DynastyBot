# Metriacanthosaurus – Mutated Individuals

Albino Metriacanthosaurus are restricted to a solitary life, though do not immediately fear other Metriacanthosaurus and learn over time that all other Metriacanthosaurus are aggressive - sometimes prompting them to be the first to aggress another. At times, individuals who do not have any bright coloring may be more successful in hunting without having to conceal themselves as effectively. Metriacanthosaurus who are Albino may discover quickly that they are hunted more often, resulting in a more flighty individual. Metricanthosaurus with mutations do not struggle with toxin output in the slightest, even if their scale color alludes otherwise. Melanistic Metricanthosaurus are treated the same as any other individuals by other Metricanthosaurus, but are assumed to have extremely limited amounts of toxin, due to their lack of colors. Metriacanthosaurus view other Metriacanthosaurus within their line of sight as a potential meal, or simply a hinderance. Outside of their courting grounds, they either cannibalize (if the other is Adolescent or below), or begin a territory challenge with the other. The only time they are not seen exhibiting this behavior is in their own quiver. Metriacanthosaurus may either challenge or hunt other Metriacanthosaurus within line of sight, but this must be done first before attempting to hunt other creatures.
