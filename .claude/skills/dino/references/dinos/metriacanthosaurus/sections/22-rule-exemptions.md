# Metriacanthosaurus – Rule Exemptions

For all species excluding Metricanthosaurus, Compsognathus, Ceratosaurus, Maip, Allosaurus, Deinosuchus, Quetzalcoatlus, Rhamphornhychus and Megalania, Metriacanthosaurus bodies may be ignored in favor of hunting a different species (4.4). After a territory challenge, Metricanthosaurus is not required to stay 30 minutes in the same POI (6.7.2.3). Metricanthosaurus males who have been cannibalized by females for their final clutch may take an egg from the final clutch.
