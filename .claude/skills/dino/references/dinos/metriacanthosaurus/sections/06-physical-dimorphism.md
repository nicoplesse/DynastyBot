# Metriacanthosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

Metriacanthosaurus has physical dimorphism. This profile does not have high T or low T individuals. ♀️ Female Metriacanthosaurus are slightly smaller than males and do not have a dewlap, but have spines down their back. Suitable subspecies include: Latronemus ♂️ Male Metriacanthosaurus are bigger than females and have a dewlap. Suitable subspecies include: Parkeri, Circumspectus Physical dimorphism for Metriacanthosaurus is mandatory.
