# Allosaurus – Overview

Large Nocturnal Terrestrial Carnivore Allosaurus form temporary  pairbonds. Sometimes, these develop into strong pairbonds. Allosaurus are sedentary. However, they are territorial towards other Allosaurus, and will not tolerate them in the same POI if wishing to hunt or nest. Allosaurus have an aggressive nature towards other Allosaurus, but are tolerant towards other species. Allosaurus are not scavengers. Allosaurus may tolerate or shun mutated individuals, dependent on individual preference. Unrelated Albino Allosaurus are always shunned from groups.
