# Allosaurus – Death

Once an Allosaurus realizes it is dying, they disperse from their group, and begin traveling alone to seek out quiet caves or places of familiarity to have their final rest in. Partners or close relatives may follow from a distance despite this, comforting the dying Allosaurus in their last moments. Allosaurus who have died during a hunt are investigated by their companions, and if given the chance, eaten as well. Though mourning may follow for days after, Allosaurus prioritise their own survival above all else. Same-sex Allosaurus pairs may still permit a third, unrelated individual to group with them to form a coalition. Once offspring have dispersed, though they may be recognized, they are largely regarded as rivals, and kept at the same distance as other Allosaurus. Allosaurus are extremely quiet when showing interest in a stranger. Allosaurus within coalitions may spar with each other, while pairs do not. Allosaurus males may commit infanticide on adolescent and below unrelated, unattended offspring, but must stop once they become protected, or the engagement is lost through typical hunting forfeits. This is not considered a hunt, but a 15 minute timer does still apply towards infanticide. Allosaurus seeking courtship outside their current groupings will leave their in-game group once the courtship process has started to group with their potential partner. Allosaurus may seek out and re-join their previous groupings after courting and nesting has taken place. In order to nest, Allosaurus will become territorial, and must push out their former groupmates if still found in the area. They may travel to a new POI if wishing to avoid this conflict. Adopted offspring are considered related and are permitted to stay with their parents once they reach Sub/Adult should they be a favored offspring.
