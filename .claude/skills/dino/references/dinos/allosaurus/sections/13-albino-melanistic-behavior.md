# Allosaurus – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Albino Allosaurus have a significantly increased chance of being born deaf, or developing deafness in their youth. Albino Allosaurus are strictly avoided by other Allosaurus, and forced into a solitary lifestyle due to their sickly appearance. Nested in Albino Allosaurus are hesitantly accepted by their family, but are typically the last choice when it comes to grouping. Rarely ever are they favored by a nesting pair, or a sibling’s choice in a dispersal companion. Though accepted by family, these individuals are treated as inferior, and are discouraged from joining in hunts or partaking in spars. All other mutations are tolerated, though those straying outside of typical dimorphism - such as melanistic male Allosaurus - face additional difficulties in courtship, and sometimes struggle to find a mate at all. Allosaurus display aggression towards others of their kind when intending to hunt or nest within the POI. Rivals are challenged with a territory challenge to win the right to hunt or nest. If simply passing through a POI, Allosaurus are not required to aggress its own kind, but will not voluntarily remain within the POI for long periods of time. However, if aggressed by others, they must opt to flee if they do not wish to fight for the right to remain in the POI.
