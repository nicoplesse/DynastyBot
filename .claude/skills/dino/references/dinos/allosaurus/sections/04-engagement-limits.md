# Allosaurus – Engagement Limits

Up to 3 Sub/Adults may work together in defense, challenges, and hunts. In groups of 2+, Allosaurus may hunt Apex tier. Adolescents do not take part in hunts alongside adults. A solitary Allosaurus may hunt up to Large tier. Up to 3 related adolescents may hunt Small tier. Adults do not assist in these hunts, but 1 Adult may defend if the engagement continues after an adolescent's forfeit. Preferred Prey Before hunting, Allosaurus must challenge ungrouped Allosaurus in line of sight or that are knowingly within the POI, whether solitary or grouped. They drive these rivals out of the POI to maintain their hunting rights. Solitary Allosaurus may hunt up to Large tier and below. 2+ Allosaurus may hunt up to Apex tier. All Allosaurus prioritize Adolescent and below Amargasaurus and Apatosaurus before all other prey options, whether they’re attended or not. Solitary Allosaurus are not required to hunt protected or Adolescent Apatosaurus offspring before all other prey, but may try if they wish. Next, Allosaurus must prioritize Stegosaurus, Kentrosaurus, and Miragaia. Then, Allosaurus must prioritize any Large or Apex herbivores within LOS, if they are able to. If neither option is available, Allosaurus may hunt anything up to Apex. Solitary Allosaurus may only hunt up to Large tier. Allosaurus males may cannibalize unrelated, unprotected Allosaurus offspring of Adolescent and below. Allosaurus do not hunt solitary adult Quetzalcoatlus. Allosaurus do not hunt each other. However, Allosaurus are not wasteful, and will feed from an Allosaurus body if one is available.
