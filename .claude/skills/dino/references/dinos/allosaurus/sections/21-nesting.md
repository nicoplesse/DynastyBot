# Allosaurus – Nesting

Allosaurus nest in dense foliage, caves, and hollow trees. The female will complete courtship by guiding her mate to a den site of her choice, inviting him to investigate and approve of the location chosen. If both Allosaurus are satisfied with the selection, then the pair will begin preparing for offspring. Once a densite has been agreed upon, ungrouped Allosaurus are no longer tolerated in the POI, and must be challenged out upon sight, regardless of whether or not the odds may not be in the pair’s favor. The female will put her focus in securing the den site, ensuring her nest is plush and warm for the fragile young, and that the surrounding area is free of anything that may harm them. The male aids by beginning to patrol further out from the den site. His duty is staying alert for threats that may risk the safety of the upcoming clutch, and returning with food to fill the nest with. If the pair already have a chosen offspring grouped with them, the offspring may choose between helping their mother prepare the nest site, or patrolling with their father. If other Allosaurus are spotted in the area, the nesting group will attempt to collect all their members before challenging the rivals out of the POI.
