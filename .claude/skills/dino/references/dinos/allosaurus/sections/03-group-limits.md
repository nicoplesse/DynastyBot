# Allosaurus – Group Limits

Up to 2 Sub/Adults Unlimited Offspring Unlimited Adoptees (however, only females or pairs with females adopt) This group is known as a pair. or: Up to 3 Sub/Adults, consisting of a pair and 1 Sub/Adult offspring; or three siblings of any sex; or unrelated individuals of all the same sex. Unlimited Offspring Unlimited Adoptees (however, only females or groups with females adopt) This group is known as a coalition.
