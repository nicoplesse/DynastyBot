# Allosaurus – Territory Challenges

Allosaurus may challenge other Allosaurus at any time of day. Those that are uninterested in courtship, and unable or unwilling to group with other Allosaurus, resort to aggressively challenging out rivals if intending to hunt or nest within the POI. Even when the odds look to be against them, all Allosaurus - including solitary individuals - are perfectly capable of responding with aggression in an attempt to intimidate and push out others of its species, and engage in what may otherwise be an unfair fight. These challenges are fierce and extremely risky for all involved. Allosaurus claws and teeth are made to tear through dense hides, with fatalities occurring more often after the fight has concluded due to blood loss and infection. Allosaurus may forfeit challenges against their own species through affectionate calls, fear calls, or a combination of the two. Once one side forfeits, the winning side may escort their wounded rivals out of the POI to ensure they leave. If unsure about going against multiple rivals, or simply uninterested in remaining within POI, individuals may flee instead rather than risk injury.
