# Allosaurus – Downtime behaviour

During the daytime, Allosaurus are often found relaxing and sleeping. Caves, shaded overhangs, and dense shrubbery away from common pathways are sought out. Allosaurus can be fitful sleepers and often readjust their sleeping positions multiple times throughout the day. Some Allosaurus may fall asleep with their head hanging downwards, before relaxing into their usual lay. During dawn and dusk, Allosaurus can often be found relaxing at water sources, drinking heavily prior to spending the day sleeping, or the nighttime searching for prey. Allosaurus may move and travel during dawn and dusk, but do not hunt outside their active hours. Allosaurus, when meeting other Allosaurus, take their time to assess potential interest in grouping up or courtship. Interactions are based on the tolerance and interest of each Allosaurus involved. While Allosaurus most often group with those that benefit them, individuals may find themselves going back to or remaining with familiar company, their temporary bonds shifting into strong pairbonds with another individual over time through successful hunts or raising offspring. A full group, though lacking the space for more members, may pause before challenging other Allosaurus to consider courtship opportunities, which they will disperse for if successful. Those not looking to expand their numbers, or not interested in courtship, turn to aggression if looking to secure hunting and/or nesting rights for the POI. Further information can be found in Aggressive Behavior. If uninterested in staying within a POI, Allosaurus instead choose to leave the POI once challenged out, knowing better than to seek out unnecessary conflict. If simply passing through, and not looking to hunt or nest, other Allosaurus are tolerated from a distance. If an Allosaurus is interested in potentially grouping up or courting another, then individuals remain quiet upon greeting, observing the other and avoiding any actions that may come across as aggression. Once mutual interest has been established through shared silence, Allosaurus begin approaching with slow, calculated steps, directing themselves into an open space where they are clearly visible to one another. If assessing for courtship opportunities - and part of a larger group - then only the interested Allosaurus from the group steps forward. If no immediate hostility follows, Allosaurus raise their claws to the side, signalling their interest by exposing their chest, and trilling softly (:question_emote:); presenting themselves as not a threat. The other Allosaurus replicates this gesture to indicate the same message. After, both Allosaurus cautiously close the distance to further investigate, taking their time to inspect each other - and, if happy with what they see, exchange friendly calls to showcase their acceptance. If at any point during the process an individual loses interest, they ward the other Allosaurus away with aggressive calls, withdrawing their tolerance. This aggression may escalate to either party beginning a challenge for the POI, or simply fleeing to avoid further escalation.
