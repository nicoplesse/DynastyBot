# Citipati – Predator Reaction

Citipati flee from predators of Large tier and above, and will react very quickly if danger is noticed. Medium or below predators that are solo or in pairs may be aggressively attacked by up to 4 Citipati (from the same harem) if they begin a hunt on the flock. Larger numbers of these predators will trigger a flee response in Citipati. Mothers call for their chicks to scatter and hide in bushes, while they dart in another direction and attempt to distract the predator, sometimes rushing at a predator to draw attention before luring it away from the chicks. Harem members stick closely together, if one member within their line of sight is attacked by a predator, the others in the harem will rush to defend it. Citipati will never defend a harem member if they are being attacked by a carnivore of Large tier and above. Individuals do not defend one another if not in the same harem, preferring to flee and regroup further away.
