# Citipati – Social Interaction

Citipati are social and live in a flock made up of a mix of unattached adults and small harem groups. They are vigilant and both genders have strong protective instincts. Social bonds are built and maintained with grooming behaviours; flock members groom one another to strengthen bonds, particularly within harem groups. Males in particular enjoy spending time preening their feathers and grooming other members of their harem. Citipati hens allow roosters to take the lead when moving between areas, letting them focus on vigilance and threat awareness, while hens take care of chicks and keep the flock moving closer together, rather than spread out and scattered. Citipati enjoy walking and resting closely with members in their harem and with their chicks, and sometimes with other flock members - apart from roosters who only show affection to their hens. Hens within the same harem form strong bonds between one another, helping each other with chicks, checking in on each other, and remaining close together when moving across the land. These bonds can strengthen into almost pair-bonds but a hen is never exclusive with a hen she is bonded to, and the rooster always plays an important role in their social and biological structure. Bringing chicks into the world, and raising them, are the two key focuses of a hen’s nature. Citipati harems share gestures of reassurance and affection between one another, and are generally peaceful and harmonious. But there are some situations that may cause the harem to break apart. If a rooster is inattentive to his hens, or a hen feels like an outsider with the other hens, she may choose to leave the harem and seek out a more suitable rooster. If a rooster accidentally or purposefully injures or kills one of her chicks (this does not include adoptees of a different species), she will reject the rooster almost immediately. Very rarely, a hen may forgive a rooster’s accidental mistake, but this depends on the personality of the hen in question. When leaving a harem, the hen faces her rooster and calls loudly in alarm :danger_call:  directly in his personal space, before running from the harem and keeping a firm distance for a short period of time, until the rooster realises what has occurred.
