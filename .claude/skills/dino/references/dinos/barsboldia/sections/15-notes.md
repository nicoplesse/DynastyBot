# Barsboldia – Notes

Barsboldia of Sub-Adult and above have a high requirement to consume Salt Rocks at regular intervals. Those that do not become afflicted with hyponatremia. In Riparia, a lack of salt rocks may be substituted with one or more sips of salt water. Barsboldia mothers adopt the first orphan under adolescent they find only after their biological calf dies. This is a form of reverse imprinting, and the mother treats this offspring as if it were her calf. They do not join the in game group.
