# Barsboldia – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Aggressive/Defensive 2 Barsboldia may enter an engagement or challenge for food and water resources. If hunted by Tyrannosaurus, and there are no offspring or adoptees of Adolescent or below present, the group flees, and the attacked Barsboldia receives no defenders. Other Barsboldia in the herd step away from the engagement, not wishing to receive accidental damage from standing too close. They are allowed to watch, but must maintain distance, and do not assist the other Barsboldia. At nesting sites, Barsboldia protect a space 5 body lengths around their calves and nests. Ungrouped creatures that get within this space are given one aggressive call :threaten_call:, before being chased out by a maximum of 2 Barsboldia. A mother defending her nesting site or offspring does not count to engagement limits. However, she must make an effort to move away from the engagement with her offspring when possible and toward the security of the herd, with the two defenders taking her place. Mothers defend a space of 2 body lengths around each calf or adoptee under Sub-Adult. Preferred Diet Barsboldia eat fruit and berries exclusively in their younger years. As they age, Barsboldia are encouraged by their mothers to move onto tougher foods, such as roots, pinecones and other vegetation. Sub-Adult & Older Barsboldia require Salt Rocks at regular intervals. They travel vast distances to consume them. Barsboldia that do not eat Salt Rocks regularly become afflicted with hyponatremia.
