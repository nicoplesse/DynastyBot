# Anodontosaurus – Solitary Behavior

Anodontosaurus fare especially well when solitary, and many individuals choose to remain alone for the majority - if not the entirety - of their lives. They are less vocal than tumbles, keeping to just low grumbles and chuffs as they meander through the POI, foraging for food and resources. These individuals usually move between POIs a lot less frequently than their more social peers. With less pressure for resources, most solitary Anodontosaurus do not feel the need to be constantly on the move. They instinctively seek out dense foliage, tall trees or rugged rock faces to better shelter themselves from predators. Solitary Anodontosaurus may still travel to Dried Lake to court and nest, driven by instinct to continue their lineage. Often, they return to a solitary life after their clutch has reached Sub-Adult.
