# Anodontosaurus – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Albino and melanistic Anodontosaurus face no drastic physical or social difficulties in comparison to their peers. However, Albino Anodontosaurus have slightly softer defensive plates along their back - but are still able to defend themselves. Albino individuals do suffer with reduced fertility levels, and are only able to produce or father a single egg per clutch. Anodontosaurus are highly food aggressive, and will defend resources they are actively eating from and two body lengths around the resource from all other species - including other Anodontosaurus. They only permit their offspring below Sub-Adult and current nesting partner to share with them. Other than defending food resources, Anodontosaurus are not aggressive with other Anodontosaurus, and tolerate them in their space. Anodontosaurus that ignore a warning or compete over resources may be attacked by rivals, but these encounters rarely alter their overall relationships. Grudges are not held, and all individuals move on once a challenge is over. Even within a tumble, Anodontosaurus do not intervene in these disputes, instead idly watching a confrontation play out. Anodontosaurus are generally tolerant of all other species that keep a distance from them. Some individuals may allow other herbivores to get closer to them, while others become highly selective, and aggress species that enter within 2 body lengths of their personal space. Intruders are first warned with deep, aggressive calls. If ignored, they are driven off with powerful swings of their clubbed tails. These are mock attacks at first, but quickly escalate into intentional physical damage if these warnings are ignored. Anodontosaurus challenges must follow challenge rules with 3 :threaten_call: in addition to various mock attacks. Anodontosaurus will not approach a species to aggress them, and will only aggress species that enter their space. Tiny species such as Compsognathus and Rhamphorhynchus are always permitted to be near them for their grooming capabilities, but may be aggressed if they become an annoyance. Halszkaraptor is generally ignored. Carnivores and herbivores that keep a respectful distance from Anodontosaurus are always tolerated. They will only be aggressive if they encroach on their personal space. Anodontosaurus is a resilient and tough herbivore, and is not particularly frightened by any carnivore regardless of its size or ferocity. Anodontosaurus is defensive, and will only react if a carnivore/herbivore comes within 2 body lengths of it. Anodontosaurus may begin with attempting to drive an opponent off with powerful swings of their clubbed tails. These begin as mock attacks first, but quickly escalate into physical attacks if these warnings are ignored. Some Anodontosaurus may opt to hunker down, and aim their heavy clubbed tails at their approaching opponent. Anodontosaurus physical engagements are usually based on an individuals experiences throughout their life. Anodontosaurus parents wedge their children in front of them to protect them from the predator, enabling their back ends to swing their tails to defend them both. Both parents aid in protecting offspring under Sub-Adult. Parents do not aid offspring if they are 4 body lengths or more away. Offspring must run to the parent in order to be defended. Anodontosaurus do not defend any other adults - including their current nesting partner.
