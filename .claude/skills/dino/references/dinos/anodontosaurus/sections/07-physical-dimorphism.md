# Anodontosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

Physical dimorphism for Anodontosaurus is mandatory. This profile does not have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Females are stockier, with shorter, rounder scutes on their backs and necks. Suitable subspecies include: Obtunsi ♂️ Males are leaner, with larger scutes on their head and neck. Suitable subspecies include: Lambei, Rasai Anodontosaurus do not have color dimorphism, and individuals can be seen in a wide variety of colors.
