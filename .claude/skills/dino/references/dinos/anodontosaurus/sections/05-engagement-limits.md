# Anodontosaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Defensive Anodonotsaurus only defend themselves and their offspring under Sub-Adult. Other adults are not defended. Both parents can work to defend their young simultaneously. Anodontosaurus stake claim over food resources they are eating from and will defend this resource and 2 body lengths around the resource from all other dinosaurs. Anodontosaurus are hardy and do not flee from any tier of dinosaur -  unless they decide the situation seems dire. Preferred Diet Anodontosaurus mainly feast on fruit, roots, nuts and flowers. Anodontosaurus require a varied diet consisting of fruits, roots, nuts, and flowers, consumed in rotation. Overconsumption of a single food type can lead to illness. (See General Behavior) Adolescent and younger Anodontosaurus prioritize fruit over other food sources due to their high nutritional value, and do not adhere to the strict dietary requirements for adults.
