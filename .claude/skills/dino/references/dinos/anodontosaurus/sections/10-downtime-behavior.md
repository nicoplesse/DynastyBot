# Anodontosaurus – Downtime Behavior

Anodontosaurus sleep heavily at night based on the security of their tough upper shells. They often find comfortable places deep in foliage, or wedged in between rocks or trees to assist in their defense. Often, an Anodontosaurus prefers to sleep on top of, or directly next to, an available food source, particularly those that they enjoy. Anodontosaurus do not migrate at night, unless threatened by predators or unable to find a suitable food resource. Anodontosaurus may court at night when at Dried Lake or Crag Bluffs.
