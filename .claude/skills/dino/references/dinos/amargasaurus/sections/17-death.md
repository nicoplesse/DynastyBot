# Amargasaurus – Death

As an elder nears the end of its life, feeding becomes increasingly difficult due to diminished strength and mobility. Starvation is the most frequent cause of death among these aging individuals, marking the natural conclusion of their life cycle. When interested to nest, Amargasaurus shed into bright colors. When more than one Amargasaurus shed into bright colors the herd instinctively travel to their nesting grounds to lay eggs. If you do not want to nest or court, do not shed into a bright color. With more than one nesting female, Amargasaurus do not know which hatchling belonged to their nest and if broody, will adopt any hatchling that follows them. Same with offspring, hatchlings do not recognize their mother and will accept any approaching adult as a parent. Amargasaurus are passive and are not aggressive with other Amargasaurus. All Amargasaurus flee Allosaurus and Torvosaurus. They may defend themselves, but must focus on escape. Amargasaurus shed into bright colors when courting and nesting. They must migrate to their nesting grounds to place a nest and hatch offspring.
