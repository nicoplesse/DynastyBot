# Amargasaurus – Nesting

When choosing a nesting spot, females dig their nests next to one another and carefully arrange thick bushes around their nest for camouflage and protection. The size of a female's egg cluster often reflects the abundance of food in the area - areas rich in berries spawn a full clutch, while an area lacking berries might result in fewer eggs. After laying her eggs and regurgitating food into the nest, females leave the wooded areas and linger nearby. If enough members are in the herd, they surround the nesting site with watchful eyes.
