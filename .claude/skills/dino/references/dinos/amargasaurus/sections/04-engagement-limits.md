# Amargasaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Flighty/Defensive Up to 2 Amargasaurus actively engage predators, while the rest move away from the engagement. Amargasaurus can charge its opponents to intimidate them, but respects forfeits and does not charge after predators fleeing the engagement. Charging is sometimes accompanied by a smack from the neck, but mostly is employed to drive a predator away. Amargasaurus flee from Allosaurus and Torvosaurus. Preferred Diet Berries, melons and bananas
