# Amargasaurus – Downtime Behavior

Amargasaurus are very vocal, even in downtime. They constantly coo to each other, almost constantly making constant noise and chatter when not sleeping. Amargasaurus enjoy wallowing in shallow water and going for brief swims. Amargasaurus enjoy the sensation of water running down their necks and tail so they dunk their heads in the water often. At night, the herd constantly cycles through those who are sleeping and those who have stayed awake to socialize with other members of the herd.
