# Amargasaurus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: & ☀️ Dry Season: 4 🥚 per 3 hour. Females can nest with multiple males at once. Separate nesting posts must be posted for each male, with the egg count of 4 divided between the males how the female sees fit. Males have no nesting limits and may nest with any number of females. Amargasaurus nest in specific POIs: Impact Crater, Grand Plains Forest, and Birchwoods. Amargasaurus may form same-sex pairs, and may adopt.
