# Amargasaurus – Physical Dimorphism

Physical dimorphism for Amargasaurus is mandatory. This profile does not have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Females have flatted or no spines along their necks Suitable subspecies include: cazaui & angulus ♂️ Males have upright, raised spines along their neck Suitable subspecies include: serratus During courtship: Both Male and Female Amargasaurus shed into bright colors when traveling to their nesting grounds to lay their eggs.
