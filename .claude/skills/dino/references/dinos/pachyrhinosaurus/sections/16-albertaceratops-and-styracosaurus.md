# Pachyrhinosaurus – Albertaceratops and Styracosaurus

Outside of nesting areas, Albertaceratops and Styracosaurus are tolerated within a mixed-species migration. Pachyrhinosaurus typically follow their own path, but in the absence of an elder cow to lead, a herd or individual is known to travel with these smaller ceratopsians. Understanding their instincts of herding behavior and tolerance with each other is beneficial should a predator target them.
