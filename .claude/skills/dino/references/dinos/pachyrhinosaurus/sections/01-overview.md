# Pachyrhinosaurus – Overview

Large Diurnal Terrestrial Herbivore Pachyrhinosaurus form harem bonds. Pachyrhinosaurus are nomadic. Males become territorial in their nesting ground. When courting and nesting, males defend small territories around food sources. Pachyrhinosaurus have a tolerant nature. Pachyrhinosaurus may group with: Albertaceratops, Nasutoceratops, Styracosaurus, and Psittacosaurus. Pachyrhinosaurus may defend other species grouped with them. Pachyrhinosaurus tolerate mutated individuals.
