# Pachyrhinosaurus – Profile Specific Feature

Pachyrhinosaurus has a profile specific disability using the eye color blue. Blue eyed Pachyrhinosaurus are considered blind. This can be inherited as a mutation, or caused by extreme head trauma. Blindness is obtained by typing /roll-illness in the 🦠・illness-rolls channel after experiencing a traumatic fight ingame (optional). It is also a mutation Pachyrhinosaurus can get upon birth (You don't have to play as a blind character, you can choose to put the blindness mutation as your [R] recessive instead).
