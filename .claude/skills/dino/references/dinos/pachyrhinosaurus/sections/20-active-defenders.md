# Pachyrhinosaurus – Active Defenders

When an engagement has started, up to 4 adults (usually bulls, and preferably shields),  charge forward to land crushing blows on the predator outside of the formation. Others stay within the formation, only stepping forward a maximum of one body length to land a hit on the predator, before stepping back into the formation. The active defenders barrels into the predator in an attempt to cripple their foe, then pace outside the formation, staying close enough to properly protect their herd and protect their exposed bodies. Other herd members remain firm in the formation to keep a strong defensive front. If the predator makes the mistake of treading too close, Pachyrhinosaurus within the formation will not hesitate to step forward and land a killing blow before retreating back into the safety of the formation.
