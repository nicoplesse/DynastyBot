# Suchomimus – Spinosaurus

Suchomimus have a unique relationship with Spinosaurus. Suchomimus are never aggressive to Spinosaurus under any circumstances. If Spinosaurus hunts them, they will choose to flee rather than fight back. Spinosaurus - unlike their more devious cousins Suchomimus - are a slow yet strongly parental carnivore that can sometimes recognize Suchomimus as smaller, deformed Spinosaurus in need of care. Due to their strong parental instincts and desire to nurture, this even extends to them wanting to nurture and protect Suchomimus. Due to this, Suchomimus prefer to place their nests as close as possible to Spinosaurus nests, hoping to offload their offspring and secure them a higher chance of survival. Some Spinosaurus will even adopt Suchomimus when they do not have young of their own. Suchomimus recognize these behaviours and instinctively choose to be near Spinosaurus, keen to be adopted and "cared for" by their larger cousins. The smaller the Suchomimus, the higher chance there is the Spinosaurus may wish to adopt them, which provides an easy life of being fed, nurtured and protected from other carnivores. This is always the Suchomimus' preference if it is possible.
