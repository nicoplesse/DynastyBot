# Suchomimus – Courtship

Courtship for Suchomimus begins with males staking claim to a temporary display site along a water source. This is usually a shallow area of a riverbank, a rocky pool, or part of a lake edge. From these tiny territories, males loudly broadcast their presence, unleashing a chorus of roars, screeches and hisses. They lash their tails into the water, creating loud splashes, and snap their jaws open and shut in the area with loud clacks. The goal for these males is to appear the boldest and most confident - making loud vocalizations like this is sure to draw attention from nearby females, but the splashing in water can also lure in aquatic predators. Males that secure the most appealing location have a higher chance of impressing a potential mate. As such, these prime spots are often hotly contested for by other males. Challenges begin with a slow and aggressive approach, with the male issuing out deep, threatening growls. This ritualized warning gives the current occupant a chance to retreat, though most stand their ground. When neither is willing to back down, a violent battle ensues -  they bite into each other's flesh, slash them with their claws, and try to shove the other into submission. These battles continue until one male backs down and submits, granting victory and the right to stay in his place. Females enjoy watching these fights, poking her head out of the water to watch the males. Once a winner has been determined, she will approach him slowly, shaking the water off of herself. The two will briefly circle one another before issuing affectionate calls and nuzzling one another -- indicating sweet, screechy acceptance.
