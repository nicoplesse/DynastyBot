# Suchomimus – Engagement Limits

3 grouped sub/adults may enter an engagement or hunt. Suchomimus may hunt up to Apex tier. Adolescent Suchomimus do not assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Suchomimus may attempt to hunt up to Medium tier. When in dispersal group: 2 Adolescent Suchomimus may attempt to hunt up to solo Large tier.
