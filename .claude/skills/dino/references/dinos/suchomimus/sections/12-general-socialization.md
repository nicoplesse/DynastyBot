# Suchomimus – General Socialization

Suchomimus are not particularly aggressive towards other Suchomimus, and do not cannibalize or adopt others of their species. They are largely unbothered by the presence of others, but will defend if unknown Suchomimus attempt to get too close to their mate or kill. One exception is when there are Spinosaurus in the area. Suchomimus will aggressively defend their right to be around Spinosaurus from other Suchomimus not in their snare, viewing others as a potential threat to their chances of getting fed. Suchomimus show affection to one another once they have established bonds. They make low growling noises, raising their heads and snapping their snouts, and shaking against each other. Suchomimus enjoy swimming - or just floating - under the water together. They will also choose to sleep beside the others in their group when they take naps. Suchomimus are a vocally aggressive species first and foremost, utilizing intimidating calls, screeches and roars in order to threaten both other species and others of their kind. Between individuals, disagreements and squabbles are common, but rarely do they escalate any further than a quick scuffle or snap at one another. Most aggression towards other Suchomimus arises when Spinosaurus is present in the area. As a snare becomes extremely possessive over their Spinosaurus, they do not tolerate any other Suchomimus in the vicinity to get close to them - viewing them as a threat to their "resource". As such, they begin a series of aggressive calls towards any other Suchomimus that gets too close to their claimed Spinosaurus. Should the warnings not be heeded, Suchomimus will launch a vicious attack - not stopping until either their opponent flees, or they reach deathscars during the battle. Suchomimus utilize water to their advantage and will use it to flee from terrestrial carnivores if a lost battle seems likely. Suchomimus will defend others in their snare from all threats and challenges.
