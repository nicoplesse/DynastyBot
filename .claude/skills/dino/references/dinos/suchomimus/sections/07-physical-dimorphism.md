# Suchomimus – Physical Dimorphism

Suchomimus have no color dimorphism, and both sexes can be either colorful, dull, or somewhere inbetween. However it is thought that more dominant individuals tend to have brighter colors. This profile does have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Females do not have a pronounced crest. Suitable subspecies include: Anguilla ♂️ Males have a more pronounced crest. Suitable subspecies include: Lapparenti, Tenerensis Physical dimorphism for Suchomimus is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
