# Alioramus – Parenthood - 2 Females

In bonded pairs, both females act as mothers to offspring, regardless of who laid the eggs. Offspring from both nests - if applicable - are pooled together, forming a single creche, with cubs free to move between both nests. Females rotate duties between guarding, resting, grooming offspring, and escorting cubs to water once mobile. Both females take it in turns to separate from their young and fetch resources, ensuring the cubs are rarely left alone. Should females be required to join the rest of the pack to hunt, young may either be stashed in the nest and made to wait, or permitted to accompany the females a short distance, depending on their age and confidence. Parenthood is significantly less stressful for bonded female pairs. Both mothers show great affection and tenderness to their young, and frequently coo at and preen their feathers gently.
