# Alioramus – Preferred Prey & Diet

Alioramus may hunt up to Apex tier. Alioramus only hunts Apex when no other prey options are available in line of sight. Alioramus must have 5 in the hunting party to hunt Apex tier. Alioramus do not hunt Apex carnivores. Alioramus prefers to hunt Medium tier and below or hatchling/juvenile individuals, and targets these first with multiple prey options in the line of sight. Alioramus cannot hunt Amargasaurus, Apatosaurus, Ampelosaurus or Argentinosaurus unless these species are Adolescent or younger. Alioramus may hunt Hatchling or Juvenile of any tier, even with parents present. However, they must clearly target the offspring. Alioramus may not hunt attended Apex carnivore offspring.
