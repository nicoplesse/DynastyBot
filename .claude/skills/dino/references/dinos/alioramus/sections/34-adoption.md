# Alioramus – Adoption

Adoption is practised only by females. Bonded pairs readily adopt orphaned cubs - with or without offspring of their own - but solitary females never do so. Orphaned cubs are often timid and require coaxing, something which is usually achieved through offering scraps of meat to win them over. Once cubs are adopted, they are treated no differently to biological offspring. Elderly Alioramus are rare, but elderly females and female pairs are more common. They are less likely to initiate hunts, and allow the males to provide for them, showing less interest in nesting. Elderly males move just as they did in their youth, but show higher exhaustion and less interest in nesting. This means that they might struggle with maintaining their role of dominance when males are constantly sparring with each other in order to impress. Additionally, their colors may fade.
