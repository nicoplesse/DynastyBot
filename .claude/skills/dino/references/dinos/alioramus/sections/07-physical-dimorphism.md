# Alioramus – Physical Dimorphism

Alioramus have slight color dimorphism. This profile may have high and low T individuals. However, this only has an effect on appearance, and does not have females acting as males and vice versa. ♀️ Females are typically darker, with less bright colors across their bodies. They can show lighter colors, but the patterning is always low contrast and muted. Melanism and other very dark skins are highly desirable in females. ♂️ Males show brighter, flashier colors. This can include streaks of white that contrast well with the other colors in their coat. Muted, dark and melanistic males are picked as a last resort as courting partners. Physical dimorphism for Alioramus is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship. Males and females have different behavioral traits, read under Social Interaction for more information.
