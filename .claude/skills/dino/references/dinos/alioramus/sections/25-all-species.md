# Alioramus – All Species

Alioramus enjoy tracking and stalking herbivore herds over long distances. They do not always hunt these species - and can sometimes trail a herd for several days without attempting to, before leaving to follow another species. They make sure to leave enough room between them and the herd to avoid provoking aggression or fear in the group. Alioramus do not tolerate scavengers near their kills. Unlike some other predators, Alioramus are not territorial towards other species, and instead migrate between keen locations in search of available prey. The only time they defend a specific area is during nesting. In this phase, males take on the role of patrolling and guarding the area surrounding the nest site, with all females in the pack focusing entirely on raising their young. They do not challenge away Apex carnivores or sauropods, and instead warn the nesting females and offspring to flee the area as soon as possible.
