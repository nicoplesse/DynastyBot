# Alioramus – Male-Only Packs Encountering Others

Encounters between male only Alioramus groups are usually cautious, rather than immediately hostile. Males pace and circle each other, vocalizing to gauge one another's confidence - and intent. If both packs have room, males usually tolerate one another and merge into a larger group without much issue. The temporary hierarchy in the pack changes, with the newest males placed at the bottom, and forced to work their way up by showing dominance, and sparring with their new packmates. Should there not be enough room, males fight for control of the POI - posturing and aggressive calling three times before launching into battle. Either group can choose to move on if they do not wish to stay in the POI, but must do so quickly to avoid the other group chasing them out too closely.
