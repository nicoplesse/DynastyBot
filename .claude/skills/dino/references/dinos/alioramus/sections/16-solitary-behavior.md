# Alioramus – Solitary Behavior

Solitary female Alioramus experience significant social strain. Driven by their innate need to form a lifelong bond with another female, they actively search for companionship throughout their lives. Even without a female partner, they will still form groups with males, though these are never enough to fully meet their emotional needs. Male Alioramus fare considerably better alone. While still social animals by nature, males are capable of maintaining stable solitary lives without any signs of psychological stress.
