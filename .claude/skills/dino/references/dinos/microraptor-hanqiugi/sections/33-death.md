# Microraptor (Hanqiugi) – Death

With their deteriorating ability to latch onto surfaces, most Microraptor find escape from predators difficult and succumb to terrestrial hunters - or are simply unable to move out of the way of passing creatures. Microraptor who can sense their waning time often seek out soft, mossy beds on the forest floor or dense underbrush to rest in, curling up to preserve any of their lasting warmth. Lifelong bonded pairs will often pass together, though when one remains alive, they will often call out in distress and attempt to rouse their passing partner.
