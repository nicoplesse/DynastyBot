# Microraptor (Hanqiugi) – Elderly & Death

Aging Microraptor begin to notice their inability to glide and fly for as long as they once were able to. Their ability to latch onto surfaces weakens, and they are more often seen tumbling to the ground, either through missing their mark, or losing significant grip strength. Some Microraptor shift their preferences in carnivore companion, preferring the territorial Tyrannosaurus or male Tyrannotitan that allows them to remain stationary. A Microraptor’s feathers may dull with age.
