# Microraptor (Hanqiugi) – Downtime behaviour

Microraptor sleeps in short bursts - this is always high up in a tree or cliff. Caves and small tunnels are far too constricting and do not offer the creature the same freedom. Microraptor with carnivore companions who migrate often will cling to their sides, their heads falling forwards or to one side as they take the time to rest during travel. In scurries, all Microraptor take time to rest in unison. The group’s chatter fades into near silence as individuals drift to sleep. Some Microraptor may choose to remain alert while the group rests, quick to rouse the scurry if predators approach. Microraptor do not function in this way with their carnivore companions and are not sentries. They only react or alert to predators they must flee from.
