# Microraptor (Hanqiugi) – Nesting Site Defence

When any species intrudes on a nesting site, both scurries and pairs react in the same way. This includes species Microraptor fear. Apex Carnivores may be excluded in this at the discretion of the Microraptor. Microraptor consider a nesting site claimed once they have courted and placed a nest in their desired location. Any species that intrudes on a claimed tree or cliff edge is met immediately with loud, screeching protests, with members swooping overhead repeatedly and aggressing the intruder. Small pecks may even be aimed at the intruder in irritation. Microraptor, towards other species, may begin to harass and aggress another species if they are within one body length of their claimed area. In scurries - even if the Microraptor has not claimed the tree or cliff edge for themselves - join in on the mobbing behavior. While assisting in defending their nesting site, Microraptor do not aggress other Microraptor from their claimed site. Pairs of Microraptor exhibit the same behavior towards species outside their claimed carnivore companions, if they have them. Microraptor are unable to issue territory or nesting challenges towards other species, as their small size does little to intimidate others. Instead, the tiny carnivore relies on harassment to deter the intruder away. If the species or individual returns within a body length, Microraptor may continue to harass.
