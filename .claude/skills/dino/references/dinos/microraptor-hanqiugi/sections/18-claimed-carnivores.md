# Microraptor (Hanqiugi) – Claimed Carnivores

Pairs of Microraptor and solitary individuals may claim carnivore companions, fiercely driving out rival Microraptors from approaching the same group. This applies to the entire group of carnivores, not just a sole individual. Groups of 2+ Microraptors are generally ignored by pairs, with the understanding that large clusters of their own kind do not claim companions. Microraptor become incessantly loud to grab the attention of nearby rivals once spotted. If the rivals are interested in attempting to claim the companions for themselves, they land within two body lengths of the claimed carnivore and face off with the rival, spreading their wings wide to provoke the opposing party. If the opposing party wishes to accept, they land nearby and mimic the action - at which point, a territory challenge is issued towards the opposing pair from either side. All challenge calls, including the accepting call from the opposing party, must be issued from the ground in order to make it clear to both parties that a challenge is taking place. Broadcasts and aggressive calls must be done with the natural sound of the Microraptor - mimicked calls may not be used when issuing challenges. Microraptor who are uninterested, already have claimed carnivores of their own, or have lost the challenge, move on quickly, and do not linger in the area out of spite.
