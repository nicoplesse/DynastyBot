# Microraptor (Hanqiugi) – Courtship

Microraptor gather in the south of Big Tree Overlook to court, collecting at the giant tree that looks over The Heights. Female Microraptor take positions within the branches and large roots that surround the area, silently observing the clearing below. Male Microraptor claim the clearing below as their stage, enticing onlookers by spreading their wings wide and vocalizing towards the viewing party. They compete fiercely with each other to maintain attention. When simple shoving and aggressive vocals do not work, males take part in non physical - yet highly attractive - challenges against each other, known as a showdown. Male Microraptor fortunate enough to have no competition within the clearing continue their standard displays as normal. Once a female Microraptor has made their choice in a male, she swoops down from her branch and approaches him, who continues to eagerly display his wings for her to inspect. If pleased, she will perform a short courtship dance towards him (using the ability “Cheer Up”), with the male returning the gesture if he likes her. Female Microraptors seeking a same-sex partner partake in passive courting within the audience. Bringing forth gifts and food, they approach spectating females and present the gift, mimicking a male’s display in spreading their wings. Male Microraptors seeking a same-sex partner purposefully partake in showdowns with other males, often surprising their rival when their fierce demeanour is replaced with affection and admiration. Once Microraptors have accepted a mate, they depart the courting ground together, spending the oncoming days learning the preferences and habits of their partner. Some Microraptor females are content to share the same male, forming a temporary harem group with the male and his female mates. This only occurs in scurries.
