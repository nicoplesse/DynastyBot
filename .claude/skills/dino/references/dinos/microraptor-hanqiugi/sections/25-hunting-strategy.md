# Microraptor (Hanqiugi) – Hunting Strategy

Up to 2 Microraptor may work together during a hunt. Microraptor may opt to hunt attended or unattended juvenile or below offspring of Deinonychus, Latenivenatrix, Psittacosaurus, and Struthiomimus. Microraptor may stalk and observe these species from a distance, tucking themselves high up into trees or cliff ledges while waiting for the opportune moment to strike. While Microraptor are relatively quiet hunters, they are extremely advantageous when it comes to using their unique ability to mimic the calls of other creatures. When working within a pair, oftentimes one Microraptor will attempt to lure adults away by mimicking calls of their own kind, serving as the perfect distraction, and allowing their partner to infiltrate the nest without issue. Equally, Microraptor may observe and utilize the calls of other species in an attempt to distract their prey into moving away from their nests. Microraptor within a scurry do not intervene or attempt to assist with calls when 2 individuals are already engaged. Instead, individuals watch silently, observing and collecting information that may prove beneficial for their next hunt. When hunting Rhamphorhynchus, tactics vastly change. Microraptor become relentless, racing in active pursuit, attempting to dive and bite at the tiny carnivore before it can flee into water. Microraptor may be attracted to the sound of herbivores, seeking out any Rhamphorhynchus that are nearby them. Even Rhamphorhynchus that are latched onto herbivores may be attacked. Microraptor do not consistently hunt the same prey and will move on after a successful hunt, not willing to risk a second encounter when their presence has already been alerted to. If Lucky Feather activates during a hunt, this is considered an individual forfeit, and the Microraptor must flee.
