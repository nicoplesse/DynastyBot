# 5. Body Down Rules

**5.1** When a body drops, combat between both parties must stop immediately.

**5.1.1** When a body drops, the party who does not claim the body must move 10+ body lengths away from it.

**5.1.2** Herbivores cannot claim bodies. In an engagement between a carnivore and a herbivore, the carnivore claims the body.

**5.1.2.1** If the engagement was between carnivores and herbivores, and 2 bodies drop, only 1 body may be claimed by the carnivore.

**5.1.2.2** If the engagement was between carnivores and herbivores, and was a challenge for territory when a carnivore body drops, the carnivore group may not claim the body and must leave the POI.

**5.1.3** If both sides are carnivores, the winning side claims the body.

**5.1.3.1** If both sides are carnivores, and two bodies drop at the same time, each side claims the opposite party's body.

**5.1.3.2** If one side is wiped out, the other side claims only 1 body.

**5.2** All players that have a claimed body have body down protection. They cannot be attacked without a proper body down contest.

**5.2.1** If you witnessed any part of the hunt, you cannot contest for the body until the 5 minute timer is up.

**5.2.1.1** If third partied by an aerial species you may immediately challenge for the body when the hunt is over.

**5.2.2** You cannot ask to give up the body (or offer the body) to others in chat. You must wait to be contested, and can freely forfeit the challenge with a 2 call :friendly_call: if you wish to give up the body.

**5.2.3** You cannot wait for a player's body down to despawn in order to hunt them.

**5.3** Once you have claimed a body, all members of the group must stay with the body until it despawns.

**5.3.1** No group members may quest.

**5.3.3** You may not body chunk by taking meat chunks of the body to make it expire faster. Any chunks taken must be held by the carnivore and eaten.

**5.3.4** If a group member is at 35% thirst or lower, they may leave the body individually (ensuring at least one member remains at the body) to get water. They do not have body down protection when getting water and must return immediately afterwards.

**5.3.5** If solo with a body, and at 35% thirst or lower, you may leave the body to get water. You do not have body down protection when getting water, and must return afterwards. Your body may get claimed by others during your absence.

**5.3.6** One member of a group may take a chunk from the body to fill a nest or bring the meat to offspring that cannot leave their nest yet. You do not have body down protection when doing so, and must return immediately afterwards. You may do this repeatedly. Your body may get claimed by others during your absence if you are the only one at the body.

**5.4** Only scavengers and those with specific instances in their profile may scavenge from a body.

**5.4.1** You must make contact with the body visually to take meat or eat from the body. Aerial scavengers must be landed.

**5.4.2** Scavengers cannot be chased more than 1 body length of the corpse, and this does not count as an engagement.

**5.4.3** If you take damage from a carnivore whilst attempting to scavenge from the body, you cannot continue to scavenge that body and must move on. This does not count as an engagement.

**5.4.4** If you are killed whilst trying to scavenge, the owner of the body down claims your body as well.

**5.4.5** If you have been challenged off of a kill, you cannot scavenge from it.

**5.4.6** If you are on a timer with the species that owns the body, you cannot scavenge from it.

**5.4.7** Taking a trophy from a body that is not yours is considered scavenging.

**5.5** You cannot gift food to other species unless specified in the profile.
