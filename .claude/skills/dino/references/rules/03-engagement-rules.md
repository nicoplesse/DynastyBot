# 3. Engagement Rules

**3.1** An engagement begins when an attack move is used within one body length of a target, even if the attack does not land.

**3.2** All species can defend themselves during an engagement.

**3.2.1** Some species can have defenders. The defenders must be within 10 bodylengths of the engagement within 30 seconds of the engagement starting, and must join in immediately.

**3.2.2** After a hunt has been started, hunters must be within 10 bodylengths of the engagement within 30 seconds of the engagement starting. They must join the engagement immediately.

**3.2.3** If your species fears a tier or specific species of dinosaur or cannot hunt it due to engagement limits, you should attempt to flee if engaged by the target and cannot turn that engagement into a hunt.

**3.2.3.1** This does not apply to contests or challenges, and you may always defend your resource.

**3.3** Active defenders cannot be swapped out during an engagement.

**3.3.1** Engaged individuals that lose line of sight of the other party for 1 minute cannot return to resume the engagement. This applies to both sides of the engagement.

**3.4** All carnivore groups may only engage in 1 engagement at a time, unless specified in profile. In the case that a territorial creature is challenged by two different species simultaneously, this rule does not apply.

**3.5** You may not join a group or invite players to your group during an engagement, regardless of where they are on the map.

**3.6** Terrestrial species should not be standing in water deeper than their ankles for more than 15 seconds throughout an engagement.

**3.6.1** Terrestrial and aerial species that are being attacked by a semi-aquatic from the water are exempt from this rule.

**3.7** Semi-aquatic species may use water freely. They can escape into water from terrestrial or aerial hunters during an engagement, but dying in water as a result of injuries from the engagement is considered body denial.

**3.8** Terrestrial and aerial species cannot attack other species while either are swimming in water, to prevent body denial. This applies to both hunters and prey. The only exception to this is if these species are being attacked by a semi-aquatic species while swimming, or if a terrestrial or aerial creature damages a semi-aquatic creature with an area of effect attack.

**3.8.1** If the semi-aquatic species dies within water from being attacked in this scenario, they are exempt from body denial.

**3.9** After any engagement, a 15-minute timer applies. During this time, you cannot engage or stalk the same species. This applies to both parties. If you’ve killed and eaten but are still hungry, you can hunt a different species. The timer only affects the species involved in the previous encounter.

**3.9.1** When defending territory, territorial species are exempt from 15 minute timers when it comes to rivals. Please see individual profiles.

**3.10** If you witnessed an engagement and wish to contest for the body, there is a 5 minute timer after the engagement concludes before you can contest.

**3.11** During the 2 minute restart timer (both before and after restart), all combat must stop immediately.

**3.12** You may not use teleport commands/shop items during combat.

**3.13** Those not directly involved in the engagement must move at least 10 body lengths away from the fight, or out of reach of the engagement.  These unengaged individuals may be hunted by other predators, without it being considered third partying.

**3.14** You may not third party engagements if you have a timer with either species in the engagement.

**3.15** All typical engagement rules apply to third partying.

**3.15.1** You must be able to hunt species you attempt to third party.

**3.16** Hunters may take advantage of challenges (courtship, territory, food bushes, etc) between herbivores without it being considered third partying.

**3.17** ONLY the following species may third party during an engagement: Deinosuchus, Concavenator, Hatzegopteryx, Quetzalcoatlus, Sarcosuchus, Spinosaurus, Tylosaurus.

**3.18** Semi-aquatic and aquatic species cannot third party other semi-aquatic and aquatic species.

**3.19** Hunts can continue by the original hunters even if third partied. Hunters may also defend their hunt and themselves from third partying species if able.
