# Austroraptor – Adoption

Orphaned chicks who are able to wiggle their way into nests without detection are “adopted,” but Austroraptor does not otherwise adopt. Elderly Austroraptor begin to dull in color, their bright plumage fading to lighter shades. Unable to hunt larger prey, they are more frequently found in the water fishing, even preferencing more coastal and isolated islands. If without a pair, elderly Austroraptor are very unlikely to visit the courting grounds and attempt to court, unable to exert the energy to consistently maintain a siege.
