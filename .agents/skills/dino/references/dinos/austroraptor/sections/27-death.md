# Austroraptor – Death

If an individual in a mated pair dies while hunting, the surviving individual cries out, rushing to the body and crouching over the top of it to try to nuzzle and rouse it. If the engagement is against another carnivore or carnivores, Austroraptor is quickly forced away from the body with aggressive calls and even bites. This may even result in the death of both individuals, if the Austroraptor is not fast enough in its efforts to flee. If the engagement is against a herbivore, Austroraptor is typically permitted to remain with the body. Austroraptor often lay in this spot, completely frozen, even for a long period after the body is gone, practically blind to other threats. Mourning is an intense time for Austroraptor, and often results in a lot of stressed plucking of feathers, screeching, and crying out for a mate that will not return. This often means that an Austroraptor whom has lost their mate will never court again. Some individuals might, but are much more difficult to impress than other Austroraptor. Austroraptor who are nearing death will separate themselves from the rest of the siege before they pass. Lifelong pairs will often separate from the group together. Tucking themselves into underbrush, Austroraptor will quietly pass away from the rest of the siege. Other members of the siege do not deeply mourn or inspect elderly Austroraptor. They may notice the elderly selecting their place of rest, but will choose to move on rather than linger near the individual. If Austroraptor have freshly paired and grouped at the courtship grounds, they may defend one another. Austroraptor may form same sex pairs, though male pairs do not form temporary bonds with a female. Offspring limits may be overridden with nest multipliers; however, result in a lot of stress for both parents, and often result in brood parasitism being attempted. Austroraptor may always attempt to defend their mate from Apex tier, but must flee when granted the opportunity. Austroraptor may hunt any unattended or attended offspring, excluding sauropods (Amargasaurus, Apatosaurus, Argentinosaurus) and attended offspring of Apex carnivores (Deinosuchus, Giganotosaurus, Spinosaurus, Tylosaurus, Tyrannosaurus, Tyrannotitan). After a standoff, neither pair may issue another standoff to one another for 30 minutes. Austroraptor may mob a solitary Large with 4 members in the hunting party. These individuals must group temporarily, and must ungroup after prey has been killed or a forfeit. Austroraptor do not accept territory challenges from other species when not at their courtship or nesting grounds. They will forfeit and move on to the next POI. In the nesting or courtship grounds, up to 4 individuals may defend the entire siege's right to remain in the area. Austroraptor may hunt other players if 75% or below hunger in the courtship grounds and nesting grounds.
