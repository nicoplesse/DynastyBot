# Austroraptor – Engagement Limits

Up to 2 Austroraptor may work together to hunt up to Medium tier. With 4 Austroraptor in a temporary mob, Austroraptor may hunt solitary Large tier. Austroraptor may hunt any attended or unattended Adolescent or below individuals, unless they are sauropods (Apatosaurus, Argentinosaurus, or Amargasaurus)-- or attended by an Apex carnivore parent. Austroraptor may still hunt attended Leedsichthys offspring. Pairs may always work together to defend one another from being hunted. If in a mated pair and attacked by a tier they cannot hunt, Austroraptor may defend themselves and their mate until they find an opportunity to flee. Austroraptor do not accept territory challenges from other species when not at their courtship or nesting grounds. They will forfeit and move on to the next POI. In the nesting or courtship grounds, up to 4 individuals may defend the entire siege’s right to remain in the area. Austroraptor avoid all Apex tier carnivores and will flee if challenged or attacked by them. This excludes Leedsichthys. Austroraptor may also risk scavenging at any time. Adolescent Austroraptor do not assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Austroraptor do not hunt other species. Preferred Prey Austroraptor prefer to feed from easy kills, such as critters, fish, and crabs. They crack open shells with their claws and by smashing them against rocks. Austroraptor do not hunt other Austroraptor, but may feed from the corpses. They will not feed from the corpse of their mate. Austroraptor often prefer prey that they can pick up in their teeth and carry away, especially when it comes to feeding offspring or a partner. If wanting or needing to hunt bigger prey, they will first prioritize scavenging if available. Then, they will hunt any attended or unattended offspring of Adolescent or below (other than sauropods - Apatosaurus, Argentinosaurus, or Amargasaurus - or attended by an Apex carnivore parent. Austroraptor may still hunt attended Leedsichthys offspring). Austroraptor do not need to mob to hunt adolescent Apex tier offspring (even though they are considered Large). They may also prioritize Deinonychus and  small Desmatosuchus, Anodontosaurus, and Peloroplites that can be carried. These species are easy prey items and a source of entertainment that Austroraptor takes to drown, or smash against rocks. If these are unavailable or out of reach, Austroraptor will then target adult Medium tiers and below. Mobs will only target solo Larges and do not hunt other prey tiers. Austroraptor may also risk scavenging at any time. Austroraptor are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects.
