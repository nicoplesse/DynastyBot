# Psittacosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

This profile does have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Psittacosaurus females, known as jills, are smaller and slimmer. Suitable subspecies include: mongoliensis, gobiensis, sinensis ♂️ Psittacosaurus males, known as jacks, are larger and more broad. Suitable subspecies include: lujiatunensis, sibiricus, sibiricus (variation 1), sibiricus Physical dimorphism for Psittacosaurus is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
