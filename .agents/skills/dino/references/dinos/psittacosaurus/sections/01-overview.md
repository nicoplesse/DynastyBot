# Psittacosaurus – Overview

Small Cathemeral Terrestrial Herbivore Psittacosaurus form temporary pairbonds. Psittacosaurus are nomadic. Psittacosaurus have a passive nature. Psittacosaurus may group with: Eotriceratops, Styracosaurus, Albertaceratops, and Pachyrhinosaurus. Psittacosaurus may not defend these species, even when grouped with them. Psittacosaurus tolerate mutated individuals.
