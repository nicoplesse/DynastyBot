# Parasaurolophus – Physical Dimorphism

This profile does have high and low T individuals. Very rarely, some females may present physically as males, and vice versa. However, Parasaurolophus still regard these individuals as their biological sex. ♀️ Females, known as does, have shorter crests than males.They greet other Parasaurolophus by remaining on all fours, dipping their heads and using (:shake_emote:). Suitable subspecies include: Tubicen, Crystocristatus ♂️ Males, known as stags, have longer and larger crests that are usually brightly colored. They greet other Parasaurolophus by rising onto their back legs, lifting their heads and using (:shake_emote:). Suitable subspecies include: Walkeri, Argonaut, Campionem Physical dimorphism for Parasaurolophus is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship. Behavioral greetings are used to help determine sex. The incorrect behavioral greeting may not be used to “trick” others into believing they are the opposite sex.
