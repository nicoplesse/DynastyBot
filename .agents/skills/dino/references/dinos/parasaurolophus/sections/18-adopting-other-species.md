# Parasaurolophus – Adopting Other Species

Both does and stags may adopt orphaned Parasaurlophus and sometimes adopt orphaned Iguanodon, Lambeosaurus, and even lone  Barsboldia These species are largely similar to Parasaurolophus, and may take a single adoptee as a surrogate fawn, treating it as if they were their own offspring. The herd will only tolerate a maximum of 1 Barsboldia adoptee in the herd at any time. Once adopted, the offspring may be permitted to shield inside a defensive circle during predator threats. Adoptees no longer receive protection once they reach Sub-Adult.
