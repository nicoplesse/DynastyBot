# Parasaurolophus – Offspring Maturity

Once offspring reach Sub-Adult, they are recognized as adults within the herd and are expected to behave as such.  This includes night patrols, defensive circles, and active defending as needed. Adults tend to remain within their natal herd whenever possible, preferring to stick with those they have closely bonded with their entire young lives. However, herds will branch off if numbers get too high or resources are too thin for their personal comfort, but are likely to return if circumstances change.
