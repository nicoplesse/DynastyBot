# Parasaurolophus – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Both albino & melanistic Parasaurolophus are accepted and cared for by their herds. Albino Parasaurolophus are especially sensitive to heat and bright light, often seeking shade during the hottest parts of the day. Their delicate skin and eyes make the Dry Season particularly harsh, so small baths or frequent stops within the shade are prioritized to help them cope with rising temperatures. Melanistic individuals do not suffer any additional health concerns. However, due to their darker scales, they may be more prone to feeling the harsher Dry Season heat, seeking shade on their migration routes often or cooling off in water. These individuals might also be more prone to socialising at night due to the cooler temperature. In order to express frustration towards another para, a Parasaurolophus may do one of the following: Sneeze directly at a Para; Bite at the air at a Para; they may NOT land this bite; Kick at the air at a Para; they may NOT land this kick. These motions are only done once; and are immediately respected by the offending Parasaurolophus. A Parasaurolophus shows recognition of these corrections by moving away at least 1 bodylength.
