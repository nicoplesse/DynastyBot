# Parasaurolophus – Solo Predator Reaction

Without the protection of a herd, Parasaurolophus can become more nervous and may opt to flee or become defensive when a predator is spotted. Solo Parasaurolophus, when attacked, continue to flee or become defensive. If a Parasaurolophus opts to fight, they may move to adjust their position, but will not chase their attackers. This does not apply to Apex predators, as they must still flee from them on sight.
