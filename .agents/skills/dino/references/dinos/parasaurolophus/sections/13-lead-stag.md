# Parasaurolophus – Lead Stag

The lead stag travels at the front, using his calls and experience to determine travel routes; however, an inexperienced stag may lead the herd into dangerous or resource-poor POIs, which can cause disruption or quiet judgement of his leadership by his herd members. Holding this position offers many advantages for the lead stag. He gains favor with the does by demonstrating strong leadership, wise decision-making, and the ability to defend the herd from predators. Typically, he also holds the largest harem. This harem, who upon seeing him repeatedly paw the ground (:claw_emote:) after courtship,  will often be the first to place their nests. If a lead stag seems unfit for the role, he may be challenged for his position by another stag. This is done by the challenging stag running to the front of the herd, cutting off the leading male and stomping his feet down before him, following it with an aggressive (:threaten_call:) call. This challenge cannot be ignored by the lead stag, and must be answered within 10 seconds of the challenge being issued. Once the challenge begins, it will fall into the Rut behavior described within Courtship. The position of lead stag may only be challenged for once per migration (session). If the herd does not agree with the winning stag's choice of direction, they may split off from the main herd. Each lead stag may only be challenged once. If this position changes to a new stag, the new lead stag may be challenged by another stag.
