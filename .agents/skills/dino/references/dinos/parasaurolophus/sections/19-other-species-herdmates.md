# Parasaurolophus – Other Species Herdmates

Parasaurolophus group and herd alongside Bonapartenykus, Camptosaurus, Citipati, Iguanodon, Lambeosaurus and Struthiomimus. These species are typically similar in temperament to Parasaurolophus, and individuals frequently form friendships and bonds with them. Citipati, Struthiomimus and Bonapartenykus are favored for both their sentry duties and their resourcefulness. Bonapartenykus are particularly useful for aiding herds in finding resources, while Citipati and Struthiomimus' alertness and loud calls serve as early alarms. When not requiring to prioritize food for their young, resources are willingly shared with these species, with Parasaurolophus grazing contently alongside them. Parasaurolophus do not protect these species even when grouped with them, but may call out in sadness at the loss of a bonded individual.
