# Achillobator – Mutated Behavior

Albino Achillobator and albino Latenivenatrix are not tolerated by Achillobator. They will shun these individuals and opt to make distance, and do not return courtship or affectionate calls. Individuals with other mutations are tolerated on a case-by-case basis, dependent on the individual's preference. Achillobator are very rarely, if ever, aggressive within their own species, and prefer to avoid one another rather than directly confront one another. Achillobator are largely tolerant to all species, but form mutualistic relationships with Latenivenatrix, a smaller raptor that responds well to the larger raptor’s calls and encouragement. Achillobator do not tolerate scavengers attempting to feed from their kills, but may tolerate them in other circumstances. Achillobator often wait in hiding near nests with offspring, taking advantage of periods where parents may temporarily abandon their nests, or while the parent is sleeping.
