# Achillobator – Physical Dimorphism

♀️ Females, called vixens, have rounded tails and have distinctive ruffs of feathers on top of their heads. Suitable subspecies include: giganticus ♂️ Males, called tods, have forked tails, or a distinct lack of feathers. Suitable subspecies include: barbatus, calvus Physical dimorphism for Achillobator is mandatory. High t and low t individuals are not observed in Achillobator.
