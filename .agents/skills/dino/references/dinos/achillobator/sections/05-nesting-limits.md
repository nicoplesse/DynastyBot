# Achillobator – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 2 🥚 per 3 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 4 🥚 per 3 hours. Achillobators nest in dens, hollow trees, or deep foliage. They will often nest nearby or within Latenivenatrix creches. Achillobators may form same-sex pairs and may adopt Achillobator or Latenivenatrix.
