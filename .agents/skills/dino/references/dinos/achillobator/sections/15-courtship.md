# Achillobator – Courtship

When interested in finding a mate, female Achillobator begin to broadcast in hope of attracting the attention of others in the nearby areas. An interested male, once within her line of sight, will begin to quietly chatter in her direction to alert her of his presence. Once noticed, the male will find a nearby hiding spot if he is not already in one. Female Achillobator approach the male with slow, careful steps, often weaving between shrubs and trees with her sides facing him to close the distance. The male will continue his chattering in a show of interest, peeking out from his hiding spot. If he loses interest or the female approaches too quickly, he will fall silent. Once the distance is closed and she can inspect the male, she will do so by circling him as she inspects his scars and plumage. During this, he will call to her affectionately. Once her inspection is complete, she will return his call before running off. The male follows after her in a brief game of chase. Their game ends when the female stops and calls affectionately to the male.
