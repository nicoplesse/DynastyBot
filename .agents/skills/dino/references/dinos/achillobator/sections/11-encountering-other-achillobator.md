# Achillobator – Encountering other Achillobator

Achillobator are widely tolerant of other Achillobator, and will openly avoid one another if spotted, if not solitary and interested in courtship. If a pair is nesting, they will expose their pups to the other briefly before huffing; this is a signal to any non-nesting pair to leave the area and permit the nesting pair space. If both pairs are nesting, it is duly understood that both sides should give one another a wide berth. Achillobator do not bicker over nesting sites with other Achillobator, and will simply opt to find a different nesting site rather than starting a challenge for one. If both pairs are migrating, both pairs temporarily pause to look at one another before turning tail and heading in opposite directions. These encounters never escalate to violence for any reason. The only time that violence may occur between packs of Achillobator - or solitary Achillobator - is for competition over kills; but once the battle is fought and won, the losing Achillobator will quickly leave the area to avoid further competition.
