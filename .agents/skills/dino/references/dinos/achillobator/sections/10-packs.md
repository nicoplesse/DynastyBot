# Achillobator – Packs

A pack of Achillobator consists of a mated pair, often offspring or adoptees, and at times, Latenivenatrix. These small familial groups are extremely tightly bonded, and are often observed sleeping close to one another, preening one another, and gifting food and large critters to one another. Achillobator are often observed playing games of chase within their packs. This is especially exhibited by the mated pair, and often initiated back and forth in periods of rest. Mated pairs commonly gaze at one another for extended periods, keeping their faces close together. Achillobator are very attuned to their mate, and begin to identify their mate’s preferences in critters, types of food, and travel patterns. Both Achillobator become more comfortable as their bond continues, and over accumulated nests. Shy Achillobator often become more emboldened by their partners, and will stand up for them fiercely when provoked to do so.
