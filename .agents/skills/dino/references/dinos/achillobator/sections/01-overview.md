# Achillobator – Overview

Medium Nocturnal Terrestrial Carnivore Achillobator may take part in all activities other than hunting during the day. Achillobator form strong bonds that may turn into lifelong bonds from mutual decision. Achillobator are nomadic. Achillobator have a tolerant nature. Achillobator are not scavengers. Achillobator shun albino Achillobator and Latenivenatrix. Other mutated individuals are tolerated or shunned based on individual preference, but never cannibalized.
