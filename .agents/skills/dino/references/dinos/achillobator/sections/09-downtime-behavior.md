# Achillobator – Downtime Behavior

During rest periods, Achillobator curl up within the nesting creches of Latenivenatrix, if they are attended by Latenivenatrix or find a rookery. Otherwise, Achillobator often flock to den sites for warmth, curling up around their offspring and each other - or otherwise in piles of foliage. Achillobator typically develop preferred sleeping and resting places, and construct elaborate nests over them. Some individuals may be extremely particular about how their nests look, and who they permit to enter it. While unwelcome intrusions from non threatening species rarely escalate to aggression, it sometimes results in disgruntled sighs and huffs. They prefer shaded or dimly lit areas during the day, and follow shadows in order to stay in the shade, even as the sun shifts.
