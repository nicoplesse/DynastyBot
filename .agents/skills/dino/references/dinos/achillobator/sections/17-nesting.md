# Achillobator – Nesting

Achillobator seek out locations with suitable coverage. This is in excess foliage, inside a fallen log, or beneath the roots of tree. Once courted, a tod will lead the vixen to a presumably acceptable nesting location. If she is satisfied with the spot, she will place her nest down. She may reject the location he led her and instead bring him to a place of her choosing. Upon the eggs being laid in the nest, the female will often change between sitting on the eggs or preening her nest, often moving about meticulously placed branches and coverage to her liking. The male, at this time, will search for small rodents and other creatures he can provide his mate with while she expends her energy on the nest and brooding over their eggs. Established Pairs: In the case of established pairs, vixens indicate interest in nesting with their mates when at “pinnacle nutrition,” a term used to refer to being well-fed, well-maintained, and overall happy with her mate. She indicates this interest by broadcasting at him in the same fashion in which they met, extending her arms and showing the undersides of her wings, and flapping her arms. Latenivenatrix may assist with this process, if present. This will prompt the tod to lead her to a suitable nesting site, or the same nesting site as their first clutch, which results in the same process repeating.
