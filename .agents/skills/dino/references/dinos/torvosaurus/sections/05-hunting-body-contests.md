# Torvosaurus – Hunting & Body Contests

Torvosaurus can only hunt during the day. Torvosaurus can hunt any tier, but may only hunt up to Large tier when solo. Adolescent Torvosaurus can assist with their parents’ hunts, up to the limit of 3. Solo Adolescent Torvosaurus may attempt to hunt up to Medium tier. Only Female Torvosaurus may attempt to hunt. Male Torvosaurus do not hunt. Instead, they may attempt to contest for a body owned to Medium tier or smaller. Hunting Party Limits: Hunting parties are limited to 3 females. Solo or paired males would rather contest for a body, but may hunt as a last resort. If fewer than 3 females are in a pack, a male may temporarily join hunts until the pack reaches 3 females. Mobbing: Torvosaurus may mob following species: Ampelosaurus, Apatosaurus, Argentinosaurus Torvosaurus may form temporary alliances with other Torvosaurus to mob hunt, with a maximum of 6 Sub/Adult females between both parties. These alliances consist of separate groups and are temporary for hunting purposes only. Males may assist in mob hunts against sauropods if there are fewer than 3 females in either pack. If a pack has enough females in it already to begin a mob, they can do so without forming an alliance with another pack. Body Contests: The same restrictions apply as hunting. Mobbing is not allowed during body contests.
