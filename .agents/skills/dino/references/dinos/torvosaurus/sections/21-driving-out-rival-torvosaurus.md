# Torvosaurus – Driving Out Rival Torvosaurus

If Apatosaurus, Argentinosaurus or Ampelosaurus are in the POI, Torvosaurus will not drive out other Torvosaurus. Solitary males or coalitions remain nomadic and do not engage with other males unless attempting to take over a pack or claim females. They prefer to just move on. Only packs or sisterhoods that are full (no space for more females) actively drive out other females from the POI. This behavior ensures that resources and space remain dedicated to their established group. Males are always driven out by packs with a male, but this is handled differently, see Pack Takeovers. Full packs or sisterhoods always drive out other packs or sisterhoods. Torvosaurus in these circumstances cannot co-exist with the other Torvosaurus in the same POI. If unwilling to hunt or drive out the rivals, they must leave the POI. 1 male and 3 females may work together to battle the rivals. Torvosaurus can either opt to hunt or kill these rivals - so long as one group is forced out of the POI. If on a timer with Torvosaurus, they must permit the other group to flee the POI, rather than hunt them outright. This behavior towards other Torvosaurus can be exhibited at any time of day - including hunting.
