# Torvosaurus – Males meeting a female or female(s)

Males greet all Torvosaurus with a raised head and low growls, and are always delighted when a greeting is returned with the bowed head of a female. The female(s) begin by circling the male slowly, sniffing and inspecting him while he stands firm, lifting his head and exposing his throat to them. They carefully evaluate his size, scars and posture - signs of his capability as a protector and future mate. If they are impressed with the male, each female will settle down beside him, chuffing softly to push him on to the next challenge. Unimpressed females instead stay very firmly stood, and begin a series of aggressive roars and hissing, aimed directly at the male's face. Males may attempt to stay for a bit longer to try and convince the females, but ultimately this only worsens the mood of the females, forcing him to retreat lest they attack him to prove their point. With impressed females, the male must then move off to secure a critter for each female. This gift is a critical part of the ritual, symbolizing his willingness to push himself for the group by engaging in a series of small hunts. Failure to deliver these offerings prompts the females to lose interest, leaving him behind and ending the courtship attempt. Once each female has been presented with their gift, the final test is initiated. The male must hold both his ground and his head high as the females escalate their evaluation of him. They once again circle him, but this time deliver a series of firm and deliberate bites to him: testing his strength, resolve, and willingness to endure discomfort for the sake of the group. This is never fatal, and males that retaliate with aggression to the females - or worse, try to flee - provokes an immediate and unified response from the females. They launch an immediate hunt on the male, killing him on the spot regardless of time of day or hunger requirements. Once he has proven himself, all members of the newly formed pack call affectionately to each other and shake, settling down together. Grooming and soft chuffing solidify their bond, marking the beginning of a harmonious pack.
