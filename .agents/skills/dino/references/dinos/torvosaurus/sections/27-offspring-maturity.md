# Torvosaurus – Offspring Maturity

As they mature into Sub-Adult, fathers turn their attention to their sons, who by now are too much of a threat to his authority to remain. He aggressively sends them out of the POI and the pack itself. Mothers - who seem to have a strong soft spot for their sons - can sometimes be seen in a low mood after their sons leave, whilst others might even show signs of relief. Up to 3 daughters are allowed to remain in the pack at Sub-Adult, but their doing so prevents the entire pack from breeding. When the pack decides they wish to court again, it is the adult females that will drive these daughters out. Otherwise, they often leave on their own accord if they catch the scent of an unfamiliar male - especially if they wish to form a pack of their own. Fathers tend to bond the most with their daughters, and can be observed grieving for a short period when they are forced to leave the pack.
