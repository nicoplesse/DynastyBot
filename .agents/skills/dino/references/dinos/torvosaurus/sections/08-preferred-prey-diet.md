# Torvosaurus – Preferred Prey & Diet

Priority hunting targets for Torvosaurus are Amargasaurus. This the main species Torvosaurus target, and must be hunted first above all other species if in the POI. When Amargasaurus is not available, and mobbing larger sauropods is not possible, they prioritize hunting ceratopsian species (Eotriceratops, Pachyrhinosaurus, Nasutoceratops, Styracosaurus and Albertaceratops). If Apatosaurus, Argentinosaurus or Ampelosaurus are in the POI, Torvosaurus will temporarily tolerate another Torvosaurus pack in the area. They may form a temporary (ungrouped) alliance to initiate a mob hunt against these species. If there are two packs in the area and these sauropods are present, these species must be the primary target for Torvosaurus. When none of these species are available in the POI, Torvosaurus has no preference and may hunt whatever it is able to. Torvosaurus can hunt any tier, but Large tier or lower when solo. Torvosaurus will readily cannibalize and hunt others of their kind. See Aggressive Behavior for more information.
