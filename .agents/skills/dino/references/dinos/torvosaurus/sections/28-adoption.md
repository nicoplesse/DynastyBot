# Torvosaurus – Adoption

Only female groupings of one to three individuals are allowed to adopt. As Torvosaurus age, they begin to experience a natural decline in physical prowess. Their hunting ability and stamina in general diminishes, making securing prey increasingly difficult. Nesting becomes less and less frequent as they approach Elder age. Decreased hormones lowers the fertility of both males and females, and their interest in raising young diminishes. This can prove difficult if a male reaches Elder status but still has very active and virile females - a feat that may result in him being driven out by those females. Elder males become especially vulnerable to a pack takeover from a much younger, stronger male. Often, these males choose to willingly forfeit and wander away. In such circumstances, if a female has been with a male for a long time, and they have successfully raised 5 clutches of offspring together or more, the female may leave the POI with him.
