# Torvosaurus – Males forming a coalition

This type of grouping is only made when a solitary male finds another, or when two brothers disperse from their natal pack. This is the only time males will tolerate another male in their vicinity. Solo males will group loosely with other solo males in order to better maximise their chances of scavenging and finding kills, but these alliances are only ever temporary. Even brothers will part from each other when the prospect of females becomes available. Coalitions usually end up with a natural hierarchy - with one male asserting dominance over the other, leading to the more submissive of the pair having to wait for his turn to eat when kills are made or claimed, and deferring to the dominant male's authority. This tends to form organically, influenced by the individual personalities of each male. Unfortunately, coalitions composed of two highly aggressive and dominant males often dissolve quickly, with neither male willing to yield or compromise. These groupings are nomadic.
