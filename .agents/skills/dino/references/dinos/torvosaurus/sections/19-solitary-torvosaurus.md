# Torvosaurus – Solitary Torvosaurus

Solitary males have a lot to prove, and spend the majority of their time patrolling on the search for females to create a pack. They avoid Large and Apex tier carnivores, and prefer to utilise their strength in bullying Medium tier and below off of their kills, leaving hunting only ever as a last resort. Scavenging or contesting for bodies is the preferred choice for these males, who lack much success when forced into hunting prey. Solitary females are far more self-sufficient than their male counterparts, and are usually successful at hunting and bringing down smaller prey in order to keep themselves fed. Although solo females are able to utilise their hunting skills efficiently whilst solo, they thrive on company and are prone to loneliness, becoming increasingly lethargic over time should they not find other Torvosaurus. Being far better equipped for survival than males, they move tactfully and quietly, keeping well-hidden until they find some form of company in other Torvosaurus. Solitary individuals of both genders avoid deep rivers or large lakes to drink or travel through. Torvosaurus are acutely aware of the possibility of becoming a meal for a larger crocodilian species.
