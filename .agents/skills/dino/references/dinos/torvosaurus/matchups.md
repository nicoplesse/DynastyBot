## Besondere Risiken

- **Same-species territory risk:** Torvosaurus have strong behavioral dimorphism, where the females are primarily responsible for hunting and providing food, and the male serves to protect the pack and drive off rival males from pack takeovers.
- **Same-species territory risk:** Torvosaurus tolerate and even share kills with Thalassodromeus - bonding with particular individuals and allowing them close. These pterosaurs help Torvosaurus by alerting them of rival Torvosaurus in the POI.
- **Cannibalism / offspring risk:** Torvosaurus will readily cannibalize and hunt others of their kind.
