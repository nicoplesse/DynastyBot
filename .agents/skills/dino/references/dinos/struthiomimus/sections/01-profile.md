# Struthiomimus – Profile

Struthiomimus form harem bonds. Struthiomimus are nomadic, but become territorial when nesting and form small territories. Struthiomimus with Deinocheirus are sedentary. Albino and melanistics are tolerated by Struthiomimus. Struthiomimus may group with: Amargasaurus, Ampelosaurus, Apatosaurus, Argentinosaurus, Bonapartenykus, Camptosaurus, Citipati, Deinocheirus, Iguanodon, Lambeosaurus, Parasaurolophus, Therizinosaurus.
