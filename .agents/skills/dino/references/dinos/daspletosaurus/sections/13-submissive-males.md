# Daspletosaurus – Submissive Males

Submissive males, while unable to directly court with females, may take part in sneaky courtship, as detailed in Courtship & Parenthood. They often act as the herders in hunts for the dominant males, and benefit from getting to feed with the other males from the kill. Submissive males are often low-testosterone, but may still be typical-testosterone-level males who prefer to not lead packs. They benefit from the direct protection of the dominant male pair, but under their supervision, are unable to court, and often directly pushed away from interacting with the females in the pack. These males additionally recognize that they will have a greater struggle attempting to overthrow existing dominant pairs, and may use this submissive position as a way to sneakily court females, hopefully have male offspring, and disperse from the group with a son as soon as they are able.
