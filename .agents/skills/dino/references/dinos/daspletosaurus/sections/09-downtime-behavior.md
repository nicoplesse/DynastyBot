# Daspletosaurus – Downtime Behavior

When the pack is ready to settle down for a brief period of time, Daspletosaurus seek out shaded spots, most often during the hottest part of the day. Often in caves or beneath overhangs that provide ample shade, Daspletosaurus will nap near any closely bonded members of their pack, sometimes laying their heads on or under another's. Unwillingly to be caught by surprise, at least one member of the pack remains awake on the lookout for any potential threats. This is often a dominant male, though any member of the pack can take up this position. Resting Daspletosaurus often communicate with each other in low grumbles and huffs to keep from drawing attention to the pack at this time. Any cubs the pack may have at the time are corrected if they become too rowdy for their tired mother, who snaps in their direction. Solitary Daspletosaurus tuck themselves away in underbrush and dark corners to comfortably sleep without disturbance or risk of being spotted.
