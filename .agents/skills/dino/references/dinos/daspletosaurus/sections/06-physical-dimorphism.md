# Daspletosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

Daspletosaurus have color dimorphism, with males having concentrated and high-contrast flashes of colors on their bodies, whilst females showcase dull colors with little contrast. ♀️ Females are dull in color, with similar patterning and color schemes across their entire body. Females are more submissive than males. ♂️ Males have high-contrast flashes of color on their body - such as on their faces, necks, or backs. Males are more dominant, and lead Daspletosaurus social groups. Melanistic male Daspletosaurus are seen as female. Physical dimorphism for Daspletosaurus is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship. This profile permits high T/low T individuals. High T females are more dominant in behavior, and may have bright flashes of color on their bodies. Low T males are more submissive in behavior, and may lack color on their bodies.
