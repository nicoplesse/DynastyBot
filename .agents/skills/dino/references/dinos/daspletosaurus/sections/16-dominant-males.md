# Daspletosaurus – Dominant Males

Dominant males are the primary protectors of their pack. They will fiercely defend their pack from all incoming threats, including other male Daspletosaurus. Dominant males chase out any submissive male that shows aggression towards them or the females in his pack. This often involves the dominant males posturing at other Daspletosaurus, showing off their strength and vitality. If the threat refuses to back down, they will engage in a battle, with the victor(s) chasing out the rival(s). This behavior is mostly showcased to submissive males attempting to court or take over the pack. Dominant males will always exile submissive males or males that lose a challenge when attempting to court their females or take over their pack. Dominant males never show aggression to the females in his pack, or risk losing their interest entirely. If a male loses a challenge, or attacks the females in his pack, the females may decide to chase him out altogether, deciding that he is not a good fit for their pack.
