# Daspletosaurus – Submissive Male Sneaky Courtship

While submissive males are not permitted to court in front of the dominant male, sometimes these males take the opportunity to impress a female after she has placed her nest - just before she lays her eggs. After watching the dominant males move from the nesting site, the submissive male sneaks in, often with an offering of food, and approaches a female. Gifting her the food, he displays by lifting his head, shaking, and pacing before her. Some females, typically inexperienced, will accept the male courting her with shakes and soft rumbles. This must be done before the nesting post is made. Submissive males will only risk courting 1 female who has already been courted by a dominant male. If rejecting him, the female who is being attempted may still take the food, and then aggress or ignore the submissive male. If he is aggressed, he will flee from the nesting site before he is caught by the dominant male(s). If he is caught in the courting process, the dominant male(s) attack the submissive male until he is outside 5 body lengths of the nesting area, not allowing him anywhere near the nesting site - and is pushed to the outskirts of the area the pack roam. The dominant male(s) may take the submissive male down to death scars, but the submissive male is never killed intentionally. The submissive male flees as quickly as possible and does not aggress back other than tail attacks. Dominant males will only aggress the submissive male if he is caught near the nests. Otherwise, they do not attack or aggress the male.
