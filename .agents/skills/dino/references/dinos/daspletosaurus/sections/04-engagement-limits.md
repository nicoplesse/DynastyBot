# Daspletosaurus – Engagement Limits

Up to 3 Daspletosaurus may take part in a hunt. One individual herds prey towards the other two hunters in its party. The "herder" flushes prey towards hunters. The hunters lie in wait to ambush their prey. After the hunt has been started, the hunting party works together to herd prey, cornering them if possible. Up to 3 Daspletosaurus may assist in defense and challenges. Up to 2 Adolescent Daspletosaurus may remain within 10 bodylengths of a hunt to observe. They may not land bites, but may assist with herding prey. If attacked by a target, they will flee, and may not return to the hunt. Adolescents hide when hunted or challenged. Hunting Limit: Solitary Daspletosaurus may hunt up to Large tier. They may also hunt protected and unprotected offspring up to Adolescent of any tier. 2+ Daspletosaurus may hunt up to Apex tier. Solitary Daspletosaurus avoid Apex carnivore pairs. Solitary Daspletosaurus, without the assistance of another, will attempt to ambush potential targets, or target offspring in nests. Solitary adolescent Daspletosaurus may hunt small tier and below. Preferred Prey 1-2 Daspletosaurus hunt Lambeosaurus before all other prey. Full hunting parties of 3 target Parasaurolophus or Lambeosaurus before all other prey. If Lambeosaurus is not available, solitary Daspletosaurus will target offspring of Adolescent and below of any tier. Daspletosaurus target herbivores over carnivores if given multiple options. Dominant males or male pairs will immediately cannibalize albino offspring when noticed. Daspletosaurus may not feed from Metriacanthosaurus corpses without suffering adverse effects.
