# Maip – Once Paired

Once they pair, Maip of any gendered pairings form a lifelong bond. This pairing remains permanent, and will only dissolve if one Maip in the pair passes prematurely, or is missing for extended periods of time If one of a pair of Maip dies, the other will mourn for a long period. Some may take another mate after grieving the old one, while others prefer to remain alone for the remainder of their lives, and take on a solitary, silent life.
