# Maip – Males

Male Maip are not often aggressive by any means. Alone, they are quick to surrender their kills to challengers - especially if the challenger is female. However, once in a mated pair or a group of siblings, this changes drastically. With a duty to protect their mates, regardless of sex, male Maip become defensive and wary of strange maip. Any attempt from a newcomer to infiltrate a nesting site, challenge for food, or even the rights to a mate will be met with heightened aggression.
