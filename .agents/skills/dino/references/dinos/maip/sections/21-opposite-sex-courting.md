# Maip – Opposite-Sex Courting

Upon seeking courtship, Maip of both sexes find their way to Burned Forest or Wollemi Forest. These locations are ideal, the areas littered with boulders and hills, which serve as high platforms for females to stand upon. Maip take to this area to find potential mates, and other Maip for interactions. Female Maip stand upon these rocky formations and shriek. Their calls echo through the sparse woods, attracting potential suitor males to her. These calls often wake potential males in the POI and neighboring ones, drawing them out for courtship. Males approach the rocks she stands upon, gently giggling and cooing to show interest in the female. The taller or more impressive the rock, the more impressive the female appears. Only one female Maip may attempt courtship at a time. If multiple females arrive at Burned Forest, they must fight for the right to court, battling until one reaches deathscars. The loser is forced to leave Burned Forest, and is only able to return the following night. Once the female Maip has decided she is ready, she will dismount from the rocks she claimed to shriek from, and give the males that have gathered an encouraging laugh. This laugh signifies the beginning of the courtship ritual. The Female then sets off to find a place to settle and rest for the rest of the night, allowing interested males to follow behind her. However, this is part of the game. Males who seek to win her approval must trail behind her, stalking the female and remaining out of sight as she travels, only alerting her to himself with quiet giggles to signify his intentions. Once she finds a place to rest, she will not permit any of the males to come too close. Each male must find their own, separate place to rest nearby, to wait it out until the female moves again. Individuals - including the female - may temporarily leave their resting place to get food and water, but promptly return once they have met their needs. Once a day cycle has passed and the next night falls, the female emerges from her resting place and begins her travels - keenly aware she is being trailed by interested males. The interested males then trail behind the female, careful to try and keep themselves hidden and out of sight. Males that follow too obviously do not get chosen as a mate. Several males may often show interest in one specific female. However, they do not fight directly for her with teeth and claws. Instead, they seek to sabotage one another. Males run to their competitor, release a shrill bark, :danger_call: alert call, and run away. This is aimed to reveal to the female where their competition is hiding, giving away his position in favor of concealing his own, and diverting attention. If the female discovers a male’s position too many times, she will chase him off aggressively before returning to her place, continuing the gauntlet. This quiet, stealthy pursuit will continue the rest of the night. By sunrise, the female's game comes to an end. She will stop once she decides she has finished, and turn around to face the remaining suitors, posturing with her body held high. (This can be showcased with the ( ? ) emote.) Upon seeing the female end the chase, any remaining males emerge from their hiding places to reveal themselves. They mimic her stance, standing tall with claws outstretched to signify they have completed the female's gauntlet. If there is more than one male remaining, the female will choose which she will bond with, standing beside her chosen suitor and hissing a mild warning to the remaining male(s), who promptly aim to return to Burned Forest, and seek out a new mate. After a new pair is formed, Maip form a lifelong bond, and rapidly begin to show large swathes of affection towards each other. Females - though dominant and aggressive - show unusual tenderness to her mate, and males grow bolder when their mate or family are faced with threats.
