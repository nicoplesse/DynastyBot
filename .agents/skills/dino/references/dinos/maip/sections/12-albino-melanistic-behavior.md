# Maip – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Albino Maip are an oddity amongst a species of highly specialized, nocturnal hunters. Their pale skin is useless at night, providing little to no camouflage for either offense or defense. This leaves them open not just for predation from other species, but their own kind as well. Albino Maip are cannibalized frequently. Adult Albino Maip are extremely rare, as these megaraptorans are often born with a multitude of health conditions, such as blindness, deafness, and bone deformities. Additionally, they are born sickly, if they even survive hatching. Regardless of rarity, albino Maip follow the same behavioral traits as non-mutated Maip; cunning, intelligent hunters. However, these unique dinosaurs avoid the sunlight at all costs, as exposure to sunlight, even in small amounts, can cause extremely painful burns. They also are much quieter than non-mutated Maip; focusing more so on survival than the satisfaction of giggles and shrieks. Due to the fact that Albino Maip are often unaware of how dangerous their mutation is for them, albinos may attempt to court with others of their kind. However, this can prove fatal if the Maip they attempt to court with is hungry enough… Albino Maip are NEVER accepted as mates. Melanism is much more common in the genetics of Maip than albinism, proving to be more of a beneficial mutation than a detrimental one. Melanistic Maip often have a better time with camouflage, especially in dense forests under the cover of night due to their dark bodies. Melanistic Maip behave the same as non-mutated Maip. However, in the day, they will seek cooler areas such as water pools and heavily shaded foliage, as these Maip have a tendency to overheat easily. Due to their nomadic nature, Maip will rarely, if ever, attack each other for food or mates. Without the pressure of defending territory, they usually pass each other by. When resources are scarce, or when a skulk has offspring, other Maip are not tolerated. Due to their cannibalistic nature and the risk other Maip pose to young pups, Maips viciously defend their caves and nesting sites from others. Aggression is displayed primarily through vocal calls - with loud calls, screeches, hisses and cackles being employed to drive the other away.
