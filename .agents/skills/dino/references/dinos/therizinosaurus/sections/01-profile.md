# Therizinosaurus – Profile

Therizinosaurus form polyandrous bonds that may turn into lifelong bonds after 5 successful nests or adoptions between the same pairing. Same sex male pairs may form lifelong bonds through raising 5 chicks to adulthood successfully. Therizinosaurus are sedentary when a solitary male or in a tiding, and are territorial as a solitary female or in a loom or sect, forming medium territories.) Therizinosaurus males have a tolerant nature, unless woken up during the day, when they are aggressive. Therizinosaurus females are aggressive at all times. Therizinosaurus may group with: Struthiomimus. Therizinosaurus also tolerate Rhamphorhynchus. Therizinosaurus may defend Struthiomimus when grouped with them. Therizinosaurus shun albino individuals, and tolerate melanistic individuals.
