# Therizinosaurus – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Albino individuals live their entire lives solo, and are never territorial. Rendered even further blind by their highly sensitive eyes, they only move and roam at night, and will be aggressively rejected by any other group of Therizinosaurus, or avoided by any other solo Therizinosaurus. As a result, Albino Therizinosaurus can never nest or court. Melanistic individuals are able to group and court as usual. Some females may prefer to group with Melanistic females, resulting in longer-lasting Looms.
