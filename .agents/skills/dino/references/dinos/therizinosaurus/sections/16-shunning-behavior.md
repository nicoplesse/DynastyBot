# Therizinosaurus – Shunning Behavior

If a Therizinosaurus is Albino, acting sickly (out of profile), or loses a dominance contest, a Therizinosaurus’s first move will be to entirely ignore it, moving away from it and preserving an even larger personal space bubble. If the shunned Therizinosaurus enters this personal space bubble, even slightly, a Therizinosaurus will warn the other by issuing one aggressive call and then flexing its claws (the <-> emote). If the shunned Therizinosaurus does not immediately make distance, Therizinosaurus will give chase, targeting the other Therizinosaurus’s tail with its claws to rip feathers from it and to make it clear that it has been shunned to other Therizinosaurus, and to give a final warning for it to disperse. If the shunned Therizinosaurus does not leave, the Dame will be the one to drive them completely out of the territory, or kill them if necessary. This behavior occurs no matter the relationship between the shunned individual and other Therizinosaurus, unless they are lifelong pairbonded– in which case, the lifelong pairbonded individual would be shunned by other Therizinosaurus as well. Struthiomimus males may attempt to court female Therizinosaurus. This behavior is tolerated and at times enjoyed - but only ever recognized as playful. Females see Struthiomimus as loud, strange offspring, and while they still require their personal space bubble, view them as helpful sentries and essential to defending one's territory. Males see Struthiomimus as cute, scrawny baby Therizinosaurus, and treat them as such. Struthiomimus are defended by Therizinosaurus, and are encouraged to flee behind an adult of their choosing when attacked. Rhamphorhynchus, while helpful with cleaning parasites and bugs from beneath a Therizinosaurus’s feathers, may not always be tolerated during the daytime, especially if they are overtly noisy. Rhamphorhynchus who take to screaming during the daytime when a Therizinosaurus is sleeping will be scolded, and at times, aggressed. Rhamphorhynchus are always tolerated at night.
