# Therizinosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

This profile does not have high and low T individuals. Males and females are both dark in coloring, with no coloring differences between male and females. However, they do have physical differences. ♂️  Males, known as roosters, are stockier, with denser and thicker feathers to help with incubating young, and quills on their backs to protect from carnivores and attacks from behind while nesting. Suitable male subspecies include: Cheloniformis (quills), Cheloniformis (variant 1) ♀️  Females, known as hens, are slimmer, with long feathers and no quills, since they are responsible for monitoring the outskirts of nesting grounds and migration paths. Suitable female subspecies include: Cheloniformis (variant 3), Cheloniformis (variant 2) OPTIONAL: The matriarch of a Sect, known as a Dame, is also  more brightly colored than her female counterparts: in shades of red, orange, and splashes of white. Dames who wear these brighter colors are often preferred by males. Physical dimorphism is mandatory.
