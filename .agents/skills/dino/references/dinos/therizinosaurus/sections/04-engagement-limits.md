# Therizinosaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Aggressive/Headstrong Up to 2 Therizinosaurus may take part in an engagement. Therizinosaurus males may be the second individual to engage in challenges and defense against predators if there are not enough females in the group. However, only males who have formed lifelong bonds with females will defend them from or assist in challenges against Apex carnivores. They may choose to do this in place of other females in the group. Males may always defend their personal space from Apex carnivores. Therizinosaurus do not initiate an engagement, unless the engagement is a challenge, or the other party in the engagement gets within 3 bodylengths of the Therizinosaurus. Female Therizinosaurus respect forfeit calls from all predators except Apex carnivores. If an unmated/temporarily bonded Therizinosaurus who is a part of a sect is attacked, the second defender will always be the Dame. If a lifelong bonded Therizinosaurus is attacked, their second defender will always be their mate. Adolescents and below will flee to hide behind the nearest parent, and may be defended by them. They will not defend themself unless cornered or alone. From Sub-Adult onwards, they will no longer be sheltered by their parents, and will be expected to defend themselves. Territory challenge between Sects, Looms, and Solitary females: In a territory challenge against another Sect, only the Dames may engage with each other. In a territory challenge against other Therizinosaurus where a Loom is involved, only one female may step forward to engage. Solitary females will fiercely defend claimed territories, but will not attempt to take over a territory already claimed by a Sect or Loom. Territory challenge against other herbivores: In a territory challenge against a different species of herbivore, up to two 2 Therizinosaurus. Both females in a loom take part in these challenges. If there are not enough females in the group, a male may take the 2nd place. Solitary males and tidings do not issue challenges for territory. One adolescent female Therizinosaurus may engage if present in a sect, but will disengage at half-health. Therizinosaurus do not challenge Struthiomimus over resources. Therizinosaurus only challenge Apex carnivores for territory, but may challenge any carnivore away from their nests. Competition with Apex predators Only Therizinosaurus females, looms, or sects have competition with Apex Carnivores, and do not tolerate them in the same POI. These engagements follow territory challenge rules. If a female does not wish to engage with the Apex carnivore, she must move from the POI. She only attacks first if she is ignored for 10 seconds after issuing a broadcast and 3 aggressive calls, if the apex carnivore visibly prepares an attack move, if the apex moves towards her, or if it enters her personal space. She will not enter the personal space of the carnivore to initiate the engagement herself, or to invite the predator to attack her. Therizinosaurus males who are in lifelong pairbonds with a female may assist her against Apex carnivores. Tidings and solitary males will always opt to leave the POI after seeing an apex predator. Males may always defend their personal space from Apex carnivores. Preferred Diet Therizinosaurus are primarily frugivorous, eating berries and fruit. At times of scarce resources, they may eat carrion or small insects.
