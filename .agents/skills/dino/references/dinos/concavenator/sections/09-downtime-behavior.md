# Concavenator – Downtime Behavior

Concavenator sleep in short bursts both during the day and at night, typically curled up together with bonded individuals on banks, in shallow water, small islands, or in deep brush. They often sleep on top of piles of trinkets, both as a show of possessiveness and for comfortability (even on top of typically rough objects!). They may also share trinkets with those that they are particularly bonded with. Concavenator are often seen basking and sleeping in the sun, and swimming around in shallow water while the rest of their gang.
