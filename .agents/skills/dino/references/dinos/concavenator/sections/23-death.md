# Concavenator – Death

Though Concavenator are not overtly emotional, some mourning behaviors have been observed. If a gang member dies near the group, others linger around the body, offering quiet huffs or gentle nudges. Particularly close companions or bonded mates may lay near the body for some time, guarding it from scavengers or simply keeping vigil before eventually moving on. Trinkets left behind by the fallen are sometimes carried off by surviving members - kept, perhaps, as temporary comfort. When a Concavenator senses that their end is near - be it from age, injury, or illness - they quietly distance themselves from the group. It is not uncommon for an elder to retreat to a favored basking site, especially near water, where they remain until passing. Others might leave entirely, wandering off during the night to find a secluded place to die alone. Concavenator do not have leaders in their groups, and follow any individual that has proven to be experienced, or knows their routes well. Despite being semi-aquatic, Concavenator spend most of their time on land, only returning to water when fishing, playing, diving for trinkets, cooling down or basking on their shores. They also use water during hunts and escaping from dangerous situations. Concavenator has no courtship grounds, but returns to Sharptooth Marsh specifically to build their nests and produce offspring. Most of Concavenator fights are personal space defence. Concavenator may hunt other players if 75% or below hunger in the nesting grounds. Concavenators' feeding strategy is chunking bodies. This is mandatory. The feeding order is first-come, first-served. Concavenators do not challenge eachother for feeding rights, they must chunk and eat while holding the meat. The chunks must be eaten, and cannot be left lying around or gifted to other Concavenators that are present. Albinos must be aggressed or cannibalized on sight. Even offspring that shed into a color that looks like Albinism will be devoured when identified. Hoarding - Concavenator with a trinket will readily defend their personal space if another enters within 1 body length. Hoarding is required in this profile. Edible items do not count as trinkets. Personal Rivals - These fights are not considered challenges or hunts and are fought as 1 on 1 battles. If a personal rival survives the initial engagement, they must be kicked from the group if they lose. Upon seeing the personal rival next time, the process is repeated.
