# Concavenator – Physical Dimorphism

Physical dimorphism for Concavenator is mandatory. This profile does not have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Females, known as jills, have bristles along their body, clustered on their back and arms. Suitable subspecies include: Spinatergus ♂️ Males, known as jacks, have no bristles and have more pronounced humps over their backs. Suitable subspecies include: Corcovatus, Carcharus
