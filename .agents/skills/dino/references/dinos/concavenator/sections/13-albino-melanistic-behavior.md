# Concavenator – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Albino offspring are cannibalized by the entire gang once their lack of pigmentation becomes visible. Those that manage to survive into adulthood face constant hostility or are immediately cannibalized upon being spotted. They live an extremely solitary life, never given the opportunity to socialize or court others of their kind, and are consistently met with aggression whenever they encounter other Concavenators. As a result, they end up adapted to a solitary lifestyle and tend to seek out the company or territories of Spinosaurus, the only species that typically tolerate their presence. Melanistic individuals receive no hostile treatment, and neither do they display any behavioral differences from others of their species. They are generally treated as equals among their kind, facing no discrimination or unusual behavior from others. In some cases, their darker coloration is even seen as a desirable trait in potential mates, offering superior camouflage in the shadows - a valuable advantage for stealth and hunting.
