# Concavenator – Driving away personal rivals

A personal rival to a Concavenator is another who has repeatedly stolen or invaded their personal space. If a personal rival is within a group a Concavenator joins, the individual will attack their personal rival on sight, attempting to drive them out of the gang for good. These fights have high consequences, and often result in death. Upon spotting their rival, aggression is dirty and escalates to physical attacks quickly. Nearby Concavenator distance themselves and watch keenly, mates and offspring pace, hopeful their partner will survive the battle. These interactions end in the death of a personal rival, or successfully driving them out of line of sight from the group. Even if a rival gives up with submissive wailing, the attacker will pursue until the rival is promptly driven out of sight from the group.
