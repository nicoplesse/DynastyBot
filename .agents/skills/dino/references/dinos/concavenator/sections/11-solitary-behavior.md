# Concavenator – Solitary Behavior

When alone, Concavenator become more cautious of others. They patrol their surroundings regularly, guard their kills and hoards more fiercely, and are notably less tolerant of intrusions - especially around feeding areas or if approached while inspecting their stash. A solo-grown Concavenator tends to be more aggressive and reluctant to share space or resources, and less forgiving of social missteps compared to those who matured in the company of other Concavenators. They also avoid approaching or being spotted by grouped up individuals outside of Sharptooth Marsh. Those looking to join others of their kind often make their way to Sharptooth Marsh, a common meeting and nesting ground for other Concavenators, hoping to be accepted into an existing gang or to form a new one with fellow loners.
