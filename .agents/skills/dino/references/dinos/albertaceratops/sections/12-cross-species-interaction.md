# Albertaceratops – Cross-Species Interaction

Albertaceratops act as sentries when herded with Pachyrhinosaurus, Styracosaurus, and Psittacosaurus. The dominant pair take turns standing guard over their sleeping herd mates during the night and watching over the young while their parents rest. Other boars assist the pair in sentry duty, taking it in turns so that no more than 3 Albertaceratops are keeping watch at any one time. They are often the first to spot a threat and alert their herd to the intruder. Albertaceratops protect all individuals that they herd with, and form cross-species defensive circles. Young cubs see playmates in other herd mates and readily run headfirst toward the species, wanting to chase and play with them. This play continues until the Albertaceratops get tired or its mother or the dominant boar calls them back to the sett. Albertaceratops are aggressive toward scavengers.
