# Albertaceratops – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Defensive / Headstrong Unlimited Albertaceratops in the same sett may form a defensive circle or flee. They stand their ground in the circle, but can move as far as 1 body length forward from the circle to land a hit, before resuming the formation. Up to 4 Albertaceratops may actively engage threats outside of the formation, which include chasing down to land hits - but not further than line of sight of the herd. These 4 defenders may chase the predator even after the predator forfeits until they leave line of sight of the herd. Other species they group with may be defended inside the protective circle, or accepted to join the formation. Psittacosaurus of any age may be actively defended. When no young are present: Albertaceratops flee from Apex predators and move away from other ungrouped herbivore species if contested off food bushes. When offspring (including other ceratopsian offspring) and adoptees are in the group: Albertaceratops are aggressive and capable of defending themselves and their herd from all carnivore and herbivore threats. Additionally, Albertaceratops are possessive and the entire sett may claim bushes to push rival herbivore species off their chosen food. Albino and melanistic individuals are never defended by the herd. Preferred Diet Flowers, nuts, roots, fruit.
