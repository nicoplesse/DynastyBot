# Albertaceratops – Courtship

The dominant pair descend into the cove, with the dominant boar bringing the dominant sow gifts of food. Together, they lay over the  nest, keeping each other company and showing affection. After eggs are successfully laid, the dominant boar retreats to the outside of the cove to monitor the other boars. Only victorious boars - those who have won a dominance battle against another boar this nesting season - are permitted the right to approach the other sows. The rest of the sows lay in wait in the cove, resting on top of their nests. Boars enter, one at a time, bringing a gift of their own to grant to the sows. If a sow is receptive to an incoming boar, she will pick up the gift he drops by her, or turn away pointedly to reject him. Sows can accept the advances of multiple boars and offer split paternity in their nests, if they wish. Boars that wish to nest with multiple sows must leave the cove and return with multiple gifts - at least one for each sow he hopes to impress.
