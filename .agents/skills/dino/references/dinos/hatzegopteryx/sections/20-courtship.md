# Hatzegopteryx – Courtship

Hatzegopteryx migrate to Red Island when they are ready to seek a mate, either in a flock with the rest of their siblings or independently. While it might not be immediate upon adulthood, all Hatzegopteryx will fly to Red Island at some point in their life, if not only to observe. This includes mated pairs that have already found their partner. Male Hatzegopteryx are the ones to initiate courting. They initially find a high up place to roost and begin calling loudly. These calls are loud and shrill, but are seemingly attractive to females, who observe them carefully. With more than one male in the area, males sometimes engage in combat with one another, much to the delight of the watching females. This is initiated with much posturing and aggressive calling, before both males take to the skies, where they swoop and dive at one another, landing heavy attacks by slamming their bodies against each other. The loser is the first one to reach half health, or to leave the air and land somewhere - whether that is by crash landing, or safely gliding down to perch. Males that win these battles are seen as extremely attractive by females, and have a much higher chance of securing a mate. Loser males, however, lose a lot of social status after losing one of the aerial courtship battles, and are largely overlooked by most females. They may redeem themselves by attempting another aerial battle against another male, or are destined to be one of the last to be picked by females. Appreciative females approach the winner of the aerial contest, affectionately calling at him and hovering in the air above him so that he can admire her. If a male has multiple females interested in him, all of them will circle above him, gliding gracefully through the sky. Then - it is reliant on the female to impress him - by bringing him a gift. These can be the scraps of a hunt, critters, or shiny and interesting items. The male will choose his potential mate based upon the gift that she brings him. Picking up the gift she gave him, he will take to the skies - where the female immediately lifts off to join him. The pair fly together in sync in an almost choreographed dance. They climb up to a great height, and then suddenly dive together, hurtling themselves towards the ground and only stopping just before they crash. Pairs where one or both individuals crash into the ground dissolve immediately, with both Hatzegopteryx (or just one, if the other died on impact) returning back to seek courtship in another potential partner. Once this first initial joint flight is completed, the pair spend time grooming and showing affection to one another. Afterwards, they leave and head back to the mainland, in order to scout out a good location to claim as territory together and build a nest. Those interested in same sex pairs court somewhat differently. They instead fly high above the lake in Red Island, calling out and showing their prowess in a combination of swoops and dives. Interested individuals observe them at first, before flying up to circle around them, calling affectionately. If the other accepts, they too will climb high up into the air to perform the very first courtship dive. If successful, and with no injured parties, the new pair will form and settle somewhere to show affection, before leaving the island to claim territory.
