# Hatzegopteryx – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 3 🥚 per 3 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 1 🥚 per 3 hour. Hatzegopteryx nest in high up places in their preferred POIs such as cliff faces, hilltops and mountains.. Hatzegopteryx may form same-sex pairs, but may not nest & will adopt a maximum of 1 orphaned offspring.
