# Hatzegopteryx – Downtime Behavior

Hatzegopteryx do not fly for far distances at night, but may do so to find AI corpses and bones to feed from, or critters to hunt. Hatzegopteryx pairs with offspring take turns going to hunt for their offspring; this looks different for every pair, but neither individual stays up the whole night. Hatzegopteryx prefer to find high places to rest. They are at their most vulnerable when sleeping, so these roosting spots are fiercely protected, and commonly the same places a pair nests within their territory.
