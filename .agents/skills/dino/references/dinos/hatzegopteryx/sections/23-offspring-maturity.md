# Hatzegopteryx – Offspring Maturity

Once their chicks reach adulthood, they will be affectionately dismissed, with parents working together to feed their chicks one final time before they disperse. Their favored child will remain with them beyond this point if there is not already an older sibling in the group, and is allowed to remain a good chunk into their adulthood - or until they leave willingly to find a mate of their own. Parents eagerly seek out their former chicks during annual migrations to Red Island. Though they are otherwise aggressive towards them when maintaining a territory - these annual occurrences provide a rare opportunity for families to meet again, and they tend to show each other high levels of affection. When an adult offspring is present with their parents and the parents nest again, the older sibling will assist their parents in bringing food and watching over their younger siblings.
