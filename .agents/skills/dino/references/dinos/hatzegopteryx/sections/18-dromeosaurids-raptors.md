# Hatzegopteryx – Dromeosaurids (Raptors)

While Hatzegopteryx will hunt just about anything, they have a strong preference for raptors such as Deinonychus, Latenivenatrix, and Utahraptor. Hatzegopteryx who hunt these raptors can be considered great hunters, and therefore prime mates. Freshly made pairs will often fly to Whistling Columns, where Utahraptors can be seen en masse performing their own courting, and may attempt to hunt a Utahraptor together. If they succeed, a lifelong bond is far more likely to develop between the pair. Hatzegopteryx have to be careful of others of their species that may have claimed territory here, and can only do so if hungry. Solitary Hatzegopteryx - keen to avoid others of their kind except when courting - prefer to avoid Whistling Columns if there are others nearby, never knowing if they are encroaching on territory and thus could face aggression. Hatzegopteryx happily swoop down into a pack of Deinonychus and pick the most brightly colored of the bunch to swoop up into the air with. Deinonychus are viewed as delicacies, and are presented as treats to mates and chicks. Hatzegopteryx enjoy terrorizing Latenivenatrix, dropping them from great heights. While one Hatzegopteryx will pick up the Latenivenatrix, the others will wait their turn to swoop down and catch it from the air until the terror or the repeated clamps kill it.
