# Pachycephalosaurus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: and ☀️ Dry Season: 4 eggs 🥚 per 2 hours. When nesting, Pachycephalosaurus females isolate themselves and nest separately from others within caves, on cliffs and rocks. Only Pachycephalosaurus females may form same-sex pairs, and may adopt. Males do not form same-sex pairs. Yearlings are considered immature and cannot participate in courtship or nesting.
