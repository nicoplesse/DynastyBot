# Pachycephalosaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Flighty / Defensive Pachycephalosaurus may have unlimited  in an engagement. Pachycephalosaurus only defend their group members. When an engagement begins - the group can defend on the ground, but should flee to a cliff, rock or cave if there is a viable opening to do so . In all engagements: hatchlings, juveniles, adolescents, and *yearlings cannot participate - and must flee or hide. They cannot fight back. Territorial Challenges & Nest Defence: Unlimited Pachycephalosaurus may initiate and respond to challenges over small territories which consist of a chosen rock, cave or cliff-face during any activity schedule. When their nest/cliff/cave/rock is approached or they are approaching a chosen territory, Pachycephalosaurus only show aggression if the rival character(s) is within 10 body lengths of thier chosen territory. Challenges between leaders in the band, and males vying for the lead buck position are always fought as 1 on 1 engagements. See more in Aggressive Behavior. Preferred Diet Berries Pachycephalosaurus may also consume carrion and bones. They do not eat fresh meat, and do not hunt critters or other species.
