# Pachycephalosaurus – Orphaned Behavior (Wild Spawn)

Without the attention of a mother, orphaned kids will pester another Pachycephalosaurus nearby in hopes of being adopted. If none are in the vicinity, the orphan will call out in hopes of being noticed. When the pangs of hunger grow unavoidable, the orphaned kid is forced to roam in search of food. Instinctively, kids will try to eat anything they can carry. It's during these early days of survival where an orphan will learn what food types are filling and what are not. Cautious and alert, Pachycephalosaurus frequently scan their surroundings. When threatened, they prefer to flee vertically - climbing swiftly to elevations that provide safety. Raised rocks, cliffs and cave structures are their ideal perches and once atop or within the safety of these areas, Pachycephalosaurus are fiercely territorial and readily push away anything that investigates closer. Horn-scraping, stretching and trotting along migratory paths are common daily rituals that maintain hygiene and muscle tone.
