# Pachycephalosaurus – Yearling Behavior (Wild Spawn & Nested)

Pachycephalosaurus grow to their adult size fairly quick, but mentally mature much slower than their bodies. Before transitioning into full maturity, Pachycephalosaurus kids experience a short time as yearlings before becoming fully independent and shedding into their adult coloration. 🌟 After spawning as a new Pachycephalosaurus, players must first pick the DR Yearling skin. They must keep this skin equipped until they complete their full adult growth stage. Only after fully grown, may the player shed into another skin. It is optional to shed out of the yearling skin once reaching full adult - you may stay as a yearling for longer than the required time. Like awkward teenagers, yearlings are paired with the carefree bliss of a juvenile and the lanky body of a fully grown Pachycephalosaurus. Their days are spent learning, following behind adults and tussling with other yearlings. Reaction to aggression: While some may be boisterous and demanding, a corrective attack from an adult frightens even confident yearlings. Recognizing their imminent danger, yearlings opt to scurry out of harm's away, quick to cry for help and seek comfort from another reliable adult they are familiar with. Predator reaction: Whether in a group or alone, yearling Pachycephalosaurus always flee from threats when attacked. A yearling might take part in initial aggressive displays, but the moment the interaction becomes physical, yearlings abandon their once confident guise and flee - often crying out during their escape. Yearlings who grow up in isolation are eager for socialization and are always accepted by any Pachycephalosaurus they encounter - even lone males, who mistake them for adult females.
