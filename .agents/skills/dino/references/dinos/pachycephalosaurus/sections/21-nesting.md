# Pachycephalosaurus – Nesting

Once arriving at a favorable cliff, the nesting female isolates herself on a ledge of her choosing. Nesting females and females with young are referred to as "nannies".  If the nanny is a leader, her presence alone typically frightens residing females off the ledge that she approaches. If a female doesn't move, the nanny will challenge for the right to nest on that spot. Once a ledge or spot in a cave is secured, the nanny will construct her nest. Decorations of these nests vary from female to female - but a common structure is seen amongst nannies when there are more than one in the band. To protect her young from stray females looking to climb the social rankings, a nesting nanny will cover her nest with large objects to conceal her eggs from view.
