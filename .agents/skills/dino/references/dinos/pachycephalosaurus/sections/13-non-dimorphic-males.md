# Pachycephalosaurus – Non-Dimorphic Males

Joining a band Non-dimorphic males, lacking the bright coloration of males, are subdued due to their mutations, and infertile. These males have no desire to breed, or assert themselves within band groupings. Instead, they approach a band purely for the protection a group provides. Approaching a band, the non-dimorphic male calls softly and, being perceived as a female by other Pachycephalosaurus, are accepted in without hesitation or fuss.
