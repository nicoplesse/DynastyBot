# Tylosaurus – Nesting

After successful courtship, bulls return to their rovers or matriline, and cows return to their matriline - or form a new one on their own. Bulls have no involvement in the raising of their own offspring. The mothers of the matriline lead the group to nesting spots. These spots are typically those where the mother was born herself - a secluded area with shallow, warm water. Within a matriline, all Tylosaurus help nesting cows construct their nests, providing support to their family members in this exciting - and relatively short - period of time. Bulls within a matriline often prefer to remain on the outskirts of the nesting areas, allowing their sisters, aunts and mother the space to build nests and forge bonds with their young offspring. While nesting, all members of a matriline become more defensive of their personal space and offspring, and do not tolerate the presence of other carnivores.
