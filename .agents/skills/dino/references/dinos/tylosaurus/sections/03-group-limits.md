# Tylosaurus – Group Limits

Up to 6 Sub/Adults, all related; consisting of a mother, her adult children, and/or her adult grandchildren. Unlimited Offspring Unlimited Adoptees This group is known as a matriline. Bloodlines are required to group as a part of a matriline. Offspring must take the name of their mother's bloodline. These bloodlines must be logged in  🩸・bloodline-logs . You MUST have been nested in or adopted (with bloodline proof) to prove relations. Adopted individuals may remain within their matriline, and must modify their bloodline post to indicate they were adopted. They may not court within their natal matriline. Up to 4 Sub/Adult females, all related. No offspring/adoptees. This group is known as a sisterhood. Bloodlines are required to group as a part of a sisterhood. These bloodlines must be logged in  🩸・bloodline-logs . You MUST have been nested or adopted (with bloodline proof) to prove relations. Up to 4 Sub/Adult males of any relation. No offspring/adoptees. This group is known as a rover. Up to 3 matrilines or sisterhoods, regardless of relations between matrilines, may migrate together. Up to 1 rover may also follow. They will not feed from each other’s kills, will not group in game, and will not assist other matrilines,  sisterhoods, or rovers in hunting or defence. This group is known as a pod.
