# Tylosaurus – Death

Tylosaurus mothers have been observed refusing to leave the bodies of deceased calves, constantly licking and nuzzling them in attempt to rouse the baby. This puts movements of entire matrilines on hold, until the body is gone or claimed by another predator. Tylosaurus who are at the brink of death often accidentally beach themselves, or sink deep to the bottom of the ocean, unable to gather the strength to unbeach themselves or swim back to the top. Despite the gentle nudges from the others in their matriline, the dying individual does not move, exhausted. This often results in dismay from the closely bonded members of their matriline - who quickly settle by their side, only leaving to go get air or to attempt to bring the dying Tylosaurus food. Once the individual has passed, Tylosaurus quickly disperse from the body, and refuse to eat from it, calling mournfully. These calls are heard loudly and for far distances. When the Tylosaurus realizes that its summoning calls are to no avail, they begin to swim away, either dispersing from their matriline entirely or leaving with their offspring.
