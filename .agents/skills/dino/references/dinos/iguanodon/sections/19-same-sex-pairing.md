# Iguanodon – Same Sex Pairing

Iguanodon can form close bonds with individuals of the same sex. These bonds are loosely formed with some individuals preferring the companionship of another, rather than engaging in nesting. Individual preference of the length of these bonds depend on many factors and individual personalities, with some preferring to remain as a pair and raise orphans side by side. Same sex pairings like these can be temporary relationships or last for a long time - with some even being permanent.
