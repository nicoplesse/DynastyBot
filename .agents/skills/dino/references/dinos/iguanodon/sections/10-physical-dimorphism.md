# Iguanodon – Physical Dimorphism

Iguanodon have color dimorphism. This profile does have high and low T individuals. Individuals differing from the typical dimorphism will not act outside of their gender's temperament. ♀️ Females are known as cows and have a more desaturated coloration. ♂️ Males, known as bulls, have at least one area on their body that contains a bright flash of color, contrasting with darker areas of their skin. Physical dimorphism for Iguanodon is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
