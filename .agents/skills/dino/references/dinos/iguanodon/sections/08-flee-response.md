# Iguanodon – Flee Response

Solitary Iguanodon flee from 3+ Large carnivores or 1+ Apex carnivores. Solitary Iguanodon of Adolescent or below will flee from 1+ Large or higher tier carnivores. Otherwise, Iguanodon do not flee when grouped with their own kind, unless success appears unlikely. This judgement may vary across individuals. Preferred Diet Berries, Flowers, Nuts and Roots. Flowers and Roots are their favourites. Berries are prioritised for calves. Young Iguanodon are only able to digest berries and do not expand their diets until they grow.
