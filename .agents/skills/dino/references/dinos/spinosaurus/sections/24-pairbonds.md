# Spinosaurus – Pairbonds

Pairbonds take time to form. If unbroken due to incompatibility, they often last a long time - if not for life. Some Spinosaurus may find themselves without their pairbond and return to Triad Falls or Wollemi Forest, taking interest in another partner to fill the void. Spinosaurus communicate primarily through body language - ranging from gruff headshakes to display affection :shake_emote: , to shrill, aggressive screeches :broadcast_emote2:  to signal displeasure. Bonded individuals spend considerable time side-by-side with one another, and eat, patrol territory and protect each other from threats. Pairbonds, while sometimes lifelong, can be broken. Infertile clutches, death of offspring, failure to defend the territory, a mismatch in temperaments and continued resentment leads to strain, and ultimately a pairbond disintegrating.
