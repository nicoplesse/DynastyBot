# Spinosaurus – Evicting rival Spinosaurus from the territory

Spinosaurus bonds are strong, but not unbreakable. When broken, an individual is either abandoned by their pairbond, or challenged out of the territory with force. Challenges for their water territory begins with violent displays of aggression and splashing within the water. Loud, thundering roars, claw swipes and aggressive displays warn the rival that the individual is ready for conflict. If deterring a rival, a bonded pair works together to display before approaching, presenting a united, overwhelming front against the threat to their territory. If the rival refuses to retreat, the encounter escalates into a deadly battle either on land or in water, continuing until death - or the other fleeing the territory. Spinosaurus territory owners may continue attacking the fleeing rival, but must stop once they leave the territory borders. Pacing at the edge of their territory, the winner watches closely, making certain that their rival is forced (10 body lengths) from the water's edge. After a challenge, the losing individual or pair is forced to move on.
