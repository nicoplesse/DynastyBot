# Spinosaurus – Engagement Limits

Hunts: 1 Sub/Adult Spinosaurus Spinosaurus may hunt up to and including Apex tier. Spinosaurus may only hunt from their preferred territory. See profile map.  They may hunt Leedsichthys, Tylosaurus, and fish and eat critters anywhere. Adolescent Spinosaurus do not assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Spinosaurus do not hunt other species. Defense and territory challenge: Up to 2 Sub/Adult Spinosaurus Protecting young and adoptees, their partner when attacked or defending/challenging for territory. Crocodilian rivalry: up to 2 Spinosaurs in a pairbond may work together to kill Deinosuchus and Sarcosuchus if spotted within their territory. Avoidance: Sub/Adult Argentinosaurus & Apatosaurus are never hunted or challenged out of Spinosaurus territory. Cross-species Adoption: Spinosaurus can adopt Suchomimus of any age. A pair or solitary Spinosaurus will defend their adoptees when they are targeted. This includes intentionally preventing their adoptive offspring from engaging in hunts or challenges. Preferred Prey Hunting in any POI: Fish, ai corpses or critters, Leedsichthys, and Tylosaurus - though this is risky, and offspring are preferred. Hunting in Claimed Territory Only: Deinosuchus and Sarcosuchus are hunted first. Otherwise, up to Apex tier prey.
