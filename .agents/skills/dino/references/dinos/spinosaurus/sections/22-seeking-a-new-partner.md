# Spinosaurus – Seeking a new partner

Within Triad Falls or Wollemi Forest, Spinosaurus look to find another to form a bond with. Some Spinosaurus are highly selective when choosing a mate, and take their time assessing potential partners. When they spot a partner with desirable traits, they approach cautiously. Walking towards the other, they shake their sails, and angle their head to the side. Direct, head-on approaches are avoided, as they are received as aggressive gestures, and may provoke defensive displays or conflict. Once they reach their intended partner - and are not aggressed away - both individuals settle beside one another with soft calls. They exchange neck shakes and vocalizations, utilizing this time to inspect the other more closely, as well as get a feeling of their overall temperaments to see if this partner is a good match. If the pairing seems incompatible upon closer inspection, one or both of the Spinosaurus' will simply rise up and wander away - a clear sign that a grouping is unlikely here. Should both individuals find mutual comfort in one another's presence, the pair will leave Triad Falls together, swimming off to find territory.
