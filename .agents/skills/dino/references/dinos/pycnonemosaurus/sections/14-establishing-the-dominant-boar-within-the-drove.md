# Pycnonemosaurus – Establishing the dominant boar within the drove

To establish which boar is the dominant boar, boars within a drove will sometimes initiate a dominance fight with one another. If one boar is very clearly more dominant than the others, they will accept him in the position. If two are butting heads often, they might prefer to fight it out. Boars will square off with one another, circling while snapping at the air. The fight consists purely of shoving one another, face-on, until one boar shoves the other far out of the space that the fight initiated. The losing boar will back off when he is pushed far enough away (5 body lengths) and accept his position as subordinate. Dominant boars hold their status until the next courting event has taken place, and once shoats reach Sub-Adult and sows are considering accepting courting advances - the boars may re-initiate a dominance fight. Pycnonemosaurus have a curious nature, and short attention spans. They follow whatever catches their attention and do not hesitate to approach other species to watch from a short distance away, or display towards them. After some consideration, watching a prey species may turn into a hunt. Pycnonemosaurus enjoy a chase, and will run down anything that flees from them, purely for fun! If they catch up, they turn away, finding the species boring if it is no longer running. Pycnonemosaurus rarely back down when met with a threat - they adopt the mentality of high risk, high reward and fiercely defend themselves and one another when met with aggression. Due to their tendency to never shy away from confrontation, it is only when it looks like all might be lost will they reluctantly retreat. Pycnonemosaurus typically have bold and outgoing personalities, they have little fear of other predators. It is only if an individual is solo that he or she will fear Apex carnivores, and flee from them. Small tier carnivore are ignored and only engaged with for defensive purposes. They are simply too entertaining for a Pycnonemosaurus to bother hunting. Carnivores larger than Small tier are seen as prime targets, and hunted as a priority over herbivores. Pycnonemosaurus tolerate scavengers as long as they have access to the corpse. Pycnonemosaurus will always target other carnivores first if both carnivores and herbivores are seen in the POI. They spend much of their lives teasing herbivores and following along curiously, but carnivores are an exciting challenge and are always the favored hunting targets. With their strong legs and endless energy, Pycnonemosaurus are endurance hunters, possessing the ability to run down their prey, biting at their flanks to weaken them before catching up to launch a full attack. Up to 3 Adol+ Pycnonemosaurus may enter an engagement or hunt. Individuals below the age of adolescent do not count towards the limit.
