# Pycnonemosaurus – Death

Solo Elders are often easy targets for predators, and call out with confusion for their drove to protect them. When no one comes to their aid, they sometimes.. give up. They stop fighting back and allow nature to take its course, reflecting briefly on all the joys they have experienced in their youth, and welcoming the safe darkness of unconsciousness. Pycnonemosaurus are very playful and can often be seen bobbing their head at other species, following them around for a short period of time. Pycnonemosaurus will chase anything that runs from them.
