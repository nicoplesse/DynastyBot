# Pycnonemosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

This profile does have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Females, known as sows, lack the horns and spikes males have, and are usually duller. Suitable subspecies include: Imperator ♂️ Males, known as boars, have horns and spikes, and flashier coloration. Suitable subspecies include: Nevesi, Taurinus Physical dimorphism for Pycnonemosaurus is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
