# Pycnonemosaurus – Engagement Limits

Up to 3 Adolescent+ Pycnonemosaurus may enter an engagement or hunt. Individuals below the age of adolescent do not count towards the limit. Solo Pycnonemosaurus flee from Apex carnivores. In a group with no adults present: Adolescent Pycnonemosaurus may hunt up to Large tier if they are solo, or any tier if grouped with 2+ members.
