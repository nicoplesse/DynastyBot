# Pycnonemosaurus – Downtime Behavior

Pycnonemosaurus often take short naps in open spaces. They do not often build nests as they will quickly move on from these resting locations at the sight of a threat or prey moving elsewhere. Pycnonemosaurus shoats will play while their parents sleep, often with much noise and scuffling, which can frustrate especially tired adults. Pycnonemosaurus will sleep multiple times in a full day cycle, often in short bursts immediately after a robust period of play or an attempted/successful hunt.
