# Pachyrhinosaurus – Death

When a member falls - whether to predators, injury, or natural causes - nearby Pachyrhinosaurus often remain in the area instead of instantly moving on. If the death is due to natural causes, bonded individuals briefly investigate the body before moving from the corpse a safe distance away. Pachyrhinosaurus males who have claimed a bush in their nesting grounds are territorial and aggressive to everything that is not female Pachyrhinosaurus, their shieldmates, or Psittacosaurus. When challenged by another species, only the male and his shield members (3 related males total) may accept the territory challenge. Blind Pachyrhinosaurus have the eye color blue. Players with blue eyes are expected to roleplay being blind. Bumping into things and if male, mistaking approaching females as rival males. Players with the subspecies associated High T females and Low T males must roleplay the behaviours associated with these imbalances.
