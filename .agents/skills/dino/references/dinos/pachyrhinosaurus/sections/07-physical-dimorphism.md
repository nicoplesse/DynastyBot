# Pachyrhinosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

Pachyrhinosaurus has mandatory subspecies based physical dimorphism. This profile does have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Females, known as cows, have no horns on their crest. Subspecies to use: Horneri Achelousaurus inspired, Canadensis V.1 / V.2 / V.3 ♂️ Males, known as bulls, have protruding horns on their crests. Subspecies to use: Lakustai V.1 / V.2 / V.3 and Lakustai retro & battle damage ⚠️ Low T and High T: Low testosterone bulls have the smallest horns protruding from their crest. Subspecies to use: Perotorum V.3 High testosterone cows have a quilled tail and a singular, tiny horn protruding from their crest. Subspecies to use: Perotorum V.1 / V.2 Blind: Both males and females can become blind. This is optional. A male or female can become blind during extreme head trauma, or if carrying the blindness mutation. When a Pachyrhinosaurus becomes blind, their eye color changes to blue.
