# Pachyrhinosaurus – Downtime Behavior

Pachyrhinosaurus spend their downtime grazing and lounging, finding particularly soft spots in grass, mud, or sand. Both males and females sleep to try to keep their energy up. They primarily rely on Psittacosaurus to act as sentries for them at night. Pachyrhinosaurus will scratch themselves on rocks and trees in order to clean themselves. Males are known to be impatient, scratching their heads against terrain and trees to release stress. Pachyrhinosaurus herds yawn often during downtime- and these yawns are contagious!
