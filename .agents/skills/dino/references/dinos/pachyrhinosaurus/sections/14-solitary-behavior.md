# Pachyrhinosaurus – Solitary Behavior

When alone, Pachyrhinosaurus are nervous. When scenting or spotting a predator, the individual will always try to flee or find somewhere defensible to stand near, rather than engage immediately. They migrate in search of companions, specifically other ceratopsian species they herd with. As a solo, they are quiet, often unheard while they travel between nesting grounds in search of company.
