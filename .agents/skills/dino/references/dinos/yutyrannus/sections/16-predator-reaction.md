# Yutyrannus – Predator Reaction

When stalked by another carnivorous species, a Yutyrannus quickly respond to their stalker with aggressive calls and broadcasts - alerting the rest of its group to the presence of its hunter. Yutyrannus who have the time to respond to a hunt will gather closely together, screeching and finding methods in order to make themselves larger and intimidate the predator away. If they are unable to see the hunt coming, or a hunt is already initiated, a Yutyrannus’s first instinct is to bite or claw at its attacker before using its tail to shove them away and make space. Yutyrannus in a group quickly rush to one another’s defence, with those in lifelong mated pairs defending each other the most fiercely, and at times even darting in to take strategic blows for their mate. Yutyrannus remain in constant communication when being hunted, watching one another’s body language for indications of what angles to take and when to run. If the fight poses too much risk to continue, a Yutyrannus group may attempt to flee; and will often split in several separate directions in order to make it more difficult for the hunter to choose a target. Yutyrannus may hunt up to Medium tiers when solitary, Large tiers when in a pair, and Apex tiers when in a group of 3 or more. They may have up to 4 individuals in a hunting party. When in a hunting party of 3 or 4, they prefer to hunt Tyrannosaurus, Giganotosaurus, and Tyrannotitan. This is not mandatory. In the Dry Season, Yutyrannus packs will target sleeping, injured, albino, or adolescent and under individuals before all other prey, not wanting to overexert themselves in a hunt. Yutyrannus sometimes initiate their hunts via ambush– with two individuals working together to spook prey towards the remaining two members in their group– or through charging in all simultaneously, working together to bait attacks and land bites on joints and tendons. Yutyrannus are highly strategic and careful within their hunts, and prioritize exhausting their prey and gradually chipping away at their endurance rather than an efficient kill. While young Yutyrannus take time to learn this lesson, the most experienced Yutyrannus know not to over-exert themselves. Yutyrannus may use up to 4 heal calls throughout the duration of one engagement, applied across the entire hunting group. Typically, these heal calls are managed by one individual in the party, with exceptions made when necessary.
