# Yutyrannus – Death

Elderly Yutyrannus who are ready to pass often do so in the comfort of one of their favored resting or nesting dens, typically isolated from the others in their clowder or colony. Often, elderly lifelong pairbonded individuals will pass together, curled up beside one another in their dens, entirely refusing to leave one another’s side. If one of the pair passes before the other, the surviving half of the pair refuses to leave their mate’s side, even for food or water, or the calls of their family or groupmates. If a Yutyrannus dies to a predator or prey, whether an adult or a kit, the other bonded  members in the group all erupt into ferocious wails. If able, they will rush to and claim the body, nuzzling it frantically in an attempt to wake it up. Upon realizing that nothing they do will rouse their fallen groupmate or family member, they will rest their heads nearby or on top of the body, and the entire group will go eerily quiet. This period of eerie silence may continue for days after the loss of a bonded individual. Lifelong pairbonds will often grieve their lost mate for the rest of their lives, especially if they have shared multiple clutches. Individuals who have lost their lifelong pairbonds may often entirely refuse to court again - but may do so after a few seasons, once the loss has settled. Yutyrannus have different behaviors varying on season. While more sociable during rainy, Yutyrannus have a tendency to be more aggressive during the dry season. Yutyrannus in clowders and colonies are playful towards other members of their group. Full groups are territorial and will challenge other carnivores of small tier and above in their POI. Groups who are nesting will challenge any small tier and above carnivore who approach within 5 Yutyrannus body lengths of the nesting site. Yutyrannus have a preference for ambushing their prey, but this is not mandatory. Yutyrannus become nocturnal hunters in the dry season, but may partake in all other activities during the day, including body challenges. Yutyrannus may use a maximum of 4 healing calls throughout the course of an engagement. Yutyrannus may only have 1 egg in dry season. This can be increased through multipliers.
