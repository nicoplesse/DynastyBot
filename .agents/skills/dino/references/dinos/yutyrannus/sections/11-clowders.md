# Yutyrannus – Clowders

Clowders, groups of up to 6 Sub/Adults, are all related individuals, or related individuals and their lifelong bonded mates. All closely bonded, they work together and share resources, including the room deepest in chosen dens that they stay in throughout the dry season. The parents of the majority of the individuals present, or the most respected and well liked, lead the clowder in hunting and choice of den. They are also the only individuals allowed the privilege of nesting. If a different pair in the group nests, they are quickly shoved from the group and the den, forced to move kits out of the territory even if they are not ready to leave the nest. All Yutyrannus in a clowder show a high degree of tolerance for the others within their group; and any individuals uncomfortable or aggressive towards another individual will quickly depart from the group, even risking the heat of dry season over being stuck with individuals they cannot stand. Clowders will not accept other groups or individuals to enter their dens, and will quickly issue challenges for them– detailed under Aggressive Behavior.
