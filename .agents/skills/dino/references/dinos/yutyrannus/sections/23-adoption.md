# Yutyrannus – Adoption

Yutyrannus individuals or same-sex pairs only adopt if they have no kits currently, and will only do so in the rainy season. Young mollies who are broody but have not courted with a tom are often the easiest route for kits to be adopted. A molly may have kits with an adoptee present, but cannot adopt if she already has her own kits present. Elderly Yutyrannus become grumpier and quieter as they age, preferring to find a quiet place away from younger Yutyrannus rather than continuing to socialize. This especially applies if they have a partner who is similar in age to them. Elderly Yutyrannus enjoy basking in sunbeams, and often fall asleep there so deeply that they do not recognize the presence of predators or prey. Elderly Yutyrannus often go blind or deaf in their age, and require the assistance of their partner or a bonded individual to guide them in their day to day lives. Elderly Yutyrannus who are blind and deaf become incredibly stressed moving through or into areas they do not recognize. Elderly Yutyrannus have even less of a tolerance for heat, and may entirely refuse to hunt prey larger than critters in the dry season. Elderly Yutyrannus may stop changing their colors from the rainy season to dry season.
