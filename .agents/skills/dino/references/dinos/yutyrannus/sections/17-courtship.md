# Yutyrannus – Courtship

Dry Season: In the Dry Season, Yutyrannus nest rarely - and only with individuals who they have already parented clutches with, producing one egg. Toms perform dances exclusively for mollies they have already paired with, too tired from the heat to perform otherwise. Rainy Season: Yutyrannus may opt to form colonies of unrelated individuals in order to nest and court. Pairs who have courted and nested before may choose to remain together. These groupings (especially of young adult Yutyrannus who have never courted or nested before) tend to be quite flirtatious with one another and entirely playful. Mollies flirt with toms by gently batting him or flicking him with her tail, nuzzling up to him and darting away, or crouching and moving into his space or getting in his way. Toms always respond positively to this– unless he is not interested in the female for any reason, and in which case he will politely turn his back. Mollies may be slightly possessive over males whom they have courted or nested with in the past towards other females, but this never escalates into a physical fight– just into an exchange of loud calls and shrieks into one another’s faces, until one submits and backs off. Mollies flirt with males they believe to be the strongest hunters, or those with favorable feather patterning. Continuous flirting in this manner towards responsive toms typically results in temporary games of chase: in which, the molly leads the tom towards another tom. She will then initiate flirting with the second tom, who if interested, will respond positively. The first tom will respond to this with a series of chortling grunts (claw emote) and posture at the opposing male. If the opposing tom wishes to challenge for the rights to nest with the molly, he will issue his own grunts in return, posturing back at the other male. The molly will then step aside to watch the challenge. Same-Sex Pairs: Females flirt and chase with other females, and males perform for one another if interested in a same sex pairing.
