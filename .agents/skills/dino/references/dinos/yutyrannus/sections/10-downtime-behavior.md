# Yutyrannus – Downtime Behavior

Yutyrannus are chronic nappers, and enjoy finding sunny places or even spots in the rain where they can take naps with other individuals in their group. In the Dry Season, this is especially prominent, with hunting parties only hunting under the cooler cloak of night. Yutyrannus take time to meticulously construct their nests, especially when it comes to regular sleeping places. Even mated pairs may build their own nests - but will at times share them together, tucked together, with their tails draped over one another’s backs and sides.
