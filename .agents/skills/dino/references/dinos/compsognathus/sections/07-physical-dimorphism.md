# Compsognathus – Physical Dimorphism

Compsognathus have color dimorphism. This profile does have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Females have more uniform and muted colors. ♂️ Males tend to have brighter, flashier colors. Physical dimorphism for Compsognathus is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
