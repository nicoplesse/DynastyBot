# Compsognathus – Downtime Behavior

Compsognathus are nearly constantly on the move, only requiring several brief naps throughout the day to keep their energy up. On colder days, Compsognathus are seen moving more sluggishly and napping more often. Compsognathus enjoy napping close to their swarm or nearby carnivore companions. They often take their most substantial naps immediately after feeding in order to help their tiny bodies digest.
