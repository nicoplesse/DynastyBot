# Compsognathus – Engagement Limits

Up to 10 Compsognathus may take part in an engagement. Compsognathus flee from Hatzegopteryx and Quetzalcoatlus. Compsognathus may hunt up to Small tier, but primarily scavenges.  Compsognathus will target unattended hatchlings & juveniles, Dryosaurus, Psittacosaurus, and death-scarred individuals of any tier. Solo Adolescent Compsognathus do not hunt other species. When in dispersal group: 2-10 Adolescent Compsognathus may attempt to hunt up to Small tier, but primarily scavenges.
