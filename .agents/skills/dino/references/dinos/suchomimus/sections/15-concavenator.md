# Suchomimus – Concavenator

Concavenator, at all times, are viewed with disdain by Suchomimus. Suchomimus perceive these smaller species to be a threat to any potential adoption between Spinosaurus and Suchomimus, and Suchomimus prefer to chase these species out whenever they spot them. Suchomimus may also hunt Concavenator from time to time.
