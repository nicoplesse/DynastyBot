# Suchomimus – Snare

Suchomimus stick to a group of 3 sub-adults/adults, which can be comprised of any sex combination, known as a snare. Some siblings may choose to band together throughout adulthood, splitting away only if there isn't room for an unrelated third that they may court with. Otherwise, completely unrelated individuals can form snares, as well as a mated pair and an adult offspring.
