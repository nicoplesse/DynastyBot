# Suchomimus – Adopted by Spinosaurus

Suchomimus that manage to ensnare a Spinosaurus into providing for them become highly territorial of their "claimed" Spinosaurus. They do not tolerate any other Suchomimus in the area - besides those in their snare - and aggressively chase them off to defend their right to remain. This also extends to Concavenator, which Suchomimus perceive to be a threat to their position with the Spinosaurus. When adopted by Spinosaurus, Suchomimus do not defend the Spinosaurus, but instead expect the Spinosaurus to defend them when attacked or hunted by others, which the Spinosaurus will gladly do. Spinosaurus provide a comfortable life for Suchomimus, including provision of food and protection, but this comes with some costs. Spinosaurus always intervenes in any engagements between Suchomimus and other species - which often leads to repeated failed hunts for a Suchomimus snare. As such, Suchomimus slip away from their adopted Spinosaurus when seeking to hunt, often taking to wandering far in land so that they can hunt undetected. When hunts are successful, Suchomimus are quick to try and devour a corpse before their adopted Spinosaurus finds out and comes to share in the kill. However, if their "parent" finds them, they begrudgingly make room for the Spinosaurus to feed alongside them. Though Suchomimus never hunt Spinosaurus - when adopted by one, they will try to drive out orphaned Spinosaurus in the vicinity, viewing them as a threat to their position. Sub/Adult Suchomimus in full snares will attempt to drive off orphaned Suchomimus before they are able to reach the Spinosaurus.
