# Suchomimus – Preferred Prey & Diet

Suchomimus readily eat fish when available. Suchomimus, in addition to fishing, are adept hunters - they stalk and kill other semi-aquatics in their vicinity, and happily take to land to hunt down terrestrial dinosaurs. Suchomimus may hunt up to Apex tier, but do not hunt Spinosaurus. Suchomimus do not actively cannibalize each other, but will eat from a body of their species.
