# Suchomimus – Scavengers

Suchomimus may tolerate scavengers, but only if they are full. Suchomimus are devious and opportunistic predators who always choose the path of least resistance: whichever outcome is the easiest, and most likely to result in success. Suchomimus prefer hunting in a team of three if they have a full snare. As fairly devious individuals, they may use one member of their snare to pretend to bask on the water's edge, enticing other carnivores to come and attack whilst they wait below the surface to lunge. Suchomimus readily target the crocodilian species: Deinosuchus (only if they have 3 sub-adult/adults in their snare) and Sarcosuchus. Suchomimus may combine ambush hunting (especially if by the water's edge, and able to surprise an attack on a thirsty dinosaur) with endurance hunting. They travel in land after their prey without limit, and will pursue for as long as they feel is necessary in order to get a successful kill. Suchomimus are adept at hunting both on land and in water - and have no preference for when or where a hunt starts. Utilizing their long, sharp claws, they slash and tear at their struggling prey. Some Suchomimus take to setting up elaborate traps near water for their prey. Small fish are sometimes placed just by the shore in an attempt to lure in other species. Suchomimus never hunt Spinosaurus.
