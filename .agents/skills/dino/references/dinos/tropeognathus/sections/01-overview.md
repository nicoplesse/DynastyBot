# Tropeognathus – Overview

Small Cathemeral Aerial Carnivore Tropeognathus hunts exclusively at night, but may perform all other activities at any time of day. Tropeognathus form harem bonds. Tropeognathus are nomadic. Tropeognathus have a tolerant nature. Tropeognathus may scavenge from claimed corpses at their own risk. Critters and abandoned corpses may be freely eaten. Tropeognathus tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.
