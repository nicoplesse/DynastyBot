# Tropeognathus – Notes

Albino Tropeognathus cannot nest in the Dry Season. Albino Tropeognathus lay 1 egg in the Rainy Season. Male Albino & Melanistic Tropeognathus never find a partner and are always perceived as females. Tropeognathus may only hunt other players if 35% or below hunger in the courtship grounds. In the nesting grounds, they may hunt at 75% hunger.
