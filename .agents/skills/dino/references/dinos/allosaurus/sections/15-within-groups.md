# Allosaurus – Within Groups

While rare, Allosaurus may express aggression towards their own packmates. Disagreements may split apart a group temporarily, or at times, permanently. Aggression can spark in response to a variety of conditions, though commonly occur from having their personal space invaded too frequently, stress that comes with raising offspring, discomfort in unsuitable temperatures and environment, or hunger. These spats are typically kept to posturing and hisses, and tail swings directed in the offender's space. Occasionally, they may swat at each other with their claws, with continuous actions causing the other individual to react in a similar fashion. These disagreements do not severely injure any individual involved. Only a small number of attacks will intentionally land before both Allosaurus move away. Over-aggression within a group is not tolerated, as Allosaurus are quick to shun and separate from those they do not get along with. An over-aggressive Allosaurus may be challenged out of the POI if they fail to leave the group alone after being exiled out.
