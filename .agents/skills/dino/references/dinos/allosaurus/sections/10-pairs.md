# Allosaurus – Pairs

Within pairs, bickering and spats rarely occur. These groupings usually consist of a courted or currently courting Allosaurus, and such individuals learn to control their outbursts as to avoid rejection. Frequently chattering and purring when in one another's company, a pair is relatively quiet in absence of the other. Trophies from kills and scraps of food are left for the other or exchanged directly. These acts of sharing and gift-giving contribute immensely to accelerating their courtship process. Allosaurus in pairs often initiate games of play with each other, chasing and nipping at each other's tails. Newer pairs often practice stalking and hunting critters together, building an understanding of each other’s strategies, and simulating how coordinated they will be in a hunt.
