# Allosaurus – Same-Sex Courtship

Allosaurus interested in same-sex courtship display the same courtship behavior as an opposite-sex pair. One individual will begin to follow and pursue the other to display their interests. These pairs cannot nest, but female pairs may adopt. Orphans adopted by female pairs may be considered related. If adopting multiple orphans, a female pair may select a favored child to keep around, much like an opposite-sex pairing would. Same-sex pairs may still allow a third unrelated individual to group with them.
