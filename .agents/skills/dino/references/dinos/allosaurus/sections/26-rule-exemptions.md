# Allosaurus – Rule Exemptions

Allosaurus are permitted to travel between POIs during dawn and dusk. Allosaurus are also able to challenge other Allosaurus for rights to a POI and to defend their territory during the daytime. 1.4 This does not permit Allosaurus to stalk prey outside their active hours. Allosaurus are able to challenge other Allosaurus in unpreferred POIs if looking to hunt there. 1.5 When adolescent Allosaurus forfeit a hunt and are then being hunted, or are fleeing from an infanticide attempt, 1 parent may step in to defend the offspring, regardless of when the engagement began. This parent is acting as a defender and must stop once the other side forfeits. 3.2.1, 4.8.2.3 Allosaurus do not gain a species timer on their own species after a territory challenge. 3.9 If adult Allosaurus who have offspring are challenged out of a territory, and the offspring are not nearby or capable of following the parent out of the POI, then the offspring may be treated as a separate group. 6.7.2 Allosaurus that have adults and adolescents within the same group may have up to 2 simultaneous engagements - with adolescents hunting separately from the adult group. In the case that both parties receive a body down, they must each remain with their own body down. 3.4
