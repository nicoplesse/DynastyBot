# Allosaurus – Coalitions

With coalitions consisting of a pair and their favored offspring, the selected offspring enjoys the attention both parents lavish them in. Trophies are often left to this offspring, who proudly carries it around for as long as they remain interested in it. Coalitions consisting of siblings and unrelated individuals are much more competitive with each other, often frequently bickering over trophies, scraps of meat, and rushing to claim the best sleeping spots. If one Allosaurus lays in a spot another wishes to have, it is common that they will sit on top of or near the individual in an attempt to get them to move, effectively acting as an inconvenience to the other. In Allosaurus groupings, coalitions are the loudest, often getting into spars and posturing matches between each other. Sparring between Allosaurus is swift and decisive, continually moving around their opponent as they swipe claws and teeth at each other’s sides. These scuffles can start for any reason, though are often engaged with the purpose of testing each other’s strength - or to settle a dispute. To begin a spar, Allosaurus position themselves in front of their chosen opponent and stare them down, clawing at the air menacingly as they hiss out aggressive calls. Should their actions be mirrored by the other, a spar will begin after a short period of posturing. Spars conclude once one individual reaches half health, or decides to move away and disengage before then. Some Allosaurus do not accept these challenges and instead turn their back and ignore the aggressing groupmate, often sitting in place to convey their refusal. This gesture is always respected by the other Allosaurus… despite the frustration it can cause.
