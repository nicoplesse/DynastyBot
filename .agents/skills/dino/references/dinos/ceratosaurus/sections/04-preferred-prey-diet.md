# Ceratosaurus – Preferred Prey & Diet

Rather than hunting living prey, Ceratosaurus will always go for prey that has already been killed. They love a challenge, and will contest for the rights to the body. Contesting for a claimed body can be done at any time of day regardless of activity schedule. If a claimed body is present in the POI, Ceratosaurus must challenge for this body before initiating a hunt on other prey, if they are able to. Ceratosaurus can hunt up to Large tier if the pack numbers fewer than 4 adults. Ceratosaurus can hunt Apex tier only when there are 4 adults in the hunting party. Ceratosaurus, however, will prioritise Albertaceratops and Nasutoceratops, or Medium tier and below dinosaurs, if within LOS. Ceratosaurus may feed from the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects.
