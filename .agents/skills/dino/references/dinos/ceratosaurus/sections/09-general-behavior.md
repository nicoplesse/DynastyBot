# Ceratosaurus – General Behavior

Ceratosaurus strongly prefer woodland habitats and forest clearings. They patrol the perimeter of whichever POI they are currently staying in, and if prey is scarce they will move to another POI - unless nesting. Patrols are not always accompanied, as Ceratosaurus enjoy solo patrols as often as grouped ones. They keep in touch with one another through soft grunts while in close proximity, and broadcast loudly when distanced from one another. Ceratosaurus will roam a POI for hours at a time, constantly on the lookout for prey. The smell of blood immediately lures in Ceratosaurus, and already wounded prey tend to be their first target. At dawn, the pack gathers at a chosen sheltered location known as a den-site. They sleep in close proximity to one another through the daylight hours, and if offspring are born they are raised at this site. Ceratosaurus will often clean their entire head in a pool of water to ensure their crests and nose horns are clean and ready to attract the attention of others.
