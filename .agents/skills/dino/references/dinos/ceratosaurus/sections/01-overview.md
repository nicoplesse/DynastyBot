# Ceratosaurus – Overview

Medium Nocturnal Terrestrial Carnivore Ceratosaurus form temporary pairbonds. Ceratosaurus are sedentary. Ceratosaurus have a tolerant nature. Ceratosaurus may not scavenge from claimed corpses, and must contest for a body. Critters and abandoned corpses may be freely eaten. Ceratosaurus tolerate melanistic individuals, but males will never be courted. Albinos of either sex will be shunned.
