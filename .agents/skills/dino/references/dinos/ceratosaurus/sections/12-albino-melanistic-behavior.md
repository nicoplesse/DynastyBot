# Ceratosaurus – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Albino Ceratosaurus are shunned and viewed as sickly individuals. Only those born into a pack are allowed to remain within the pack if there is room, though are the first to be pushed out. Albino Ceratosaurus are not accepted into packs and are never courted. Melanistic Ceratosaurus are viewed as female, regardless of gender. Males are never courted due to their lack of a bright crest. Females face no issues during courtship.
