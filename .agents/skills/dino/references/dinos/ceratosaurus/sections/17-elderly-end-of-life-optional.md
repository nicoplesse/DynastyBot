# Ceratosaurus – Elderly & End of Life (Optional)

Ceratosaurus are tough and resilient dinosaurs, rarely showing much slowing down as they age. They may experience hearing loss and increased fatigue, but no slowing of movement or loss of vision. As Ceratosaurus age, their skin displays more scars, showing their long life spent in battles with opponent and hunting dangerous prey. What were once striking, complex patterning with bright colors, fades into muted tones and indistinct patterns. Elderly pack members focus more and more on the whelps in the pack, growing fond in their old age, and can be found snoozing in the den-site while listening to youngsters playing with one another. Older Ceratosaurus take longer and longer to wake from sleeping, as their hearing isn’t as good as it was in their youth and they feel more tired. Pack members show concern to this behaviour, gathering around the Elder to nudge and call at them to help wake them up. With their growing concern, pack adults are less willing to allow an Elder to go on patrols alone, or spend any time alone at all. This is for fear that the Elder does not hear the broadcasting calls when the pack communicates across the POI. When an Elder finally passes away, the pack gather together to mourn their dead. They spend time sleeping next to the corpse, and will defend it from predators and scavengers until it decays naturally. Pack members mourn their dead from a distance, if a member dies as part of an engagement or failed hunt. They do not defend carcasses, and respect the opposition that managed to kill them.
