# Ceratosaurus – Orphaned Behavior

Orphaned whelps are very flighty without the safety of a pack. They stick to dense undergrowth and heavily wooded areas, and tread cautiously. Orphans are keen to seek out a Ceratosaurus pack to join. This must be done carefully as adult males will kill stranger-whelps if discovered in the open. If the orphan manages to reach a den-site and sneak up to pack-offspring, the adults will not be able to tell it apart from their blood relations. They treat it as one of their own, and adopt it without question. Orphaned Ceratosaurus are only adopted if the pack have similar-age offspring already present. An orphaned Ceratosaurus that reaches adulthood without a pack may behave differently from those who have been raised with one. They remain flighty and will take pieces of food quickly, retreating to a "safe" distance before coming back for another.
