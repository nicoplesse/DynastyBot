# Eurhinosaurus – Color Dimorphism

Outside of the breeding season, they shed their skin regularly depending on their environment, changing their color, but not patterns, to better fit into their surroundings and hide from predators. Both males and females shed into bright colors during nesting season, sometimes also changing their skin patterns. Females prioritise blue skins, while males prioritise green skins. Color dimorphism for Eurhinosaurus is mandatory, the changing of environmental camouflage is optional, however courting color changes are mandatory.
