# Eurhinosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

♀️ Females have shorter noses and a heavier build than males. Suitable subspecies include: communis. ♂️ Males have longer noses and slimmer builds than females. Suitable subspecies include: longirostris and pelagicus. Subspecies dimorphism for Eurhinosaurus is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
