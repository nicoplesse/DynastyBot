# Eurhinosaurus – Overview

Medium Cathemeral Aquatic Carnivore Eurhinosaurus form promiscuous bonds. Eurhinosaurus are sedentary. Eurhinosaurus have an aggressive nature when resources are low, but are otherwise tolerant to other species. Eurhinosaurus may not scavenge from claimed corpses and must contest for a body. Critters and abandoned corpses may be freely eaten. Eurhinosaurus tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.
