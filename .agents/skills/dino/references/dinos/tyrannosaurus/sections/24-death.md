# Tyrannosaurus – Death

When one of a pair dies, the other is usually quick to follow. If both are elder, the remaining partner does not take another mate, but younger individuals may do so after a brief grieving period. Tyrannosaurus form lifelong, monogamous pairs with the opposite sex only. Males must court females with food offerings or are rejected. Tyrannosaurus will cannibalize unrelated young of Adolescent and under. Tyrannosaurus only respect forfeits from other Tyrannosaurus if they are visibly making an attempt to flee the POI, and are repeatedly heart :friendly_call: calling or fear :danger_call: calling. If the losing Tyrannosaurus stops, eats, drinks, quests, or etc on the way out of the territory, the winning Rex may kill them. If Tyrannosaurus fails an ambush against carnivores, and the carnivores begin to hunt the Tyrannosaurus, they may defend themselves, but may not continue their own hunt. Should these carnivores forfeit the hunt, Tyrannosaurus will respect the forfeit call, ending the engagement. Male and Female Tyrannosaurus that are actively courting may not engage the same prey within 15 minutes of the other, regardless of whether the engagement (hunt or challenge) was forfeited or won, unless the other party is an Apex Terrestrial Carnivore. Tyrannosaurus must challenge out all other Apex terrestrial carnivores from the POI. Additionally, they do not receive a species timer for challenges on Apex terrestrial carnivores after a challenge. 6.2 Tyrannosaurus should not immediately try to hunt the species they have just challenged from the POI, unless the other is on deathscars. 6.5 Tyrannosaurus cannot  co-exist in a POI with other Apex terrestrial carnivores. They must either challenge for the right to remain in the POI, or move to a different one. Tyrannosaurus may not interrupt the hunts of other Tyrannosaurus, Giganotosaurus, and Tyrannotitan, or any other carnivore to issue a territory challenge. If unwilling to wait for the hunt to conclude, they may seek out new territory. Tyrannosaurus do not hunt Giganotosaurus or Tyrannotitan unless at desperate hunger (35%), or if the rival is Albino, deathscarred or aged Adolescent and below. They may always eat from their corpses. Tyrannosaurus do not hunt Tyrannosaurus, unless the rival is Albino or aged Adolescent and below.  If they kill them following a challenge, they can eat from their corpse. Tyrannosaurus ignore body downs in order to challenge Large+ tier carnivores from its territory. If the aggressor is successful in the contest, then they claim the body down as well as the territory. This challenge is a territory challenge, not a body contest. 6.7.4 Tyrannosaurus are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects.
