# Sarcosuchus – Engagement Limits

Up to 3 sub/adult Sarcosuchus may enter an engagement or hunt. Sarcosuchus flee from Apex carnivores when on land. Sarcosuchus do not flee Apex carnivores when in water. Sarcosuchus may hunt up to solitary Large tier when on land. Sarcosuchus may hunt up to Apex tier when in water, or if the prey is within 2 Sarcosuchus body lengths of the water. Sarcosuchus may third party an engagement if either side come within 2 body lengths of the water the Sarcosuchus is already in. Sarcosuchus tolerate scavengers. Adolescent Sarcosuchus do not assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Sarcosuchus may attempt to hunt up to Medium tier. Adolescents and younger do not participate in hunts unless they are in a dispersal group with no adults present. In a group with no adults present: Up to 2 Adolescent Sarcosuchus may hunt up to Medium tier. Adolescent Sarcosuchus may only begin hunts in water, or if the prey is within 2 Sarcosuchus body lengths of the water. They cannot hunt when on land at any time. Adolescent Sarcosuchus cannot third party an ongoing engagement.
