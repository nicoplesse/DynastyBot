# Sarcosuchus – Adoption

Females never adopt unrelated offspring, and instead see them as potential prey. Any unrelated Sarcosuchus of adolescent or below may be freely hunted by adult females. Males do not take this stance, and instead are quick to adopt unrelated offspring if there is no female present in their float. They approach any lost orphans of adolescent or below and call affectionately to them, drawing them into their group for protection.
