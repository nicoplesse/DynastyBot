# Sarcosuchus – Preferred Prey & Diet

During the day, Sarcosuchus hunt only from the water. Hunting engagements must be initiated either within the water on swimming prey, or on prey that is within 2 Sarcosuchus body lengths of the water. When hunting from the water, Sarcosuchus may hunt up to Apex tier. If they land a hit and their prey runs in-land, they may pursue indefinitely after them. If not, they must forfeit the hunt. At night, Sarcosuchus may also travel onto land to hunt prey. When hunting on land, Sarcosuchus may only hunt up to solitary Large tier. Sarcosuchus does not hunt healthy adult Deinocheirus, but may attempt to hunt unprotected Deinocheirus offspring, up to adolescent. Should an adult return to protect the offspring, Sarcosuchus will forfeit the hunt. Unhealthy Deinocheirus are death-scarred or albino. Sarcosuchus does not hunt adult Spinosaurus. Sarcosuchus prefers smaller prey that they can seize in their jaws, clamping down and dragging them deep into the river depths to drown.
