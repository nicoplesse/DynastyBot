# Sarcosuchus – Dry Season Territorial Behavior

Sarcosuchus will not tolerate other floats in their stretch of the water during the Dry Season. Solitary individuals will ignore other solitary individuals, but become aggressive if the other encroaches on their space. Full floats always push out other Sarcosuchus, unless in a same sex group and encountering an individual or more of the opposite sex. If uninterested in courting and nesting, they continue to push the float out. Other floats and individuals are given warning calls first - 3 x :threaten_call: - before a vicious attack is launched, ending only when the others leave the POI, or beat them in battle. Should the float not wish to push the other group out, they must leave themselves. Mud Pools Mud Pools are the "mud" resources scattered across the map. Mud pools are defended from other Sarcosuchus - including groupmates - and even other species in the Dry Season. Sarcosuchus valiantly defend their prime mud pool, and only begrudgingly forfeit if they reach less than half health. Prime or abundant spots in the Dry Season are highly desirable for Sarcosuchus, and it is common for spats to break out among members of a float for the best pool. When all male floats encounter female(s) When an all male float detects the presence of a nearby female, they scramble into the water in an attempt to find the largest fish to present to her. The female will inspect her offerings, choosing to form a new float with up to two males that impressed her the most. The decision is entirely hers, and males respect the decision the female makes. When all female floats encounter male(s) The sight of a male - or all-male float - causes chaos within an all female float. As males scrambled to locate a fish to present to them, a bloodthirsty brawl breaks out between females. Only 1 female may form a float with up to 2 males, and even previously affectionate female companions may turn on each other to secure their choice of mates. Battles are quick but brutal - ending only when one member forfeits or reaches deathscars. The winner approaches the males that impress her the most and forms a new float with them. Should there be a third male left behind, the other female(s) may opt to group with him. Another battle must break out if two females remain to determine which female gets the right to form a new float. Orphans Male Sarcosuchus always attempt to adopt orphaned Sarcosuchus of adolescent or below. Females, however, see them as potential prey, and will hunt them if hungry enough.
