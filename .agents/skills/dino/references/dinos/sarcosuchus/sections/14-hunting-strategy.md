# Sarcosuchus – Hunting Strategy

Sarcosuchus are ambush hunters and always attempt to conceal their presence until they are able to land a bite. Sarcosuchus adopt different hunting strategies based on the time of day. Sarcosuchus primarily exist on a diet of meat and fish, and when not swimming or basking, the rest of their time is spent stalking for prey - usually just under the surface of the water. Prey small enough to be carried will be clamped and dragged straight into the water where the Sarcosuchus will attempt to drown them and tear their flesh with their powerful jaws. Otherwise, Sarcosuchus will bite and rip at flesh from their struggling prey. Sarcosuchus does not hunt healthy adult Deinocheirus, but may attempt to hunt unprotected Deinocheirus offspring, up to adolescent. Should an adult return to protect the offspring, Sarcosuchus will forfeit the hunt. Unhealthy Deinocheirus are death-scarred or albino. Sarcosuchus does not hunt adult Spinosaurus. Sarcosuchus females may attempt to hunt unrelated Sarcosuchus of adolescent or below if the young are unprotected. Sarcosuchus may 3rd party a creature during a hunt if the hunter OR the hunted come within 2 bodylengths of the water the Sarcosuchus is already in. All 3rd partying must be done from water.
