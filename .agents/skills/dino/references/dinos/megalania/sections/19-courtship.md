# Megalania – Courtship

Megalania of both variants seeking to court travel to Hot Springs. This is a universal courting ground for both variants, though Temperate and Arid variants always ignore one another in this region. Females slip into a pool at one of the hot springs and begin to call :broadcast_call:  loudly, seeking to draw in interested males while swimming around their claimed pool. Their scent is carried on the steam, and males flock to the area from great distances, tongue-flicking the air as they approach. Different Variants never accept courtship advances from one another.
