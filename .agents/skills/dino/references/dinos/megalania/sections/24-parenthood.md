# Megalania – Parenthood

As eggs begin to hatch, the male approaches to observe his young being born, taking note of his offspring. Once the final egg has hatched, the male turns and leaves the female, disappearing to continue his travels elsewhere. Males have no input on parenting, and may even cannibalize their offspring if they encounter them at a young age. Females remain in the general vicinity, but do not show affection towards her young, and do not allow them to get too close. They aggressively defend their young if attacked by others, and attempt to ward off rivals and predators. Mothers begrudgingly allow their young to eat from their kills - but only after she has eaten her fill and is resting. Young that attempt to feed or scavenge before this time are aggressed away before being attacked - and sometimes even killed entirely if the mother is feeling particularly irritable. Once the youngest offspring is midway through Juvenile, the mother abandons her nest and begins her nomadic adventures again. Offspring of Adolescent and below are permitted to follow and benefit from her kills, but must not get too close without facing her wrath. Should any of her offspring get to deathscars, mothers may turn on them and cannibalize them. Albino offspring is immediately cannibalized if they do not retreat fast enough upon discovery. At Sub-Adult, offspring disperse from each other and their mother.
