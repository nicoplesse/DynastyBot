# Megalania – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 2 🥚 per 2 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 5 🥚 per 1 hour. Megalania nest anywhere in their preferred POIs, and opt to decorate their nests with thick foliage to keep them hidden - especially from other Megalania. Megalania do not form same-sex pairs. Megalania females sometimes reproduce asexually through parthenogenesis. They produce a maximum of 2 🥚 at this time, that are always male. ♂️
