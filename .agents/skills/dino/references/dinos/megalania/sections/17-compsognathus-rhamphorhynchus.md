# Megalania – Compsognathus & Rhamphorhynchus

Megalania have no symbiotic relationship with other species, but sometimes tolerate the tiny scavenger species Compsognathus and Rhamphorhynchus when they are basking. These tiny species have nimble snouts, capable of picking off dead scales and pests, which helps them in the shedding process. However, Megalania will not hesitate to hunt either species if hungry. Megalania firstly enjoy scavenging - preferring the easier option where possible - but are skilled and adept hunters, sometimes capable of taking down prey up to Large tier alone. They opt to ambush where possible, skulking in bushes, foliage and rocky areas to remain hidden. They are low to the ground, and ambush is often a simple task for Megalania. When a hunt is initiated, they pursue for a long time, employing an endurance hunting tactic. They only give up when the likelihood of success seems low, or if they lose interest. Megalania do not hunt other adults of their species, unless they are a different variant to them. However, they will hunt unrelated Megalania of Adolescent and below of any variant.
