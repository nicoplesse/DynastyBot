# Kaiwhekea – Aggressive Interaction

Kaiwhekea are tolerant to their own species, and try to avoid conflict where possible - except during courtship. Ignoring is always the first option they consider when encountering aggression from other Kaiwhekea, and males that accidentally encroach too close to another male's females will quickly slip away, aware of the offence they may cause. Most Kaiwhekea greet each other warmly during migrations. If a male wishes to fight another male for his females, they bite and smack against each other in the water, fighting until one male backs down.
