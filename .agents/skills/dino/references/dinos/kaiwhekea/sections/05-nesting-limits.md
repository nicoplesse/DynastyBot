# Kaiwhekea – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 4 🥚 per 4 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 2 🥚 per 4 hour. Kaiwhekea nest anywhere along the coastline or in shallow pools on coastal islands in their preferred POIs. Their nests are constructed out of kelp, branches and rocks. Kaiwhekea females may form same sex pairs.
