# Kaiwhekea – General Behavior

Kaiwhekea are known as fast and agile swimmers, they swim near the surface, and enjoy leaping out of the water as a form of play with other Kaiwhekea. They can be spotted basking in shallow waters or even on land for a short period of time. With their long neck, small head, and sharp interlocking teeth, Kaiwhekea hunt small, soft-bodied prey such as fish or turtles. They use their keen eyesight to spot prey and avoid larger predators that share the ocean, unless desperate. Kaiwhekea are known to digest pebbles and shells to help digest the food they eat. Kaiwhekea take a great interest in terrestrial species they encounter near the water. Some attempt to get their attention by fetching them objects from the bottom of the seafloor to present as interesting trinkets.
