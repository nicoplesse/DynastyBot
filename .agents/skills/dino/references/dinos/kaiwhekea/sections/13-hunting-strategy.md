# Kaiwhekea – Hunting Strategy

Kaiwhekea are opportunistic, intelligent and coordinated hunters. They can hunt up to Large tier, but will prioritise unattended Leedsichthys offspring first, and then Medium tier above others, when in the area. They flee from adult Spinosaurus and Deinosuchus. Kaiwhekea take it in turns swimming in to get bites on their prey.
