# Kaiwhekea – Cross-Species Interaction

Kaiwhekea are tolerant to other species and try to avoid conflicts at all times. When finding new species they approach them with curiosity, often leading to being attacked if they turn out to be a predator. Kaiwhekea often swim to the bottom of the seafloor to retrieve an interesting item to present to a terrestrial species, enjoying the unusual interactions. Kaiwhekea always flee from adult Spinosaurus and Deinosuchus.
