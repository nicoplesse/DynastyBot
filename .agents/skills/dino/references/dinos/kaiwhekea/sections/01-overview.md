# Kaiwhekea – Overview

Medium Cathemeral Aquatic Carnivore Kaiwhekea form promiscuous bonds. Kaiwhekea are nomadic. Kaiwhekea have a tolerant nature. Kaiwhekea may not scavenge from claimed corpses and must contest for a body. Critters and abandoned corpses may be freely eaten. Kaiwhekea tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.
