# Kaiwhekea – Elderly & End of Life (Optional)

When Kaiwhekea reach old age, they depart from their bale and seek deeper, hidden areas of the ocean. They will become less vocal and sometimes even shed into darker or duller coloration. Females that have formed a same sex bond can be found leaving a bale together. Elderly Kaiwhekea become weak near the end of their lifespan, with most dying due to lack of strength to bring them to resurface.
