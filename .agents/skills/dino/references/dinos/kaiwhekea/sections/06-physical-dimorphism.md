# Kaiwhekea – Physical Dimorphism

♀️ Females are slimmer than males, and have duller colors. Suitable Subspecies include: tenui ♂️ Males are bulkier, with contrasting coloration. Suitable Subspecies include: katiki, grandis Physical dimorphism for Kaiwhekea is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
