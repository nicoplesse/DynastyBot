## Besondere Risiken

- **Same-species territory risk:** Kelenken may hunt any protected hatchling or juvenile, unless it is protected by an apex carnivore or maip 1.10.1 If there is a territory challenge issued between two full casts, all sub adult and adult Kelenken within the cast may engage.
