# Kelenken – Hunting Strategy

Kelenken may hunt up to solo Large tier, but specialize in Medium tier and below. If a Medium or lower tier is present in the POI, it must be targeted by Kelenken before solo Large tier. Kelenken will hunt Latenivenatrix or Deinonychus first, and Achillobator or Utahraptor next, before all other prey. Kelenken may attempt a hunt on any hatchling or juvenile, regardless of size tier, even if protected by an adult. The exception to this is offspring protected by adult Apex carnivores, or Maip. Kelenken are adept at hunting prey at both day and night, and work together in their cast to hunt down unsuspecting prey. Prime targets are sleeping dinosaurs or baby dinosaurs, which Kelenken will swarm on and take turns ripping apart with their strong beaks, or kicking with their powerful legs. If the hunt is not a swift or efficient kill, Kelenken enjoy chasing and pushing their prey to the limit, running them down or waiting them out until they panic. Pairs work together to distract protective parents, leading them away from their offspring and making quick work of their prey. Kelenken do not hunt one another, even if desperately hungry. They can still contest another Kelenken for a body down, and may eat from Kelenken remains if presented with them.
