# Kelenken – Cross-Species Interaction

Kelenken have excellent vision and are attracted to quick moving prey. Because of this, Kelenken will often chase down the speedy Compsognathus and Dryosaurus even if not hungry, purely following their instincts but making a sport out of it. If the Kelenken manages to catch up to the creature, they typically lose interest and only attack if actually hungry. Kelenken despise Latenivenatrix and Deinonychus, and these are the first dinosaurs they will hunt, if they spot them within line of sight (following regular hunting requirements). Both species tend to form large groups so Kelenken are quick to target any that they see in hopes of reducing the population and preventing any risk to future chicks. Kelenken flee from adult Apex carnivores and Maip. Kelenken do not tolerate scavengers.
