# Kelenken – Elderly & End of Life (Optional)

As Kelenken age, they naturally slow down, becoming less active and spending more time resting or observing their surroundings. Elderly Kelenken are often found perched in shaded areas, watching over the cast rather than actively participating in hunts or territorial POI patrols. Elderly Kelenken can become quite cantankerous and demanding of other pairs within the cast - often clacking their beaks at them and encouraging the younger individuals to hunt for them. Kelenkens that are in a lifelong pair grow even closer to one another in old age, and exhibit signs of distress when parted from their other half. Unfortunately, when one of the pair dies, the other follows shortly afterwards. The heartache causes a deep sadness for the remaining member of the pair, causing them to refuse to eat or hunt for food. They remain near the body of their fallen loved one where possible, and usually die of starvation. If the mate has died due to a predator hunting them, they may defend the corpse for up to 1 minute before allowing the predator to claim it.
