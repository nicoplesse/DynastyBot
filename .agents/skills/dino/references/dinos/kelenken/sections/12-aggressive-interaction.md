# Kelenken – Aggressive Interaction

While chicks are hatchling age, and unable to leave the nest or move about freely, parents are extremely protective over them. Kelenken do not tolerate other adults approaching their chicks until the chicks have at least reached juvenile age, and are able to leave the nest. This applies to any pair, even those within their cast. Once a cast is full with 8 sub adults or adults, Kelenken will not tolerate other Kelenken within the vicinity. Casts will angrily size up against other casts, hissing and beak snapping to drive them out. These displays are typically a display only and one side will normally back down and leave the area - but the cast will resort to attacking if the other Kelenken do not leave. Kelenken do not hunt one another, even if desperately hungry. They can still contest another Kelenken for a body down, and may eat from Kelenken remains if presented with them. POI Challenges between Casts & Other Kelenken: A full cast (8 members) of Kelenken will not tolerate other Kelenken within their current POI. If another Kelenken or group of Kelenken is spotted, and it is not another full cast of 8 Sub/Adults, 4 Kelenken may work together to drive the other Kelenken out. POI Challenges between Full Casts: If there is a territory challenge issued between two full casts, all sub adult and adult Kelenken within the cast may engage.
