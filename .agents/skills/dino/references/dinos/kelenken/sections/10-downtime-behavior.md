# Kelenken – Downtime Behavior

Kelenken sleep in short bursts at any time of day, and often sleep in shifts, with bonded individuals taking turns staying awake to watch over one another. Kelenken prefer to rest in deep shrubbery or small dens, but may even sleep in hollowed out trees and logs. To stay dry, they often seek shelter to sleep in when it rains or snows. During this time, they socialize quietly with one another, preening each other’s feathers and only leaving the den if hungry or thirsty.
