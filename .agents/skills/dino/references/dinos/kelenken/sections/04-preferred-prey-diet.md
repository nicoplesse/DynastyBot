# Kelenken – Preferred Prey & Diet

Kelenken may hunt up to solo Large tier, but specialize in Medium tier and lower. If a Medium or lower tier is present in the POI, it must be targeted by Kelenken before solo Large tier. Kelenken will hunt Latenivenatrix or Deinonychus first, and Achillobator or Utahraptor next, before all other prey. Kelenken may attempt a hunt on any hatchling or juvenile, regardless of size tier, even if protected by an adult. The exception to this is offspring protected by adult Apex carnivores, or Maip. Kelenken flee from adult Apex carnivores and Maip. Kelenken do not hunt one another, even if desperately hungry. They can still contest another Kelenken for a body down, and may eat from Kelenken remains if presented with them. Kelenken do not tolerate scavengers. Kelenken are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects. See 📜・server-guideOPTIONAL - Illnesses & Injuries for more information.
