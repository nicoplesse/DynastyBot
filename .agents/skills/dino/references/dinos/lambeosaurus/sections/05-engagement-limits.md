# Lambeosaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Flighty / Defensive When met with 1+ Apex predators, Lambeosaurus opt to flee rather than stand and fight. If they are in a herd of less than 5 adults, they will also flee 3+ Large. Lambeosaurus may opt to flee an engagement at any time, regardless of the size and number of carnivores hunting them. Against Large, Medium and below carnivores: Lambeosaurus may have 3 individuals entering an active engagement. This consists of the one attacked, and two defenders. The three in the engagement rush to the front of the herd to create a barrier between them and the rest of the herd members. The other members of the herd rush away from the engagement. Lambeosaurus are not intimidated by pairs or less of Small tier or any Tiny tier carnivores. They will be watched, but do not provoke a stampede or defensive behavior unless they attack. Defenders are only allowed to use a maximum of 3 buff calls during an engagement/challenge that directly involves them. This limit applies to Primal Mend, Healing Call, Rejuvenating Roar and Guiding Beacon. Lambeosaurus can use water to its advantage to flee from predators. It may defend itself from predators while in water, but must continue to cross while doing so. Lambeosaurus may not stand in the water for extended periods to deter predators, and must cross instead. A Lambeosaurus may cross back and forth as many times as is necessary, but must have appropriate stamina to do so. Preferred Diet Berries, fruit, flowers, nuts, and roots
