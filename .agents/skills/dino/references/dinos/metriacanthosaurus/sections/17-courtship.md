# Metriacanthosaurus – Courtship

When interested in courtship, Metriacanthosaurus will travel to Mudflats or Sharptooth Marsh. Metriacanthosaurus become tolerant of other Metriacanthosaurus here and do not challenge or hunt other Metriacanthosaurus within the courtship grounds. All Metriacanthosaurus must enter the courtship grounds ungrouped. If already pairbonded, Metriacanthosaurus will not enter the courtship grounds. Metriacanthosaurus who have left their pairbond for any reason may come back to the courting grounds to seek a new mate. Metriacanthosaurus who arrive at Mudflats carry mushrooms to assist them in finding a mate. This substance is highly prized by others, and serves as an attractive gift to a potential partner. Extremely colorful Metriacanthosaurus are the most desired as mates, and often attract the most attention at the courting grounds. Upon spotting a potential mate, Metriacanthosaurus begin to bob their heads up and down at the other, clasping their mushroom between their teeth. Once they feel they’ve grabbed the attention of a potential mate, they spread their arms in a wide display, lifting their heads upwards and shaking their bodies. An interested Metriacanthosaurus returns these gestures, before both individuals pick up their gifts and begin their inspection of each other, moving slowly in a circle. Halfway through the circle, both Metriacanthosaurus rotate and move the opposite way, sure to flash their colors on both sides. Once they have inspected each other's colors, both Metriacanthosaurus move closer to each other and place the mushroom at their feet, and begin a dance of mimicry. This dance is performed with soft chirps and upwards shakes of their heads. Once the dance is complete, they chatter softly to each other, and leave the courtship grounds to find a place to nest. Metriacanthosaurus can be distracted by a flashier, more colorful individual and are able to walk away from the courtship process in an attempt to court another instead. If two Metriacanthosaurus are interested in the same individual, they engage in a short battle - beginning with one aggressive call, before quickly running at each other and biting into each other's sides. This fight will only take place during the first step of courtship. Those that have begun their courtship dance will not be fought over. The individual that reaches half health first quickly flees from the engagement and is not chased by the winner - who is instead eager to continue the courtship.
