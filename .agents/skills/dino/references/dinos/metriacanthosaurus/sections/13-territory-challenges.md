# Metriacanthosaurus – Territory Challenges

If another adult Metriacanthosaurus is within line of sight and not within their quiver, the individual's first instinct is to intimidate the other. Pacing from side to side or circling, Metriacanthosaurus show off their colorful scales, sniffing the air and lifting their head in a show of confidence. Males favor shaking their heads as well - especially those with a brightly colored dewlap. A duller Metriacanthosaurus is often intimidated by a brighter, more poisonous individual, and may choose to flee rather than fight for the right to stay in the POI. If neither flee from the sight of the other, a territory challenge begins. If the challenge is accepted - the fight is quick and brutal, tearing into each other and clawing at each other's sides with vicious swipes and bites. The winning Metriacanthosaurus does not have to remain in the POI, but the losing Metriacanthosaurus must move on from the POI.
