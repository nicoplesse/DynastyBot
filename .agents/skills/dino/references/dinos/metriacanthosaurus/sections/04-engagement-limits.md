# Metriacanthosaurus – Engagement Limits

Up to 2 Metriacanthosaurus may enter an engagement. Metriacanthosaurus do not flee from anything, but will avoid Apex tier carnivores. Metriacanthosaurus may hunt up to Large. Adolescent Metriacanthosaurus do not participate in engagements. Preferred Prey Metriacanthosaurus target other Metriacanthosaurus before any other dinosaurs in their line of sight. If they are adult, they will challenge them; if they are adolescent, they will eat them. Then, Metriacanthosaurus target Struthiomimus, Citipati, Utah, and Kelenken. Then, Metriacanthosaurus target adolescent and below Therizinosaurus. If none of these options are within LOS, Metriacanthosaurus may hunt any other species up to Large. Metriacanthosaurus may cannibalize other Metriacanthosaurus of Adolescent or below. Offspring raised by a mated pair must flee the POI before their parents notice they have reached sub-adulthood.
