# Metriacanthosaurus – Solitary Behavior

Metriacanthosaurus are well adept to living solitary lives and sometimes opt to remain on their own throughout their lives. Metriacanthosaurus who have previously been part of a quiver whose dynamic was unsuccessful for any reason may grow a distaste for other Metriacanthosaurus. Solitary Metriacanthosaurus most commonly trail groups of carnivores or herds at a distance. Carnivore groups typically draw the most attention to a solitary Metriacanthosaurus ,as these are the easiest opportunity to scavenge from a meal. If aggressed by a group of carnivores outside their hunting limit, Metriacanthosaurus will move on to seek other opportunities. Once interested in hunting, the once noticeable and bright Metriacanthosaurus will seemingly disappear, weaving through coverage and shadowed areas to stalk its prey. Metriacanthosaurus are unable to continue stalking during the day, preferring instead to rest and seek out its interest once night comes again.
