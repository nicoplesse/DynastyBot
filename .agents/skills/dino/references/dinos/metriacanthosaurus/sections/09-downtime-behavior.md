# Metriacanthosaurus – Downtime Behavior

Metriacanthosaurus are sluggish through the day when exposed to the sunlight for too long, and spend time seeking out cooler caves or shaded areas to rest in. Dry season is especially brutal, and lots of Metriacanthosaurus seek caves with a water source where they can soak their scales in an attempt to cool themselves as they rest. If there are no caves it can reach in the area, Metriacanthosaurus instead favors dense underbrush to shade itself.
