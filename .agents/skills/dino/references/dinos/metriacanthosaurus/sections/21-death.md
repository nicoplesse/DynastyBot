# Metriacanthosaurus – Death

Metricanthosaurus often die uncharacteristically in open areas, to heat, overexertion, or otherwise. Despite the fact their scales have gone dull from lack of the toxin in their systems, consuming these bodies has the same effects for any species unable to digest their toxins. When an elderly female is raising her final clutch, she will lay down in the open and pass away. At this point, her young will feed from her body, to receive the final dosage of venom their young bodies need to support their growth into adulthood. Metricanthosaurus bodies may only be fed from by Compsognathus, Ceratosaurus, Maip, Allosaurus, Meglania, Rhamphorhynchus, Deinosuchus, Quetzalcoatlus, and other Metricanthosaurus without averse side effects.
