# Metriacanthosaurus – Overview

Medium Nocturnal Terrestrial Carnivore Metriacanthosaurus form strong pairbonds. Metriacanthosaurus are nomadic. Metriacanthosaurus have a (tolerant nature towards other species and aggressive nature towards their own kind. Metriacanthosaurus are scavengers, and may scavenge from corpses freely. Metriacanthosaurus cannibalize albino individuals, and tolerate melanistic individuals; although melanistic individuals are viewed as weak.
