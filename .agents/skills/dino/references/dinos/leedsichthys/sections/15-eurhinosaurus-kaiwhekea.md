# Leedsichthys – Eurhinosaurus & Kaiwhekea

Eurhinosaurus and Kaiwhekea are two other primary predators of Leedsichthys offspring. Using their speed, they quickly catch up to schooling pups and indiscriminately catch and kill. Female Leedsichthys outright kill any Eurhinosaurus or Kaiwhekea offspring upon discovery.
