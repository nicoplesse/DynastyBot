# Leedsichthys – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: Females may lay up to 10 🥚 per 2 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: Females may lay up to 5 🥚 per 2 hours. Female Leedsichthys may split their clutches between multiple males if they wish, so long as the egg count is not exceeded. Nesting timers do not reset until the female Leedsichthys has given away all her eggs. See Courtship and Parenthood for more information. Leedsichthys may form same-sex bonds. These bonds do not raise offspring together, but enjoy reuniting with one another and migrating alongside each other.
