# Dilophosaurus – Group Limits

Up to 8 Sub/Adults, comprising of a dominant breeding group of up to 5 members, and relatives of the breeding group Unlimited Offspring Dilophosaurus in this group do not adopt. This group is known as a leash. Or: Up to 5 Sub/Adults, all the same sex, of any relation Up to 5 Adoptees This group is known as a mask. Or: 1-2 Adult females Unlimited Offspring This group is known as a sneaky nesting group. Dilophosaurus of Adolescent or under may form unlimited dispersal groups, but must form same-sex groups at Sub/Adult.
