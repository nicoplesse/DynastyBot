# Dilophosaurus – Infanticide

A dominant reynard will commit infanticide on a few different occasions. This often is partnered with exiling the female. If one of his daughters has been sneakily courted, upon discovery of the pups, he will quickly cull them, and exile his daughter. If the pup is solitary and unattended, or otherwise clearly unrelated to him, he will cull the pup. A vixen may attempt to defend her pups from the reynard at any time, but may be faced with exile as a result. When committing infanticide, a dominant reynard does not need to follow body down rules or abide by the 15 minute timer. He will not stop until all offspring he intends to cull are dead or have fled. If a pup’s true parentage has been successfully hidden from the dominant male into adulthood, he will not cull the individual; instead, he will swiftly exile them. Exile A dominant reynard will exile another individual by issuing a broadcast :broadcast_call:  call directly in their face, then an aggressive call :threaten_call:. This initiates a flight response in the now-exiled Dilophosaurus. The dominant male may spur them on with nips to their tails and flanks, but will never chase beyond the POI border, and will never kill another Dilophosaurus unless they are refusing to flee. Other members may take part in the chasing and aggressive calls, but will never land a bite themselves, in fear of retribution from the dominant reynard. Dilophosaurus only tolerate solitary Compsognathus around themselves and their food. However, if desperate hunger, they may attempt to hunt the Compsognathus - though this typically fails!
