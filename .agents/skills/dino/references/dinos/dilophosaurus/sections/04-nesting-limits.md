# Dilophosaurus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 2 🥚 per 2 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 4 🥚 per 2 hours. Dilophosaurus nest in very thick undergrowth or caves big enough for them to enter. They build their nests out of reeds, roots, and branches. Dilophosaurus may form same-sex pairs and groupings within a mask, and may adopt. Within a leash, same-sex pairs also form, but may still participate in nesting with the opposite sex. Those not willing to nest must disperse from the leash.
