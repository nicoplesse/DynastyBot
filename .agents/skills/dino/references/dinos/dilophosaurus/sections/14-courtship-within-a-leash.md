# Dilophosaurus – Courtship within a leash

Once vixens are settled into a leash grouping, courtship is initiated by them, whenever they feel ready to nest and rear offspring. Broody vixens look for a suitable rock or higher location near the dominant reynard, and leap on top of it to begin her wailing, courtship screams. This clear indication is obvious for the dominant male. If he wishes to nest with the female, he approaches her with affectionate calls, and sits down beside her performing spot, shaking to show his acceptance. However - dominant males are not always interested, and may choose to reject his female's advances. This is signalled with an :threaten_call: aggressive call. The female, while accepting of the rejection, responds sadly, :danger_call:fear calling and shaking her head to soothe her own frustation.
