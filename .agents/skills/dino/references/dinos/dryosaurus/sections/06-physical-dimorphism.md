# Dryosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

Dominant individuals with access to berries sport brighter skins (see Social Interaction for further detail). ♀️ Females are known as does. Females lack quills and have a smooth appearance. Suitable subspecies include: phosphaticus, canaliculatus ♂️ Males are known as bucks. Males have quills over their body. Suitable subspecies include: altus, elderae Physical dimorphism for Species is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
