# Dryosaurus – Eotriceratops

Dryosaurus that have had exposure to these ceratopsians learn over time that Eotriceratops are highly aggressive. Thrill-seeking Dryosaurus thrive on the adrenaline from deliberately targeting Eotriceratops’ resources to steal food whenever they get the chance - this is especially common in adolescent Dryosaurus trying to prove themselves.
