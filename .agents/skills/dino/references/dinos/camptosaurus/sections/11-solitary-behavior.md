# Camptosaurus – Solitary Behavior

Outside of colony grounds, with no home ranges or hierarchies to maintain, Camptosaurus give very little attention to others of their kind, past a brief inspection. Even males lose their need for dominance, quickly becoming aware that females pay no mind to courtship without a claimed home range to nest in. If during this time, a Camptosaurus finds interest in joining a colony, they may seek out and invite other lone Camptosaurus in the area on their journey, valuing safety in numbers.
