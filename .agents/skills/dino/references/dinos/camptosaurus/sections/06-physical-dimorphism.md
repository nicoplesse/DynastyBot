# Camptosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

This profile does have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Female Camptosaurus do not have spikes along their spine. Suitable subspecies include: Dispar ♂️ Male Camptosaurus have spikes along their spine. Suitable subspecies include: Conditus, Latas Physical dimorphism for Camptosaurus is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
