# Camptosaurus – Albino & Melanistic Behavior

Mutated individuals do not receive any additional attention. Camptosaurus are a typically peaceful species, but are not opposed to aggressive behavior in specific instances. These occurrences are far more common between males than females, but are never fatal between either party. A Camptosaurus that causes the death of another is immediately shunned and exiled from the colony, with all witnessing Camptosaurus remembering this individual for life. If the offending Camptosaurus is ever found within another colony by a witnessing Camptosaurus, they will be shunned and exiled from that colony too. Uniquely, stretching is regarded as threatening behaviour between Camptosaurus. Rising to their full height shows off an individual’s scars and creates the illusion of size, typically used to show displeasure or annoyance at an individual or situation before escalating to true aggression. Most challenges and disputes are met with this threat display before all else.
