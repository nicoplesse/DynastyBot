# Camptosaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Flighty / Defensive Camptosaurus can have up to 1 in an engagement. However, a male Camptosaurus may defend a female who is actively raising his offspring. Camptosaurus flee from Achillobator and Large tier+ Carnivores. Adolescents and below do not participate in defences, and will always choose to flee, unless cornered. Preferred Diet Camptosaurus prefer berries, but will eat anything available to them.
