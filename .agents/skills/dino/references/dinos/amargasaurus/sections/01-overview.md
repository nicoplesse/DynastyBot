# Amargasaurus – Overview

Large Diurnal Terrestrial Herbivore Amargasaurus form promiscuous pairbonds. Amargasaurus are nomadic. Amargasaurus have a passive nature. Amargasaurus may group with:  Apatosaurus, Argentinosaurus, Camptosaurus, Citipati, Struthiomimus. They may not defend these species, even when grouped with them. Amargasaurus tolerate mutated individuals.
