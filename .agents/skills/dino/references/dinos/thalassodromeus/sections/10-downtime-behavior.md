# Thalassodromeus – Downtime Behavior

Thalassodromeus struggle with their vision at night, often resulting in crash collisions and even death. As a result, Thalassodromeus prefer not to fly at night. Thalassodromeus take lots of short naps but spend the rest of the time preening, socializing with their flock, and traveling across the ground to hunt critters and fish. Thalassodromeus may be seen communally roosting on cliffsides and on tree tops, sleeping close to those closest to them.
