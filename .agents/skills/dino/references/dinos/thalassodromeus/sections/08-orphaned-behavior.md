# Thalassodromeus – Orphaned Behavior

Orphans sadly lack parental influences and so - despite their initially noisy state - once they realise an adult will not be coming to provide for them, they are forced to develop quicker than they normally would. This results in orphaned Thalassodromeus attempting flight from a much younger age. Though poor fliers at first - they readily adapt as their muscles strengthen, and quickly become self-sufficient where possible. As Thalassodromeus do adopt, orphans instinctively try to find others of their species in the hopes of being nurtured by another. Their highest chance of success for this is to find a same sex pair - but even if they fail to find surrogate parents, Thalassodromeus orphans will usually be allowed to join an existing flock and stay nearby for mutual company. Thalassodromeus orphans quickly learn how to fish and instinctively dive into the water to retrieve whatever they can catch. This skill appears to come naturally to Thalassodromeus - as even those without tutors swiftly become adept at fishing.
