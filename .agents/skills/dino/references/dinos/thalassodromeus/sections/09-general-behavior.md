# Thalassodromeus – General Behavior

Though appearing somewhat dimwitted with their playful antics, Thalassodromeus are actually intelligent pterosaurs, capable of recognizing their own reflections in water or mirrored surfaces, and memorizing successful routes taken to find potential prey. Flocks are mostly nomadic, moving to a new spot every morning if they so choose. Thalassodromeus can be found deep in land at Gondwa most often traveling along waterways such as rivers, and frequenting bigger lakes and freshwater sources. While migrating, Thalassodromeus fly close to each other, usually with the oldest individuals positioned near the front of the flock, guiding the others on where to go. Thalassodromeus are incredibly vocal during the day. Their seemingly endless chatter can be provoked by anything - movement or sight of other dinosaurs often springs a flock into relentless chatter. At night, their noisy behavior subsides mostly - though individuals may still make the odd chattery remark as they drift in and out of sleep. As they have poor night vision, Thalassodromeus always choose to find a place to perch before nightfall, with flocks gathering together on one ledge or a series of ledges nearby.
