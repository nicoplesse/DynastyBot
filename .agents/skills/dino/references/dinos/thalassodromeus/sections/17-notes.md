# Thalassodromeus – Notes

Unlimited numbers of Thalassodromeus may attempt to scavenge from other predator's kills at their own risk. If attacked, they must move on from the body and not attempt to scavenge from it again. Albinos and melanistics can never form pairbonds. When an opposite sex pair donates one of their eggs to a same sex pair, the nesting post must have one egg deducted. A second nesting post must be posted for the donated egg, with the biological parents reacting to it, but specifying that the egg has been donated to a same sex pair nearby. Chicks born into this situation know no difference, and readily accept their same sex parents as if they were their biological parents. Thalassodromeus may enter water in an effort to escape an engagement. Thalassodromeus may only hunt other players if 35% or below hunger in the courtship grounds. They may hunt at 75% hunger in the nesting grounds.
