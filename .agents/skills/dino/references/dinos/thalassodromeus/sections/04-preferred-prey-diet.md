# Thalassodromeus – Preferred Prey & Diet

Thalassodromeus primarily exist on a diet of freshwater fish, which they hunt themselves from rivers, lakes and streams further in land. In addition, Thalassodromeus may hunt up to Medium tier healthy individuals. They may also hunt any hatchling or juvenile that has no adult within 2 body lengths. They may also hunt any dinosaur on deathscars. However, being highly opportunistic, Thalassodromeus prefer not to hunt at all unless no other food sources are available. Thalassodromeus will opt to scavenge from any claimed corpse when possible, regardless of their number grouped in game. Scavenging from corpses can be risky and Thalassodromeus will not fight back if attacked, opting instead to simply fly away.
