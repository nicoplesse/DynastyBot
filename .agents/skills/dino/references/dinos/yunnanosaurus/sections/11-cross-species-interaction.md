# Yunnanosaurus – Cross-Species Interaction

Regardless of season, Yunnanosaurus do not tolerate any ceratopsians within 5 body lengths of their group. Yunnanosaurus will aggressively drive away any ceratopsian species from a resource they are defending. Yunnanosaurus also dislikes Rhamphorhynchus, who often attempt to approach them in hopes of cleaning insects from their hides. Yunnanosaurus take great offense to this, and will drive away any irritating Rhamphorhynchus that enter their personal space. Dry Season Yunnanosaurus is tolerant to all herbivore species except ceratopsians during the Dry Season; the high temperatures and dry weather bring about a sense of peace and calm to these sun loving dinosaurs. Despite their docile nature during the Dry Season, Yunnanosaurus do not group with any other herbivores; protecting their resources while travelling is of the utmost importance to them. Rainy Season During the Rainy Season, Yunnanosaurus are aggressive to all other species of any age, and are territorial. Herbivores are seen as a threat to resources that must be protected for future offspring, and carnivores are not tolerated at all. Yes, even hatchling Citipati or excitable Compsognathus Apex species are ignored unless an Apex carnivore hunts an Yunnanosaurus, which will cause them to flee. Friendship with Lurdusaurus Despite living separate lives from each other for half the year - with Yunnanosaurus moving back to the eastern regions in the Rainy Season - both Yunnanosaurus and Lurdusaurus form unique bonds with one another. A friendship begins in the Dry Season, when Yunnanosaurus migrate west in search of water sources. Forced into more humid biomes, they often happen across territorial groups of Lurdusaurus in the waters. Lurdusaurus share a kinship with Yunnanosaurus; both species have similar smooth skin and long bodies, and are intolerant of most carnivores. When a Yunnnanosaurus crosses the water, Lurdusaurus greet their terrestrial cousins with calls of joy and sometimes leap out of the water to perform for them. Both species find one another entertaining, Lurdusaurus greatly enjoy the sight of their long necked friends with their odd appearance, and Yunnanosaurus find great amusement in the parading of Lurdusaurus in the water, sometimes pausing by the shore in hopes that a Lurdusaurus will leap for them. Yunnanosaurus will sometimes even remember their Lurdusaurus friends and seek them out when the Dry Season begins to exchange friendly calls and spend a few moments together. As Yunnanosaurus travel often during the Dry Season, Lurdusaurus may attempt to follow them through rivers, or may simply bid them farewell until they meet again.
