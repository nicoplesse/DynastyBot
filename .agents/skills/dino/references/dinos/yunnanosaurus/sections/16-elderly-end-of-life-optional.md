# Yunnanosaurus – Elderly & End of Life (Optional)

As Yunnanosaurus age, their coloration begins to dull and their skin becomes darker - showcasing any scars they may have earned in their youth. Despite their age, elderly males still form territories during the Rainy Season, which they maintain to the best of their abilities. Due to their dull coloration, they often have difficulty attracting any females and may be rejected much more often. Elderly Yunnanosaurus slow down and become more sedentary rather than nomadic in the Dry Season. They prefer not to travel as often, and sometimes prefer to lay in caves or take shelter under trees. When they do travel, they lag behind the group, unfortunately leaving them open to being picked off by predators. Despite their age, elderly individuals are respected heavily by their group and are never left behind. As they advance in their years, and their life begins to come to a close, these ancient Yunnanosaurus are unable to travel at all. They lay down in a favorite resting spot, and reflect on the adventures of their youth in their final moments. Group members with close bonds to the dying elder will stay close by, keeping them company until the end, and paying their respects to the body before moving on. Yunnanosaurus will always remember their friends that have passed away.
