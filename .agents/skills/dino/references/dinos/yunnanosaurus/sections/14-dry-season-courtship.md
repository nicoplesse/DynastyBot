# Yunnanosaurus – Dry Season Courtship

During the Dry Season, courtship only rarely occurs. Yunnanosaurus are more tolerant of one another, and emotions are typically calm. Without any drive to claim a territory and put on a courting display, nesting in the Dry Season is a lacklustre affair. If a male notices a female and wishes to nest with her, he remains in his ramble but follows the female in her rove, calling softly towards her. Should the female be interested in his advances, she may fall behind her group and consider his offer. The male circles her, calling softly and gently, and she either turns away and rejoins her rove to signal rejection, or responds with friendly calls and sits to show acceptance. Once the pair have successfully courted, the female returns to her group and the male returns to his. The rove will work together to build the nest and raise any offspring, without the presence of males. Males will only nest with one female at a time in the Dry Season, due to their lower fertility levels. There is no jealousy between males, and males do not challenge one another. Male and female Yunnanosaurus do not group in-game at this time. If the male is within a ramble, the group may stay within the same POI to observe any offspring that hatch, and may leave marks on nearby trees or rocks. This is to encourage any young sons to join their ramble, once they mature and leave the rove they were born in.
