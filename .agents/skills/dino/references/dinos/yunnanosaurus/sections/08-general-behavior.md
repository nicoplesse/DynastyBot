# Yunnanosaurus – General Behavior

Yunnanosaurus are very expressive dinosaurs, using loud vocalizations and body language to communicate with each other. Males in particular have loud voices and display often. Yunnanosaurus have a smooth but tough, leathery hide that protects them from the sun’s rays and shields their bodies from insects. Despite this, when the opportunity presents itself they can be seen enjoying shallow water. Social interactions are generally performed during the daytime, when individuals can display their colors more easily and can be seen by the whole group if they posture. This species migrates mostly at night due to the cooler air and to avoid the risk of dehydration from the heat of the sun. If resources are exhausted during the daytime, Yunnanosaurus will not wait for nightfall and will migrate to another POI to search for food or water. This is only done once in the day - all other travelling is restricted to night. Yunnanosaurus prefer arid habitats during the Rainy Season, to avoid the excessive humidity of more vegetated biomes. However, in the Dry Season, Yunnanosaurus migrate back to lush areas when their familiar water sources dry up. In the Rainy Season, Yunnanosaurus have higher fertility rates and gather in mixed sex groups for nesting. They become territorial, claim medium territories, and showcase highly aggressive behaviors to most other species. This drastically changes in the Dry Season, with Yunnanosaurus splitting into single sex groups, and adopting a nomadic lifestyle - as well as a more tolerant nature.
