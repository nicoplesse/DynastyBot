# Yunnanosaurus – Aggressive Interaction

Rove and Ramble Challenges Yunnanosaurus during the Dry Season aren’t inherently aggressive towards each other and will almost always accept stranger Yunnanosaurus into their group if they are of the same sex. Females may become aggressive towards males during the Dry Season, as they are less tolerant of them and prefer to avoid them altogether as they are outside of the breeding season. If the females have adoptees in the Dry Season, they will always be aggressive towards males. Yunnanosaurus respond to aggression by taking on a taller stance and beating on their chest aggressively towards the threat. When aggression is shown by a female towards a male he will always back down and flee from her with a fear call. Males of this species generally show respect and deference towards females, and if they show aggression it is always towards other males. Males may demonstrate their strength to one another by swinging their necks towards one another and standing tall to posture at them. Clade Challenges During the Rainy Season, males and females come together to form a clade for breeding purposes. At this time, Yunnanosaurus within a clade will become territorial. A clade challenge may be for the females, territory, or both. When two clades come across each other, a territory challenge is immediately issued. During the challenge only the males of the clade will fight with one another, females will stay back and posture or call aggressively. When challenging for females, at least one mature female must not actively have young so that she may be courted. If no female is available to court, males will seek females elsewhere. If one group has only one male, he may choose to either go on with the challenge, or may choose to forfeit. A female will never physically engage during a clade challenge. Females during these challenges will form a line on either side of the engagement and stay with their own clade to call out encouragement to their males, and attempt to intimidate the opposing side with aggressive calls and tail slamming. If the fight comes too close to them, the females will flee in the opposite direction and always maintain a safe distance. A forfeit occurs when either side issue a friendly call, or if the engaged males on one side both reach half health. The forfeit ends all combat, and the losing side leaves the POI, having lost the challenge. If the rival males win, females choose to stay in the current territory with the winning males to ensure hers - as well as her group's - safety. Females never travel with males. In the case a male has lost his territory to one reason or another, females will find him weak and unsuitable. They instead will choose to move on separate from unimpressive males to seek out a new clade. While all females may make their own choices, most prefer to follow the Lead Female's decision.
