# Latenivenatrix – Herbivores

Up to 3 Latenivenatrix without Achillobator companions can sometimes turn their attention to herbivores - particularly herbivores that are Large tier or higher, and impossible for them to hunt. Approaching cautiously, they carry small objects such as sticks, bones, flowers or other items, and drop them nearby these species to gauge their reactions. This behavior appears to be borne out of curiosity as opposed to antagonistic, serving as a way to assess the species' temperament and how threatening they may be to the dromaeosaurid. If tolerated, Latenivenatrix may try to venture closer to these larger species, seeking to pick insects and parasites from their bodies. However, any sign of aggression will prompt them to withdraw. When permitted, these Latenivenatrix may trail behind these herds. Unable to hunt these herbivores, this behavior is thought to be strategic, serving as a way for them to benefit from a form of passive protection by reducing the likelihood of being targeted by predators. They never alert herbivores to potential threats, preferring to withdraw from the herd when other predators are spotted to minimize drawing attention to themselves. Latenivenatrix with Achillobator companions - or in a rookery with 4 or more members - do not act out this behavior, but all Latenivenatrix struggle to resist the opportunity to groom and clean a willing sauropod.
