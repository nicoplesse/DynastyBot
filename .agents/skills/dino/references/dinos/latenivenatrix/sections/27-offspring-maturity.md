# Latenivenatrix – Offspring Maturity

Parents remain extremely doting on their offspring even into adulthood. Some may continue to feed them, even after the individual is full grown. Latenivenatrix offspring may opt to disperse through their own choice, or stay in their rookery if there is room. If there is no room, they must disperse; but are often followed by individuals whom they have closely bonded with throughout their youth. Parents often follow their chicks as well to disperse - though this varies from individual to individual.
