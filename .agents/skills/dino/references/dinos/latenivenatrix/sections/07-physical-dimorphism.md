# Latenivenatrix – Physical Dimorphism

Latenivenatrix has physical dimorphism, centered around a slight difference in body shapes and the ends of their tails. This profile does not have high and low T individuals. ♀️ Females are stockier than males, and have rounded tails. Suitable subspecies include: formosus ♂️ Males are sleeker, and have forked tails. Suitable subspecies include: inequalis, mcmasterae Physical dimorphism for Latenivenatrix is mandatory.
