# Latenivenatrix – Parenthood

Latenivenatrix parents are very devoted to their chicks, but the entire rookery pitches in together to raise young, as typically a rookery nests simultaneously. Each individual is keen to contribute and form a connection with all chicks nested in, and all are often encouraged to play together. If play behaviors go too far, parents are quick to gently correct the chick, and instead display and encourage how play should be done. Parents constantly sing to their offspring, and express great excitement when chicks return the song, jumping around and flaring their wings. This often begins a vicious cycle between parents and chicks, and much "excited-running" around within the creche. If a majority of the rookery is gone on a hunt or for food, some individuals are left behind to act as babysitters, in order to ensure that no chick wanders off too far from the creche. Wandering chicks are immediately ferried back to the shelter of the nesting area, and kept a very close eye on to ensure it does not happen again. Parents constantly nuzzle all chicks that approach them. This often means that the same chick will constantly cycle back to individuals whom they most enjoy being nuzzled by - as they will receive this every time they approach and chirp.
