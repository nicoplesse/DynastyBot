# Latenivenatrix – Engagement Limits

WITHOUT ACHILLOBATOR: Up to 6 Latenivenatrix may participate in an engagement or hunt. 1+ Latenivenatrix may hunt Medium tier and below. 6 Latenivenatrix may hunt Large tier and below. They cannot hunt Amargasaurus. WITH ACHILLOBATOR: Up to 3 Latenivenatrix may participate in an engagement or hunt with Achillobator. Hunting limit with Achillobator: With at least 1 Achillobator, 2+ Latenivenatrix may hunt up to Apex tier, excluding Apatosaurus. They may only hunt solo Apex carnivores. Territory defence: All grouped Latenivenatrix and their bonded Achillobator may participate in defending their shared small nest territory. Driving out Deinonychus: All grouped Latenivenatrix may participate in driving Deinonychus out of the POI. Achillobator do not assist. Latenivenatrix do not tolerate scavengers. Latenivenatrix may defend their bonded Achillobator companions and adoptees at any time. Latenivenatrix flee from Hatzegopteryx, Quetzalcoatlus, and Kelenken, regardless of whether they are with Achillobator companions or not. Latenivenatrix do not hunt Sub/Adult Amargasaurus, Apatosaurus or Argentinosaurus. They may hunt Adolescent and below Amargasaurus. Adolescent Latenivenatrix do not assist their parents in their hunts, nor do they take part in hunting with Achillobator. They may, however, hunt critters independently, but often will simply continue to be fed by their parents.
