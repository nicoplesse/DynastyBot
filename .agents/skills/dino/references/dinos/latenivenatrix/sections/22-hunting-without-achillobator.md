# Latenivenatrix – Hunting (without Achillobator)

Latenivenatrix hunt exclusively at night, and rely on their agility and coordination to wear down prey, as opposed to brute force. Their targeted prey depends entirely on their own numbers, with smaller groups opting for smaller and easier prey. Latenivenatrix look to hunt Lambeosaurus, Styracosaurus, Pachycephalosaurus, Citipati, Camptosaurus and Dryosaurus first and foremost if multiple prey options are available in the POI. When huntable prey is spotted, Latenivenatrix may signal this with loud, echoing calls before the hunting party grows quiet, and may attempt to creep forward in a coordinated effort to position themselves ready to cut the targeted individual off. Hunting party members spread out, seeking to cut off escape routes and circle around their target. The hunt begins suddenly, with individuals rushing in to slash and tear at the flesh of their victim. If the hunting party is spotted first or are unable to surprise their prey, a vicious attack is launched instead in an attempt to overwhelm their prey. They pounce and cling onto their prey with their powerful claws, biting at them as the victim tries desperately to shake them off. Once they take their prey down, all members of the rookery descend upon the corpse. Rookeries have no feeding hierarchy and feeding is communal, with the corpse freely shared amongst the group.
