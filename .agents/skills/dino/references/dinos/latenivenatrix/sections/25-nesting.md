# Latenivenatrix – Nesting

Even if not nesting, the entire group opts to place nests, to act as shelter for offspring and to act as the foundation of a small nesting territory. These nests are placed as close together as possible, with no individuals attempting to nest separately. Mates will place their nests all in the same area, to act as a small hub where their offspring will live the most of their early lives. The male’s nest is typically the host to the offspring, but if a male is nesting with more than one female, one female may invite the offspring to her own nest. Same-sex pairs or trios do not court outside of their pair or trio and so do not produce biological offspring, but relish in assisting the rookery in raising offspring, and readily adopt orphans they encounter.
