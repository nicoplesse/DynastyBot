# Alioramus – Predator Reaction

Alioramus avoid Apex carnivores where possible. Whilst not outright fleeing on sight, they keep their distance, and flee from engagements or hunts started by these species - only defending themselves as needed. When being hunted, Alioramus take the same coordinated and endurance based strategy they do when hunting. If a packmate is clamped or restrained by a hunter - even an Apex one - the defenders rush to try and save them, preferring not to leave a member of their group behind. This is especially true for females - who will fight to the bitter end to save their female companion. Alioramus are endurance hunters, enjoying long-lasting hunts where they follow prey and targets for long distances, aiming to exhaust them. Alioramus are also coordinated hunters, communicating with short cut off calls or long, loud calls. Each Alioramus in a hunting party moves in synchronization with others in the pack, tag teaming targets, and quickly moving out of reach of prey as soon as a bite has been landed. Alioramus use loud calls to indicate: the presence of prey, threats or simply to indicate excitement during an engagement. If faced with difficulty during a hunt, Alioramus will “test” different strategies in order to successfully hunt their prey. Alioramus must hunt Medium tier or below prey or any (unattended or attended) offspring of any tier before attempting a hunt on anything else in LOS. Alioramus may hunt up to Apex tier, but do not hunt Apex carnivores, or any Sauropods, such as Amargasaurus, Ampelosaurus, Apatosaurus, or Argentinosaurus, unless the sauropod is adolescent or below. Alioramus only hunts Apex tier species with 5 in the hunting party, and if no other options are available within line of sight.
