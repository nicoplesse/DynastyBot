# Alioramus – Male-Only Packs

Male only Alioramus packs operate under a very different dynamic than mixed or female only groups. While they provide security through sheer aggression, they lack the cohesion and stability that females bring to a pack. Males have no permanent leader in a male only pack. Dominance is tested regularly through sparring and aggressive posturing, with the 'leader' of a male only pack often changing between scuffles. The strongest male - or "leader" - at any given time enjoys priority access to food and resting spots, but his authority is never set in stone - and liable to change as soon as another male bests him to claim his spot. Males constantly engage in sparring matches where they lunge, snap and scratch each other to assert dominance. The lowest-ranking male - essentially the one that loses the most sparring matches within the group - may suffer unintentional mistreatment from his packmates. More dominant members control access to food, forcing him to wait until they have eaten their fill, which frequently results in malnutrition and missed meals for these weaker males.
