# Alioramus – Parenthood - Males

Lacking nurturing instincts, males play no role in rearing cubs, but will still assist females by bringing them food as needed. Males are not permitted to interact with or get near to the nesting site or cubs below Adolescent, and are met with fierce aggression from the female(s) if they attempt to do so. While male Alioramus lack any nurturing instinct, they are intensely curious about cubs. As young begin to stray from their mothers at Adolescent, males - now finally permitted to do so - investigate them with interest. Male interactions with offspring - including Adolescents - are often rough and poorly moderated, with cubs squeaking and rushing back to their mothers when bitten too harshly, or chased for too long. This is quickly moderated by the females, who intercept any behavior like this and reprimand the male.
