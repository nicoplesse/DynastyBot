# Alioramus – Death

If a female within a female pair is lost, the other female falls into a period of intense grief - going completely still and refusing to leave her body, staring down at it. She may call for her, nuzzling her in encouragement to rouse. She will not feed from the body of her fallen companion and will aggressively defend it from the other members of her pack, refusing to let them close. Though males do not mourn as deeply as females, the loss of a familiar packmate - especially a brother, long-term companion, or female in the group - often results in short-lived agitation. Affected males become more aggressive, roam further afield - or disperse from the group entirely in search of others. Alioramus's Hunter's Mark does not override the LOS rules during engagements. Alioramus avoid Apex carnivores and do not hunt them. They flee when hunted - except when a packmate is clamped or restrained. Defenders may rally to their packmate's aid. Sparring does not go below half health. Alioramus may hunt other players if 75% or below hunger in the courtship grounds.
