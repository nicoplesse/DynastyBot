# Alioramus – Female-Only Packs

Female only Alioramus only ever consist of a bonded pair of females. Alioramus packs never exceed more than 2 females. These packs are notably more cautious and skittish than mixed sex groups. Without males to provide assertiveness and physical pressure, female only pairs avoid confrontation whenever possible, and steer clear of perceived threats. Hunting success is reduced without additional pack members. Females rely more on opportunistic feeding and critters, and are more likely to abandon hunts early if success does not seem guaranteed.
