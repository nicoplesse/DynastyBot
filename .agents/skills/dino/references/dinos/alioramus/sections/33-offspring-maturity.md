# Alioramus – Offspring Maturity

Once cubs are Adolescent, females permit males to come closer and interact with cubs, though keep a watchful eye over them. Adolescent females gradually have affection from their mothers reduced, preparing them for when they reach maturity. As cubs reach Sub Adult, clear sex based divergence occurs. Females are driven out of the pack by both mothers in a series of warning calls and aggressive stances - a situation that escalates to biting and attacking if the warnings are not heeded. An exception occurs when a mother lacks a bonded partner. Should she be alone, a single daughter may be permitted to remain as her lifelong bonded companion. Males, however, are permitted to remain within the pack long term, provided space allows. Sub Adult males gradually integrate into male hierarchy through sparring and social testing, but start out at the bottom of the pack's rankings. If there is not enough space, the other males drive the newly aging Sub Adults out. They may be permitted to trail behind the pack for a while, but are not allowed within the group.
