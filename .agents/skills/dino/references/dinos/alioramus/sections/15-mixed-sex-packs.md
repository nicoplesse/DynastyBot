# Alioramus – Mixed Sex Packs

At the heart of every functional pack is a bonded pair of females, whose leadership dictates the group's cohesion, survival, and long-term success. These females act as the foundation of the group, leading its movements, making key decisions, and ensuring stability among its members. They lead the way on migrations, and make key decisions on which prey to stalk or hunt. Males contribute physical pressure and confidence, while females provide cohesion and restraint. Together, these roles create a balanced and adaptable group with the highest chance of success.
