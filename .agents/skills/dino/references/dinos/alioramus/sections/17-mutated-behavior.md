# Alioramus – Mutated Behavior

Albino Alioramus are born with both social and survival disadvantages, due to their coloration. While not automatically shunned, they are viewed as high risk companions, particularly during hunts or when hiding from larger, more dangerous predators. Albino females suffer little to no problems within a social grouping - but males are kept on the edges of pack life, with packmates sometimes refusing to sleep beside them at rest periods. Melanistic females are highly desirable due to their dark patterning, allowing them to blend in better with the deep shadows and dark shrubbery of their preferred biomes. As males display their colors within courtship, Melanistic males are much less preferred and are typically regarded as submissive males (unless having proven himself otherwise).
