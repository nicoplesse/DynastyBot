# Eotriceratops – Overview

Apex Diurnal Terrestrial Herbivore Eotriceratops form harem bonds. Eotriceratops are nomadic. When in a herd, Eotriceratops males are territorial of the herd and 5 Eotriceratops bodylengths around the herd. Eotriceratops females are tolerant in nature, while Eotriceratops males are aggressive. Eotriceratops matriarchies and herds may group with Psittacosaurus. Eotriceratops females may defend grouped Psittacosaurus. Males aggress Psittacosaurus away if they attempt to use them as a meatshield. Eotriceratops tolerate mutated individuals.
