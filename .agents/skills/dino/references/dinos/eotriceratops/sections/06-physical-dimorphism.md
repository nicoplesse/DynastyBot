# Eotriceratops – Physical Dimorphism

Eotriceratops has mandatory behavioral dimorphism between sexes. See more under General and Social Interaction. Eotriceratops allows high testosterone and low testosterone individuals; however, they must abide by the behaviors tied to their biological sex. For example, cows will not be outwardly aggressive to calves, and bulls will not be affectionate towards calves, regardless of testosterone levels. Cows must remain defensive, and bulls must remain headstrong. ♀️ Females, known as cows, have duller and less contrasting frills, and tend to be more uniform in color across their entire body. ♂️ Males, known as bulls, have brighter and more colorful frills and bodies, at times with more intense patterning. Physical dimorphism for Eotriceratops is mandatory. Albino, leucistic, gynandromorph, and melanistic bulls are viewed as low testosterone, and may form bull groups, but will not be selected for courtship, and may not group with cows. Albino, leucistic, and gynandromorph and melanistic cows are not treated any differently from other cows, but albino, leucistic, and gynandromorph individuals are never selected by bulls for courtship. Bulls may court melanistic cows depending on their own preferences.
