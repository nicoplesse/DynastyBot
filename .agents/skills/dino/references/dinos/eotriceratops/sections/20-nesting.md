# Eotriceratops – Nesting

Once these initial pieces of courtship have completed, bulls follow (but do not group in game) the matriarchy to the nesting grounds. Bulls keep a respectful distance from the cows unless approached by them. During this time, if a solitary bull that has successfully courted the herd is attacked, the matriarch may step up to defend him. When the group of Eotriceratops arrives at the nesting grounds, the matriarch will be the first to place her nest – typically the most central – and the rest of the cows work together to build an interconnected structure of nests. This is often built of branches, roots, bushes, and other materials in order to provide a united front, and a warm safe place for all calves to rest and play while growing at the nesting grounds. Bulls do not assist in this behavior, and stick to patrolling 10 body lengths away from their nesting matriarchy. Bulls that get within 10 body lengths of the nests are aggressively sent out.
