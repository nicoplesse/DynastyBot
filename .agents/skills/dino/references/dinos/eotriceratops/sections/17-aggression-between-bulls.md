# Eotriceratops – Aggression Between Bulls

Bulls that have claimed herds will not tolerate other ungrouped bulls within LOS and will challenge them. A solitary bull in ownership of a herd will challenge other solitary bulls and bull pairs, but not bull groups with 3+ members. Bull pairs in ownership of a herd will challenge all bull groups in order to drive them out of the POI. This behavior is not exhibited at nesting grounds or the courtship ground. Any bull may always defend his claim over a herd, whether solitary or paired. Bulls must ungroup to take part in bullfights, and all bullfights happen 1v1. If a bull is challenged for his herd, he will bullfight to maintain the claim, in the same fashion he would at the courtship grounds. The matriarch watches the bullfight to determine a winner. If she is not satisfied with the reigning bull’s performance, and the herd has offspring of Juvenile or below present, she will reject all bulls and move on. However, if offspring are Adolescent or above, she may choose to accept the winning bull into the herd, replacing the current bull. This counts as courtship– and begins a migration towards the nearest nesting grounds, if not otherwise interrupted. Once offspring are sub-adult, the herd may immediately nest again. Outside of this, bullfights are done for courtship purposes or to practice amongst bull groups.
