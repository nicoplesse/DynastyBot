# Eotriceratops – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 5 🥚 ☀️ Dry Season: 2 🥚 Eotriceratops may nest again when all offspring are sub-adult, dead, or none are present in game. Eotriceratops nest in communal locations depending on season. Their nests are constructed out of reeds, branches, and roots. In the Rainy Season, Eotriceratops travel to Broken Tooth Canyon, Rainbow Hills, or Tallbrush Coasts to nest. In the Dry Season, Eotriceratops travel to Green Valley, Rockfall Hill, or Hollow Hills to nest.
