# Eotriceratops – Group Limits

Matriarchy 12 Sub/Adult Females, led by a dominant female known as the matriarch. Unlimited Offspring Unlimited Adoptees Bull Group Up to 6 Sub/Adult Males (2 if closely bonded) No offspring or adoptees Herd Up to 14 Sub/Adults, comprising a maximum of 2 males and a maximum of 12 females This group is lead by a female known as the matriarch. Unlimited Offspring Unlimited Adoptees This group only forms once a matriarchy and up to 2 males have courted and raised offspring to Juvenile.
