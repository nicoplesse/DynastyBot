# Eotriceratops – Solitary Behavior

Solitary female Eotriceratops often look for other matriarchies to become a part of, or seek to form their own matriarchies. Still as gentle as any other female, solitary cows can still approach and be courted by bulls in the same way full matriarchies can. Solitary female Eotriceratops still migrate regularly, and often have tiny Psittacosaurus as companions, rather than their own species. However, a lonely life tends to not suit females – some females may even attempt to be accepted into existing matriarchies at communal nesting grounds, as detailed in Social Behavior. Solitary bulls, however, can thrive on their lonesome. While some bulls, especially as they age, seek support from another bull, younger bulls and those that prefer to have their own matriarchies learn the regular migration paths, and fiercely patrol and defend them from rival bulls.
