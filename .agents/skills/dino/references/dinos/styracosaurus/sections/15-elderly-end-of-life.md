# Styracosaurus – Elderly & End of Life

As they reach old age, arthritis, old wounds, broken bones and horns begin to take their toll on elderly Styracosaurus bodies. They lose some of the shine and brightness of color in their scales, and gradually become slower, falling behind during migrations. Elderly stags struggle to gather enough strength to best younger males in bullfights, and find themselves often forced to withdraw from fights soon after they begin. The dominant stag of the herd loses interest in claiming does, and naturally ends up forced to withdraw from his leadership position. Elderly Styracosaurus often have memory issues, forgetting common migration routes or sometimes even individuals. This behavior can be alarming and frightening for both the Styracosaurus experiencing it, and the - often close relative - seemingly forgotten by them.
