# Styracosaurus – Overview

Medium Cathemeral Terrestrial Herbivore Styracosaurus form harem bonds. Styracosaurus are nomadic. Styracosaurus have a tolerant nature with their own species and other ceratopsians they herd with, and aggressive to other species. Styracosaurus may group with: Pachyrhinosaurus, Albertosaurus and Psittacosaurus. Styracosaurus may defend these species. Styracosaurus tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.
