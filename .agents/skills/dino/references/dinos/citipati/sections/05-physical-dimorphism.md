# Citipati – Physical Dimorphism

Males are known as roosters, females are known as hens, offspring are known as chicks. ♀️ Hens have a stocker build with shorter feathers, and are more subdued in colour, often featuring grey, cream, and brown. Suitable female subspecies: jacobsi, osmolskae, khondti ♂️ Roosters have longer head feathers and striking colours, such as bright blue, orange, or red. Suitable male subspecies: ganzhouensis Physical dimorphism for Citipati is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
