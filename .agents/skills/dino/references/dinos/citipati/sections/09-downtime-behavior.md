# Citipati – Downtime Behavior

Roosters patrol while hens sleep during rest periods, or find high perches to sit on and observe the flock. Hens may also join him on these perches, especially if particularly bonded with the rooster. However, if hens have offspring, they battle to keep the offspring in nests. This often gives the hens a lot of stress, especially as it goes further into the night and with the more chicks they have. Roosters diligently and carefully help his hens with this process, and preen her often to help release stress. Citipati may be observed quietly socializing and preening themselves and others at this time. They do not call often, only really resuming their typically chatty tendencies when the rooster broadcasts in the morning. Roosters may flirt with posturing and gifts, but will not fight each other. Additionally, hens will not steal offspring from each other to avoid stress. Citipati, especially Citipati chicks, will huddle towards the nearest source of warmth, typically other Citipati in their flock or other species within their mixed species groups.
