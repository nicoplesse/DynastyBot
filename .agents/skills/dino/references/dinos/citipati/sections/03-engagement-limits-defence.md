# Citipati – Engagement Limits (Defence)

Predator Reaction: Flighty / Defensive Up to 4 Citipati may enter an engagement, these must be from the same harem group. Individuals do not defend one another if not in the same harem, preferring to flee and regroup further away. Citipati always flee from carnivores of Large tier and above – individuals will fight back if cornered but will receive no defenders. Preferred Diet Berries, fruit, nuts, roots, flowers, mushrooms, eggs, insects, shellfish, bones. Will very rarely eat meat, fish, and carrion if no other food is available.
