# Citipati – Overview

Medium Diurnal Terrestrial Herbivore Citipati form harem bonds. Citipati are nomadic. Citipati have a passive nature. Citipati may group with: Amargasaurus, Ampelosaurus, Apatosaurus, Argentinosaurus, Bonapartenykus, Camptosaurus, Iguanodon, Lambeosaurus, Parasaurolophus and Struthiomimus. Citipati may not defend these species, even when grouped with them. Citipati tolerate leucistic and melanistic individuals.
