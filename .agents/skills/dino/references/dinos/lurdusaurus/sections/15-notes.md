# Lurdusaurus – Notes

Lurdusaurus from coastal regions are known to have more blue, gray and white in their colors. While Lurdusaurus from more inland regions are known to be more green, brown, and cream. This is an optional behavior. Only all-female floats will adopt unrelated Lurdusaurus. Lurdusaurus males may commit infanticide as per profile, this will reset a females nesting timer.
