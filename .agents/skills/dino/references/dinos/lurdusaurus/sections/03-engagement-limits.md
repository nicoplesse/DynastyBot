# Lurdusaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Aggressive / Headstrong 5 individuals may enter an engagement. Sub/Adult males are the primary defenders and leap to the defence of others before females. A mother, however, always viciously defends her offspring under Sub-Adult. Lurdusaurus may flee to the water at any point during an engagement or hunt to avoid being attacked. They are able to move in and out of the water as needed. Lurdusaurus flee Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus when there are less than 5 Adult individuals to help defend. Lurdusaurus retreat into the water (or flee towards water if not nearby) when hunted by 2+ Large or 1+ Apex terrestrial carnivores. Preferred Diet Lakeweed, reeds, kelp, berries, shellfish, fruits, salt. Lurdusaurus are also able to eat flowers, nuts and roots but will only do so if desperate.
