# Tyrannotitan – Setts During Courtship

When a pair of bonded boars are interested in courtship, before an inspection by the sows can occur, the boars will engage each other in a spar. This clash between the boars is expected by any sow interested in a pair. The males turn on each other with vicious snaps and tears at each other's sides, hoping to appear as the stronger and more dominant of the two. While appearing brutal and bloody, boars will only take each other to half health and hold no grudges after the battle. Once the spar has ended, boars will take a few steps from each other, showing off their fresh wounds and the blood dripping from their sides. The older or more confident sow will select the male she and her pair (if she has one and are also interested in the males) will nest with by beginning the courtship with circling. The winner of these spars are selected based on the size and strength of the boar. He will appear more impressive to a sow if he showed no hesitation and with the blood spilled during battle covering his hide. Only one boar within a sett may be selected for courtship at a time. If at any point in time the sow or stand is challenged out of the POI, the boars who have courted them do not follow and the bond is severed. The boar or sett who owns the territory are freely permitted to court the winning sow or stand. Should related offspring become orphaned, boars will aggress them out of the territory, but will not kill them.
