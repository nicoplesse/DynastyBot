# Tyrannotitan – Courtship

Boars interested in courting will claim a territory or enter another boar’s territory in an attempt to claim the POI. A boar who has claimed a territory will attract the attention of other Tyrannotitan with their booming calls in hopes to attract any sows in the nearby areas. Sows interested in courting will follow the calls of other Tyrannotitan that echo through the night, often replying to them with their own calls to locate the other. Crossing into a boar's territory, the sow will broadcast to attract the attention of the boar. Once the two meet and exchange greetings, the boar will remain still in an area where the sow can make her approach and begin to inspect him. The close proximity between strangers is initially stress-inducing for both Tyrannotitan, often showing this by yawning periodically. The sow will circle the boar several times, inspecting his well-earned scars delivering occasional nips to his sides and tail to test his resilience and tolerance. A sow will do this until she is satisfied with her inspection. During inspection, the boar will grumble softly at the sow and repeatedly lower and lift his head in deep bows, similarly to how he greeted her, to show off his strength. Once she is finished, she will then remain in one spot, shaking off her body softly as her confidence in the interaction grows and chattering to the boar circling her as he inspects her in turn. The boar will also deliver nips as he circles her, stopping at her head once he is satisfied. Once pleased with each other, the two issue soft, excited calls of affection before the sow leads her boar to a nesting site of her choice. If the sow is in a stand and her pair is also interested in the chosen boar, the process is repeated before moving to select a nesting site.
