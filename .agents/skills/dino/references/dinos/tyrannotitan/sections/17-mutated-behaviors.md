# Tyrannotitan – Mutated Behaviors

Albino individuals are cannibalized on sight, regardless of age. They may never form a bond with other Tyrannotitan and are rejected from courtship and nesting. Melanistic Tyrannotitan, in a similar fashion, may be more preferred for courtship than other options, as their dark scales make them harder to see while hunting. These individuals often struggle with maintaining their body temperature, and are often observed basking in water. Tyrannotitan are primarily aggressive to other, unrelated same-sex individuals.
