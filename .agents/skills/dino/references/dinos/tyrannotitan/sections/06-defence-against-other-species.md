# Tyrannotitan – Defence Against Other Species

Up to 2 Tyrannotitan may work together in defence against being hunted. If there are 2 Tyrannotitan in a sett or stand, their primary defenders will always be each other. If there is a solitary courted pair within the same POI, and they are within LOS within 30 seconds of the hunt starting, they may defend each other. If there is one female and a male pair, the courted male of the pair may defend her. The remaining male must move 10 bodylengths away. Individuals who are not grouped and related, and have not courted may not defend each other. A solitary male is expected to defend himself if he has courted a female pair. If the father of a clutch is within LOS of an offspring being hunted while the mother is not, the father may rush to the clutch’s defense. Once the mother reaches the engagement (within 30 seconds), she may also defend with him. Female Tyrannotitan always defend their grouped offspring of Adolescent and below from threats if they are within LOS. Up to 2 bonded Adolescent Tyrannotitan may defend each other if attacked, but must prioritize fleeing if their parents arrive to defend them.
