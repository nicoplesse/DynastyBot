# Tyrannotitan – Nesting

Tyrannotitan nest in dense underbrush or rocky outcrops. Sows will guide their newly courted boar (and his pair if he has one) to their chosen nesting site within his territory. Some sows, especially in newer terrain, may take time to inspect their surrounds and ensure they are satisfied with the location of their nest. Other sows may select the nearest location they feel is decent and begin constructing their nest. Sows within a stand will nest with the same boar at the same time, constructing their nests close to each other in order to best protect their young. Some sows within a stand may opt not to nest alongside their pair and instead remain as a secondary guardian over the whelps. Once her nest is created, the boar will leave the sows and resume his patrol of the territory. Boars leave a wide berth of the nesting sows and do not approach them while the sows are sat upon their nests. Solitary boars who have courted a solitary sow may patrol at a closer range if the nesting becomes a temporary nesting alliance once offspring have hatched.
