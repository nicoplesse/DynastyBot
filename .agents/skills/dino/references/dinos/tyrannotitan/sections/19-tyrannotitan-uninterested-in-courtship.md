# Tyrannotitan – Tyrannotitan Uninterested in Courtship

Tyrannotitan who are uninterested in courtship, upon discovering an opposite sex individual, will begin to attempt to ignore the other individual(s), but will tolerate them in the same POI. If courtship attempts are persistent, Tyrannotitan may issue one aggressive call in the direction of the individual in order to drive them off. These signs are quickly respected, and may at times even result in the individual who failed courtship to leave the POI entirely in search of other opportunities. If a boar discovers a sow who is unwilling to court, he will tolerate her within his territory for the remainder of the night cycle and through the day. Upon the second night, if she is still within the territory, the boar again postures at the female in an attempt to gain interest in courtship. Should she reject him again, she must leave the POI and move on. If the sow who rejects him a second time has offspring, the boar will follow her closely, leaving one body length between them in order to escort her and her offspring from the POI. Should the sow not leave the POI after this, the boar is permitted to cull one of her offspring if she lingers within the territory. In this instance, the sow does not defend the offspring, taking the aggression from the boar as a sign to flee the POI.
