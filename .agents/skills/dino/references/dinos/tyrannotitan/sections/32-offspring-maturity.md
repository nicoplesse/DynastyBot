# Tyrannotitan – Offspring Maturity

By sub-adult, if her children have not already dispersed, she will send her offspring off with a final bellow, allowing them to move on from the territory and severing the bond. The chosen offspring of each parent will grow to be their closest bond throughout their lives. Sub-Adults who have paired off together will begin to roam and protect each other, facing the harsh life of the Tyrannotitan. Once all her offspring have left her, the sow herself will move on from the boars territory, again resuming her nomadic lifestyle.
