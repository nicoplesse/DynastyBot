# Tyrannotitan – Temporary Nesting Alliance

Temporary nesting alliances may not group under any circumstances. If both the boar and sow are solitary, they temporarily form a nesting alliance. This is non-grouped, but the boar and sow may defend each other temporarily when the sow resides in the boar's territory after courtship. This is to maximize the chances of raising their young to adulthood, and ideally providing a related same-sex companion for each other. Males and females will not assist one another with territory challenges against other predators, including other Tyrannotitan. If the female is challenged out of the territory, the male of the nesting alliance may not follow, and the bond is disbanded. Females may assist males with defending bodies she is actively eating from, or feeding her whelps with, but will move away from the body when she is done eating. Solitary boars may defend their offspring under Sub-Adult. Males and females will not assist one another with initiating or accepting territory challenges against other predators, including other Tyrannotitans, and will not start hunts on other species together. They may only defend each other when being hunted.
