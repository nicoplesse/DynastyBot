# Tyrannotitan – Group Limits

Up to 2 related Sub/Adults of the same sex. Female pairs (sisters, half-sisters, or mother/daughter) are known as a stand, and can be joined by unlimited offspring. Male pairs (brothers, half-brothers, or father/son) are known as a sett. A solitary male may group with one related adolescent male offspring. Unlimited Offspring Tyrannotitan do not adopt. Orphans may not group together unless biologically related (at least half-siblings). Orphans that have been nested in and are Adolescent or below may remain grouped together if abandoned by their mother, or if their mother dies. Adult individuals of the opposite sex never group - even when nesting. ALL Tyrannotitan that group together must log their relations in 🩸・bloodline-logs before they can group. You MUST have been nested in and have proof via a nesting post to prove relations.
