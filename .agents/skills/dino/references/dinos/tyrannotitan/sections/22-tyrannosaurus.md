# Tyrannotitan – Tyrannosaurus

Tyrannotitan will not tolerate Tyrannosaurus within the same POI as them. Tyrannotitan and Tyrannosaurus do not share territories more often than not, but when spotted, may be challenged out or result in a Tyrannotitan leaving the POI immediately. Tyrannotitan do not hunt Tyrannosaurus unless Albino, deathscarred, or Adolescent and below, but may eat from their corpses.
