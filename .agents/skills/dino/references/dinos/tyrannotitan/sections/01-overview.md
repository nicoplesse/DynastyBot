# Tyrannotitan – Overview

Apex Nocturnal Terrestrial Carnivore Tyrannotitan form harem bonds. Tyrannotitan males are territorial and claim large territories, consisting of an entire POI. They allow one solitary or a bonded pair of females within their territory and permit those and any offspring they father to share kills with them. Tyrannotitan females are nomadic, but become territorial and claim large territories when nesting. A female will drive out other ungrouped females they see within a shared male’s territory. Tyrannotitan males have an aggressive nature towards ungrouped Tyrannotitan males and other carnivores, and a passive nature towards females. Tyrannotitan females have an aggressive nature towards ungrouped Tyrannotitan females, tolerant towards other carnivores, and a passive nature towards males. Tyrannotitan are not scavengers. Tyrannotitan tolerate Tiny tier scavengers at all times, and all scavengers during the day, unless woken up by them. Tyrannotitan cannibalize albino individuals. Other mutated Tyrannotitan are tolerated.
