# Quetzalcoatlus – Group Limits

3 Sub-Adults / Adults of any relation or sex 3 Offspring. Quetzalcoatlus do not adopt, unless in a female same sex pair. Due to being at risk of being cannibalized, orphaned Quetzalcoatlus will typically avoid other Quetzalcoatlus. A Quetzalcoatlus group is known as a committee. Multiple groups of Quetzalcoatlus that exist in the same area are called a procession. They may not group together and must remain as separate groups.
