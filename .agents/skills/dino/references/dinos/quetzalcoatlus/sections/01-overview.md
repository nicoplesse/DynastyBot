# Quetzalcoatlus – Overview

Large Cathemeral Aerial Carnivore Quetzalcoatlus form temporary bonds. Quetzalcoatlus are nomadic. Quetzalcoatlus have a tolerant nature. Quetzalcoatlus may scavenge if solo at their own risk, but must contest for a body if in a group of 2 individuals or more. Albino and melanistic may be tolerated, but are sometimes shunned. Quetzalcoatlus may, in desperate situations, cannibalize their own offspring. If an easy meal, they will cannibalize orphaned Quetzalcoatlus of adolescent and below as well.
