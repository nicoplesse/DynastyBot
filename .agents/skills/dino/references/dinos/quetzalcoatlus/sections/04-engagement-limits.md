# Quetzalcoatlus – Engagement Limits

Quetzalcoatlus have no engagement/hunting limits beyond their grouping. Processions do not work together to mob others. Quetzalcoatlus do not tolerate other scavengers feeding from their kills. Solo Quetzalcoatlus may hunt up to Medium tier. 2+ Quetzalcoatlus  may hunt up to Large tier, and albino Apex tier. Quetzalcoatlus may also hunt offspring of adolescent and below of any tier. Adolescent Quetzalcoatlus will not engage in defense against predators when an adult or one of their siblings is being hunted. However, one Adolescent Quetz may engage in a hunt with the rest of their committee. Solo Adolescent Quetzalcoatlus may attempt to hunt up to Small tier. Quetzalcoatlus will avoid Hatzegopteryx if outnumbered. However, Quetzalcoatlus will hunt them if they outnumber them.
