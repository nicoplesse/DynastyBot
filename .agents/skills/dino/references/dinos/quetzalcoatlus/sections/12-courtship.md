# Quetzalcoatlus – Courtship

Quetzalcoatlus males are always the ones to initiate courting with females, typically attempting to impress them by completing a successful hunt, bringing them items of interest, or chunks of meat for them to eat. If the female accepts his gifts, he will take to great vocal and visual displays, stretching his wings before her in an attempt to further impress her. If a female accepts his approach, she will clack her beak at him and they will draw close together, pressing their beaks together briefly, a shared moment of connection before they take to the skies in order to find a high perch to nest. Male-Male and Female-Female pairs are sometimes formed; Male pairs will not adopt and will still elect to cannibalize all orphaned Quetzalcoatlus. Female pairs will adopt, but only rarely, and only if neither female views the chick as an easy source of food. Female pairs that do attempt to adopt orphaned Quetzalcoatlus often have to chase down these orphans, who flee from them by instinct. Persistence will cause the orphan to give in and accept them as their new parents. Male pairs are often loud and showy about each other and their relationship. Their goal is always to impress the other. They will dive, spin through the air, flare their wings, and spoil their mate with gifts in order to continue to maintain his affections. If a male gets bored of another male, he will leave in search of other opportunities. Female pairs spend a lot of time preening each other; they will share perches, working together to hunt. The elder or more dominant of the females will be the one to initiate the beginning of courting - she will flare her wings, help with hunting, and bring her mate thoughtful, specific gifts. Females adopting chicks only occurs if the females are not hungry, and if they are both broody. An extended period of a female being broody but having no chicks to raise will result in the pair splitting.
