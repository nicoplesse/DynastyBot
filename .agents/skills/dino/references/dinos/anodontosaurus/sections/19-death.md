# Anodontosaurus – Death

As an Anodontosaurus approaches the end of its life, its pace slows significantly, and it gradually loses the ability to protect itself from threats. Although Anodontosaurus do not mourn their dead, companions or loosely bonded individuals may become withdrawn after a loss. However, this is a temporary quiet period and they recover quickly, afterwards resuming normal behavior. Anodontosaurus have strict dietary requirements that result in illness if not adhered to. Anodontosaurus must consume a varied diet of different fruits, roots, nuts and flowers to be consumed in rotation to maintain their health. Sub-adult/Adult Anodontosaurus must consume at least 2 different food types per 1 in-game day cycle. Anodontosaurus are mostly sedentary but may travel long distances to find particularly tasty food, with particularly experienced individuals leading the way. Anodontosaurus that do not adhere to their strict diet will suffer from a Gastroenteritis Infection (exhibited by the Gastroenteritis Infection skin on the Dynasty Morph skins mod). This illness will not pass until the player has eaten another food type and the skin has completely shed. This is a mandatory portion of profile, and sick individuals must shed into this skin to demonstrate their illness. Anodontosaurus mainly feast on fruit, roots, nuts and flowers. Anodontosaurus require a varied diet consisting of fruits, roots, nuts, and flowers, consumed in rotation. Overconsumption of a single food type can lead to illness. (See General Behavior) Adolescent and younger Anodontosaurus prioritize fruit over other food sources due to their high nutritional value and do not have to adhere to the strict dietary requirements for adults.
