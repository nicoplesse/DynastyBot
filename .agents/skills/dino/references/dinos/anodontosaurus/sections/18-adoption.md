# Anodontosaurus – Adoption

Nesting Anodontosaurus may choose to adopt orphaned offspring they come across, provided the pup is similar in age to their clutch. Adults attempting adoption may often coax a nervous orphan closer by offering food, or placing preferred berries and fruit within reach. Anodontosaurus that are not nesting are far less likely to adopt stray orphans - but may choose to do so, depending on the adult's preference. Orphaned Anodotosaurus pups may be wary of adults and often flee from them, even during gentle adoption attempts. Anodontosaurus generally lead solitary lives, so the aging of other individuals is rarely noticed by others - even those in tumbles. Some elders within tumbles choose to leave the group entirely, and spend their remaining years along. Due to their considerable body weight, older individuals may develop hip dysplasia. This makes turning, moving efficiently and defending themselves increasingly difficult.
