# Anodontosaurus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 3 🥚 per 2 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 5 🥚 per 2 hour. Albinos only produce 1 egg in either season. Females that nest with male albinos will lay just one fertile egg from this pairing. Anodontosaurus nest in dense foliage and bushes. Anodontosaurus may form same-sex pairs, but may not nest, & may adopt. Anodontosaurus who are temporarily sick with a Gastroenteritis Infection are temporarily infertile and may not nest.
