# Anodontosaurus – Courtship

When seeking a mate, Anodontosaurus instinctively travel to Dried Lake. This is the only time Anodontosaurus forgo the need to sleep. Anodontosaurus become Cathemeral while courting at Dried Lake. Courtship is not driven by either sex. Instead, it is initiated by the most confident individuals. Courtship begins when a confident individual steps into the center of a larger gathering - or open arena type place - and bellows to summon a challenger. Once a challenger accepts and steps forward, the two circle briefly before charging into a bout of spinning and heavy tail strikes against one another - a battle that continues until one participant falls to half health. Males and females freely challenge one another, with no gender limits on who may compete. This often results in a lot of same-sex pairings, though these couples do not produce biological offspring of their own. Anodontosaurus courtship works like a pyramid, with the winner of a challenge moving on to summon another challenger. When courtship is over, pairs may form with impressive couplings producing much healthier offspring. Only healthy Anodontosaurus are selected as potential courting partners. Those suffering with Gastroenteritis are ignored during courtship challenges. Individuals form a temporary pairbond with the other they select, and remain with them until their offspring has reached Sub-Adult before moving on. Sometimes a bond remains strong, and a pair continue to travel together in a tumble.
