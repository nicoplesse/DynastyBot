# Halszkaraptor – Courtship

Courtship begins with hens bending their necks back and lifting their heads to the sky, while letting out soft coos. This gesture includes raising her tail high, and is performed deliberately in front of males she has interest in, for multiple occasions until the message is clear. Drakes that show interest in the performing hen watch her intently, before returning her display with one of their own - a mixture of bobbing and rocking in a dance-like motion. Impressed hens return the gesture, and the pair perform a dance together, cooing and chirping affectionately. This is finished off with the hen's original display, matched by the drake who copies her moves. Once this is finished, the drake follows along with the hen as she continues her performing for other nearby drakes - but may also slip away to court other hens at the same time.
