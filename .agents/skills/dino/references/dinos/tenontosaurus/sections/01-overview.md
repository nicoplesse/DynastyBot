# Tenontosaurus – Overview

Medium Cathermal Terrestrial Herbivore Tenontosaurus form polyandrous bonds. Tenontosaurus glades are territorial and claim medium territories. Tenontosaurus kindles are nomadic. Tenontosaurus females have an aggressive nature. Males have a tolerant nature. Tenontosaurus may group with: Bonapartenykus. Males may defend Bonapartenykus, while females do not. Albino and melanistic do not exist in Tenontosaurus. Tenontosaurus that display "blight" symptoms are shunned by the rest of the group.
