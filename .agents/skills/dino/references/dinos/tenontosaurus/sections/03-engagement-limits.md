# Tenontosaurus – Engagement Limits

Predator Reaction: Headstrong / Defensive Up to 4 Tenontosaurus may enter into an engagement. Females are headstrong, and may ignore an opponent’s forfeit. Males respect forfeits. Females take priority in engagements. In the absence of enough females, males may engage. Adolescent females may participate in challenges, and do not count towards the engagement limit. They must flee after receiving 2 hits. Tenontosaurus flee from 3+ Large carnivores and all Apex carnivores. Matriarchs cull offspring under Sub-Adult that are not hers if discovered within her territory. No species timer is set when committing infanticide. Preferred Diet Tenontosaurus primarily eat roots, nuts, and leaves, but also eat berries and flowers. A varied diet is essential to their survival. The matriarch ensures that unrelated males and young eat first.
