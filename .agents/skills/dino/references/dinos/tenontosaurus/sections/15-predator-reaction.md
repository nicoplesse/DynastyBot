# Tenontosaurus – Predator Reaction

Tenontosaurus flee if attacked by 3+ Large, or any Apex carnivores. They scatter in different directions, with some subordinates attempting to lure the predators away from the bucks, young, and matriarch. Otherwise, Tenontosaurus are willing to fight and defend themselves, their group members and their territory. Females take the lead in engagements, with the matriarch and subordinates stepping in to defend the others. Bucks shy away from this responsibility - but will step up if needed, such as when in a kindle grouping, or when not enough does are available. Tenontosaurus fight back with extreme brutality, kicking and slamming their tails into the predator. They take turns running back and forth, attempting to overwhelm the hunters. Does are headstrong, and females will chase after forfeiting predators until they leave the territory - or, in the case of a solitary female, out of line of sight. Bucks, meanwhile, are defensive, and will respect a forfeit from a predator, as long as they leave the area.
