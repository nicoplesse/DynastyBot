# Tenontosaurus – Cross-Species Interaction

Tenontosaurus fear all raptor species - their natural predator - and prioritize challenging them over other intruders. If multiple species enter the territory, then Achillobator, Deinonychus, Latenivenatrix, and Utahraptor are targeted for expulsion first. Despite their territorial nature, they enjoy the company of and welcome Bonapartenykus to their group. The two species have a similar quiet nature, and Bonapartenykus aid Tenontosaurus in cleanliness, with bucks specifically bonding with them closely. In return, a glade provides a safe territory and nesting area. Migration is somewhat common compared to other territorial species, and is most often triggered by the sight of Barsboldia within their territory. The large hadrosaurs can easily use up the glade's resources - but also serve as useful defence. Barsboldia are too bulky and powerful to chase out, so instead a glade will follow behind a herd as it leaves, using them as a meat shield as they search for a new territory. If a glade finds Barsboldia whilst already migrating, they may begin following them. Bucks within kindles may care for young of similar size to fawns, but will quickly stop caring for them once they notice the young are behaving oddly. Tenontosaurus do not find chasing out Tiny dinosaurs necessary unless they are loud and irritating. Semi-aquatics within their territory are typically ignored, and not seen as a threat to the glade. Glades will only attempt to chase them out if they have been attacked first.
