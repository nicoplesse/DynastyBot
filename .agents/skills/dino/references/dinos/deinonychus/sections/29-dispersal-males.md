# Deinonychus – Dispersal - Males

Males are generally free to leave a dynasty at will, and often do so - especially if they have gone multiple seasons without being chosen for nesting. Dispersal for males is typically quiet. Males tend to slip away unnoticed, often during moments when the dynasty is distracted, such as during travel, feeding, or following a confrontation. Males rarely make formal goodbyes. Their departures are casual and non-disruptive, and the rest of the group usually does not chase them or stop them. Many males leave in pairs or small groups, especially if they have formed strong bonds with other males. These companions may go on to form a troop together. Occasionally, a particularly cunning male can lead a dual life, quietly moving between two dynasties, and nesting with multiple females. This type of behavior is rare, simply due to the demands placed on the male to support multiple females. Unrelated males can be welcomed back into a dynasty at any time, at the whim of the mother. Related males are only allowed back in if they are the direct sons of the mother. Males that witness a rival dynasty besting the one they are part of may choose to disperse later, and track down the winning dynasty.
