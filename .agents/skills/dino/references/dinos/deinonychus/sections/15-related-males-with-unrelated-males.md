# Deinonychus – Related Males with Unrelated Males

Related males have little influence over the dynasty's decisions, but may attempt to sabotage unrelated males they dislike. This is most commonly done by stealing, hiding or damaging gifts intended for females, particularly if the suitor is courting the male's mother or sisters. By reducing the quality of a rival's offerings, the related male can make him appear less desirable. Related males may also assist unrelated males that they do like and wish to keep within the dynasty. Having grown up alongside the females, related males often know which gifts are most likely to be appreciated. They may help favored suitors locate suitable offerings to provide assistance during courtship, or accompany them while gathering gifts.
