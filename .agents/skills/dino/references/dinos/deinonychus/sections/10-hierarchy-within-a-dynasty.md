# Deinonychus – Hierarchy within a Dynasty

The Mother Deinonychus groups that have females present have a strict social hierarchy that centers around a dominant head female, known as the mother. She is the most assertive and aggressive member of the group, and the one individual all other females are related to in some way. She is the main breeding female. The mother's authority is absolute, and her position is never challenged directly, and she does not pass leadership on to others. A dynasty is formed by one single female, sometimes alongside her sisters, and all other females within the group are either her descendants or close relatives. Her Offspring (Under Adolescent) The mother's recent offspring below adolescent rank directly below her. They receive the most affection and protection, and even poor behavior is often overlooked. The Preferred Male This is the mother's current chosen mate, who holds a unique position above all other adults, except her and the young in the group. He is allowed to sleep near her and eat before the other dynasty members. However, his status depends entirely on the mother's favor, and can be revoked at any time. Favored Daughters Daughters of the mother who have proven themselves through bullying, sparring, successful hunts or victories over rival dynasties are often favored by the mother, and become favored daughters. These daughters enjoy greater status, and often receive better treatment and access to food or nesting spaces. Favored Sons Sons of the mother that have proven especially loyal may be given a temporary favored son status when the group has a body. A maximum of one son may be chosen as 'favored' and allowed to eat from a corpse at the same time as the favored daughters. This position may switch each time the group has a body if the Mother has many sons she prefers. Favored Granddaughters Favored granddaughters are those chosen by the Mother, from offspring of the favored daughters. They rank just below their mothers as favored granddaughters. Other Female Relatives Remaining daughters and sisters of the mother fall next in the hierarchy. While still respected, they do not enjoy the same privileges as favored daughters. Other Offspring (Under Adolescent) Other juveniles and hatchlings within the group - those not directly born to the mother - come next. They are still given care and protection, but receive less tolerance for misbehavior. These are not permitted to be in the queue for food at a carcass. Their own mothers must wait their turn to be fed and then take a chunk for their offspring. Other Granddaughters & Nieces Other granddaughters and nieces rank next, and must wait patiently as their own mothers feed from the carcass before they may take their turn. All Other Males Males not selected as the mother's preferred male are lower ranking. Among them, unrelated males rank slightly higher than related males, as the dynasty prioritizes genetic diversity and will exile related males first when space is limited. Albinos, Non-Dimorphic Females & Plucked Females Regardless of sex or relation, albinos consistently rank at the very bottom of the hierarchy. They are shown less tolerance, and bullied by all other females within the group. This is the same for females who have been plucked and are still growing back their headfeathers. Hierarchy within a dynasty primarily determines the feeding order and how much favor an individual receives from the Mother. It is not a strict dominance hierarchy, and daughters are not expected to constantly submit to favored daughters - but may opt to do so to try and form alliances. Individual females of any rank or placing may spar, squabble and bully one another in order to compete for the Mother's attention. Granddaughters may spar with daughters, daughters may challenge favored daughters, etc. Females may harass and even bully the preferred male - so long as they are willing to face the consequences from the Mother if caught doing so! A female's rank reflects the Mother's preference, rather than social dominance over others.
