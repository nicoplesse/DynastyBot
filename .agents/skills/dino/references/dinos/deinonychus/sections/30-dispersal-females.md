# Deinonychus – Dispersal - Females

Female dispersal is rare. Dynasties are matriarchal and protective of lineage, and females are expected to remain loyal unless exiled. A female may choose to leave only under extreme circumstances - usually due to repeated scolding by the mother, excessive bullying from peers, or consistently being passed over for status or breeding. Females slip away in a manner similar to males, and are sometimes accompanied by a sister or daughter. As they cannot join other dynasties, those who leave must form their own. Females who attempt to return to their original dynasty are rarely welcomed. Only daughters or sisters of the mother are welcomed back in, while granddaughters and nieces are rejected. However, granddaughters and nieces may be permitted to join their own mother's dynasty.
