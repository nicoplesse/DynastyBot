# Deinonychus – Allied Female Sparring

While most disputes are settled through individual sparring between females, entire female alliances may occasionally challenge one another. Alliance sparring can involve up to three females per side, with the winning side crowned by the last female to reach half health. These alliance spars can be fought as: 3 females x 3 females 3 females x 2 females 2 females x 2 females These spars begin the same way as individual sparring. If an individual female is challenged by an alliance of 2-3 females, she immediately submits or flees if she is unable to gather her own support. The Mother may break up alliance spars before they start to allow 1v1 spars if she wishes. Losing females may turn to immediately bullying other, lower-ranking females or males afterwards.
