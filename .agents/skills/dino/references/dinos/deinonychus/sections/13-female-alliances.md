# Deinonychus – Female alliances

Within a dynasty, females often form powerful bonds with a particular sister - or, more commonly, their own daughters. As a result, nesting is crucial for most daughters, ensuring they raise potential allies that can help improve their status and provide support against other females in the group. These females often band together and form tightknit cliques. They groom one another and work together to isolate, harass and undermine other females in an effort to increase their own influence with the Mother.
