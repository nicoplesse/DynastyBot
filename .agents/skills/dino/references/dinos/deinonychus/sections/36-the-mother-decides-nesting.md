# Deinonychus – The mother decides nesting

Granddaughters and nieces are never permitted to nest. If caught courting - or worse, actually nesting in offspring - they may be exiled (temporarily or permanently), and their offspring slaughtered upon discovery. The mother nests only with her preferred male, with no other female allowed access to him. Attempts to court him are punished, and the mother will drive away both males and females who get too close to her preferred male. Sometimes, females may sneakily nest with the preferred male, when the mother's back is turned. If the female manages to raise the offspring to adolescence without the mother noticing, they are often accepted unknowingly. However, if caught, the female and the preferred male face the mother's wrath. She almost always attempts to cull the offspring, and may temporarily - or even permanently - exile one or both offenders. Any parents attempting to sneaky nest should leave their children out of the in-game group until they are adolescent. Females who displease the mother are blocked from nesting entirely. Attempts to court are interrupted and stopped, with both individuals reprimanded swiftly. In extreme cases, the mother may deliberately choose a female's male partner as her next preferred male. In contrast, females who remain in good standing are usually permitted to nest. The mother encourages maternal behavior within her dynasty with leading by example, believing that a nurturing environment increases the survival and success of her own bloodline.
