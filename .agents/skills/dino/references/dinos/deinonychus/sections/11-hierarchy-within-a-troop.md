# Deinonychus – Hierarchy within a Troop

All male groups are vastly different to groups containing females. Relationships between males are never as tense or based on dominance as they are among females. Troops do not have a strict rank system. While no male asserts dominance, others defer to the oldest or most confident individual, especially during migrations and when decisions must be made. This deference is subtle and not enforced. Males live in troops cooperatively. They groom each other frequently, share warmth during rest, and rarely fight. Disagreements are resolved through gentle shoving, vocal grumbles, or simply walking away. Meals are usually shared, with the finder or the one who landed the killing blow eating first. Hoarding or aggressing each other off of kills is extremely rare, usually reserved only for times when food is scarce. Troops are more cautious than dynasties. Without a female's natural aggression to back them, they avoid direct confrontations and often yield to larger predators or rival groups. However, they are clever and opportunistic, and adept at surviving by stealth and speed. Troops can consist of either related or unrelated males. Males often welcome other males into their group, but do not adopt or permit others below Sub-Adult to group with them.
