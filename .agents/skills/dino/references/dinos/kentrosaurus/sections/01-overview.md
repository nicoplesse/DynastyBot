# Kentrosaurus – Overview

Medium Cathemeral Terrestrial Herbivore Kentrosaurus form promiscuous bonds. Kentrosaurus are nomadic, but become sedentary when joining a stegosaurid herd. Kentrosaurus have an aggressive but avoidant nature. Kentrosaurus may group with: Miragaia, Stegosaurus. Kentrosaurus may defend these species. Kentrosaurus shun albino individuals, but tolerate melanistics.
