# Utahraptor – Offspring Maturity

Once adult skins come in, the Alphas once again assess young. Utahraptor born into the pack with different skins or colorations are quickly exiled from the pack by Alphas. This can be upsetting for the parents - but Alphas have been known to even exile their own offspring - those would-be Betas - should they not match the family bloodline skin or coloration. Parents have two choices: they can either watch their offspring be exiled and accept the Alpha's decision, or they may opt to leave with them, breaking away to create their own family pack as the new Alphas, with their offspring becoming new Betas. This decision is a huge one and results in them severing ties with the rest of their family - so is kept as a last resort by parents that are especially attached to a particular child.
