# Utahraptor – Solitary Behavior

A solo Utahraptor, shaped by the harsh life of an orphan or wandering between hunting grounds in search of a mate - or simply exploring - is a quiet and observant hunter. Their keen eyesight helps them scout herds, spot other carnivores, and identify potential mates. Lone Utahraptors will avoid others of their own kind, steering clear of rivals to avoid conflict. Only when interested in courtship will a solitary Utahraptor linger along the edges of an occupied territory. As solitary hunters without the support of a family, they may choose to target smaller prey, often the vulnerable offspring of herbivores. Utahraptor are social creatures and their ultimate goal is to find a mate and form their own pack, or get welcomed into another. Some individuals prefer to remain solitary for the entirety of their life. They can be seen trailing behind herds in the distance, keen to take advantage of opportunities that may come up.
