# Utahraptor – Nesting

This new bond results in a single egg in opposite sex pairs. The first clutch often takes longer to come into fruition, but the pair will still build a nest together each season to prove their dedication to each other. Seasons of unhatched eggs and deceased offspring can drive wedges between partners, perhaps signifying a difference in their combined genetics that make producing offspring together difficult. This can put strain on their relationship - but family members are quick to help soothe tensions. Once in a lifelong pair together, nesting happens whenever the pair opts to do so. They will work together to build a nest somewhere in the pack's territory, spending much time lovingly constructing it and filling it with soft, downy feathers and resources to protect and keep their future young warm.
