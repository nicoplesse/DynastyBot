# Utahraptor – Death

When a Utahraptor dies, or its mate is lost - whether through tragedy in battle, or absent for a year - the survivor grieves in ways both subtle and profound. If witnessing the death, the partner and pack watch from a distance, retreating to a known territory and sleeping side by side for comfort. Their nest, once a place of shared warmth, crumbles into neglect. Their energy shifts, pouring into the well-being of the pack's young rather than their own future. Utahraptor pack ranking summary: Alpha - founding pair Former Alpha - Parents to the new Alpha who have stepped down Beta - offspring nested by the current alpha pair Delta - grandchildren to the alpha pair and siblings related to one of the Alpha pair Omega - great grandchildren, offspring to only one of the Alpha pair, and offspring to the Alpha pair’s siblings (Delta). Omegas are forbidden from nesting. No further generations (great great grandchildren, etc) are permitted in the pack, and must be exiled. Unrelated - mates that are bonded to individuals in the bloodline. Utahraptor do not need to nest to be considered lifelong bonded. Same sex pairs are allowed in packs and can play valuable roles in nest site defence and raising of chicks. Parents with 2 or more eggs can give a hatched chick to a same-sex pair to raise, allowing the broody pair to contribute, and taking some stress off the overwhelmed parents. See social for more information. If one Alpha parent dies, and the other Alpha choosed to take a new mate and remain in their role, all current Beta ranked individuals are demoted to Omega rank. Beta ranked individuals must be the offspring of both Alphas. When a mate is deceased (death ingame, or character deleted), the bloodline must be updated to reflect the death. A new mate may only be courted once the nesting timer is expired. Alphas may exile members of their pack at their own discretion, and based on their own rules, so long as the profile is respected. Utahraptor are territorial, and claim a territory in their preferred POI which they only defend from other Utahraptor packs. A pack of any size can hunt in an unpreferred POI up to the engagement limit, but only when other Utahraptor are not in the POI. When entering a new, unpreferred POI, they will move on if spotting others of their kind. If a child disperses to start their own pack as an Alpha, and their parent(s) chooses to go with, the parent will be classed as "Former Alpha" even if they were not Alphas previously. They receive this rank with the benefits and restrictions of the role. If parents of any rank disperse to start their own pack as Alphas, their offspring of any age may choose to go with them. Any dispersals must immediatley edit their bloodline log to reflect this change. Utahraptor nest defence count as territory challenges, and can only be done if the rival is within the POI borders of the pack's territory. One Alpha may oversee new mates or offspring nested in. Utahraptor can seek another mate when mate is absent or has died. If absent, it is 14 days from the last nesting post (if the last nest you made was 14 days ago and you want to take a new mate, you can). If there has been no nesting posts between the pair, it may be 14 days from the most recent interaction. If an Alpha loses their mate and opts never to re-court again, they remain as the sole Alpha, but cannot nest until finding a new partner. Utahraptor do not have exemptions to 6.7.2.1. If Utahraptor stop or sit within the territory boundaries of a territory they have just lost, they may be attacked and may continue to be aggressed, even if they have correctly forfeitted. In order for a forfeit to be considered valid, 2+ fear calls must be heard AND paired with fleeing. A single fear call, just fleeing, or fear calling without fleeing, is not sufficient to indicate a forfeit. The territory challenge may continue if the forfeit was issued incorrectly. Utahraptor characters that group with another must have a bloodline log post. If nested, players must do this immediately at hatchling. Bloodline posts are only submitted by the player who owns the character. When your Utahraptor character dies, you must find and update your bloodline log post to state its death and include a hammertime of the date. Omegas may nest; however, it would be considered a profile break for an Alpha to not exile the Omega, if the Omega is caught, or if the offspring (of any age) are found. All Utahraptor nesting posts must list the clutch number for each parent involved in the pairing. (ex. Mother Clutch #: 1, Father Clutch #: 2). Nesting pairs may not always have the same clutch number, if one of the individuals in the pair has had a previous mate. When taking an egg from a Utahraptor nest, the clutch numbers of both parents must be included in the offspring's bloodline log. This is in place for all Utahraptor nests made and Utahraptor born after Sonntag, 2. August 2026 18:00. Nests and bloodline logs from before this time may be edited to include the correct information, but is not required.
