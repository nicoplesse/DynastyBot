# Utahraptor – All Utahraptor become elders after 20 clutches.

Players wishing to play their Utahraptor as elders without reaching 20 clutches may do so. Elders are infertile, and cannot nest. Elders may willingly opt to pass down their Alpha status to a preferred child and their mate. Some opt not to, and prefer to remain in the Alpha role, while others are happy to pass the role down to younger and more physically fit candidates.
