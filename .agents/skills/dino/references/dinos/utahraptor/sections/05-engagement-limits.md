# Utahraptor – Engagement Limits

Engagement with other species: Up to 5 Utahraptor may participate in hunts and defense. Territorial behavior: Utahraptor do not challenge other species out of their preferred POI. However, if they are challenged by another territorial species, up to 5 Utahraptor from the same pack may fight for the right to remain in the POI. Hunting limits: Solitary Utahraptor can hunt up to Medium tier prey. 2-4 Utahraptor can hunt up to Large tier prey. A full hunting party of 5 members can hunt Apex tier prey if no other prey options are in LOS.  Otherwise, they may hunt Large tier and below. Utahraptor cannot start a hunt if ungrouped Utahraptor are nearby. If rivals appear mid-hunt, the hunt continues until a body drops or is forfeit. Afterward, the rivals must be chased out of LOS. Adolescent Utahraptor do not assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Utahraptor do not hunt other species. Challenges against other Utahraptor In a Compact Territory: A maximum of 5 Utahraptor on both sides may engage. In a Grand Territory: The entire pack may engage. Encountering Utahraptor outside of preferred POI: Utahraptor become avoidant of others of their kind. They opt to move on from the POI if spotting other Utahraptor already there. Preferred Prey Utahraptor can hunt prey up to Apex tier. Exception: Utahraptor will only hunt Apex tier if there are no other prey options in LOS. Utahraptor are unable to eat the bodies of Metriacanthosaurus without suffering averse side effects. See 📜・server-guideOPTIONAL - Illnesses & Injuries for more information.
