# Utahraptor – Profile Specific Feature

In a family pack, all related members must share the same colors from the current Alpha pairs combined skins for easy identification. These colors are referred to as "the pack's color scheme". This means all related Utahraptor must have at least 1 color from the pack's color scheme. Offspring that hatch with a different color will be shunned and exiled. Further details can be found under "Social Interaction." Color restrictions are mandatory for all related pack members. Those who do not align with the bloodline must be exiled. Unrelated mates can be any color or skin, though Alphas may set their own rules for mate selection. Some may reject potential mates based on skin, color, subspecies, or even the quality of the offering presented. See Courtship & Parenthood for more information.
