# Utahraptor – Defending their territory (POI) from other Utahraptor:

When an unfamiliar Utahraptor crosses the border into a pack's territory, the entire pack will growl menacingly to intimidate and chase off rivals from approaching any further. The approaching Utahraptor group must aggressive call :threaten_call: 3 times across any of its groupmates to show their desire to challenge for the territory. If no aggressive calls are given by the approaching Utahraptor, the defending Utahraptor group must aggressive call :threaten_call: 3 times total across any of their members to warn that they will attack, allowing the approaching Utahraptor time to leave the territory. If the warning is ignored or the challenge is accepted with an aggressive call :threaten_call: by the intruder, then: In Compact Territories, up to 5 chosen individuals will leap forward to battle up to 5 members of the other pack. In Grand Territories, the entire family pack will charge forward, aiming to eliminate the rivals. Oftentimes one pack will forfeit when faced by the sheer pack size of their rival - preferring to relocate than compete so severely outnumbered. Utahraptor territorial challenges are a vicious battle. These fights are extremely aggressive, often brutal, and can result in death of the entire challenging pack if neither side forfeits their claim. Victory is determined by which family pack manages to eliminate their rivals, or drive them out the POI. Forfeiting the POI is signaled the fear call :danger_call: played  repeatedly while fleeing the POI. If one member of the pack forfeits, the entire pack must forfeit also, and must withdraw from the POI. The victorious pack must allow the fleeing pack to leave, but may escort the losers out without attacking if a proper forfeit is given, to ensure they leave the POI. Utahraptor are not cannibals, but are not wasteful, and will eat from a Utahraptor body if one is available. Utahraptor are territorial, but are only territorial to their own kind. Other species are not chased out, and Utahraptor may contest when challenged for the right to remain in the POI.
