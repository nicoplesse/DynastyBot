# Argentinosaurus – Overview

Giant Cathemeral Terrestrial Herbivore Argentinosaurus form harem bonds, that sometimes develop into lifelong pairbonds after 5 clutches. Argentinosaurus are sedentary. Argentinosaurus have a passive nature. Argentinosaurus may group with: Amargasaurus, Apatosaurus, Camptosaurus, Citipati,  and Struthiomimus. Argentinosaurus do not defend these species, and do not allow themselves to be used as a meatshield. Argentinosaurus tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.
