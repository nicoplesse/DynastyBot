# Argentinosaurus – Elderly & End of Life (Optional)

Argentinosaurus are considered elders once they: As bulls, sire a total of 15 fertile clutches. As cows, raise at least 6 fertile clutches. Elders are considered respectable and noble, and often become much more hardheaded than they were in their prime. These elders - if they choose to group back with their siblings again and form a village - often become the leader of the group. With their expansive knowledge of migrational routes and threat responses, they are knowledgeable and wise. As their huge bodies begin to decline, Elder Argentinosaurus start to slow down. They often begin to trail at the back of a gathering, and usually become a predator’s main target. Elder bulls begin to give up on courtship, and may instead decide to observe bullfights and relax with their loyal harem. They spend much of their adult and elder lives grooming, bonding and strengthening their relationships. Elder cows may still become receptive to courtship, however their clutch sizes will dwindle until they are no longer fertile, usually to no more than 1 egg per clutch. They become disinterested in bullfights once they recognize their inability to breed, and will opt to rest and bond with their harem mates instead of court or nest. Once an individual recognizes their time has come, they often slow down, lingering much farther behind the herd. Often, one of the other individuals will recognize this, and call out in alarm. The rest of the group will circle back as the elder settles down, their body failing. Comforted by the rest of the herd, the elder often slips away swiftly and in peace shortly after this stopping of the herd. Once the individual is confirmed to have passed, the herd moves on to avoid being near the body. While the group keeps moving, all members often retain an uneasy air about them, uncomfortable after the passing of their herdmate. Individuals close to the deceased can become depressed, refusing to eat and sleeping much more often. If one member of a harem-bonded tower passes, the entire group will become heavily depressed. They seek comfort and security in the others and sadly, in a lot of cases, many of them meet their own fate shortly afterwards, long considered to be due to heartbreak.
