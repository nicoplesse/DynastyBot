# Deinosuchus – Engagement Limits

Only 1 sub/adult Deinosuchus may enter an engagement or hunt. Deinosuchus do not defend their partner, nor assist in hunting. Grouped individuals will share kills with one another. Mother Deinosuchus defend offspring up until adolescent. All hunts must begin from water in their preferred POIs. Deinosuchus may third party any engagement that reaches within 2 body lengths of water. Deinosuchus may pursue their prey in land until their stamina runs out. If they do not inflict fracture or reduce their prey to deathscars in that time, they must forfeit and return to the water. They may not stand, sit or lay to regain stamina to continue pursuing in land. Deinosuchus may utilize water to their advantage and move back and forth from water as needed during a hunt. Deinosuchus cannibalize albino individuals. Deinosuchus tolerate scavengers. Adolescent Deinosuchus do not assist with their parents’ hunts. Solo Adolescent Deinosuchus may attempt to hunt up to solo Large tier.
