# Stegosaurus – Adoption

All orphaned offspring of adolescence or younger are tolerated into the zeal - if not adopted entirely by the group. Females with no - or few - young often waddle over to the frightened baby and show them affection, encouraging them to follow her. Orphans rapidly get absorbed by Miragaia within the herd if they are present. Orphans get protected by all members of the herd as if they were biologically related. Orphans that age up (and are female) are sometimes permitted to stay if there is room for them. The male shares parental care between all the young in his herd - including Miragaia and Kentrosaurus young, though preferential treatment is always given to his own. The elder phase of a Stegosaurus's life begins after investing significant energy in continuing their bloodline. A fulfilling life transitions the zeal into a tightly bonded group, marked by each female in the harem successfully producing over 3 clutches with the male. Once lifelong bonded, the male and his females become inseparable. The need to compete with others of their species diminishes with age, with Stegosaurus rapidly becoming lazier. More time is spent lounging in the sun, with less time spent patrolling and managing resources. Sometimes, the will to even push out other Stegosaurus and Stegosaurid herd fades considerably, with less interest shown in territorial behaviours and more time spent migrating through favourite locations. Colors fade often with age, with once bright individuals losing some of the saturation in their scales and plates.
