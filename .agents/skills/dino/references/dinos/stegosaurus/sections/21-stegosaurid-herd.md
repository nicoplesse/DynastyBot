# Stegosaurus – Stegosaurid herd

When grouped with Miragaia and Kentrosaurus, up to 2 Sub/adult Stegosaurus, 1 Miragaia and 4 Kentrosaurus can work together to actively chase away the threat. These active defenders stop pursuing once a predator leaves 10 body lengths from the herd. (If the entire herd is only the active defenders, and a predator forfeits, the herd can pursue until the threat is killed. Once 1 body falls, combat must stop.) Any other stegosaurid in the grouping forms a defensive circle and does not chase. These defenders will still attack if a predator enters their personal space. If winning the fight seems unlikely, and a fellow stegosaurid reaches heavy death scars, those in the defensive circle begin to move away - hoping the active defenders notice and follow behind. Each group is unique. Some active defenders blindly rush at threats, while others have organized roles during times of danger. The engagement limit when an individual in mixed-species group is hunted is: 2 sub/adult Stegosaurus, 1 sub/adult Miragaia and 4 sub/adult Kentrosaurus.
