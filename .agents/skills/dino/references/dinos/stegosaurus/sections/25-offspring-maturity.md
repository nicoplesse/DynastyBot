# Stegosaurus – Offspring Maturity

As offspring reach Sub-Adult, they get pushed out of the zeal and wider stegosaurid herd by their father, who body blocks them and moves the group on from their current territory and into a new one. Sons split off and leave the area entirely and solo - though may travel (un-grouped) temporarily with their brothers until they find other stegosaurus or stegosaurid species they can group with. Daughters are permitted to trail behind the herd, un-grouped, so long as they leave a wide enough berth to not encroach upon the zeal's space (10 body lengths away) . They will be aggressed if they get too close. Daughters may often hover for a while in particular if there are young Miragaia or Kentrosaurus about to age out of the stegosaurid herd, providing them with companions to form their own stegosaurid herd.
