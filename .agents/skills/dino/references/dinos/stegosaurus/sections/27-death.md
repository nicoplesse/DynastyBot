# Stegosaurus – Death

When a Stegosaurus dies, the body is abandoned. A bonded partner might glance over the lifeless corpse, but ultimately, life resumes. A male with a full harem might never replace the female that was lost, as their lifelong bonds stay firm till death claims each individual. Females go through a long mourning period. Even if impressed by another male, she does not nest with him while still grieving the loss of her previous bull. The decision to move on is unique to each female - some remain unboned, while others slowly rekindle the bond with another. Physical Dimorphism is mandatory, and subspecies based. Males and females may be any skin that is not listed as a mutation. Stegosaurus male challenges end on a body drop, or forfeit call (:friendly_call:). The winner must respect the forfeit call. If a Stegosaurus, Miragaia, or Kentrosaurus in a mixed-species group is targeted by a predator, a set number of sub/adults will act as active defenders: 2 Stegosaurus, 1 Miragaia, and 4 Kentrosaurus. All other stegosaurids in the group form a defensive circle and only attack if the predator enters their personal space (within one body length). As a headstrong species, Stegosaurids ignore any forfeiture or surrender attempts made by predators. If the herd has individuals that stand their ground, the active defenders stop pursuing once the predator has retreated beyond 10 body lengths from the defending herd. However, if the “active defenders” consist of the entire herd - meaning no individuals are holding their ground - then the fight continues until the predator is killed, or the active defenders decide to give up. Stegosaurus may NOT switch subspecies after juvenile. Aggressive response: When migrating; Kentrosaurus, Miragia and Stegosaurus tolerate ungrouped individuals and other species.. When at a claimed resource; Kentrosaurus, Miragia and Stegosaurus do not tolerate anything within 10 body lengths of their resource. Stegosaurid herds cannot exceed the following group limits: 1 Sub/Adult male Stegosaurus + 3 Sub/Adult female Stegosaurus. 2 Sub/Adult Miragaia 8 Sub/Adult Kentrosaurus Offspring/adoptees under Sub-Adult for all species are unlimited.
