# Giganotosaurus – Infanticide

If a nest is discovered by a male who did not father the nest, he may attempt to kill the kits. The parents will fiercely defend their offspring. However, the male's main goal is the kits - if he successfully kills all of them, the parents will give up their attack on him, and the female’s nesting timer will be reset. Males do not need to stop at an offspring's body drop when slaughtering kits. However, if one of the parents’ body drops during the kits being killed, he must claim the body as his own, and cannot chase after fleeing kits. Females whose kits have been killed in this way immediately flee from the nesting site and entire POI, forcing the male who killed her kits (and the male who fathered them) to go out in search of her. If either successfully find her, they must attempt to court her in the typical fashion - but risk being rejected completely outright. Giganotosaurus generally tolerate most herbivores and carnivores within the same area as long as they are not interested in hunting, unless they are Tyrannosaurus or Tyrannotitan. Giganotosaurus tolerate scavengers, and enjoy the presence of grooming scavengers such as Compsognathus and Rhamphornhycus.
