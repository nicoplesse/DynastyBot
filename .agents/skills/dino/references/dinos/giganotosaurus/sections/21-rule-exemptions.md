# Giganotosaurus – Rule Exemptions

Giganotosaurus may challenge other Giganotosaurus, Tyrannotitan, and Tyrannosaurus for their body down, even though they cannot hunt them (6.4.1). This is considered a territory challenge, and the losing party must leave the POI or risk being killed. If the Giganotosaurus is successful, they reserve the right to remain in the POI and claim the body down. Giganotosaurus may not interrupt the hunts of Giganotosaurus, Tyrannotitan, and Tyrannosaurus, or any other carnivore to issue a territory challenge. If unwilling to wait for the hunt to conclude, they may seek out new territory. Giganotosaurus must challenge out all other Apex terrestrial carnivores from the POI. Additionally, they do not receive a species timer for challenges on Apex terrestrial carnivores after a challenge. 7.7 Giganotosaurus should not immediately try to hunt the species they have just challenged from the POI, unless the other is on deathscars. Giganotosaurus cannot co-exist in a POI with other Apex terrestrial carnivores. They must either challenge for the right to remain in the POI, or move to a different one. Giganotosaurus do not hunt Giganotosaurus, unless the rival is Albino or aged Adolescent and below.  If they kill them following a challenge, they can eat from their corpse.
