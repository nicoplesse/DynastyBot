# Giganotosaurus – Physical Dimorphism

Giganotosaurus males are larger than females. Giganotosaurus males flush their crests when interested in courtship. This profile may have high t and low t individuals. High t females may be larger in size, and have flashes of color on their crest. Low t males may be unable to flush their crest, and may be smaller in stature. ♀️ Female Giganotosaurus are smaller in size than their male counterparts, and a bit more nimble. Suitable subspecies include: Variation 3, Variation 4, Slim ♂️ Male Giganotosaurus are larger than females, best for taking down large prey. Suitable subspecies include: Stocky, Standard, Variation 5 Physical dimorphism for Giganotosaurus is not mandatory, but individuals differing from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.
