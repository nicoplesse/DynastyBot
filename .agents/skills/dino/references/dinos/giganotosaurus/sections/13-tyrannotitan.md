# Giganotosaurus – Tyrannotitan

Giganotosaurus have a fierce rivalry with the nocturnal predator Tyrannotitan. They refuse to share POIs with Tyrannotitan in any capacity and immediately challenge them for the right to remain and to keep the resources. If they lose the challenge, they are forced to leave the POI immediately. If unable - or unwilling - to challenge, they must leave the POI. They cannot co-exist in the same POI as Tyrannotitan. Giganotosaurus may challenge Tyrannotitan if they encounter or are awoken by them at night. Giganotosaurus must attempt to challenge Tyrannotitan before hunting anything else in the POI. Giganotosaurus must challenge adult Tyrannotitan first, and cannot hunt them. However, any individuals that are death scarred, Albino, or adolescent and below may be hunted by Giganotosaurus. Giganotosaurus may not interrupt an ongoing fight or break body protection rules in order to start a challenge with a Tyrannotitan.
