# Giganotosaurus – Tyrannosaurus

Giganotosaurus are somewhat wary of Tyrannosaurus, noting how highly territorial and aggressive this species is. Despite this, they may accept challenges in order to retain the right to stay in the POI, or must leave to find a new one.  They cannot co-exist in the same POI as Tyrannosaurus. If a fight is lost against a Tyrannosaurus, they must immediately flee the POI. Giganotosaurus may challenge Tyrannosaurus if they encounter or are awoken by them at night. Giganotosaurus cannot hunt Tyrannosaurus - they must attempt to challenge them. However, they may hunt albinos, deathscarred, and adolescent and below individuals. Giganotosaurus may not interrupt an ongoing fight or break body protection rules in order to start a challenge with a Tyrannosaurus. Giganotosaurus will first challenge any other Giganotosaurus (if paired), Tyrannotitan or Tyrannosaurus out of the POI, if within LOS. They cannot hunt these species unless they are deathscarred, albino, Adolescent or below. Otherwise, they prioritize Apex herbivores and Large tiers above other prey. Giganotosaurus may hunt any tier, but will not hunt Apatosaurus or Argentinosaurus when solitary. They only do so when in a pair, unless the individual is deathscarred, albino, or adolescent and below. Giganotosaurus are endurance hunters, who prioritize exhausting their prey. While they will land one ripping and tearing bite on their prey, tearing deep bleeding wounds into them, they do not rush fights and prefer to wear their targets down slowly to the point of exhaustion, before rushing in to finish them off. Giganotosaurus pairs work together closely, giving each other cues to go in one after the other to deepen the wounds the other left. A successful pair can leave a prey weak and exhausted, bleeding out and passing due to their wounds rather than by an onslaught of attacks.
