# Giganotosaurus – Nesting

After courtship is accepted, the female will pick an appropriate place for her nest, with the male following closely behind her. He aids her to build her nest, and together they fill it with meat to provide sustenance for their hatching young. The female picks a quiet and isolated place away from more populated or busier areas, ensuring that kits are not discovered in any time away she must take to drink or feed herself.
