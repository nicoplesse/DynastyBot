# Deinocheirus – Engagement Limits (Defence)

Predator Reaction: Defensive Sub/Adult Deinocheirus may have 1 defender, for a total of 2 engaged. The defender must be their mate. Unpaired individuals, even if grouped together, do not defend each other. Offspring of Adolescent and below may have 2 adult defenders - which must be their biological or adopted parents. 1 Deinocheirus may defend their adopted Struthiomimus groupmates. Deinocheirus fear Spinosaurus, and avoid them where possible. They only defend themselves and their mates/offspring from these species when fleeing is not possible. Deinocheirus may attempt to chase away up to Large tier carnivores. They avoid conflict with Apex carnivores where possible. Unless aggression is shown, Deinocheirus ingore Deinosuchus and Sarcosuchus. Preferred Diet Deinocheirus eat a varied diet, but ultimately balance it out with a combination of fish, shellfish, berries, fruits and plants.
