# Deinocheirus – Overview

Apex Cathemeral Semi-Aquatic Herbivore Deinocheirus form lifelong pairbonds. Deinocheirus are sedentary. Deinocheirus have a passive nature. Deinocheirus  may group with: Halszkaraptor, Struthiomimus. Deinocheirus may not defend Halszkaraptor, even when grouped with them. Deinocheirus tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.
