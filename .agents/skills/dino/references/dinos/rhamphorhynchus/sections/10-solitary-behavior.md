# Rhamphorhynchus – Solitary Behavior

Solitary Rhamphorhynchus do not struggle as severely as some other species when solitary, but may show signs of incorrect grooming and preening, and must prioritize fishing and scavenging in order to satiate their own hunger. These individuals will often seek out herbivore herds to follow as a form of companionship alongside themselves, but will trail behind at a distance, especially if shown aggression to.
