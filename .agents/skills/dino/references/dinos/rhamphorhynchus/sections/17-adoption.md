# Rhamphorhynchus – Adoption

Same-sex and opposite-sex pairs and harems will immediately adopt any orphaned chick they find, and will provide for it the same way as any other parents. Elderly Rhamphorhynchus are a rare sight; but prefer to travel exclusively by water or latched onto the side of a herbivore. Flight and moving on the ground provides a great deal of stress on their joints. Elderly Rhamphorhynchus are often much quieter than their younger counterparts, either to avoid predation or due to encroaching deafness.
