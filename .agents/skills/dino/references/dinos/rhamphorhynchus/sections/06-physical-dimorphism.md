# Rhamphorhynchus – Physical Dimorphism

Rhamphorhynchus have no physical dimorphism. This profile does have high and low T individuals; they exhibit the opposite color patterning than that of their biological sex every season. (i.e a high t female is bright in the dry season). However, this results in confusion during courting. ♀️ Females, known as pens, are more colorful in the wet season, and desaturated in the dry season. ♂️ Males, known as drakes, are more colorful in the dry season, and desaturated in the wet season.
