# Apatosaurus – Offspring Maturity

By Sub-Adult, offspring no longer receive the protection of their parents and become fully included members of the pod. They stay within their natal pods unless circumstances change, forming deep friendships and bonds with other members of the pod. With young now considered full grown, bulls return to their natal pods and part from their female mate. However - some individuals grow a deep bond with their female and form a lifelong bond, whereby the bull integrates into her pod permanently.
