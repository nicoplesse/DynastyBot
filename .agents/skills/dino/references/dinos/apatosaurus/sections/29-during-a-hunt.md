# Apatosaurus – During a hunt

When hunted, Apatosaurus defend only themselves, while the rest of the pod and herd move away from the engagement, keen not to be caught in any crossfire. Only if a calf of Adolescent age is attacked does an Apatosaurus receive defenders. Relatives of the calf - always the mother and father, or other relatives if those are unavailable - up to a maximum of 2 adults may step forward to defend the adolescent. Apatosaurus may attempt to charge and bluff charge at predators in attempts to deter them, raising their tail skyward to deliver sharp, ferocious lashes.
