# Apatosaurus – Pods

Within a herd, pods are the true social core of Apatosaurus life. Headed by a matriarch - usually the oldest female - they are close-knit familial groups, with each individual related in some way to the matriarch. Pod members remain in close contact, and travel adjacent to one another, falling in line behind the matriarch during migrations. The matriarch's position is never challenged for or questioned, with family members looking to her for reassurance and direction, and respecting her guidance for the group. Physical affection is common, with pod members leaning against one another, brushing necks and maintaining body contact while resting. Bulls remain in their familial pod until they find a mate - during which they transfer into her pod. This occurs directly after courting, and may be a temporary situation - until young are reared to Sub-Adult, that ends in them returning to their family pod - or permanent, when they form a lifelong bond.
