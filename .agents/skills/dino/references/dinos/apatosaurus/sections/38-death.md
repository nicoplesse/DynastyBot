# Apatosaurus – Death

When nearing the end of their life, Apatosaurus begin to fall behind the herd more noticeably, eventually forcing their pod to separate from the main group entirely. Eventually, this slowness results in a full stop, with the elderly individual dropping down to rest and pass away peacefully. The pod never leaves the individual's side during this period, and mourning behavior is frequently observed after an Apatosaurus passes.
