# Apatosaurus – Interactions outside of pods

Even beyond family ties, Apatosaurus are broadly tolerant of one another and often mingle freely at feeding grounds, mud wallows, and narrower passages on the migration route. Relations between different pods are usually controlled by the matriarch of each corresponding pod. Matriarchs that are friendly with one another may prefer to migrate with their pods moving alongside one another, and tolerate more free intermingling between pod members. Those that have more strained relations may opt to put distance between their pods, with matriarchs pushing to drive their pods away from the rivals. Bachelor bulls prefer to stick together at the end of the herd, but may attempt to court and socialise with different pods during migrations and rest stops. Lone females, however, may move between multiple pods, happy to interact with any members of the herd that tolerate their presence.
