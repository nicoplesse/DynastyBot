# Apatosaurus – Solitary Behavior

Solitary Apatosaurus - most often older bulls, or individuals wandering in search of a herd - tend to be quieter, actively listening out for the distant calls of any other Apatosaurus in the area. Though fully capable of surviving independently, it is rare that individuals opt for an entirely solitary existence, with many preferring to group with other sauropods, sentry species or other Apatosaurus when given the chance.
