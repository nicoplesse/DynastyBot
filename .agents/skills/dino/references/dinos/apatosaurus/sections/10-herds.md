# Apatosaurus – Herds

Apatosaurus herds consist of multiple pods and solitary individuals travelling together as one large mass. They are not led by one pod in particular, and typically follow whichever moves first. Movement throughout the herd is fluid, with pods drifting nearer or farther from one another depending on the terrain, resources - and personal relationships. While each pod maintains its own familial relationships, herd life offers collective protection and shared vigilance, with the passing of migratory knowledge between different generations.
