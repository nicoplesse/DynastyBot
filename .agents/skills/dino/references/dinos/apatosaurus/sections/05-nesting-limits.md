# Apatosaurus – Nesting Limits

🌧️ Rainy Season: 2 🥚 per 2 hours. ☀️ Dry Season: 4 🥚 per 1 hour. Apatosaurus nest in Volcano Island on Riparia, and Burned Forest on Gondwa. Apatosaurus may form same sex pairs. These pairs are considered to be their own pods, or may join with either individual's birth pod.
