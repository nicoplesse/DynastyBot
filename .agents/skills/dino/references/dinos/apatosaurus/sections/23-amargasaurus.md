# Apatosaurus – Amargasaurus

Apatosaurus are tolerant of Amargasaurus and may readily travel alongside them during migrations - particularly as mixed herds offer added vigilance, and safety in numbers. Though they may herd and group together, Apatosaurus tend to remain somewhat aloof, usually keeping their own pods intact rather than fully mingling socially with these smaller cousins. Interactions between the two species are peaceful but reserved: whilst Amargasaurus are gentle and affectionate towards Apatosaurus, Apatosaurus prefer to maintain personal space. In the Dry Season, Apatosaurus can become more irritable and less tolerant of Amargasaurus crowding their favored resources, and sometimes drive them off with bluff displays if they continue to press their boundaries.
