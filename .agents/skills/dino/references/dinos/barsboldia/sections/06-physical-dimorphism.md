# Barsboldia – Physical Dimorphism

♀️ Females, known as cows, are stockier. Suitable female subspecies include: Gibbosi ♂️ Males, known as bulls, have a more pronounced back hump or spikes. Suitable male subspecies include: Dorsum, Sicinskii. Physical dimorphism for Barsboldia is not mandatory, but those who do not conform to the species dimorphism receive no interest during courtship.
