# 9. Nesting Rules

**9.1** Courting may only be performed as described in profiles.

**9.1.1** Ensure to follow the profile in order to court realistically. Unrealistic courting should be rejected.

**9.1.2** Do not knowingly commit incest.

**9.1.3** For some profiles, infanticide (killing of same-species offspring) can be used to reset the nesting timer. Please refer to profiles.

**9.2** A nesting site must be claimed or placed after courting before offspring can be born. This must be in the species' preferred POIs.

**9.3** Nesting can only be done between opposite sexes and adults. Certain species allow same sex pairs to adopt and court. Both parents must be online and alive to post a nesting post.

**9.3.1** Once a courting is successful and a nesting site has been claimed, a nesting post must be made within 30 minutes.

**9.3.2** You cannot nest again if you have biological offspring in game that are adolescent or below.

**9.3.3** Nesting timers apply. You cannot nest indefinitely nor exceed egg limits without shop perks. Refer to profiles for species sex specific timers.

**9.3.4** You should stay for a minimum of 30 minutes after hatching offspring.

**9.3.5** If the nest has been posted, and the parent who did not post the nesting post dies, offspring may be brought to the nest.

**9.4** Nesting posts must not be deleted.

**9.4.1** Nesting posts expire after 30 minutes. Eggs cannot be claimed after the nest expires, and parents should send invites as soon as possible, if not in an engagement.

**9.4.2** Biological relationships between characters are only established via nesting.

**9.4.3** You may ask offspring not to quest, to resemble their parents, to roll/not roll on the bots, and to keep chat to a minimum.

**9.5** When taking an egg, you must be ready to be invited and must be in game.

**9.5.1** We will not reinstate growth if you claim an egg and are teleported whilst on the wrong species.

**9.5.2** You are required to stay for a minimum of 30 minutes after taking an egg. This timer begins as soon as you are in the nest in-game.

**9.5.3** Do not take multiple eggs at once, or ask parents to hold an egg spot for your friend.

**9.5.4** Do not remove a hatchling from a nest unless in the following circumstances: you are uncomfortable with the player, the hatchling took an egg and is not available in game for teleportation within 5 minutes, or you see the hatchling trying to join multiple nests at a time.

**9.5.5** If a parent dies, they may take an egg in their nest. Parents may not take eggs from their own nests if they are living.

**9.6** Offspring may be abandoned (or killed if the species commits infanticide) by their parents if:

**9.6.1** There is specific behavior outlined in the profile.

**9.6.2** The offspring is behaving unrealistically or outside of profile.

**9.6.3** The offspring is not following the rules outlined in the nest.

**9.7** All nestings must be logged in the appropriate nesting channel for the server the nesting occurred on.

**9.7.1** You may not use the nesting channel to post a nest if you are two same sex individuals and you wish to adopt. The looking-for channel may be used instead.

**9.7.2** If you are adopting an orphan, you must meet them in game first and adopt them into your nest second. You cannot teleport an adoptee via the nest feature.

**9.8** In-game nests ("vanilla nests") may be placed as decorative/resting areas without offspring.

**9.9** Nesting may only happen after successful courting in game, and a proper post or bot post has been logged with both parents information.
