# 4. Hunting Rules

**4.1** To start a hunt or body contest inside preferred POIs, a hunting party must have at least 1 hunter at 75% hunger or less (at the top of the stomach icon). The hunter with enough hunger must log their hunger with the !hunger command in Local or by sending the percentage in Group chat within 15 minutes before initiating a hunt or body contest.

**4.2** To start a hunt outside preferred POIs, all hunters must have hunger at 35% or less (just above the bottom of the stomach icon). All members of a hunting party must use the !hunger command in local and type their result in Group Chat within 15 minutes before initiating a hunt.

**4.2.1** After a successful hunt in a non-preferred POI, the entire hunting party has a 15 minute timer before they can hunt in any non-preferred POI again.

**4.3** Hatchlings, juveniles or any Albino skin (including other skins that appear Albino), and those on deathscars may be hunted at any time of day, regardless of activity schedule.

**4.3.1** An individual on deathscars is considered to have no size tier, and may be hunted by any carnivore.

**4.3.2** Hunts can continue if they start during the hunter's active hours and extend into their inactive hours. e.g. Diurnal species hunting into the night.

**4.3.3** Hatchlings and juveniles may be hunted at any time, even if they have adults present.          ᴘᴀꜱꜱᴡᴏʀᴅ: sunfish         You must be able to hunt the adult's tier during your activity schedule to hunt protected offspring outside of activity schedule.

**4.4** You may not start a hunt if there is an unclaimed body within your line of sight. The body should be claimed first. AI corpses do not count.

**4.5** During an active hunt, hunting parties must remain focused on the original target species. Hunters must forfeit before switching to hunt another species, unless the hunt is occurring within a mixed-species herd or group - in which case hunters may target any species that is part of that same group.

**4.5.1** When switching targets, a 15 minute timer applies to any species that traded hits during the engagement.

**4.6** Our stance and description of body denial - Do not make your body unreachable when on heavy deathscars or bleeding to death during an engagement.  This includes jumping or moving along cliffs, running into water, or dying in places where your body can’t be reached by the species hunting you.

**4.7** Prey can cross water during a hunt but cannot go back and forth to tire out the hunter. You may only cross water if you have enough stamina to make it across without drowning.

**4.8** A hunt ends when:

**4.8.1** A body drops on either side of the engagement.

**4.8.1.1** A body that drops by a third party during a hunt does not count. The hunter may choose to claim the body instead and stop the hunt, but is under no obligation to do so.

**4.8.2** A 2 call :friendly_call: from any hunter in the hunting party ends the hunt for the entire party. In carnivore vs. carnivore hunts, both parties must issue a 2 call :friendly_call: to end it.

**4.8.2.1** If a party forfeits, they cannot restart the hunt (but can still defend themselves).

**4.8.2.2** Herbivores with the Headstrong tag in their profile can ignore a forfeit from their hunter, and may continue attacking until the predator flees the POI, or line of sight.

**4.8.2.3** In carnivore x carnivore hunts, the hunted party may continue the hunt despite a forfeit from their hunters, regardless of activity schedule and hunger requirements.

**4.8.3** When all hunters have lost sight of the prey for 1 minute.

**4.8.4** When any member of the hunting party touches healing water at Hunter's Thicket or Hot Springs.

**4.8.4.1** During a hunt, prey may not run into healing water. This includes semi-aquatic species.

**4.8.4.2** Prey should not try to bait the hunting party into entering the healing water.

**4.8.5** When no attack has been made within a body length of the prey in 10 minutes or more.

**4.9** Any hunter during a hunt that reaches heavy deathscars automatically forfeits the hunt for that hunter, and must move 10 body lengths from the engagement.  The only exception to this is if the prey is also on heavy deathscars.

**4.9.1** The prey does not need to stop engaging unless the hunter has issued a forfeit call. A forfeit call from a hunter ends the engagement for all hunters.

**4.10** Herbivores may attempt to eat/drink while being hunted. Carnivores are not permitted to stop to eat, but may attempt to drink during a hunt.

**4.10.1** Any member of the hunting party that eats during an engagement forfeits the hunt for themselves individually.

**4.10.2** If the hunter has the ability to take a meat chunk from their living prey, they may use this ability without forfeiting the hunt.

**4.10.3** If a carnivore is the one being hunted, they may attempt to eat/drink freely.

**4.11** Any member of the hunting party that sleeps during an engagement forfeits the hunt for themselves individually, and must move 10 body lengths from the engagement.  Sitting down to regain stamina is allowed.
