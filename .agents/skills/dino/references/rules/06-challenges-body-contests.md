# 6. Challenges & Body Contests

**6.1** Challenges may only be done during a species active schedule. Species may defend their resource at any time.

**6.2** You cannot start a challenge if you are on a 15 minute timer with that species. This does not apply to defending your resource.

**6.3** You must respond within 10 seconds of the challenge being issued. This can be an acceptance of the challenge or a forfeit. You may not stand out of reach of the challenging species in order to ignore them.

**6.3.1** To accept a challenge, you must respond with an aggressive call :threaten_call: before engaging the challenging party.

**6.3.2** To forfeit a challenge, you must affectionate call :friendly_call: at the challenging party, and move away from the challenged resource.

**6.4** All engagement rules apply to challenges.

**6.4.1** If you cannot legally engage that species (or fear that species), you cannot initiate a challenge on them.

**6.4.1.1** This does not apply to defending your resource if challenged by others.

**6.5** A challenge ends when either a body drops, either side leaves the contested resource, or one member of either party affectionate calls :friendly_call: to forfeit the fight. The opposing party wins the challenge.

**6.5.1** If either party in a challenge flees until they lose LOS of the other party for 1 minute, this is considered a forfeit and the other party wins the challenge even if they lost LOS.

**6.6** Carnivores may not reply to challenge calls with a hunt.

**6.7** Territory challenges must begin with 1 broadcast call :broadcast_call:, followed by 3 x  aggressive calls :threaten_call: in succession from the challenging group to the owner of the territory before an engagement can begin. You must give players 10 seconds to respond to a challenge before attacking.

**6.7.1** Territory challenges can only be initiated by creatures that are listed as territorial.

**6.7.2** If you lose or forfeit a territory challenge, you must flee the POI immediately. If not sprinting, you must continuously forfeit call (:friendly_call: or :fear_emote:, per profile) to show the winning party that you have acknowledged their right to the territory, and are attempting to leave. You cannot return to the POI for 15 minutes.

**6.7.2.1** Failure to leave the POI in a timely manner can result in the territorial species continuing to attack or aggress your character. You cannot sit down to regain stamina, and must continue moving until out of the POI or territory.

**6.7.2.2** If you successfully challenged a species out of your territory and they return within the 15 minute timer, you are able to attack (and kill) on sight.

**6.7.2.3** If you win a territory challenge, you must stay within the territory for 30 minutes before relocating and challenging for another POI - unless successfully challenged out by another party within your timer.

**6.7.2.4** If a territory challenge is between a herbivore and a carnivore, and a carnivore dies, the rest of the carnivore's group may not claim the body, and must leave the POI. Otherwise, they may be attacked.

**6.7.3** Territorial species can challenge nomadic or sedentary species to force them out of their territory if they win.

**6.7.3.1** If a territorial species loses the challenge, it keeps its territory but must tolerate the other species for 15 minutes.

**6.7.3.2** Nomadic and sedentary species do not have to remain in the POI for 30 minutes after winning a territory challenge.

**6.7.4** Players with a body down cannot be challenged out of a territory until the body despawns, unless profiles specify otherwise.

**6.8** Challenges for nesting sites are the same as territory challenges. The "territory" is considered the nesting site.

**6.8.1** A nesting site is considered to be either: a cave, burrow/den, visual nest placed in the world, or a particularly dense piece of undergrowth for good cover. Some species may share nesting sites; please refer to individual profiles.

**6.8.1.1** A nesting site territory is only considered a defendable resource "territory" when the creature has live offspring in the nest.

**6.8.1.2** Nesting site challenges can be initiated by any species, not only territorial ones.

**6.8.2** If losing a nesting site challenge, you cannot re-challenge for that nesting site and must move on. This is reset once offspring of the owner of the nesting site have aged up or they have left the nesting site.

**6.8.3** You may not destroy other players' nests unless you have successfully contested for their nesting site. If no one is present at the nest, you must wait 10 minutes to see if the owner returns before you destroy it.

**6.8.3.1** Nests that end up accidentally damaged or destroyed during an engagement where the target was sitting on top of the nest are exempt from this rule.

**6.9** Resource or body contests are a challenge for a body down/player corpse, water source, or herbivore food resource. They must begin with 1 aggressive call :threaten_call:, followed immediately after by a bite or aggressive move. You must allow the owner of the resource 10 seconds to reply. If they do not reply, you may attack.

**6.9.1** Only carnivores can contest for a body.

**6.9.2** Only herbivores can challenge for food bushes or herbivorous food sources.

**6.9.3** Resource contests must happen during the species' activity schedule. Where they are for body contests, the challenger must match hunting hunger requirements for the POI.

**6.9.4** The defenders must give at least 3 x aggressive calls :threaten_call: to show the resource is being defended.

**6.9.5** You cannot eat, drink or take a chunk from the resource being contested for.

**6.9.6** A contest ends or is won when:

**6.9.6.1** Either party let out a friendly call :friendly_call:. The opposing party will then claim the resource.

**6.9.6.2** A body drops. The body is claimed (alongside the original body if during a body contest) by the opposing party.

**6.9.6.3** One member of one party intentionally leaves the area. The opposite party will claim the resource.

**6.9.7** If this is a body contest, and the body being challenged for despawns before or during the challenge, the challenger may turn it into a hunt.

**6.9.8** If you lose a resource or body contest, you must move out of line of sight of the resource and the owners of the resource.

**6.9.9** You should not move further than 10 body lengths from the contested resource during the engagement.

**6.9.10** Challenges for herbivorous food or water should not be fatal. The loser should move on and find a different source of food or water.
