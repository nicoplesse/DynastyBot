# 8. General Rules

**8.1** Mixpacking is not allowed for carnivores. Mixed grouping is allowed for certain herbivores as per profiles.

**8.1.1** If in a mixed herbivore grouping, you should not use certain abilities if you have those that buff a group overall. Specific abilities are listed on individual profiles.

**8.2** Subspecies can only be changed if you have mistakenly chosen the wrong one to match the species dimorphism, or to follow a behavior laid out in profile. You may never change your character's sex.

**8.3** You must use realistic skins where possible as specified in profiles. Where dimorphism is mandatory, you must follow subspecies, behavioral and color dimorphism. If it is optional and you choose not to follow the dimorphism, your character may be considered "low testosterone male" or "high testosterone female" and may be rejected during courtships. This varies depending on the profile.

**8.4** Cannibalism and infanticide is not permitted unless specified in the profile.

**8.4.1** Infanticide (killing of same-species offspring) is considered an engagement, and must abide by engagement rules, unless otherwise specified in profile.

**8.5** Players are expected to use common sense and act responsibly in all situations. If unsure about something, consider whether it aligns with realism, the spirit of fair play, and respect for others.

**8.5.1** If someone is rulebreaking against you, you cannot rulebreak in retaliation.

**8.6** Ensure that you abide by our Discord rules in game as well as in Discord.

**8.7** Simulating the act of "mating" in game is not permitted.
