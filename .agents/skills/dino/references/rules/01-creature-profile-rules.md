# 1. Creature Profile Rules

**1.1** Profiles always override server rules where there is a conflict.

**1.2** You must follow species profiles. Minor behavioral changes to fit different personalities are fine, but a blatant disregard of the profile is not permitted.

**1.2.1** Species acting wildly out of profile must be shunned by others.

**1.3** The grouping & engagement limits specified in profiles is mandatory.

**1.4** Activity schedules in each profile are mandatory. Your character must use their inactive time as a rest period (sleeping, sitting, relaxing), but may take part in social behaviors outlined in profile and walk short distances in the POI to eat or drink. Actions like migrating, hunting, and initiating challenges are limited to active hours.

**1.4.1** Territorial creatures may patrol their territories in small amounts during their inactive period. They may not challenge for the right to claim a new territory, but may defend their current territory.

**1.5** Players can travel the entire map, but may only court, nest, and claim territory in their preferred POIs.

**1.6** Unless otherwise specified in profiles: hatchlings, juveniles and adolescents may form unlimited groups.

**1.7** Herbivores and carnivores may not protect other species of the opposite diet at any time ("carebearing"/"mixpacking") unless otherwise stated in profile.

**1.8** Act realistically in the game. Refer to What does "Realism Roleplay" me… for specifics.

**1.9** This server features permadeath. If your character dies, it resets to hatchling and loses all relationships and mutations. Respawning as the character means treating it as a completely new character. Relationships may not be maintained by any party.

**1.10** All hatchling, juvenile, and characters with the DR Yearling and DR Stunted skins, are cathemeral and have no tier.

**1.10.1** If the offspring is protected, i.e. within 2 body lengths of an adult caregiver, and the adult is a size tier you cannot hunt, then the offspring cannot be hunted by you unless otherwise specified by profile.

**1.10.1.1** This also applies to cross-species adoptions.

**1.10.2** Adolescents are considered one tier below their adult tier. For example: an Adolescent Tyrannosaurus would be considered a Large.

**1.11** All creatures may defend their personal space (a radius of 1 body length around them) if continuously aggressed, invaded or engaged with by others. A player must aggressive call :threaten_call: the invader at least once before attacking if not attacked themselves. When defending their personal space, they are not counted as an active defender.

**1.11.1** When the invader leaves their personal space, attacks must stop.

**1.12** Dynasty Realism features genetics and skin mutations. Rolling is only required if you want a mutation skin as your dominant (worn) skin. You may only roll once per character's lifetime. More info in this resource.
