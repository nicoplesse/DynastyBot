# 2. Chat Rules

**2.1** Global chat must only be used to ask for player names politely. Whispers should be used to politely discuss rulebreaks. This must be after an engagement, not during.

**2.1.1** Do not accuse players of rulebreaks in your message, you must ask for names only and speak further by /whisper. e.g. "name of the rex that killed a red achillo, please?"

**2.1.2** Only one player from your group can make the name request.

**2.2** You must add "nb" at the end of your request to indicate it's nothing bad if you just want to talk to the player, rather than point out a rulebreak. You may not say "potentially nb."

**2.2.1** Players do not have to respond to "nb" requests if they don't want to.

**2.3** Player(s) must reply to a name request issued in Global chat. You  must reply with a "." or any similar symbol.

**2.3.1** If multiple requests are posted at the same time, you may add more detail to make it clear e.g. ". (rex)".

**2.3.2** Do not add additional details e.g. “. but I was challenging you not hunting you”.

**2.4** After giving your name, further communication must only be via /whisper.

**2.4.1** Players who inform others of rulebreaks must be kind and aim to educate, especially if no loss has occurred.

**2.5** Local chat may be used freely, so long as you abide by our discord rules for chat conduct and are not currently in an engagement.

**2.6** If you type in Global chat by mistake, you must send another message with “wc” for “wrong chat”, no further clarification is needed.

**2.7** If you notice another player misusing Global chat, you may whisper them to advise them of the rules, but you are not allowed to respond to them in Global chat unless you are a staff member.
