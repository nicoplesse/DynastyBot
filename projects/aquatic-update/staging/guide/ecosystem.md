# Why This Suggestion
Today, every animal that lives in the water is a hunter.
All four aquatics, Tylosaurus, Leedsichthys, Kaiwhekea and Eurhinosaurus, are classed as carnivores, and Spinosaurus and Deinosuchus hunt the same waters.
There is no prey side in the sea. Nothing in the water plays the role the Parasaurolophus plays on land: a social, passive, robust herd animal that the hunters live from.
Without prey, hunters have nothing to do, and players have little reason to choose a life in the water.

## The Goal
Bring the water to life with a working food chain, built on the model of the land:
- **A prey side first.** Kaiwhekea becomes the "Parasaurolophus of the sea", Eurhinosaurus its fast, loud companion. Both eat only fish and never hunt players.
- **A gentle giant.** Leedsichthys never hunts, follows its own route and travels with a flotilla of Eurhinosaurus.
- **Hunters with their own niche.** Every hunter gets its own time, its own space and its own prey, so they do not compete for the same animals in the same place.
- **Behaviour instead of rule protection.** Prey survives through its own behaviour: night watches, rest places, speed and the group. The hunters win through clever play, not through numbers.
- **Few stat changes.** Only where a role cannot work without one.

# How the Ecosystem Works
## Three Sides
**The prey side:** Kaiwhekea and Eurhinosaurus. They eat only fish, shellfish and squid, flee from Apex and Giant carnivores, and defend themselves only with a limited number of defenders, just like the Parasaurolophus.
**The gentle giant:** Leedsichthys. It never hunts, wins almost every duel, and stands its ground against Livyatan in the deep.
**The hunters:** Tylosaurus, Sachicasaurus, Livyatan and Xiphactinus in the water, with Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus at the shores and in the channels.

## Every Hunter Has Its Own Niche
- **Tylosaurus** rules the open water by day and night, in the oceans and the channels.
- **Sachicasaurus** rules the shallows by night: kelp, reefs and beaches.
- **Livyatan** comes from the deep and takes care of the big ones: other Apex hunters.
- **Xiphactinus** hunts in batteries in the warm channels and the lakes connected to them.
- **Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus** keep the shores, rivers and channels, and meet the sea only at Palm Islands.

## No Safe Place, Only Better Times
Kaiwhekea and Eurhinosaurus have no place where nothing can reach them.
In the channels wait Xiphactinus, Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus. In the oceans wait Tylosaurus, Sachicasaurus in the kelp at night, and Livyatan everywhere except Palm Islands.
For the Eurhinosaurus, the route becomes the game. For the Kaiwhekea, it is the choice of colony and resting place.

# The Three Waters
The water of Riparia is split into three zones.

## Oceans
Kelp Vale, Coastal Bluffs, the Abyssal Depths, Palm Islands and Stonebed Shoal.
Cool, open water with the most valuable fish.
Home of Tylosaurus, Sachicasaurus and Livyatan. The Abyssal Depths are the deep eastern ocean, and Livyatan's home.

## Saltwater Channels
The warm channels between the islands: Volcano Islands, Twisted Forest, Stillwater Bog, Mudflats and Coastland Swamp.
Home of Xiphactinus, Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus. Tylosaurus passes through. The Leedsichthys route runs through them.

## Freshwater Rivers
Big Tree Overlook, Tallbrush Coasts, Cliff Edge Falls and Redwood Wind.
Of the water hunters, only Spinosaurus lives here. Flotillas and bales may visit the lakes and fresh water connected to the channels, but it is never a safe harbour.

## Palm Islands: The Front
Palm Islands is the only place where Sachicasaurus meets Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus, and they fight over territories there.
Livyatan never comes to Palm Islands. For Sachicasaurus, it is the safest and at the same time the hardest territory.

# The Roles
## Kaiwhekea "Kai" · The Parasaurolophus of the Sea
**Medium Diurnal Aquatic Piscivore** · modelled on sea lion colonies, elephant seals and dolphin schools
- Lives in bales of up to 16, led by a lead cow, escorted by up to 2 dominant bulls. Up to 4 Eurhinosaurus may join.
- Roams by day between fishing grounds and colonies in the channels and oceans, and hauls out on land many times a day.
- Eats only fish, shellfish and squid. From Sub-Adult it needs large fish regularly.
- Flees from Apex and Giant carnivores on sight. Up to 2 active defenders, and up to 6 adults form a protective ring around the calves.
- A Kaiwhekea seized by a Sachicasaurus is lost.
- Every Kaiwhekea has its own greeting. Courtship is a ritual around these greetings.

## Eurhinosaurus "Eurhino" · The Fast Shoal and Sentry
**Medium Cathemeral Aquatic Piscivore** · modelled on tuna with the whale shark, sailfish, dolphins and salmon
- Lives one of four ways of life and changes freely: alone, as a free shoal, as a Leedsichthys' flotilla, or with a Kaiwhekea bale.
- Loud, playful and fast. Sleeps with one eye open, so in a shoal of 3 or more one always stays awake.
- Eats only fish and crustaceans. Survives through speed, not strength.
- Flees from Apex and Giant carnivores and from Xiphactinus batteries of 3 or more. Up to 3 may defend.
- Makes the run upstream to its nesting river as one school.

## Leedsichthys "Leeds" · The Gentle Giant
**Apex Cathemeral Aquatic Filter Feeder** · modelled on the whale shark with its tuna shoal
- Never hunts, and wins almost every duel.
- Stubborn: follows its own loop through the warm channels, with trips into the oceans, and changes it for no one.
- May travel with one Eurhinosaurus shoal as its flotilla. Nobody defends anybody.
- Often stops to watch a Kaiwhekea courtship.
- The rival of Livyatan: never flees from it, and drags every fight into the deep.

## Tylosaurus "Tylo" · The Coastal Apex
**Apex Cathemeral Aquatic Carnivore** · modelled on the orca and the leopard seal
- Stays as it is: matrilines at the coast, the only Apex in both the oceans and the channels.
- Must hunt Kaiwhekea, Eurhinosaurus and Leedsichthys before anything else.
- Its equal is Sachicasaurus: Tylosaurus rules the open water and the long fight.
- A single Tylosaurus flees from Livyatan. Tylosaurus pairs are the only hunters allowed to hunt an adult Livyatan, and they rarely dare it.

## Sachicasaurus "Sachi" · The Night Hunter of the Shallows
**Apex Nocturnal Aquatic Carnivore** · modelled on the jaguar, the saltwater crocodile and the Tyrannotitan
- Males hold one half of an ocean POI, ten in all, or a secondary territory in Volcano Islands or Twisted Forest. Males without one are wanderers and only fish.
- Hunts by night in kelp, reefs, shallows and at beaches, and waits for resting bales and flotillas.
- The only hunter that can seize a grown Kaiwhekea.
- Flees from Livyatan, and is safe from it in the shallows and on the high rock ledges of the Abyssal Depths.
- Females raise their young in the nursery at Tallbrush Coasts. Scars never heal, and tell the story of every Sachicasaurus.

## Livyatan "Livy" · The Hunter from the Deep
**Giant Cathemeral Aquatic Carnivore** · modelled on the sperm whale and the orca
- Lives in every ocean except Palm Islands, centred on the Abyssal Depths.
- Cows live in units of up to 3, young bulls in bachelor pairs, adult bulls alone. Always hunts alone.
- Hunts by sound: Echolocation, Dreadful Clicking, Concussive Blast, then the bite.
- Must target solitary Tylosaurus and Sachicasaurus first. Never follows prey into the shallows.
- Never hunts styracosternans or their herd companions, and protects them in the water.
- All Livyatan court in the bay of Coastal Bluffs.

## Xiphactinus "Xiph" · The Raptor of the Sea
**Medium Cathemeral Aquatic Carnivore** · modelled on piranhas, sharks, goatfish and tarpon
- Lives in batteries of up to 6 in the channels and the lakes connected to them. Never in the open ocean, never on land.
- The fastest swimmer in the game. Loses every duel alone.
- "From the outside pure chaos, from the inside pure control": the battery cuts off its prey and closes the boil around it.
- Hunts up to Medium tier, up to Large with 4 or more, and a lone Deinosuchus with 6 or more.
- Flees only from Tylosaurus.

## Deinosuchus "Deino" · The Crocodile of the Channels
**Apex Cathemeral Semi-Aquatic Carnivore**
- Stays lazy, territorial and deadly at the shore.
- Lives only in the warm saltwater channels and at Palm Islands, and no longer travels the open oceans.
- Fights Sachicasaurus over territories at Palm Islands.

## Spinosaurus "Spino" · The Lord of the Rivers
**Apex Cathemeral Semi-Aquatic Carnivore**
- Keeps its lakes, rivers and channel territories.
- Of the open oceans, only Palm Islands stays claimable. The other sea areas belong to Sachicasaurus, Livyatan and Tylosaurus.

## Tropeognathus "Trope" · The Fisher in the Sky
**Small Cathemeral Aerial Carnivore**
- Not a water animal, but part of the picture: follows flotillas from the air and dives in at their fish.
- Never groups with them, and leaves the water when Eurhinosaurus or Kaiwhekea drive it off.

# Who Hunts Whom
- **Kaiwhekea** is hunted by Tylosaurus, Sachicasaurus (the only one who can seize an adult), Xiphactinus batteries (when a Kai is cut off from its bale), Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus. Livyatan only takes it when hungry or without larger prey.
- **Eurhinosaurus** is hunted by Tylosaurus and Xiphactinus above all. It is the prey a battery can take for sure, if it catches one.
- **Leedsichthys** is hunted by Tylosaurus, by any two Sachicasaurus after the hunting call, and may be hunted by Livyatan. Its young are hunted by Xiphactinus batteries.
- **Xiphactinus** is hunted by Tylosaurus, and by the crocodiles that can carry it.
- **Tylosaurus and Sachicasaurus** are the targets of Livyatan, and each other's rival.
- **Livyatan** has one hunter: Tylosaurus pairs.

## Who Catches Whom
Sprint swimming speed: Xiphactinus 1200 › Eurhinosaurus 1050 › Kaiwhekea 990 › Tylosaurus 900 › Livyatan 750 › Leedsichthys 600.
- Xiphactinus catches everything. The prey's answer is an early warning and a sudden turn.
- Kaiwhekea and Eurhinosaurus outswim Tylosaurus and Livyatan in open water. The big hunters must ambush.
- Sachicasaurus does not need to chase: what it has seized does not escape.
- Livyatan catches no other Apex in open water. It only works from the deep.

## A Word on Fights
All fight numbers behind these profiles are bite duels. In water, agility and skill decide almost every fight: a single Tylosaurus can beat a Spinosaurus by circling it, even though it loses the bite duel. Rules that depend on a close duel are provisional until they are tested in game.

# Day and Night
- **By day:** Kaiwhekea bales roam, fish and haul out. Sachicasaurus does not hunt.
- **At dawn and dusk:** Xiphactinus batteries are most active. The Kaiwhekea lead cow calls the roll.
- **By night:** Sachicasaurus hunts the shallows, and the dominant Kaiwhekea bulls keep the night watch. Eurhinosaurus sentries sleep with one eye open.
- **Any time:** Tylosaurus in open water, Livyatan from the deep.

# What Changes on the Server
## New Rules
1. **New classification "Piscivore":** eats only fish, crustaceans and shellfish, and never hunts players. For group and mixed group rules (8.1), it counts like a herbivore.
2. **Leedsichthys and its flotilla:** one Leedsichthys per Eurhinosaurus group, no defence in either direction, no tier protection. Looming Presence may not be used in mixed groups (modelled on 8.1.1).
3. **Territories in water:** Sachicasaurus holds half an ocean POI, the way Spinosaurus holds split territories inland. Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus share the channel territories. Only the water counts; land animals in the same POI are not affected.
4. **Habitats:** the oceans for Sachicasaurus, Livyatan and Tylosaurus. The channels for Xiphactinus, Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus, with Tylosaurus passing through. Palm Islands is the only front between Sachicasaurus and Deinosuchus or Spinosaurus.
5. **Third party for Livyatan:** an exception to 3.17 and 3.18, only to protect styracosternans and their herd companions from an active hunt in open water.

## Stat Changes
- **Kaiwhekea:** health 750 → 1000, land sprint 100 → 167. It should not fall at once, like the Parasaurolophus.
- **Livyatan:** combat weight 6000 → 8000, health 950 → 2000, oxygen doubled. The second Giant after Argentinosaurus, able to beat single Tylosaurus and Sachicasaurus, and pairs of them.
- Leedsichthys already received combat weight 6500 and health 2000 from Dynasty. Tylosaurus, Eurhinosaurus, Xiphactinus and Sachicasaurus stay unchanged.

## New Species
Three mods join the server: **Xiphactinus** and **Livyatan** by Arazoa, and **Sachicasaurus** by Primordial Tyrants.

## Step by Step
If the server prefers not to take everything at once:
1. Rebuild Kaiwhekea, Eurhinosaurus and Leedsichthys. This needs no mod.
2. Add Livyatan, with its own niche in the deep.
3. Add Xiphactinus, because the prey side needs a fast hunter.
4. Add Sachicasaurus last, because it overlaps most with Tylosaurus.

# Dictionary
## Groups
**Bale** · a Kaiwhekea group of up to 16 Sub/Adults, with up to 4 Eurhinosaurus.
**Shoal** · a Eurhinosaurus group without a limit and without a leader.
**Flotilla** · a Eurhinosaurus shoal travelling with one Leedsichthys.
**Colony** · several Kaiwhekea bales hauled out together on a beach or rock. Each bale defends only itself.
**Battery** · a Xiphactinus group of up to 6 Sub/Adults, with no fixed ranks.
**Juvenile school** · up to 12 young Xiphactinus growing up together in Coastland Swamp.
**Brace** · two related Sachicasaurus males holding a territory together.
**Drift** · two related Sachicasaurus females travelling together.
**Unit** · up to 3 Livyatan cows that mated with the same bull, with their young.
**Bachelor group** · two Sub-Adult Livyatan bulls. It splits up when one reaches Adult.
**Matriline** · a Tylosaurus family of up to 6, led by the oldest cow.

## Roles in a Group
**Lead cow** · the experienced Kaiwhekea cow who guides the bale and ends quarrels with a head shake.
**Dominant bulls** · up to 2 Kaiwhekea bulls who defend the bale and keep the night watch.
**Aunts** · Kaiwhekea cows who help raise the calves of others.
**Protective ring** · up to 6 adult Kaiwhekea who surround the calves while the rest of the bale flees.
**Scouts** · up to 2 Eurhinosaurus who swim ahead of a free shoal to find fish.
**Sentry** · the Eurhinosaurus that stays awake while the others sleep.
**Ripper, blocker, biter** · the three hunting roles of a Xiphactinus battery, each with its own attack.
**Wanderer** · a Sachicasaurus male without a territory. Wanderers only fish.

## Places
**Saltwater channels** · the warm channels between the islands: Volcano Islands, Twisted Forest, Stillwater Bog, Mudflats, Coastland Swamp.
**Colony islands** · islands where Kaiwhekea bales with young stay until the young reach Sub-Adult.
**Birth beach** · the beach where a Kaiwhekea bale nests, chosen by its lead cow, on any coast.
**Half territory** · one half of an ocean POI, held by a Sachicasaurus male.
**Secondary territory** · a Sachicasaurus territory in Volcano Islands or Twisted Forest, for males who find no ocean territory.
**Nursery** · the shallow north coast of Tallbrush Coasts, where Sachicasaurus mothers raise their young.
**Rock ledges** · the high ledges of the Abyssal Depths, where Sachicasaurus is safe from Livyatan.
**Courting bay** · the bay of Coastal Bluffs, where all Livyatan court.
**Nesting river** · the river a Eurhinosaurus school runs up to nest.

## Behaviour and Rituals
**Piscivore** · eats only fish, crustaceans and shellfish, and never hunts players. Counts like a herbivore for group rules.
**Greeting** · the personal combination of calls and emotes of every Kaiwhekea. Cows take on the greeting of the bull they choose.
**Roll call** · at dusk the lead cow calls, and every Kaiwhekea answers with its greeting.
**Large fish need** · from Sub-Adult, Kaiwhekea must eat large fish regularly, which draws them into the oceans.
**Haul-out** · going on land to rest, like sea lions.
**Ways of life** · the four lives of a Eurhinosaurus: alone, free shoal, flotilla, or with a bale.
**The run** · the Eurhinosaurus school's trip upstream to its nesting river, without fishing or stopping.
**The boil** · the drumming ring of a Xiphactinus battery around cut-off prey. Each member darts in, bites once and returns.
**The frenzy** · Xiphactinus courtship: females chase the males until only half are left.
**Dawn hum** · every territorial Sachicasaurus male hums at dawn, and interested females answer.
**Flank display** · Sachicasaurus courtship at the surface, where both show their scars.
**Heart call** · the call that ends a Sachicasaurus courtship duel below 50% health.
**Mating bite** · the bite that ends every Sachicasaurus courtship.
**Pair night** · after courtship, a Sachicasaurus pair groups until the dawn after their first full night, and may hunt together.
**Hunting call** · the call after which any two Sachicasaurus may hunt an adult Leedsichthys together.
**Echolocation** · Livyatan's emote that shows targets within 150 m for 5 seconds.
**Air rule** · a Livyatan below 25% oxygen must break off and surface. This counts as a forfeit.
**Styracosternans** · hadrosaurs, Iguanodon and Lurdusaurus. Livyatan never hunts them or their herd companions, and protects them in the water.
**Body contest** · taking a body from another predator by challenge (6.9). The only way a Livyatan feeds from another's kill.
