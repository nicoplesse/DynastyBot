# Livyatan
"Livy"
Livyatan are giant raptorial sperm whales and the lone hunters of Riparia's deep oceans. They hunt by sound, rising from the dark below their prey, and hold their breath twice as long as any other air-breathing aquatic. Cows live in small units of up to three, young bulls roam in pairs before they grow into loners, and the Leedsichthys is their oldest rival.

%% banner Overview
**Giant Cathemeral Aquatic Carnivore**
Livyatan form temporary bonds. Bulls court the cows of a unit during the courting season, then leave.
Livyatan cows live in small units of up to three. Sub-Adult bulls may form bachelor pairs, adult bulls are always solitary.
Livyatan are nomadic within the open oceans: Kelp Vale, Coastal Bluffs, the Abyssal Depths and Stonebed Shoal, centred on the Abyssal Depths.
Livyatan never enter Palm Islands, apart from passing its deep outer edge on the way to and from the courting bay.
All Livyatan court in one place: the bay of Coastal Bluffs. See ✦ Courtship.
Livyatan have a tolerant nature towards other Livyatan, and an aggressive nature towards large aquatic predators.
Livyatan are not scavengers. They do not feed from carcasses they find, and may only take a body from another predator through a body contest (6.9).
Livyatan never hunt styracosternans or their herd companions, and protect them from other predators in the water. See ✦ Styracosternans & Their Companions.
Livyatan tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.

## Profile Specific Unique Feature
A Livyatan whose oxygen falls below 25% must break off the engagement and surface. This counts as a forfeit.
Livyatan hold their breath about twice as long as other aquatics that must surface to breathe. See ✦ Holding Their Breath.
All Livyatan court in the bay of Coastal Bluffs. Bulls travel there alone from every direction, and keep apart on the way with slow clicks. See ✦ Courtship.

## Group Limits (In-Game Group)
Up to 3 Sub/Adult cows that have mated with the same bull
Unlimited Offspring
Unlimited Adoptees

This group is known as a unit.
Daughters of a unit's cows may stay in the unit while there is room, even if they have not mated with that bull, such as a mother with her two daughters.

Up to 2 Sub-Adult bulls

This group is known as a bachelor group.
The bulls do not need to come from the same unit. Once one of them reaches Adult, the bachelor group splits up and both bulls live alone from then on.
Adult bulls are always solitary. They never group, not even with a unit during courtship.
Larger groups give a Livyatan away. A hunter that relies on silence and surprise cannot travel in a loud family.

## Engagement Limits (Defence & Hunts)
Livyatan always hunt alone. Only one Sub/Adult Livyatan may take part in a hunt, and members of its unit or bachelor group do not join or assist.
All members of a unit may defend their calves.
Bulls of a bachelor group may defend each other when attacked.
Livyatan may hunt up to Apex tier.
As a Giant, an adult Livyatan cannot be hunted by species limited to Apex tier, such as Sachicasaurus, which may only defend themselves against it.
- The one exception are Tylosaurus pairs: they may hunt adult Livyatan at any hunger level, but only when no Kaiwhekea, Eurhinosaurus or Leedsichthys are in the POI.
Livyatan keep a wider distance from engagements they are not part of, as the server rules ask of Giant species.
Livyatan only hunt in open and deep water. They do not follow prey into the shallows, or onto the high rock ledges of the Abyssal Depths, and never attack creatures on land or within one body length of the shore.
A Livyatan whose oxygen falls below 25% must break off the engagement and surface. This counts as a forfeit (4.8.2): it may not restart the hunt, but may still defend itself.
Livyatan do not hunt Parasaurolophus, Lambeosaurus, Barsboldia, Iguanodon, Lurdusaurus, or the companions of their herds. See ✦ Styracosternans & Their Companions.
Livyatan may only third party engagements to protect these species. See ✦ Styracosternans & Their Companions.
Adolescent Livyatan do not assist with hunts.

## Preferred Prey
Livyatan must target solitary Tylosaurus and Sachicasaurus first, if they are present in the POI. Tylosaurus and Sachicasaurus pairs may be hunted as well.
- Sachicasaurus only count while they are in open water, as Livyatan do not follow them into the kelp, the shallows or onto the high ledges of the Abyssal Depths.
Only when none of these are present, or at 35% hunger or below, may Livyatan hunt Kaiwhekea and Eurhinosaurus.
Livyatan may hunt Leedsichthys at any time, but are never required to. See ✦ Leedsichthys.
Spinosaurus and Deinosuchus keep to the saltwater channels and Palm Islands, where Livyatan do not go. Adults that stray into open, deep water may be hunted.
Livyatan never hunt styracosternans: Parasaurolophus, Lambeosaurus, Barsboldia, Iguanodon and both Lurdusaurus. They also never hunt the companions of their herds: Bonapartenykus, Camptosaurus, Citipati, Struthiomimus and Halszkaraptor.
Livyatan never feed from the bodies of these species, and never contest them.

## Nesting Limits

Livyatan nest in sheltered, deeper water close to the islands of Coastal Bluffs, near the courting bay. Units from the south may nest at the edge of Stonebed Shoal instead. This is the only time units come close to the shallows.

%% banner Growing Up
### ✦ Offspring Behavior (Nested)
Livyatan calves hatch near the islands and stay close to their mother's side.
Calves cannot dive deep or hold their breath for long. While their mothers dive to hunt, the rest of the unit stays near the surface with the calves and guards them in turn, like sperm whale babysitters.
At Juvenile, the unit leaves the islands and returns to open water. Calves begin to follow on short dives.
Calves learn to click and listen by copying their mothers.
Young bulls leave their unit at Sub-Adult. They may team up with another young bull in a bachelor group, or roam alone. See ✦ Bachelor Groups.
A unit has room for three cows, so a mother may keep up to two daughters with her. Other daughters leave to start a unit of their own.

### ✦ Orphaned Behavior (Wild Spawn)
Orphaned calves call constantly, and search for any unit that answers.
Units readily adopt orphans.
Calves that find no unit stay close to islands and reefs, where they can surface safely, until they are large enough to venture into deep water.
%% banner General Behavior
Livyatan hunt by sound. In the darkness of deep water they send out clicks and listen to the echoes, which tell them where prey is long before they can see it.
They spend much of their time deep below, and rise to the surface to breathe in long, slow rolls. Refilling such a large body takes a few breaths longer than it does for smaller aquatics.
Livyatan are not fast. They conserve their strength, cruising slowly, and only burst into speed for the final attack.
Units are vocal among themselves, with soft coda clicks, but fall completely silent while stalking prey.
Livyatan never enter Palm Islands. Their shallow waters are no place for a hunter of the deep. Only bulls on their way to and from the courting bay pass its deep outer edge, without stopping or hunting.

### ✦ Holding Their Breath
A Livyatan can stay under water about twice as long as any other aquatic that must surface to breathe. Its huge body is its air tank.
- Like modern sperm whales, it does not dive on a lungful of air. It stores most of its oxygen in its blood and in its muscles, which are dark, almost black, from the oxygen-binding myoglobin they hold. The larger the body, the larger this store.
- A large body also uses less oxygen for its size than a small one.
- During a dive, the heart slows down. The blood then supplies mainly the brain and the heart, while the muscles live on the oxygen stored in them.
- Modern sperm whales regularly stay down for 45 minutes to an hour.
Individuals that wear Pressurized Hide stay down longer still, about half again as long.

### ✦ Downtime Behavior
Units rest together at the surface in a tight formation, with the calves in the middle.
After every dive, cows greet each other with long exchanges of clicks.
Bachelor pairs rest side by side. Adult bulls rest alone, drifting near the surface between long dives.
%% banner Social Interaction
Units are close, female-led families. Cows share the care of all calves, rest together at the surface in a tight formation, and greet each other with long exchanges of clicks after every dive.
A unit is led by its oldest cow, who remembers the best hunting grounds.
Although units live together, every Livyatan hunts alone. Members spread out before a dive, and meet again at the surface.

### ✦ Units
A unit is made of up to 3 cows that mated with the same bull, or a mother with her daughters, together with their calves.
The cows nest close together and raise their calves as one family.
When two units meet, they may travel together for a while, but they do not group in game.

### ✦ Bachelor Groups
Young bulls that have just left their unit often travel in pairs, like the bachelor schools of young sperm whale bulls.
They rest side by side at the surface and practise ramming each other, but each dives and hunts alone.
As they grow, they drift apart: once one of them is an adult, they go their separate ways.

### ✦ Bulls
Adult bulls lead a solitary life. They range far, and only seek out units during the courting season, at the bay of Coastal Bluffs.
Two bulls that meet outside of courtship usually ignore each other.

### ✦ Albino & Melanistic Behavior
Both albino and melanistic Livyatan are accepted by their units.
%% banner Aggressive Interaction
Livyatan are tolerant towards other Livyatan outside the courting season.
Cows do not fight over bulls.

### ✦ Ramming Contests
During courtship, rival bulls fight by ramming each other head-on with their massive heads. They bite at each other's heads, and use Tail Slams.
Only Bite and Tail Slam are used. Piercing Bite, Dreadful Clicking, Concussive Blast, Sonic Disruption and Leviathan's Call are not.
These contests end when one bull turns away, at the latest when one falls to 50% health. They leave deep scars on the head, but rarely kill.
The loser leaves the courting bay, and may not return before the next in-game day. Two bulls contest each other at most once per in-game day.
Contests only happen in the courting bay. Cows never join them.
%% banner Cross-Species Interaction
Livyatan see most of the ocean as prey or as competition. The few species they spare, they spare completely.

### ✦ Styracosternans & Their Companions
Livyatan hold a strange fondness for hadrosaurs, Iguanodon and Lurdusaurus, and for the species that travel with their herds. They seem to see a certain beauty in them, much like wild orcas, which leave humans in peace. Perhaps it is the deep, resonant calls of the hadrosaurs, carrying far through the water.
On this server these are Parasaurolophus, Lambeosaurus, Barsboldia, Iguanodon and both Lurdusaurus, and the companions of their herds: Bonapartenykus, Camptosaurus, Citipati, Struthiomimus and Halszkaraptor.
Livyatan never hunt them.
When any carnivore hunts them in the water, Livyatan step in:
- Livyatan may third party an active hunt on one of these species while it is in the water.
- This applies against any carnivore, not only Apex carnivores: terrestrial hunters such as Tyrannosaurus or Tyrannotitan that follow their prey into the water, as well as marine hunters such as Tylosaurus and Sachicasaurus.
- This applies only in open, deep water, never within one body length of the shore.
- Once a protected animal has died, Livyatan may no longer step in. They never contest its body, as they never feed from these species.
When a predator hunts anything else, Livyatan never third party. See ✦ Other Predators' Kills.
These species feel at ease on the islands and coasts along Livyatan waters, where an attack from the water is rare.

### ✦ Leedsichthys
Leedsichthys are the closest thing to the great baleen whales Livyatan once hunted. Lone hunters track them by sound along their migration routes and rise from below.
The two giants of the ocean are bitter rivals. A Leedsichthys turns towards the clicking instead of fleeing, and every hunt becomes a struggle in the deep, like a sperm whale against a giant squid.
In a straight fight, a grown Livyatan beats an adult Leedsichthys only by a hair, and a Leedsichthys never flees. It drags the fight into the depths, where the whale runs out of air.
A Leedsichthys is nearly a Livyatan's equal, so a Livyatan is never required to hunt one, and chooses its moment.
Experienced Livyatan avoid fathers with pups, which do not even let go when the Livyatan breaks off to breathe, and fight until the whale is gone.

### ✦ Tylosaurus & Sachicasaurus
Livyatan see these predators as competitors and as prey.
A Livyatan that hears a Tylosaurus or Sachicasaurus in its waters hunts it down, or drives it out.
Sachicasaurus keep to the kelp and the shallows, where Livyatan do not follow. Some even hold territories in the Abyssal Depths, on the high rock ledges between the deeps. Echolocation finds those that stray into open water, or drop from their ledge to hunt.
Tylosaurus pairs are the only hunters of adult Livyatan, and even they rarely dare it. Those that do strike and pull away, trying to wear the giant down. A Livyatan that keeps them in a straight fight wins.

### ✦ Kaiwhekea, Eurhinosaurus & Xiphactinus
These are too small and too fast to be worth the chase.
Livyatan usually ignore them, unless they are hungry or no larger prey is available.

### ✦ Spinosaurus & Deinosuchus
Spinosaurus and Deinosuchus are treated with caution near the coast, where they have the advantage. In open water they are fair game.

### ✦ Other Predators' Kills
Livyatan are opportunists. Rather than join another predator's hunt, they let it make its kill, then rise from below and claim the body with their calls, much like sperm whales that steal fish from fishing lines today.
This is a body contest (6.9).

### ✦ Other Terrestrial Species
Other terrestrial species are of no interest to Livyatan. Units sometimes watch them from a distance when nesting near the islands.

### ✦ Predator Reaction
Livyatan fear little in the ocean. Only Tylosaurus pairs may hunt an adult Livyatan.
All members of a unit may defend their calves.
A Livyatan that must surface for air breaks off the fight, as its oxygen rule demands.
%% banner Hunting Strategy
Livyatan hunt from below and by sound, not by speed.
1. **Finding:** A clicking scan (Echolocation) reveals prey within 150 meters.
2. **Approaching:** The hunter dives below its target and closes in silently from the dark.
3. **Weakening:** Out of combat, a burst of Dreadful Clicking slows the target and weakens its armour.
4. **Stunning:** At close range, Concussive Blast stuns the water around the target. It loses speed and acceleration, and the hunter can close the gap.
5. **Striking:** Piercing Bite tears deep wounds and weakens the target's own bite. Tail Slam drives it away from the shore.
A Livyatan always hunts alone. It prefers targets that are alone, but a grown Livyatan can overpower a Tylosaurus or Sachicasaurus pair as well.
Only a bale of three or more Kaiwhekea standing together could turn the fight against it.
When a hunt fails and the target escapes into the shallows, Livyatan let it go.
%% banner Courtship & Parenthood
Livyatan form temporary bonds. A bull may court every cow of a unit, and takes no part in raising the calves.
All Livyatan court in one place: the bay of Coastal Bluffs. Sperm whale bulls leave the cold polar seas to find the cows in warm waters. Livyatan bulls leave the cold deep in the same way, and travel to the warm, sheltered water of the bay from every direction.

### ✦ The Bay
The courting bay is the large bay on the eastern side of the Coastal Bluffs island, north of Scale Lake. It opens to the east onto the Abyssal Depths, and deep water reaches right under the cliffs.
Its surface water is warm and sheltered, right beside the cold deep. Calves are born close by, and their mothers can still dive to hunt.
At the start of the courting season, units travel to the bay and wait there. They rest at the surface in their tight formation and greet each other with coda clicks. Units lie close to one another in peace.
Livyatan court nowhere else.
During the courting season, Livyatan do not hunt inside the bay. Ramming contests are the only fights there. Every Livyatan may still defend itself.

### ✦ The Journey
Bulls travel to the bay alone, each from its own waters. They should rarely meet on the way.
- **From the Abyssal Depths:** straight into the mouth of the bay, from the east.
- **From Kelp Vale:** along the northern cliffs, and round the north-eastern point of the island.
- **From Stonebed Shoal:** north through the open water along the outer edge of Palm Islands. This is the only time Livyatan pass Palm Islands. They keep to the deep outer water, and do not stop or hunt there.
On the way, a bull gives slow, powerful clicks (:broadcast_call:) every few minutes. They carry far, and tell every other bull where he is.
A bull that hears another bull's clicks does not swim towards them. He slows down, waits, or keeps to his own way, so that the bulls reach the bay one after another. The slow clicks of sperm whale bulls are thought to keep rivals at a distance in the same way.
If two bulls meet on the way after all, they pass each other at least 5 body lengths apart. There are no contests outside the bay.

### ✦ Courtship
1. At the mouth of the bay, the newcomer stops and gives his slow clicks. Bulls already in the bay answer with their own.
2. The newcomer may court any unit that no bull is courting yet.
3. To court a unit that already has a bull, he must beat that bull in a ramming contest. See ✦ Ramming Contests.
4. A cow that wants a bull swims alongside him and answers his slow clicks with soft coda clicks (:friendly_call:).
5. The two then roll together at the surface, touching, belly to belly, as sperm whales do when they court.
A cow that does not answer is not interested. The bull does not follow her, and may try another unit.
A bull may court every cow of a unit, and a unit has one bull per season. He stays close to it, without grouping, until the eggs are laid.

### ✦ Nesting
Most units nest close together in sheltered, deeper water near the islands of Coastal Bluffs, close to the bay. Units from the south may nest at the edge of Stonebed Shoal instead.
Once the eggs are laid, the bull leaves the bay alone, by the way he came. He keeps clicking, so that the bulls do not meet on the way back either.

### ✦ Parenthood
Cows of a unit raise their calves as one family.
While the mothers dive to hunt, the rest of the unit stays near the surface with the calves and guards them in turn.
At Juvenile, the unit leaves the islands and returns to open water.

### ✦ Offspring Maturity
Young bulls leave at Sub-Adult. They may form a bachelor group with another young bull until they are grown, then live alone.
A mother may keep up to two daughters with her in the unit. Other daughters leave to start a unit of their own.

### ✦ Adoption
Units readily adopt orphaned Livyatan calves, and raise them like their own.
%% banner Elderly
Old Livyatan dive less deeply and spend more time near the surface.
Old bulls become even more solitary.
Old cows remain with their unit, which shields them as it shields its calves.

### ✦ Death
When a Livyatan dies, it sinks slowly into the depths, feeding the deep for years to come.

## Profile - Quick View
Livyatan are Giant Cathemeral Aquatic Carnivores.
Livyatan cows live in units of up to three, usually sharing the same bull, with unlimited offspring and adoptees. Sub-Adult bulls may form bachelor groups of two, adult bulls are always solitary.
Livyatan are tolerant towards their own kind, and aggressive towards large aquatic predators.
Livyatan always hunt alone, and may hunt up to Apex tier. They must target solitary Tylosaurus and Sachicasaurus first, and may hunt Leedsichthys without ever being required to. Pairs of Tylosaurus or Sachicasaurus are no match for them either.
Livyatan hunt from the deep by sound: Echolocation, then Dreadful Clicking, then Concussive Blast, then the bite. They never follow prey into the shallows, or onto the high rock ledges of the Abyssal Depths.
Livyatan hold their breath about twice as long as other aquatics. A Livyatan below 25% air must break off and surface.
Livyatan never hunt styracosternans (hadrosaurs, Iguanodon, Lurdusaurus) or their herd companions, and may third party only to protect them. They take other predators' kills by body contest.
Leedsichthys are their rivals, and drag every fight into the deep. Their only hunters are Tylosaurus pairs, which rarely dare it.
Livyatan live in every ocean except Palm Islands, centred on the Abyssal Depths.
All Livyatan court in the bay of Coastal Bluffs. Bulls travel there alone from every direction, keep apart on the way, and ram each other only in the bay. The cows choose.
