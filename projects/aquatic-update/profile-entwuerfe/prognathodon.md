# Entwurf: Prognathodon als nächtlicher Revierhalter

Neues Profil für den Mod `ArazoaProg` (`UGC_M_Z857QV302V_SK`). Dynasty hat dafür noch kein Profil. Der Text unten ist auf Englisch, wie zur Einreichung.

**Idee:** Der „Tyrannotitan der Meere“, aber als Lauerjäger statt als Brutalo.
- **Vom Tyrannotitan übernommen:**
  - Männchen halten ein Revier, allein oder als Bruderpaar.
  - Weibchen ziehen umher und nisten im Revier eines Männchens.
  - Er ist nachtaktiv.
- **Von seiner Ausstattung kommt die Jagdweise:** still am Grund lauern, Erstschlag von unten, Gift, Sauerstoff- und Ausdauer-Entzug.
- **Lebensraum:** Prog lebt nur in den Ozeanen. Die Salzwasser-Flussengen zwischen den Inseln betritt er nicht.
- **Halbe Reviere:** Ein Männchen hält einen halben Ozean-POI, so wie Spino im Inland geteilte Reviere hat. Es gibt acht: Kelp Vale West und Ost, Coastal Bluffs Nord und Süd, Palm Islands Nord und Süd, Stonebed Shoal Nord und Süd. Sie sind im Tab auf der Karte eingezeichnet. Landtiere im selben POI bleiben unberührt.
- **Abyssal Depths ohne Reviere:** Dort gibt es keine Buchten zum Nisten, und es ist Livys Zuhause. Prog darf es durchqueren und dort jagen.
- **Palm Islands als Front:** Palm ist der einzige Ozean, den Prog mit Deino und Spino teilt, und der einzige, in den Livy nie kommt. Dort gibt es harte Revierkämpfe.

**Warum diese Werte** (Health 700 → 900, Armor 1 → 1,1, Heilung 0,83 → 1,5, also wie Tyrannotitan):

| Kampf | Mod-Werte | Neu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Prog gegen 1 Tylo | verliert (16 s) | gewinnt (19 s) |
| 2 Prog gegen 2 Tylo | verliert | gewinnt (24 s) |
| 1 Prog gegen 1 Kai | gewinnt (15 s) | gewinnt (15 s) |
| 1 Prog gegen 2 Kai, die sich stellen | verliert (9 s) | verliert (13 s) |
| 2 Prog gegen 1 Leeds | gewinnt (20 s) | gewinnt (20 s) |
| 1 Prog gegen 6 Xiph | verliert | verliert |
| 1 Prog gegen 1 Livy (Health 1200) | verliert | verliert (27 s) |

- Mit den Mod-Werten könnte er kein Revier gegen Tylo halten. Mit den neuen Werten kann er es, gegen Livyatan aber nicht.
- Gegen Beute ändert sich fast nichts. Ein Kai fällt in beiden Fällen nach 15 s.
- **Nachtwache:** Kai fliehen vor Apexen auf Sicht, wie der Para. Die Nachtwache schlägt also Alarm, statt zu kämpfen. Wer bis dahin nicht gelähmt ist, entkommt, denn Kai ist mit 990 schneller als Prog mit 820.
- **Nest:** Nur dort stellen sich Kai, mit bis zu 2 Eltern gegen einen einzelnen Apex, und 2 Kai schlagen einen einzelnen Prog. Er muss also aus dem Hinterhalt einzelne Tiere erwischen und darf keine Nester angreifen.

**Deino und Spino auf Palm Islands** (neue Werte, ohne Blutung):

| Kampf | Ohne Shell Crusher Song | Mit Song (Armor −15 %) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Prog gegen 1 Deino | verliert knapp (22 s, Deino behält 10 %) | gewinnt knapp (21 s, Prog behält 4 %) |
| 2 Prog gegen 1 Deino | gewinnt (12 s) | gewinnt (11 s) |
| 1 Prog gegen 1 Spino | verliert (13 s, Spino behält 51 %) | verliert (13 s, Spino behält 43 %) |
| 2 Prog gegen 1 Spino | gewinnt (14 s), ein Prog stirbt | gewinnt (12 s), beide überleben |

- Das sind reine Bissduelle ohne Wendigkeit. Im Wasser ist Prog als voller Aquatic der Wendigere und schwimmt um die halben Aquatics herum, so wie Tylo um Spino.
- **Deino:** Im Bissduell auf Messers Schneide, im Wasser mit Vorteil für Prog.
- **Spino:** Im Bissduell schafft ein einzelner Prog ihn nie, im Wasser trotzdem. Deshalb darf Prog adulte Spino auch allein jagen (entschieden am 03.11.). Das kommt selten vor, weil beide nur Palm Islands teilen.

**Was der Entwurf festlegt:**
- **Herausfordern:** Er fordert Tylosaurus und fremde Prognathodon-Männchen aus seinem Revier heraus. Auf Palm Islands auch Deinosuchus-Männchen und Spinosaurus, und er nimmt deren Herausforderungen an.
- **Deino und Spino:** Er jagt sie gern, muss aber nicht. Adulte Deino und Spino darf er auch allein jagen.
- **Xiphactinus:** Die beiden begegnen sich kaum, denn Xiph lebt nur in den Flussengen.
- **Flucht:** Vor adulten Livyatan flieht er.
- **Beaching wie Tylo:**
  - Er darf Tiere jagen, die bis 1 Körperlänge vom Wasser entfernt stehen. Zuerst packt er, was er clampen kann (Breaching Clamp, Tragkraft 2500), und zieht es ins Wasser.
  - Adulte Apex-Landfleischfresser greift er nur an, wenn sie schwimmen.
  - Lange am Strand zu liegen, ist für ihn gefährlich. Junge sterben, wenn sie länger als 5 Minuten gestrandet sind.
  - Gejagt wird auch hier nur nachts.

**Realismus:** Prognathodon hatte ein massives Gebiss für große und hartschalige Beute wie Meeresschildkröten. Das Kai-Profil ist von Meeresschildkröten inspiriert, deshalb ist Kai seine bevorzugte Beute. Gift und Nachtaktivität sind kreative Freiheit; das Gift kommt aus den Fähigkeiten des Mods.

---

Prognathodon was a large mosasaur of the Late Cretaceous, reaching up to around 12 meters, and found across the seas of North America, Europe, Africa and beyond. Its short, deep skull and massive, robust teeth set it apart from other mosasaurs. They suited crushing large and hard-shelled prey such as sea turtles.

Profile inspired by jaguars, giant groupers, Tyrannotitan and Prognathodon paleobiology.

## Overview

**Apex Nocturnal Aquatic Carnivore**

- Prognathodon may take part in all activities other than hunting during the day.
- Prognathodon form harem bonds.
- **Males:**
  - Males are territorial. They claim one half of an ocean POI as their territory.
  - A male allows one solitary female, or one bonded pair of females, within his territory, and permits them and any offspring he fathers to share his kills.
- **Females:** Females are nomadic, but become territorial at their nest site when nesting.
- **Nature:**
  - Males have an aggressive nature towards ungrouped Prognathodon males and towards Tylosaurus, and a passive nature towards females.
  - Females are tolerant towards other species, and passive towards males.
- Prognathodon live only in the oceans. They do not enter the saltwater channels between the islands.
- Prognathodon are not scavengers.
- Prognathodon tolerate albino and melanistic individuals.

## Genetics

Skins that are considered mutations for Prognathodon include: Albino, Melanistic.
Players must roll in genetics-rolls to use these skins in game. This is one roll per species, per character.

## Group Limits (In-Game Group)

- **Same-sex pairs:** Up to 2 related Sub/Adults of the same sex.
  - Male pairs (brothers, half-brothers, or father and son) are known as a brace.
  - Female pairs (sisters, half-sisters, or mother and daughter) are known as a drift. A drift may be joined by unlimited offspring.
- **Solitary males:** A solitary male may group with one related Adolescent male offspring.
- **Opposite sexes:** Adult individuals of the opposite sex never group, even when nesting.
- **Bloodlines:** All Prognathodon that group together must log their relations in bloodline-logs.

## Territory

- **What a territory is:**
  - A male's territory is one half of an ocean POI, like the segmented territories of Spinosaurus inland. There are eight:
    - Western and Eastern Kelp Vale
    - Northern and Southern Coastal Bluffs
    - Northern and Southern Palm Islands
    - Northern and Southern Stonebed Shoal
  - The territories are marked on the profile map.
  - The Abyssal Depths hold no territories. Prognathodon may cross them and hunt there.
  - Land within the same POI is not part of the territory. Terrestrial species there are not affected.
- **Challenges:**
  - Males claim and defend their territory at night.
  - During the day they may patrol it in small amounts, and defend it if challenged (1.4.1).
  - Males challenge Tylosaurus and ungrouped Prognathodon males out of their territory. They follow 6.7: one broadcast call, then three aggressive calls.
  - **Palm Islands:** Palm Islands are the only ocean Prognathodon share with Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus. There, males also challenge Deinosuchus males and Spinosaurus, and respond to their challenges.
- **Tolerated species:**
  - Males do not challenge Kaiwhekea bales, Eurhinosaurus shoals or Leedsichthys. These pass through freely by day, and become prey by night.
  - Males do not challenge Livyatan.
- **Losing a territory:** If a male or brace loses a territory challenge, any females nesting with them either follow or leave the POI without them.

## Engagement Limits (Defence & Hunts)

- **Numbers:** Up to 2 grouped Prognathodon may take part in a hunt or a territory challenge.
- **Tiers:** Prognathodon may hunt up to Apex tier. Adult Leedsichthys may only be hunted by a brace or drift.
- **Fleeing:** Prognathodon flee from adult Livyatan. As a Giant, an adult Livyatan cannot be hunted by Prognathodon, but a brace may stand its ground in defence.
- **Spinosaurus & Deinosuchus:** Prognathodon may hunt adult Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus, alone or as a pair, but are never required to.
- **Adolescents:** Adolescent Prognathodon do not assist with hunts.
- **Defence:** Females always defend their grouped offspring of Adolescent and below.

## Preferred Prey

- **Preferred:** Prognathodon prefer Kaiwhekea, especially individuals resting at the edge of a sleeping bale.
- **Next choices:** Eurhinosaurus and the young of any species.
- **Rival apex species:** Prognathodon like to hunt Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus at Palm Islands, but may also leave them be.
- **Leedsichthys:** Adult Leedsichthys that pass through a territory at night are prey for a brace or drift.
- **Tylosaurus:** Tylosaurus that refuse to leave a territory may be hunted.
- **Terrestrial species:** At night, Prognathodon may hunt terrestrial species that swim, or stand within one body length of the water (see Beaching).

## Beaching (same rules as Tylosaurus)

- **Reach:** Prognathodon may hunt creatures within 1 body length of the water. They always target those they can easily clamp first, in order to drag them into the water.
- **Apex carnivores:** Adult Apex terrestrial carnivores are only hunted when they are swimming in water.
- **The lunge:** Prognathodon may stalk the shore at night, looking for individuals drinking from or intending to swim across the water. They lunge out of the water and use Breaching Clamp or a bone-breaking bite to pull the target back into the water with them. This is dangerous, especially against carnivorous prey.
- **Third party:** Prognathodon may third party engagements that enter the water, or come within 1 body length of the water.
- **Staying beached:** Prognathodon avoid staying beached on land for long. The further they go on land, the higher the risk of drying out and dying to the heat.
- **Offspring:** Nippers below Adolescent die if beached for more than 5 minutes. Their mother, or the father if he is near, must push them back into the water, at considerable risk to herself.

## Nesting Limits

- 🌧️ Rainy Season: 3 🥚 per 4 hours.
- ☀️ Dry Season: 2 🥚 per 4 hours.

Females nest in sheltered, shallow coves within a male's territory. A female drives out other ungrouped females she sees near her nest.

## Physical Dimorphism

- ♂️ Males are bulkier, with broad, heavily scarred jaws.
- ♀️ Females are sleeker, with smoother skin.

Suitable subspecies and skins depend on the options the mod offers. Physical dimorphism is not mandatory, but individuals that differ from the typical dimorphism may face difficulties during courtship.

## Growing Up

### Offspring Behavior

- **Hatchlings:** Prognathodon hatchlings, known as nippers, hatch in the shallow coves where they were laid. They stay hidden among rocks and kelp.
- **Mother:** Their mother guards the cove at night and rests close by during the day.
- **Juveniles:** Juveniles follow their mother on short night trips, and learn to lie still on the seafloor and wait.
- **Adolescents:** Adolescents begin to hunt fish and critters on their own.
- **Sub-Adult:** At Sub-Adult, young males leave to find a territory of their own, often together with a brother. Young females may stay with their mother as a drift.

### Orphaned Behavior

Orphaned nippers hide in the deepest crevices they can find, and only come out at night to hunt small fish. They grow up quiet, cautious and solitary.

## General Behavior

- **Days in hiding:** Prognathodon are creatures of the night and of hiding places. By day they rest in caves, under cliffs and in dense kelp, and barely move.
- **Waiting:** As dusk falls they leave their shelter and settle on the seafloor near places where prey rests. They lie still for long stretches, using Diver Hum to make each breath last longer.
- **Air:** Prognathodon need more air than any other aquatic, so they keep to coastal waters where the surface is never far away.
- **Sound:** They are quiet hunters. Males make a deep, low hum at dusk, which carries through their territory and warns rivals that it is taken.

## Social Interaction

- **Males:** Males are solitary or live in a brace with a brother. Braces hold their territory together, and share every kill.
- **Females:** Females travel alone or as a drift with a sister or daughter. They move between territories, and choose the male whose territory offers the best hunting and the safest coves.
- **Harem:** A territorial male shares his kills with the females he allows in his territory, and with his offspring.
- **Lifelong bonds:** By mutual agreement, a female and a male may return to each other season after season.

## Aggressive Interaction

- **Rival males:** Rival males are met with open hostility. A male who finds another male in his territory gives the broadcast call and three aggressive calls, then attacks.
- **Females:** Females drive away other ungrouped females from their nests, but otherwise avoid each other peacefully.

## Cross-Species Interaction

- **Kaiwhekea:**
  - Kaiwhekea are Prognathodon's favourite prey.
  - By day, bales cross a territory unharmed while its owner sleeps.
  - By night, the bale's resting place decides everything. Experienced bales rest outside known territories.
- **Eurhinosaurus:** Eurhinosaurus are taken when the chance arises. Their loud calls often betray the predator's position.
- **Xiphactinus:** Xiphactinus live in the saltwater channels, which Prognathodon do not enter. The two rarely meet.
- **Leedsichthys:** Slow Leedsichthys that drift through a territory at night are prey for a brace or drift.
- **Tylosaurus:** Tylosaurus are rivals and are challenged out of the territory. A Tylosaurus that stays may be hunted.
- **Livyatan:**
  - Livyatan are the only animals Prognathodon truly fear.
  - Its clicks find them even in the deepest cave.
  - A Prognathodon that hears clicking leaves its territory until the Livyatan has gone.
  - Palm Islands are the only ocean Livyatan never enter.
- **Spinosaurus & Deinosuchus:**
  - At Palm Islands, Prognathodon and the two river giants fight hard over territory.
  - Prognathodon like to hunt them, but may also leave them be.
  - In open water a Prognathodon out-turns both river giants. It wins by staying mobile and striking from below, never by trading bites, because in a standing fight a Spinosaurus bites harder.

## Hunting Strategy

Prognathodon win with the first strike.

1. **Lurking:** On the seafloor near resting prey, motionless, with Diver Hum to stay down longer.
2. **Striking from below:** Breaching Clamp grabs prey near the surface. Toxic Lunge launches the hunter into its target.
3. **Taking the escape:** Neuro Toxin Snap drains the target's sprint stamina and oxygen, and Fin Tear slows it. A Kaiwhekea is faster than a Prognathodon. If the first bites do not take its sprint, the hunt is lost.
4. **Finishing:**
   - Charged Bite poisons the target.
   - Shell Crusher Song weakens the armour of everything nearby.
   - Tail Slap drives the prey away from its group.
5. **Breaking off:** When the bale's night watch raises the alarm, the bale flees. A Prognathodon that has not crippled its target by then lets it go. Nests defended by both parents are avoided, because two Kaiwhekea are enough to defeat a lone Prognathodon.

## Courtship & Parenthood

- **The male's hum:** In the courting season, territorial males hum through the night.
- **Visiting females:** Females travel from territory to territory, judging both the male and the coves his territory offers.
- **Choosing:** A female who chooses a male swims slow circles around him, and the two rest together on the seafloor until morning.
- **Nesting:** She then nests in a cove within his territory.
- **Defending young:** The male defends the territory, and with it the nest. If the father is within sight of his offspring being hunted while the mother is not, he may defend them.

## Elderly & End of Life (Optional)

- **Old males:** Old males can no longer hold their territory against younger rivals. They retire to quiet caves at the edge of the coast and hunt only what passes their door.
- **Old females:** Old females stay with a daughter in a drift.
- **Death:** When a Prognathodon dies, it sinks to the floor of the cove it knew best.

## Rule Exemptions

- **Territory:** A Prognathodon territory covers one half of an ocean POI, water only (proposed water territory rule). Terrestrial species on land within the same POI are not affected.
- **Beaching and third party:** The same as Tylosaurus: hunts within 1 body length of the water, and third party in engagements that enter the water or come within 1 body length of it.

## Profile – Quick View

- Prognathodon are Apex Nocturnal Aquatic Carnivores.
- They live only in the oceans. Males hold one half of an ocean POI as their territory, alone or as a brace of brothers.
- Females travel alone or as a drift, and nest within a male's territory.
- They hunt by night from the seafloor. The first strike must take the prey's sprint, or the hunt is lost.
- At the shore they follow the same beaching rules as Tylosaurus.
- They challenge Tylosaurus and rival males, and at Palm Islands also Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus, which they like to hunt. They flee from Livyatan.
