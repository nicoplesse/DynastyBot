# Entwurf: Leedsichthys in der Kai-Gruppe

Leedsichthys bleibt ein friedlicher Riese, und seine Werte bleiben gleich. Neu ist, dass er mit einer Kai-Gruppe gruppieren darf, ohne dass einer den anderen verteidigt. Außerdem ändern sich seine Beziehungen zu den Arten, die künftig nur noch Fisch fressen. Der Text unten ist auf Englisch, wie zur Einreichung.

**Warum Abstecher ins Süßwasser (vom Nutzer, 05.11.):** Die Routen sind sehr streng. Abstecher ins Süßwasser bringen Abwechslung und führen die Flotilla dorthin, wo die Landtiere trinken. Krill gibt es im Spiel auch im Süßwasser, und Leeds schwimmen dort hinein. Welche Seen und Süßwasser-POIs mit den Flussengen verbunden sind, steht nicht in den Kartendaten und muss im Spiel geklärt werden.

**Meine Sorge dabei, und warum sie sich auflöst:** Von den Wasser-Jägern lebt im Süßwasser nur Spino (Sprint 750), und Kai (990) und Eurhino (1050) schwimmen ihm davon. Ohne Grund würde das Süßwasser also zum sicheren Hafen. Den Grund liefert die Neugier auf Landtiere: Sie zieht die Flotilla ans Ufer und den Kai an Land, und dort warten die Fleischfresser vom Land. Dazu kommt der Xiph, der in die verbundenen Seen folgen darf. Ein sicherer Ort wird es so nicht.

**Warum die Route so läuft:** In den flachen Flussengen zwischen den Inseln ist das Wasser warm. Leeds zieht regelmäßig hinaus in die Ozeane, um sich abzukühlen. Kai und Eurhino finden dort die Fischarten, die für sie am wertvollsten sind. So haben beide Seiten ihren eigenen Grund für dieselbe Route, und Kai brauchen den Leeds nicht, um sie zu finden.

**Warum es keinen Schutz gibt:**
- **Kein Leibwächter:** Leeds gewinnt fast jedes Duell, weil er doppelte Armor hat. Nur der Livy schlägt ihn. Als Leibwächter würde er Kai und Eurhino unangreifbar machen.
- **Kein Stufenschutz:** Schutz über die Stufe würde Xiphactinus die Beute nehmen. Kai und Eurhino würden vom Leeds abhängig.
- **Was bleibt:** Er ist Gesellschaft und bietet Deckung, die man durch Bewegung nutzt. Beides ist klein und hängt vom Spielen ab.

**Warum Looming Presence in der Mischgruppe verboten ist:**
- Die Fähigkeit senkt die Armor aller nicht gruppierten Tiere bis CW 4250 im Umkreis von 50 m um 25 %. In den Kurven hat sie keine Abklingzeit, ist also sehr wahrscheinlich passiv.
- In der Gruppe würde sie jeden angreifenden Xiphactinus schwächen. Das wäre ein heimlicher Schutz über Werte.
- Vorbild für das Verbot ist Regel 8.1.1, die schon heute Gruppen-Buffs in Mischherden einschränkt.

## Overview (replaces the grouping line)

- Leedsichthys may group with one Kaiwhekea bale, which may include Eurhinosaurus. Only one Sub/Adult Leedsichthys may be part of such a group, together with its own offspring. A group with a Leedsichthys is known as a flotilla.
- Leedsichthys never defend the species they group with, and those species never defend them.

## Group Limits (addition)

- **Own species:** Unchanged. 1 Sub/Adult, and unlimited offspring if the parent is male.
- **Mixed group:** In addition, one Leedsichthys may join a Kaiwhekea bale of up to 16 Sub/Adults (Kaiwhekea and Eurhinosaurus combined).

## Engagement Limits (addition)

- Within a mixed group, a Leedsichthys only defends itself and its own offspring.
- It does not intervene in hunts against grouped Kaiwhekea or Eurhinosaurus, and does not move to block predators on their behalf.
- While grouped with other species, Leedsichthys may not equip Looming Presence.
- Abilities that weaken, slow or pull other creatures may only be used when the Leedsichthys itself, or its own offspring, is under attack, or when it engages a Livyatan. This covers Undertow, Inhale, Crushing Pressure and the debilitating calls.
- **Predator Reaction towards Livyatan:** Aggressive. Fathers with grouped offspring: Headstrong.
  - Leedsichthys never flee from Sub/Adult Livyatan, and do not tolerate them within 5 body lengths. They may attack them, and try to drag the fight into deep water.
  - Without offspring, a Leedsichthys respects a Livyatan's forfeit.
  - A father with grouped offspring is Headstrong towards Livyatan. He may ignore a Livyatan's forfeit, including a Livyatan that breaks off to surface for air, and keep attacking until it leaves the POI or his line of sight (as in 4.8.2.2). He wants the danger gone for his pups.
  - Livyatan calves are chased away, as Tylosaurus offspring are, but not hunted.
  - This applies to Livyatan only. Towards all other species, Leedsichthys keep their usual Defensive reaction.

## Migration (replaces the migration paragraph)

- Leedsichthys follow a great loop through the warm saltwater channels between the islands of Riparia: the Volcano Islands, Twisted Forest, Stillwater Bog, the Mudflats and Coastland Swamp.
- From the channels they regularly head out into the open oceans: Kelp Vale, the Abyssal Depths, Palm Islands and Stonebed Shoal. The shallow channels are warm, and the cooler ocean water lets them cool down before they return.
- Kaiwhekea and Eurhinosaurus share this route. The oceans hold the fish they value most, so bales and shoals often travel out together with a Leedsichthys.
- **Fresh water:** Krill gathers in fresh water too. Leedsichthys may leave the loop for detours into the lakes and fresh waters connected to the channels, alone or with their flotilla.
- Leedsichthys still readily abandon an area once the krill begins to decline. The route is marked on the profile map.

## Sleep (addition)

- A Leedsichthys that travels with a group tends to sleep with it at night, resting beneath or beside the sleeping bale.
- Sleeping together brings no protection. The bale keeps its own night watch, and the Leedsichthys does not wake to defend it.
- Leedsichthys without a group keep sleeping in short bursts, with no particular preference in where and when.

## Cross-Species Interaction (replaces the Eurhinosaurus & Kaiwhekea paragraph; adds Livyatan and Xiphactinus)

**Kaiwhekea & Eurhinosaurus.**
- Both species now live on fish and pose no threat to Leedsichthys or its pups.
- Bales and shoals often travel with a Leedsichthys, gathering beneath and beside it, much like pilot fish around a whale shark.
- Leedsichthys enjoy this company. Some slow their pace so the smaller swimmers can keep up.
- Pups soon learn to school alongside the young of both species.

**Tropeognathus.**
- Tropeognathus follow flotillas from the air and dive in when the flotilla reaches a gathering of fish. Leedsichthys stay passive towards them, as they do not eat fish. Driving them off is left to Kaiwhekea and Eurhinosaurus.

**Xiphactinus.**
- Xiphactinus packs are now the most dangerous hunters of Leedsichthys pups in the saltwater channels, where the batteries live. They use their speed to split the school.
- Fathers recognise their silhouettes and gather their pups at the first sight of a pack.

**Livyatan.**
- Livyatan are the only predators that regularly target adult Leedsichthys, and the two giants of the ocean are bitter rivals. Livyatan always come alone, rising from the deep.
- A Leedsichthys never flees from a Livyatan. Fleeing would be useless: the whale is faster, and in deep water there is nowhere to hide. When clicking echoes through the water, a Leedsichthys turns towards the sound, the way humpback whales rush in when they hear killer whales.
- It drags every fight into the depths. A Livyatan must breathe, a Leedsichthys never does, and Crushing Pressure steals what air the whale has left. Whoever needs air first loses.
- Fathers fear the Livyatan more than anything else. A father with pups does not stop when the whale breaks off. He follows it, even to the surface, until the danger to his pups is gone.
- Elderly and Albino Leedsichthys live in the deep ocean, the home of the Livyatan, and meet it more often than any others.
- Livyatan hunt in every ocean on the route except Palm Islands.

**Tylosaurus.**
- Unchanged: one of the most prominent predators, especially of pups.

**Land animals.**
- Leedsichthys are especially curious about land animals. When one walks along the shore, a Leedsichthys often follows it slowly through the water, as far as the depth allows, and raises its head above the surface to watch it. Whale sharks follow boats in the same calm way.
- The flotilla shares this curiosity. Eurhinosaurus and Kaiwhekea leap around the giant in excitement, and Kaiwhekea may go ashore to greet herbivores.
- As with every species, a Leedsichthys leaves a land animal alone once it shows discomfort.

## Rule Exemptions (addition)

- **Forfeits (4.8.2.3):** Leedsichthys keep respecting the forfeits of other carnivores, with one exception: a father with grouped offspring may ignore the forfeit of a Livyatan (see Engagement Limits).

- Leedsichthys may group with a Kaiwhekea bale (exemption from 8.1, under the proposed piscivore rule). There is no defence between the species, and Looming Presence may not be equipped while grouped (8.1.1).
